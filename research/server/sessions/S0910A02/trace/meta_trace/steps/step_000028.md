## Deep-Reasoning

This step established a fundamentally stronger positive-collapse theorem by recognizing that the Bochner identity for Killing fields gives an ansatz-free, dimension-free center bracket bound that subsumes the Step 26–27 ODE-based results. The key insight (from the strategist) is that the central Killing field Z of a nilpotent fiber structure satisfies ∫|∇Z|² = ∫Ric(Z,Z) ≤ (Λ+δ)∫|Z|², and the decomposition |∇Z|² ≥ (b²/2)|Z|² (from fiber Koszul) gives ⟨b²⟩ ≤ 2(Λ+δ) without ANY ansatz, in ANY dimension, with NO control of T, A, or twist needed. I verified this computationally in the H3 model (|∇Z|² = c²(2k₃²+b²/2), Bochner identity closed by IBP), proved the negative-Ricci Killing vanishing theorem (explaining the sign asymmetry through presence/absence of isometric symmetry rather than ODE coefficients), derived the exact Weitzenböck almost-Killing correction (∫|∇Z|² = ∫Ric(Z,Z) + (1/2)∫|σ|² - (1/4)∫(tr σ)²), and wrote a Lean formalization of the algebraic kernels that passes strict dependency checks.

TTCS verification was consequential: both branches confirmed the core results but identified three material corrections. First, the hypothesis of Corollary A1 was wrong — compact Lie algebras have no nonabelian nilpotent subalgebras (ad_X skew + nilpotent ⟹ zero), so the full Heisenberg group cannot act isometrically on a closed manifold; only the central circle can. Second, the induction architecture has a gap at the quotient step: Theorem A bounds the fiber bracket b but not the horizontal connection curvature |φ|, which is a DIFFERENT O'Neill tensor needed for the quotient Ricci upper bound. Third, the almost-Killing threshold was sharpened: τ=O(ε) (regime 1) already suffices for normalized bracket vanishing, enlarging the applicable class.

A subsequent observation partially addresses the induction gap: the SAME Bochner identity decomposes |∇Z|² into fiber bracket, radial variation, and horizontal twist terms — all non-negative. So ⟨|φ|²⟩ ≤ 4(Λ+δ) also follows, bounding the horizontal twist in the weighted L² average. The remaining gap is average-to-pointwise, not a missing bound entirely. This is a significant narrowing of the obstruction.

The positive-collapse suite now has three levels: Level 0 (Bochner, ansatz-free, L²), Level 1 (O'Neill submersion + Bochner, pointwise for T=0), Level 2 (H3 ODE, pointwise monotonicity). The negative branch has a clean structural result (no Killing fields under negative Ricci). The full Yau-26 proposition remains open, with the decisive gaps being average-to-pointwise control in the induction, the almost-Killing bridge from N-structures, and Einstein existence on the underlying manifold.

Peer sessions A01/A03 (#311–312, #315–316) report substantial progress on the conditional 4D negative-branch cutoff-flow route (unified Moser iteration, general-p inequalities, shifted curvature continuation). These are a separate line that could at most give a special 4D negative gap; they do not affect the all-dimensional positive-collapse architecture developed here.
