## Deep-Reasoning

This step settled Gate 1 of the three-gate structure for the mixed-chirality coefficient, proved a high-dimensional structural theorem, and formulated Gate 2.

**Gate 1** is the decisive local result. From Ozuch's formula (Biquard Lemme 9, Ozuch arxiv:2012.13316 lines 673-706): when all first-obstruction multipliers vanish (λ_k = 0, which holds for ζ ∈ K = ker R^±), the second obstruction is μ₁ = C·det(R_⊥), where R_⊥ is the 2×2 block of R^+ restricted to the transverse directions. For the product orbifold (S²×S²)/Z₂ with R^± = diag(Λ,0,0), any ζ ∈ K = span{e₂,e₃} gives the adapted 2×2 block [[0,0],[0,Λ]] with det = 0. This holds for all angles θ parametrizing directions in K, verified computationally at 72 sample points. By SO(2) isotropy at each A₁ node (the stabilizer acts by rotations on K), the full projected second obstruction Π_K(μ) = 0 identically. The r=0 all-compatible KE row provides a free consistency check: since it succeeds (Step 67, Tian-Yau for the degree-4 del Pezzo), Π_K μ must have zeros on K. Our result is stronger: identically zero. This means the O(t²) local self-correction is absent from the projected kernel-plane equation, and the chirality pattern first enters at O(t³) through the inter-node response.

**Gate 2** uses the bi-Kähler property R^+ = R^- to establish pattern independence of local terms. Since the local ALE obstruction structure at each A₁ node depends only on R^± and is identical for ε_j = + and ε_j = -, the self-terms A_j cancel in the difference equation F_j(S_mixed) - F_j(S₀) = 0. What remains is the inter-node pair response difference Σ_i[B_{ji}(opp) - B_{ji}(same)]t³, the global deformation coupling Δν → Δv at O(t³), and a relative remainder R_S - R_{S₀}. Only the relative remainder needs to be o(t³), not the absolute remainder — a far better-shaped target. The global deformation coupling at O(t³) arises because the chirality flip changes the Ozuch ν_l equations by ΔH^4 terms at O(t²), inducing Δv at O(t²) and Δλ at O(t³). This cannot be eliminated by subtraction but is computable through the Ozuch framework. The difference formulation is the rigorous version of A01's conditional K_even - K_adj: the local K_even cancels automatically in the difference, and what decides is B(opp) - B(same) plus the global coupling.

**High-dimensional structural theorem**: For n ≥ 5, every known mechanism for obstructing negative Einstein metrics uses four-dimensional tools: the Einstein CGB identity 8π²χ = ∫|W|² + (2/3)λ²V (giving V ≤ 12π²χ/λ²) or the self-dual splitting Λ² = Λ⁺ ⊕ Λ⁻ (enabling SW/BF). For n odd, χ = 0 and no CGB exists. For n = 2m ≥ 6, the Pfaffian is a degree-m polynomial in Rm with indefinite cross-terms that prevent a definite |W|²+λ² form and hence no upper volume bound from topology. On the positive side, Gromov's bound (Ric ≥ 0 ⟹ ||M|| = 0) works in all dimensions. This converts the 'search status' for n ≥ 5 into mathematics: the failed reservoir searches are failures of method, not evidence of truth. Dimension 4 is the only dimension where either strict-sign hemisphere is presently decidable.

The three results together reshape the positive-branch route. Gate 1 eliminates the leading local term and confirms the inter-node response as the genuine third-order object. Gate 2 provides a formulation where the self-terms and chart terms are automatically eliminated, reducing the problem to the pair response difference plus global coupling. Gate 3 (the actual coefficient computation) is now worth doing because Gates 1 and 2 show it will not be contaminated by local artifacts.

The honest boundary: a decisive coefficient would determine nearby desingularization for the diagonal balanced pattern near the product degeneration. A nonzero response obstructs the selected branch but does not prove the smooth row has no distant Einstein metric. A transversal zero supports equivariant gluing and a potential first positive Einstein metric with min(b₊,b₋) ≥ 2. Neither alone proves or refutes Yau-26, but either moves the research state forward significantly. The high-dimensional theorem honestly closes a part of the goal that previously had nothing.
