Construction 3 (obstruction-amplitude) is the most substantial and novel. Multiple singularities don't cancel (C⁰ is pointwise sup). Higher-order terms are O(t) and cannot annul the O(1) obstruction. The TTCS also precisely identified the core gap: the argument applies to metrics already in the gluing normal form; placing arbitrary C⁰-pinched noncollapsed metrics INTO this normal form requires a new theorem (the 'approximate-to-exact bridge'). Ozuch Theorem 0.2 does this for exact Einstein, not approximate.

Construction 4 (calibration) identifies #_k(S²×S²) as the most informative test family: Sha-Yang gives Ric > 0, Hitchin-Thorpe is satisfied, sign-blind CGB filter is silent, but Einstein existence is unknown for k ≥ 2. P_4^+ true would produce Einstein metrics on these manifolds — resolving a major open problem. A01 confirmed no direct LeBrun implication for this family.
