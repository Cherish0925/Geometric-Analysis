# Deformation theory of scalar-flat Kähler ALE surfaces

JH

Jiyuan Han

JA

Jeff A. Viaclovsky

## Abstract

We prove a Kuranishi-type theorem for deformations of complex structures on ALE Kähler surfaces. This is used to prove that for any scalar-flat Kähler ALE surface, all small deformations of complex structure also admit scalar-flat Kähler ALE metrics. A local moduli space of scalar-flat Kähler ALE metrics is then constructed, which is shown to be universal up to small diffeomorphisms (that is, diffeomorphisms which are close to the identity in a suitable sense). A formula for the dimension of the local moduli space is proved in the case of a scalar-flat Kähler ALE surface which deforms to a minimal resolution of $\mathbb{C}^{2} / \Gamma$C 2/Γ, where $\Gamma$Γ is a finite subgroup of $U \left(\right. 2 \left.\right)$U(2) without complex reflections.
