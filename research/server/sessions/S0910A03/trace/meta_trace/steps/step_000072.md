## Deep-Reasoning

This step produced a structural obstruction to the T²-invariant graph-manifold construction for Yau 26 negative branch, built from three independently verified mathematical results.

The first result is the conformal gauge elliptic structure. Replacing the degenerate Gaussian gauge g=dt²+e^{2a}dθ²+e^{2b}dx²+e^{2c}dy² (which has no θ-diffusion for the lapse a) with the conformal gauge g=e^{2φ}(dt²+dz²)+e^{2b}dx²+e^{2c}dy² produces a genuinely elliptic system. The principal symbol matrix of the natural three-equation system (E_{tt}+E_{zz}, E_{xx}, E_{yy}) in unknowns (φ,b,c) has determinant 2(τ²+ξ²)³, nonzero for every nonzero covector. This explains why all previous Gaussian-gauge BVP experiments (Steps 69–71) produced large E₀₀ and E₀₁ residuals: the system was degenerate, not merely stiff.

The second result is the anisotropy drift-Laplacian. Subtracting the E_{xx}/g_{xx} and E_{yy}/g_{yy} equations gives Δδ+∇σ·∇δ=0 where δ=b−c and σ=b+c. The TTCS verification confirmed this derivation, independently derived the equivalent divergence form div(e^σ∇δ)=0, and proved the rigidity corollary: on [0,R]×S¹ with Neumann at t=0, if δ is non-constant then oscillation at t=R strictly exceeds oscillation at t=0. If δ(R,·)=const, then δ≡const. TTCS also corrected my orbit-area formula: Δρ=6e^{2φ}ρ exactly, with NO −|∇δ|² term. The subharmonicity is unconditional.

The Frobenius analysis at the cusp boundary (where the drift coefficient σ'=2/(1−t)→∞) shows that the degenerate singular point has indices p=0 and p=3. Both fundamental solutions are finite at the cusp. Numerical integration confirms that Fourier modes of δ with initial amplitude 1 at the center reach finite but amplified values at the cusp: 1.175 for n=1, 1.813 for n=2, 3.339 for n=3. The amplification increases with mode number, meaning high-frequency anisotropy oscillations are amplified even more.

The third result is the Yamabe impossibility on flat T³. The conformal Yamabe equation Δu=(3/4)u⁵ on flat T³ has no positive solution: at the maximum of u, Δu≤0 contradicts (3/4)u⁵>0. Therefore no metric with R=−6 lies in the flat conformal class on T³. In the T²-invariant ansatz h₀=dθ²+e^{2f(θ)}dx²+e^{-2f(θ)}dy², the scalar curvature is R(h₀)=−2(f')², which is ≤0 and equals 0 only when f is constant (flat). The Yamabe minimizer for R=−6 exists in the negative-Yamabe class (f nonconstant), giving the orbit anisotropy d=b₀−c₀=2f(θ), which is necessarily z-dependent.

The obstruction chain: (1) The Koiso center condition forces R_h=−6. (2) On T³, this requires negative-Yamabe class, hence nonconstant f(θ). (3) The drift-Laplacian amplifies the resulting z-oscillation of d toward the cusp boundary. (4) Flat hyperbolic cusps have constant T² cross-section (d=const). (5) The O(1) mismatch between center oscillation and cusp constancy cannot be absorbed by the O(δ·log(1/δ)) correction available to approximate Einstein forcing. The TTCS verification confirmed that the anisotropic lattice quotient (Step 69) only shifts d by a constant and cannot absorb z-oscillation, so the lattice construction does not evade the obstruction.

The isotropic impossibility (s'+s²=1 vs s'+3s²=3 forcing s²=1, incompatible with s(0)=0) is algebraic and independent of the fiber metric. Only s≡±1 (cusp or anti-cusp) solves both equations. This confirms that any reflected Einstein core must be essentially anisotropic.

What the obstruction does NOT block: (a) General 4D Einstein construction without T² symmetry — the drift-Laplacian is specific to T²-invariant metrics. (b) The positive branch entirely. (c) Surface-bundle E_D counterexample (where Einstein nonexistence is the open question). (d) Higher dimensions where fiber dimension ≥4.

A02's Step 74 results (ℓ-classification, higher obstruction subcriticality, product propagation P₄⁺→n≥6) combine with this negative result to sharpen the Yau 26 landscape: the T²-invariant graph negative counterexample and the local positive-branch higher-obstruction method both face structural limits. The two remaining approaches are tower rigidity (first obstruction forces all higher ones) and global existence bypassing nearby metrics, or alternatively Einstein nonexistence on surface bundles through monodromy-specific arguments.
