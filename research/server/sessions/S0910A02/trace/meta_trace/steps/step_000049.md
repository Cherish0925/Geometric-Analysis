## Deep-Reasoning

This step re-centered the positive noncollapsed line around the exact Yau--26 norm rather than around exact Einstein gluing. The active proposition asks only for input metrics satisfying a dimension-only Ricci operator pinching interval and for existence of some Einstein metric on the same smooth manifold; it does not ask whether the pinched metric is perturbable to an Einstein metric. That distinction changes the meaning of the Ozuch/Biquard obstruction tower. The first obstruction is critical because the refined EH insertion produces a term of the form \(t\lambda o\), and in the core metric \(g_t\sim t g_{EH}\) raising an index gives an order-one nonscalar Ricci-endomorphism defect when \(\lambda\ne0\). But a second obstruction term \(t^2\mu o\), and similarly every higher term, has endomorphism size \(O(t)\) or smaller. Thus higher obstructions can prevent exact Einstein desingularization while still leaving a family with \(\|\operatorname{Ric}-\Lambda g\|_{op}\to0\). This makes the first-obstruction vector condition \(R_\varepsilon(p)\zeta=0\) the decisive local condition for explicit near-pinched approximate metrics, not merely the first rung of a tower that must be climbed to block pinching. Conversely, the necessity of that condition for arbitrary pinched degenerations is still not proved: the compactness/obstruction bridge needs a normalized flux theorem at the \(t\)-scale of the ambient quadratic signal, not an unnormalized boundary functional that vanishes trivially from \(h_i\to0\). The corrected H4-dual object is therefore a quantitative two-scale statement: after writing \(\tilde g_i=g_{EH}+h_i\) with length scale \(\epsilon_i\) and metric scale \(t_i=\epsilon_i^2\), one must show that \(t_i^{-1}h_i\) (or equivalently \(\epsilon_i^{-2}h_i\)) converges in a compatible neck gauge to the ambient quadratic tensor \(H_2\), so that the normalized boundary Wronskian \(\mathcal B/t_i\) reads \(c\langle R_\varepsilon(p)\zeta,\zeta_{(k)}\rangle\). Cheeger--Naber convergence supplies only \(h_i=o(1)\), so this rate is precisely the missing analytic theorem and cannot be treated as almost closed.

The bi-Kähler product quotient then becomes a construction laboratory rather than a higher-obstruction loophole. At \((S^2\times S^2)/\mathbb Z_2\), both chiral blocks are \(\operatorname{diag}(\Lambda,0,0)\); hence for every orientation choice at every \(A_1\) point there are two-dimensional kernel directions killing the vector first obstruction. For any fixed finite orientation assignment, the refined EH-glued metrics should therefore satisfy a genuine Yau--26 positive pinching estimate, with rate at worst \(O(t^{1/2})\) in the MV model or \(O(t^{1/2-\beta/4})\) if quoted through Ozuch's weighted estimate. This is a real mathematical advance in understanding the wall: product-quotient mixed rows produce candidate fixed smooth 4-manifolds with \(\delta_+\to0\). They do not refute Yau--26 until the actual smooth diffeomorphism types are computed and at least one is proved to admit no Einstein metric of any sign. Endpoint all-compatible/all-reversed rows are harmless controls because, once their Del Pezzo diffeotype identification is established, the Yau--26 output metric may be an unrelated known positive Einstein metric. Mixed rows are the sharp targets. The full problem remains far from resolved: positive collapse is untouched by this local construction, the peer negative IL-flow theorem remains restricted and not independently checked by A02, dimensions \(n\ge5\) lack a corresponding stratified flux/field-production theorem, and no main theorem is formalized in Lean.
