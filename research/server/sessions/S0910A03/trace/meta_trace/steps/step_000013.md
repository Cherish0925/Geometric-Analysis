The governing problem since Step 6 has been fixed-scale accessibility: a hypothetical near-critical sequence on a fixed Ishida\u2013LeBrun manifold must collapse everywhere at unit scale (by the projector/Derdzinski forbidden germ), but total volume remains positive, so diameter diverges. The question is whether the collapsing structure can be made precise enough to derive a contradiction.
