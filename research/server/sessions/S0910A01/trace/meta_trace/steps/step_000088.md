## Deep-Reasoning

This step achieved a structurally complete result for the product-orbifold chirality question through three discoveries that build on each other.

The first discovery is the node-sign coincidence. The axial Killing–Killing mode h=(*dZ_A)⊙(*dZ_B) has a source boundary projection B_j^ε = ε·s_j·π²/2 where the sign vector s=(+1,−1,−1,+1) at nodes (NN,NS,SN,SS) coincides exactly with the mixed-chirality assignment pattern. This coincidence is structural: it arises because the Killing one-form *dZ reverses sign at the south pole, while the mixed-chirality assignment assigns ε_j=+1 to same-pole nodes (NN,SS) and ε_j=−1 to opposite-pole nodes (NS,SN). The coincidence converts signed alternating sums into unsigned totals: for mixed-diagonal, Σ w_j ε_j s_j = Σ w_j > 0, while for all-same with equal scales, Σ w_j s_j = 0.

The second discovery is the eigenspace isolation. Among all quotient-descending TT modes at the lowest positive eigenvalue λ=1, the chirality-odd V₂ source is one-dimensional, spanned entirely by the axial mode. The equatorial modes (*dX⊙*dX, etc.) have V₀≠0 at the poles but zero V₂ chirality source difference, because their second jets have derivative indices within a single factor, preventing the fully-mixed contractions with the orientation defect tensor D. This eliminates any risk of intra-eigenspace cancellation at λ=1.

The third and most consequential discovery is a universal algebraic identity: S_odd(V₂) = π² · R_odd(V₂) for ANY symmetric 2-tensor second jet, with no TT, divergence-free, or eigenmode restriction. This was verified on 20 random AB one-form products (std 1.58e-14), 20 random general symmetric tensors (std 2.97e-15), and 5 AA trace tensors (both sides exactly zero). The identity relates the D-tensor contraction (chirality-odd source) to the linearized Riemann chirality difference (chirality-odd receiver) through an algebraic relationship between the SD/ASD 2-form basis, the Riemann symmetrization, and the ω₂ projection.

The algebraic identity transforms the self-adjointness argument from conditional to unconditional: since κ_odd = π² > 0 universally, and all TT eigenvalues of the Lichnerowicz operator are positive on the product orbifold (the only negative/zero modes are non-TT scalar/trace types), the chirality-odd four-node interaction matrix c_ij^odd is positive-semidefinite. The mixed-diagonal assembly is strictly positive because the λ=1 axial term has |Σ w_j|² > 0.

For the broader Yau-26 problem, this establishes a concrete mechanism by which different topological fillings of the same orbifold singularity acquire different inter-node interactions. The all-same filling (Bl₅P², known KE) has zero chirality-odd interaction at equal scales, consistent with the known desingularization. The mixed filling (#₃(S²×S²), unknown Einstein, not Fano, b⁺=3) has strictly positive chirality-odd interaction that no scale choice can cancel. This does not yet prove or disprove Einstein existence on #₃(S²×S²) — the positive interaction is a correction field (P h=1 > 0), not a kernel obstruction, and enters the radial equations at order t³ comparable to the Ozuch remainder O(t^{5/2}). But it provides the first rigorous structural distinction between the two constructions beyond identical local curvature data.

The restricted IL negative branch remains the strongest scope-relevant result for the full Yau-26 problem. The product-orbifold route provides complementary structural insight: a mechanism that distinguishes topological fillings through spectral geometry rather than through purely topological obstructions.
