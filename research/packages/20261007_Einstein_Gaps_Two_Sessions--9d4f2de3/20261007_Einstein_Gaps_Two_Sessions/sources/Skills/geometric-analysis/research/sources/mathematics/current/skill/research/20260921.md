# Ricci Pinching and Einstein Existence

The research concerns a distinction between approximation and existence: a
closed smooth manifold may admit increasingly accurate pointwise solutions of
the Einstein equation without any known method for producing an exact solution.
The current work develops integral obstructions, actual approximating metrics,
and global restrictions on the same smooth objects. The original question
remains the objective; these three lines organize the available mathematics,
not all possible ways to solve it.

## Problem and success criteria

For every dimension $n\ge2$, does there exist an interval $c_n<C_n$, depending
only on $n$, such that

$$c_ng\le\operatorname{Ric}_g\le C_ng$$

on a closed connected smooth $n$-manifold implies that the same smooth manifold
admits some Einstein metric? The output need not resemble the input or preserve
its sign, symmetry, complex structure, fibration or limiting geometry.

Write

$$\delta_\sigma(g)=\|g^{-1}\operatorname{Ric}_g-\sigma I\|_{L^\infty,\mathrm{op}},
\qquad e_\sigma(M)=\inf_g\delta_\sigma(g),\qquad \sigma=\pm1.$$

In dimension $n$, the interval statement is equivalent to $P_n^+\lor P_n^-$,
where $P_n^\sigma$ asserts a dimension-only sufficient small-defect threshold
for Einstein existence. A complete affirmative solution covers every dimension.
A complete negative solution defeats both signs in one dimension, possibly on
different manifolds. For a branch counterexample, a sequence of non-Einstein
smooth manifolds with defects tending to zero is sufficient; fixed topology is
stronger but not required. Scaling and the exact quantifiers are in
[the foundations](20260910.tex) and [the earlier synthesis](20260916.md).

## 1. Integral rigidity beyond geometric compactness

The [integral rigidity paper](20260921_rigidity.tex) begins with actual monopole
classes, not formal cohomology vectors. For the blow-up family

$$M=X\#k\overline{\mathbb{CP}}^2,\quad A=c_1^2(X)>0,$$

with $X$ a closed minimal complex surface of general type and $b_+(X)>1$, the
twelve-round argument establishes the all-sign estimate

$$\mathcal E(g)+\left(\frac13-\frac{k}{A}\right)_+\ge c_*>0,
\qquad
\mathcal E(g)=\inf_{\lambda\ne0}
\frac{\int|\operatorname{Ric}_g-\lambda g|_g^2\,d\mu_g}
{\lambda^2\operatorname{Vol}_g(M)}.$$

The constant is uniform in $X,k,g$. There is no assumed bound on diameter,
injectivity radius, Sobolev constant or full curvature. Since
$\mathcal E(g)\le4\delta_-(g)^2$, the specified pointwise near-Einstein gap
previously left open now follows. This does not establish the older proposed
harmonic-chart property (SC), nor give a theorem for arbitrary four-manifolds.

The proof replaces geometric regularization by intrinsic integral control.
The [positioning note](../references/positioning-20260921.md) explains why this
relative gap is distinct from an absolute Ricci-energy lower bound, and in
what sense it quantitatively strengthens the classical Einstein obstruction.
Weighted monopoles give bounded rescaled spinors. Covariant integration by
parts and the relevant spinor-connection curvature give a second-order
integral estimate without a uniform Sobolev inequality. A smooth polynomial
Weyl model can then be used in the weak Bianchi identity. Testing against its
own divergence transfers derivatives off the small Ricci error. Near equality
forces the same averaged squared divergence to tend both to zero and to
$1/16$.

The abstract extension keeps the square cost of the selected actual class;
special monopole classes provide a particular applicable input. It is stronger
than the earlier exact-Einstein theorem in its near-Einstein conclusion, but
has different hypotheses. An Euler/monopole budget by itself is not automatically
the required actual-class input.

The next mathematical questions concern the reach of this mechanism:

- Choose applicable actual classes metricwise while minimizing their negative
  square cost. Establish which period planes can realize the needed choice.
- Identify whether a proposed calibration model is genuinely forbidden or
  realized by an exact Einstein geometry before attempting a new contradiction.
- Retain sources in self-testing, boundary flux in localization, and the
  common PDE behind several classes. Formal cancellation is not solvability
  of a new coupled gauge system.
- Seek different positive-branch and higher-dimensional structures when the
  four-dimensional monopole input is unavailable.

The [earlier exact-gap proof](20260916_uniform_gap.tex),
[local rigidity and flow analysis](20260916_rigidity.tex), and
[original integral proof](../references/rounds/round4_rigidity.md) remain
available as distinct arguments, including useful methods not needed in the
new proof.

## 2. Three construction mechanisms and the exact equation

The [construction paper](20260921_construction.tex) keeps three geometrically
different sources of small residuals.

| Fixed smooth object | Mechanism | Consequence and remaining question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marked $A_1$ resolution | Correct the nonzero Einstein source in the EH core; slowly match pure-Weyl quadratic data in a logarithmic annulus | $\delta_\sigma=O(|\log a|^{-1})$. Determine the full smooth topology's Einstein existence, not merely a particular desingularization. |
| Dehn-twist surface bundle $E_D$ | Long-collar metric variation; balanced double degeneration; three scalar corrections | Pointwise approximation, cubic residual and a weighted all-tensor local exclusion neighborhood. Does an exact solution exist outside that neighborhood? |
| Closed sheared product blocks | Separate mapping-class variation on long collars from integer shear on a collapsing throat | A larger specified class of fixed closed topologies has $\operatorname{Ric}\approx-3g$, with all fixed-order curvature derivatives bounded. Can the full, possibly nonsymmetric equation be solved? |

For the third construction the actual boundary map is

$$\Psi_e(\phi,x)=(-\phi+u_e(x),f_e(x)),$$

with fixed integral shear and orientation-preserving fiber diffeomorphism.
On the resulting closed manifold the manuscript proves

$$\|G_\ell^{-1}\operatorname{Ric}_{G_\ell}+3I\|_\infty
\le C(|\log\ell|^{-1}+\ell),\qquad
\operatorname{Vol}(G_\ell)=\frac{4\pi^2}{9}\chi(M).$$

The constants may depend on the finite marked gluing data. The volume formula,
the complete Ricci blocks and the separation of supports are part of the
construction. This is not the failed argument that attempted to fit an
ever-growing AH core into fixed finite-volume blocks.

For the specified balanced $E_D$ family, the enlarged repair analysis gives

$$\|h\|_{C^2(G_\varepsilon)}\le c_*\varepsilon
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\delta_-(G_\varepsilon+h)\ge c_1\varepsilon^3.$$

The perturbation is not restricted to an invariant ansatz. The estimate uses
the weighted response paired with the obstruction, including the transfer
kernel and nonlinear signs. It does not assert global Einstein nonexistence.

Further improvement should change the available topology, admissible repair
space, sharp mechanism or exact solvability, not only reduce a coefficient.
The new manuscript develops full kernel/complement reduction, low/high-mode
Schur feedback, weighted inverses, two-end response with shared marked data,
and a posteriori contraction criteria. Every criterion compares residual,
inverse and nonlinear bounds on the same complete spaces. A left preconditioner
must be injective to infer the full equation from its preconditioned zero;
gauge recovery is a separate geometric step.

The earlier [construction paper](20260916_construction.tex) and
[extended manuscript](20260912.tex) retain the explicit EH core, all Weyl jets,
surface-bundle formulas, angular analysis, spikes, flux and algebraic examples.

## 3. Global determination on the same smooth manifold

The [global paper](20260921_global.tex) asks what can constrain *every* Einstein
metric on the constructed smooth types. Local symmetry of the approximate
input is not an assumption on the output.

The self-glued examples $M(h,g;u,f)$ have new proved structural restrictions
under the hypotheses stated in the original topology arguments. Nonzero shear
obstructs virtual Kählerness; the non-surface-bundle result has an additional
infinite-order mapping-class hypothesis. Ordinary basic first-Chern classes
and the numerical monopole invariants are determined separately. The symmetry
argument concerns continuous local **isometric** symmetries: any hypothetical
Einstein metric has no nonzero local Killing field. Finite isometries are not
excluded, and breaking symmetry need not require a large perturbation.

For these closed shear examples,

$$\frac{2\chi+3\tau}{\alpha^2}=1,$$

whereas the integral-rigidity threshold is $2/3$. Thus construction and
obstruction have not yet met on these objects. This ratio is not asserted for
every resolution or every other family in the Skill.

The paper distinguishes several remaining global problems:

- **Attainment:** determine whether the relevant curvature infimum is attained
  on the original smooth manifold. One degenerating minimizing sequence does
  not rule out another smooth minimizer.
- **Group-induced geometry:** extract actual analytic information from
  monodromy, integral shear and the Bass–Serre action. A useful equivariant
  map needs existence, regularity and an applicable Einstein identity;
  Kähler-source rigidity cannot be imported for a general Einstein source.
- **Full matching:** combine core, ends, constraints, lattice and boundary
  response in a single solution. Free-circle exact boundary connectivity and
  actual two-ended approximate families show a distinction worth exploiting,
  but neither settles the fully nonsymmetric closed problem.
- **Alternative existence:** algebraic smoothing or a changed complex structure
  may produce an Einstein output on types not already excluded from being
  virtually Kähler. On the non-virtually-Kähler shear examples a different,
  nonsymmetric and non-Kähler construction would be needed. Either kind of
  existence theorem would resolve its object.

Graph manifolds, algebraic fillings, positive-branch toric objects and higher
dimensions retain their own mathematical questions. A new representation or
cross-field method is useful when it gives a statement that returns to the
original smooth-category problem; it need not fit the current division of work.

## How the ideas developed

The progression was not twelve independent discoveries. Several rounds
strengthened one proof, while others changed its object or showed why a
seemingly useful implication was false.

| Stage | Change in the mathematical question | Retained derivation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–2 | Ricci-box and spectral estimates exposed the gap between first-order calibration and controlled curvature transfer; charged connector equations exposed missing mixed blocks. | [Initial arguments](../references/rounds/README.md), [round 2](../references/rounds/round2.md) |
| 3–4 | Actual weighted monopoles supplied the missing second-order control. The contradiction moved from a regular geometric limit to a weak Bianchi test on the original metric, then from pointwise to integral Ricci error. Exact nonlinear local models developed in parallel. | [Round 3](../references/rounds/round3.md), [round 4](../references/rounds/round4.md) |
| 5–6 | Tracking the actual class square clarified generality. On the construction side, the relevant inverse was the response seen by the obstruction, not an unavailable uniform full maximum-norm inverse. This enlarged the all-tensor exclusion neighborhood. | [Round 5](../references/rounds/round5.md), [round 6](../references/rounds/round6.md) |
| 7–8 | Filling budgets and critical gauge energy separated topological obstructions from models that could actually realize them. Representation and transport calculations tested what the existing detectors could see. | [Round 7](../references/rounds/round7.md), [round 8](../references/rounds/round8.md) |
| 9–10 | Free-circle reduction gave an exact global boundary obstruction, while explicit two-ended approximate families showed that its near-Einstein extension could not be presumed. Physical scales, boundary data and topology became indispensable. | [Round 9](../references/rounds/round9.md), [round 10](../references/rounds/round10.md) |
| 11 | Separating long-collar variation from throat shear gave an actual closed construction with compatible volume and fixed gluing. The problem moved to its precise smooth topology and variational quantities. | [Round 11](../references/rounds/round11.md) |
| 12 | Saturating scalar energy constrained the whole ordinary basic set; group structure constrained every Einstein output's local isometric symmetry. The remaining decision became genuinely nonsymmetric, not another invariant-ansatz calculation. | [Round 12](../references/rounds/round12.md) |

Two changes are especially reusable. Integral control can sometimes replace
geometric compactness if the equation supplies a bounded, differentiable test
object. A gluing obstruction can sometimes be studied through its receiver
rather than through a uniform inverse for every tensor. Neither principle is
automatic: the first needs the actual solvable gauge equation and curvature
budget, and the second needs the full kernel and nonlinear response.

## Reading and continuation

Read the relevant new paper with the linked original proof when using a
specific result. The [proof index](../references/rounds-index.md) retains the
twelve-round arguments and computations; the [research map](../references/research-map.md)
also locates the older manuscripts and independent web contributions.
[Reproduction instructions](../references/reproduction.md) run without access
to old Sessions. Numerical and symbolic checks establish their declared finite
identities, while the analytic and topological proofs are given in the papers.

The imported next-stage manuscripts supply concrete open questions as well as
new organization of established work. Their proposed extensions are written as
questions or conditional statements, not silently promoted to theorems.
No fixed number of papers, route, object or method is prescribed. The next
result should be selected by which unresolved mathematical implication it can
change: applicability of an obstruction, realizability on a new smooth type,
exact repair, or full Einstein existence/nonexistence.
