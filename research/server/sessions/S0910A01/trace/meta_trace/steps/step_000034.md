The t^{-1} output remains a positive-time input for high-p Moser on dyadic slabs, not something to integrate from time zero.

The step also repaired the tensor starting point inherited from Step 33. In the all-lower curvature convention, the pure scaling test under partial_t g=-2g gives partial_t |Rm|^2=4|Rm|^2 and invariance of int |Rm|^2 dmu in dimension four; therefore the normalization/conformal piece is not pointwise damping. The moving-volume term should enter the curvature reaction through |s+4|=|tr E|<=CF, not through a premature C delta K_p estimate. The pre-absorption dissipation from a Delta F^2 - 2a|nabla Rm|^2 is -pD_p^Rm - p(p-2)D_p^F, not the older -pD_p^Rm - p(p-3)D_p^F. The signed Hessian-a route is the appropriate first smoothing route: integrate by parts on the tensor nabla_i nabla_j a and use the available tensor dissipation, but it requires the real derivative structure |nabla B| <= C F |nabla Rm| for the actual curvature contractions. Pointwise evolving cutoff-Hessian control is a later continuation input and should not be inserted into the first smoothing estimate.

A03 Step 35 supplied a sharper defect-energy mechanism that this step inspected and incorporated. Reading A03's unregularized J6 note and rerunning its verifier showed that J6 persistence does not need the curvature cubic moment Y. Taking s=|E|^2, J6=int eta^2 a^4 s^3, X6=int eta^2 a^5 s^2|nabla E|^2, and w6=eta chi^5 |E|^3 avoids norm regularization because s^3 and w6 are smooth enough at E=0 and no division by |E| appears. The defect potential is linear in F: P6=int eta^2 a^5 F s^3=int F w6^2. Two-factor Holder plus Sobolev gives P6 <= ||F||_2 ||w6||_4^2 <= C gamma(X6+r^{-2}J6), using only K_2^eta. For small gamma this yields J6'+cX6 <= C(1+r^{-2})J6 and hence J6(t)<=exp(C(1+r^{-2})t)J6(0). For T=theta r^2, initial Ricci pinching gives J6(T)<=C delta^6 V. This supersedes the earlier regularized three-factor route for J6 persistence, while the integrated-potential mechanism remains needed for q=3 curvature propagation and the curvature coefficient in later Moser iteration.
