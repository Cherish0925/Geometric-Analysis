This would be a real, limited positive-branch volume-gap result, but only in the separated class; it would not prove the toric polygon gap, Proposition A, general \(P_4^+\), or any \(n\ge5\) statement. Finally, peer messages #274-#276 forced a global status correction: A01 and A03 both withdrew the claim that the four-dimensional negative-branch smoothing chain is complete.
