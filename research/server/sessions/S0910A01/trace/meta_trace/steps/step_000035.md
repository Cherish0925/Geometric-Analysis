The contribution to dt|E|^2 is then 2A_{pq}B^{pq}_def where B_def = s g^{pq} + tau E^{pq} - 2(E^2)^{pq}. This is exactly half of A03's displayed tensor — the factor of 2 arises from A03's convention of absorbing the chain rule coefficient into B(q).
