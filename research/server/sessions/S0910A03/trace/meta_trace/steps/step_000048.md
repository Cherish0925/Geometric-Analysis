## Deep-Reasoning

Step 47 joined a valuable terminal mechanism to an analytic construction that was still incomplete. This Step separated three mathematically different layers that must not be conflated. First, an exact degenerate normalized cutoff flow must start for each smooth datum. Second, homogeneous weighted estimates on that exact flow must produce a sequence-uniform observation time despite collapse. Third, if a maximal exact flow stopped before that time, positive-time derivative estimates must produce a smooth endpoint and restart. The terminal density and calibrated Einstein-germ contradiction remain downstream of all three.

Primary-source reading gives a cleaner route for the first and third layers. Yang's Theorem 8.2 explicitly proves short-time existence for ∂tg=-2χ²Ric by either a transported-coefficient metric/diffeomorphism system or the regularized equations ∂tgε=-2(χ²+ε²)Ric; its Approach 2 states that curvature and derivative energies provide an ε-independent time and smooth ε→0 convergence. Wang's Theorem 4.1 and Section 4 give the corresponding extension mechanism: bounded χ²Rm on a finite interval yields all-order weighted curvature, cutoff-jet, and unweighted curvature estimates and smooth extension. Neither source literally contains the added normalized term -2χ²g, but that term is zeroth order. A valid normalized theorem therefore requires an explicit perturbation argument in Yang's smooth-tame system or a complete all-covariant repetition of the Yang–Wang energies. Step 44 establishes the same-order linear perturbation but explicitly leaves Yang's neighborhood-uniform high–low inverse imported; Step 45 gives a plausible all-order regularized tensor family but explicitly leaves full tensor provenance and nonlinear PDE existence to further examination. These files cannot yet support the unconditional wording in Step 47.

The coefficient-regularization logic must be reordered. For ε>0, the exterior evolves by -2ε²(Ric+g), so frozen complement, stationary collars, disjoint-support summation, localized Jm and Kq identities, and the exterior-free global Q estimate are unavailable. A common ε-independent start time may only come from global per-datum smooth geometry: the unweighted T0 energy, the coupled current pair (Um,Cm+1), subsequent Tm estimates, fixed-background connection differences, and compactness. After a smooth ε→0 limit produces the exact a=χ² flow, the sequence-uniform localized first-exit argument may begin. Thus the localized smoothing estimates cannot be used to construct the very exact flow on which their support identities depend.

The continuation logic has a simple repair that avoids a uniform curvature bound at t=0. The curvature Moser endpoint is of order γ/t, so Section 6 of Step 47 cannot infer a finite bound for U on all of [0,T]. Instead, let T* be a hypothetical finite maximal time of an already-started exact flow. If T*>0, then on [T*/2,T*) the estimate |aRm(t)|≤Cγ/t gives a finite bound depending on T*. Since U=a(Rm+G), U is bounded there. The staggered sequence T0→(U1,C2)→T1→(U2,C3)→… can then run on that positive-time slab, yielding a smooth endpoint and a per-datum restart. This contradicts maximality. The argument needs only some positive per-datum starting time; the sequence-uniform time comes later from the exact-flow first-exit estimates.

The geometric hypothesis in Step 47 also needs repair. Simply connected complex surfaces of general type and b+(N)=0 do not by themselves provide the monopole classes used in the scalar–Weyl inequalities. The original Ishida–LeBrun source shows that for two or three simply connected symplectic summands with b+≡3 mod 4, Bauer–Furuta nonvanishing gives all independent sign choices ±c1(Xj) as monopole classes. The more general connected-sum source uses almost-complex summands with b1=0, b+≡3 mod 4, nonzero mod-2 Seiberg–Witten invariants, the relevant Bauer congruence assumptions, and a negative-definite N to obtain classes Σ±c1(Xj)+Σ±Eℓ. The safest theorem formulation is to assume this full monopole sign family explicitly and then list the source hypotheses as sufficient examples. The smoothing module must likewise retain the initial quantitative assumption ||Ric+g||∞≤δ: it is essential for the J6 seed, Q(0)=O(δ²Vol), defect Moser iteration, and small metric drift; Ricci lower control plus a curvature budget alone does not supply these.

Consequently, the core-ball terminal repair from Step 47 remains useful, but the restricted negative gap is not yet established. Its genuine mathematical bottleneck is now sharper: prove a normalized Yang–Wang start and positive-time extension theorem with the exact coefficient weights, then run the sequence-uniform estimates only on the resulting exact flow. Family uniformity must be recomputed after the monopole assumptions, curvature budget, support constants, and normalization perturbation are all explicit. The full Yau Problem 26 remains much further away: positive pinching, other four-manifold topologies, dimensions at least five, dimension-only constants, and Lean formalization are untouched.
