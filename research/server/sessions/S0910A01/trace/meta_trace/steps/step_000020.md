## Deep-Reasoning

This step materially sharpened the analytic core of the four-dimensional negative LeBrun/Ishida-LeBrun route. The Step 19 Whitney reduction remains the right geometric representation: on balls adapted to the cutoff level, the degenerate operator becomes uniformly parabolic after rescaling, and the four-dimensional Sobolev-volume cancellation remains independent of collapse. The new point is that the curvature and defect fields should not be treated as two unrelated unknowns in the high-power curvature layer. Since E=Ric+g is a contraction of Rm plus the metric and the current Levi-Civita connection preserves the current metric, nabla E is a contraction of nabla Rm on every smooth time-slice. This allows the Hessian cutoff terms produced by h=-2aE, a=chi^2, to be integrated by parts and then absorbed into the full tensor curvature dissipation rather than into a separate high-power defect-gradient energy. The resulting schematic local inequality for F=(1+|Rm|^2)^{1/2} is closed in curvature: d/dt int eta^2 F^q + c q int eta^2 a F^{q-2}|nabla Rm|^2 is bounded by the curvature reaction int eta^2 a F^{q+1}, regular cutoff potentials involving |da|^2/a=4|dchi|^2, and localization terms a|nabla eta|^2F^q. TTCS independently supported this structural simplification and also corrected two important details: the linear aRm term should be regarded as part of the algebraic curvature variation rather than solely from a nabla^2E, and the regularized norm changes the Whitney Sobolev product to C sqrt(1+K0) r^2 because F contains a constant part. This changes the route from a coupled high-power curvature-defect iteration to a cleaner curvature-power theorem plus a separate unweighted defect-energy theorem. At the same time, the exponent bookkeeping shows that direct all-exponent critical absorption is not justified: the smallness threshold worsens with q, so the better analytic path is Li-style, using local L2 persistence to create a positive-time finite supercritical exponent such as L3 or L^{q0}, then applying a positive-time mean-value argument. The other load-bearing correction is for Q(t)=int |E|^2. The inherited Step 19 inference from an inequality containing an additive epsilon X term is invalid for the LeBrun scale, because int X dt may be O(A), leaving O(epsilon A) rather than o(A). The repaired estimate must use the triangular structure of the E equation under h=-2aE: every term is linear or quadratic in E or nabla E, and curvature appears as a potential multiplying |E|^2, not as a curvature-only source. Absorbing a|Rm||E|^2 by the same four-dimensional Sobolev product yields a finite-neighbor inequality dQ_alpha/dt <= C r^{-2} sum Q_beta, hence support-summed multiplicative control over time theta r^2. This is exactly the form needed to preserve Q/A -> 0. The full Yau 26 problem is still far from solved: this only strengthens one analytic bridge inside the critical four-dimensional negative laboratory. Positive pinching, dimensions n>=5, the original interval statement, and Lean formalization remain open.
