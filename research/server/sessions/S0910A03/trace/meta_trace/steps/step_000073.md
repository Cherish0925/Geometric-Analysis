## Deep-Reasoning

The full Yau 26 problem remains unresolved: no dimension-only interval has been proved, no fixed dimension has been shown to defeat both normalized signs, and no complete Lean formalization exists. This Step repaired two scope errors that had begun to redirect the session group, then converted the negative graph-neck route from an incorrectly closed route into a sharply localized construction problem.

For the diagonal orthogonally transitive metric g=e^{2phi}(dt^2+dz^2)+e^{2b}dx^2+e^{2c}dy^2, the exact anisotropy equation div(e^sigma grad d)=0, with d=b-c and sigma=b+c, still gives valid finite-cylinder rigidity. But for E=Ric+3g the equation is div(e^sigma grad d)=-e^{sigma+2phi}(E_x-E_y). Step 72 had neither a geometry-uniform inverse estimate for this sourced operator nor an upper bound on collar length. The normal balance only supplied a lower length scale under particular endpoint data. Consequently, the former O(delta log(1/delta)) forcing assertion and the resulting exclusion of approximate T^2 necks were withdrawn. The equations themselves describe only the diagonal orthogonally transitive subclass, not every T^2-invariant metric.

The decisive constructive test is the expanding tail
 g=dr^2+e^{2r}[dz^2+N^{-2}e^v dx^2+N^{-2}e^{-v}dy^2].
Writing B=dr^2+e^{2r}dz^2 and L v=v_{rr}+3v_r+e^{-2r}v_{zz}, direct substitution into all five Ricci components gives E|_B=-(1/2)dv tensor dv, E_x=-(1/2)Lv, E_y=(1/2)Lv, with base-fiber mixed components zero while the base rz component is generally nonzero. Hence the exact pointwise operator norm is (1/2)max{v_r^2+e^{-2r}v_z^2, |Lv|}. For v=chi((r-r0)/K)v0(z), this yields ||E||_infinity <= C(K^{-1}+K^{-2}+e^{-2r0}), uniformly in the lattice parameter N. The symbolic computation in workspace/scripts/step73_expanding_tail_verify.py reduced every formula residual to zero. Its data file workspace/data/step73_expanding_tail.json records, for v0=sin z and r0=6, grid maxima 0.7406712, 0.3563953, 0.1763899, 0.08796675, and 0.04395483 for K=4,8,16,32,64. Analytically K||E|| tends to 45/16=2.8125 for the chosen smootherstep. Thus an order-one orbit-ratio oscillation can be removed with arbitrarily small full Ricci defect once the geometry has entered a sufficiently long expanding region.

This tail also clarifies the normal equation rather than evading it. Its shape operator is diag(1,1+v_r/2,1-v_r/2), so H=3, |S-I|^2=v_r^2/2, and E_rr=-v_r^2/2. The negative normal residual exactly pays the tail's own shear pointwise. It does not pay the earlier deficit accumulated in moving from a reflection center with H=0 to an almost-cusp entry slice.

The special calculation extends to a general adiabatic tail. For h_r=e^{2a(r)}q_s, s=(r-r0)/K, and Q=q_s^{-1} dot q_s, the Gaussian formulas give S=a'I+Q/(2K), H=3a'+tr(Q)/(2K), explicit rr and tangential defect formulas, and E_{r dot}=(div Q-d tr Q)/(2K), whose g-norm contains e^{-a}. Under uniform positive definiteness and bounds on q_s, its inverse, parameter derivatives, Ric(q_s), and the Codazzi term, choosing a'=1-epsilon eta(s) gives ||Ric+3g||_infinity <= C(epsilon+K^{-1}+K^{-2}+e^{-2a0}). If q_s is stationary near both endpoints and q_1 is flat, the outgoing end is an exact flat hyperbolic cusp germ. TTCS independently verified these formulas and corrected important details: volume preservation improves only the normal path term, while the full defect generally remains O(K^{-1}); a smooth attachment requires endpoint-neighborhood constancy and agreement of all jets; and the mixed Codazzi term requires spatial derivative control.

This creates a precise core-entry interface. A core must reach a large slice h=e^{2a}q with S=(1-epsilon)I+P, epsilon and P small, e^{-2a}|Ric(q)| small, and enough jet control to begin a bounded path from q to the desired flat cusp metric. Merely having P tend to zero is insufficient: in the tail parametrization the initial path must realize Q(0)=2KP, and Q/K, (dot Q+Q^2)/K^2, the scaled Codazzi term, and higher jets must remain small. In the stronger exactly umbilic case the matching is simpler.

In a CMC, pointwise-volume-preserving foliation gauge, which is an additional structural assumption, an exact core satisfies a''=1-(a')^2-|P|^2/3, P'+3a'P=e^{-2a}Ric(q)^circ, e^{-2a}R(q)+6(1-(a')^2)+|P|^2=0, and div_q P=0. At a reflection center a'=0, P=0, and R(q)=-6e^{2a}. These equations expose the real mechanism: once expansion and scaled intrinsic curvature control have both been achieved, the traceless shape is damped and the tail can finish the construction. Expansion alone does not control e^{-2a}|Ric(q)|, and the elliptic Cauchy nature leaves growing spatial modes. Source study found that Witten-Yau/Cai-Galloway excludes the flat or other nonnegative-Yamabe two-ended Poincare-Einstein version, but not negative-Yamabe conformal infinity; no retrieved theorem supplied or excluded the required reflection-symmetric R x T^3 core.

The Step also preserved the distinction between the elliptic principal subsystem and the full Einstein equations. The determinant 2(tau^2+xi^2)^3 for (E_tt+E_zz,E_x,E_y) is useful, but these three equations do not force E_tt-E_zz and E_tz to vanish. Future construction must solve a full Einstein-DeTurck problem and eliminate the DeTurck field, or prove propagation of the Hamilton and momentum components from compatible data. Numerical convergence of only the three elliptic equations would repeat the earlier incomplete BVP.

The second group correction concerns products. A particular four-dimensional near-positive-Einstein input can be multiplied by a positively Einstein sphere to produce higher-dimensional near-pinched input families. This does not prove P_4^+ implies P_n^+ for arbitrary n-manifolds, and absence of arbitrary Einstein metrics does not pass automatically to products because an output Einstein metric need not split. Group message #512 circulated both corrections; A02 and A01 acknowledged them in messages #513, #515, and #519. Supersession notices were inserted into the Step 72 note, status, and route map.

A01's concurrent positive mixed-row work reports that the relative product deformation on (S^2 x S^2)/Z_2 has Einstein-operator eigenvalue -Lambda rather than zero, so the Ozuch infinitesimal Einstein space is reported to vanish; first obstructions vanish for Eguchi-Hanson directions in the two-dimensional kernel of R^plus or R^minus, and one second-order component vanishes by the rank-one curvature structure. This is promising but not yet an existence theorem. The remaining second components still require source-level verification, and Ozuch explicitly describes an infinite sequence of higher obstructions. Moreover, Ozuch Question 0.8 and Conjecture 2.22 are stated for Ricci-flat degeneration, whereas this product quotient has positive Einstein constant. Thus these results cannot yet be used as a sufficient positive desingularization theorem.

The main conceptual advance is therefore a decomposition of the negative construction into a solved long-tail module and an unsolved core-entry module. Exact anisotropy propagation is not the decisive obstruction; reaching a large, almost umbilic, scaled-curvature-controlled slice while satisfying all Einstein components is. This is substantially narrower and more constructive than the previous formulation. Success would combine naturally with graph four-manifolds already known to exclude every Einstein metric, but it would address only the normalized negative branch in dimension four. The positive branch, other dimensions, the complete quantifiers, and formalization remain open.
