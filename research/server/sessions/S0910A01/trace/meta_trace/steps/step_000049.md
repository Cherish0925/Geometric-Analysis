## Deep-Reasoning

This step achieved a genuine strategic reversal in the understanding of product-quotient surfaces within the Yau 26 problem. The Strategist's insight about the hyperkähler parameter identification was the key: the kernel of R_+ on a product Kähler–Einstein orbifold corresponds to the (2,0)+(0,2) directions, which are exactly the complex smoothing directions—not the Kähler resolution directions. This means the kernel-directed Eguchi–Hanson replacement that kills the first Ozuch obstruction points toward Q-Gorenstein smoothing of the canonical model, not toward holomorphic resolution.

The mathematical chain is: if the canonical model Z = (C_1 × C_2)/G with only A_1 nodes admits a global Q-Gorenstein smoothing, then the smooth fiber Z_t has ample K_{Z_t} (by openness of ampleness in flat projective families), hence carries a negative Kähler–Einstein metric (by Aubin–Yau), and is diffeomorphic to the minimal resolution S (by Brieskorn's simultaneous resolution for ADE singularities plus Ehresmann's theorem). Therefore the smooth four-manifold underlying S carries an Einstein metric.

TTCS independently verified this chain with six corrections, all incorporated: the smoothing parameter t is proportional to ζ_C^2 (not equal) due to the Weyl Z/2Z action; the local kernel direction identification does not automatically give global integrable QG smoothing; the Burns–Wahl comparison H^2(T_Z^0) ≅ H^2(T_S) requires the specific RDP result; and the Euler characteristic computation χ(T_S) = 2K^2 - 10 only determines when the sufficient condition H^2 = 0 can or cannot hold, not whether the smoothing exists.

The obstruction analysis shows: for K^2 = 2 (χ = -6) and K^2 = 4 (χ = -2), the sufficient condition H^2(T_S) = 0 is numerically possible and could be verified for specific named families. For K^2 = 6 (χ = 2 > 0), the sufficient condition fails and alternative arguments (e.g., Lee–Park's cyclic cover technique) would be needed. For K^2 = 8, no singularities exist and the product KE metric already gives Einstein.

The b_+ = 1 observation eliminates IL overlap: since the IL connected-sum form requires m ≥ 2 general-type blocks giving b_+ ≥ 2, no A1-only product-quotient resolution can be in the IL hypothesis set.

Peer feedback confirms: A03 (#379) acknowledges the mechanism as 'conceptually clean' and reports a major advance on the normalized Yang flow module (critical exponent p = n/2 makes the normalization reaction vanish). A02 (#380) confirms the reversed-chirality positive estimates and correctly notes that the positive S^2 × S^2 mixed rows are not covered by the Aubin–Yau mechanism.

The deepest structural insight is that the product-quotient front no longer competes with the restricted IL theorem—it operates on completely different topologies (b_+ = 1 versus b_+ ≥ 2). The two mechanisms are complementary. For the full Yau 26 problem, neither provides a dimension-only forcing theorem, but together they expand the class of manifolds where Einstein existence is established or plausibly expected under tight Ricci pinching.
