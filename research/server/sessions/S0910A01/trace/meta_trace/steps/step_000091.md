## Deep-Reasoning

This step sharpened two different parts of the Yau--26 research state. On the positive product-orbifold branch, the K-reflection from Step 90 remains a genuine simplification, but its proper mathematical scope is narrower than some Step-90 wording suggested. It is a theorem about the K-equivariant linear pairwise background problem for the local desingularization coefficient on (S^2 x S^2)/<(r_pi,r_pi)>, not about the complete nonlinear Ozuch/Biquard gluing chart and not about all Einstein metrics on the resulting smooth manifold. The useful surviving structure is now much cleaner: cross-parity terms vanish by K, the checkerboard relative equation keeps only adjacent ordered node pairs, and the product-background odd receiver is exactly the sparse Step-89 second-jet functional. This last point is important because it removes a false proliferation of possible receiver corrections. Since the product curvature annihilates omega_2^+ and omega_2^- and has only one-factor support, frame/Hodge/length variations and curvature-times-V0 corrections do not enter the four-distinct odd component. Thus V0-nonzero equatorial modes may still matter through the source or gauge side, but not through a hidden pointwise odd receiver correction at the product linear layer.

The spectral computation was also repaired. The quotient generator is a simultaneous pi-rotation, so descent is governed by azimuthal m-parity, not ell-parity. Factor trace-free tensor modes have the corrected product eigenvalue P=(ell_F(ell_F+1)+ell_O(ell_O+1))/2-1 and begin at P=2 in the K-odd quotient sector, not at P=4. The AB one-form sector keeps independent ell_A and ell_B. The lower bound P>=1 in the K-odd quotient linear sector survives, but the Step-90 executable enumeration is superseded and should not be imported. A TTCS review confirmed the main repairs and added further precision: the tensor-harmonic ledger still needs a complete electric/magnetic decomposition, and the fixed reflection K_A does not allow one to identify all AA/BB parity labels by factor exchange without changing the reflection. This means the spectral lower bound is usable, while the executable ledger remains a low-mode map rather than a full spectral proof.

The first corrected heat computation in the AB one-form sector gives a stable adjacent value near -0.03144249732 in the principal Step-89/91 normalization, with the axial lambda=1 part tending to -9/128 and higher AB modes partly cancelling it. The computation uses heat regularization, independent degrees, m-parity descent, and the simplified receiver. It also shows why self terms must not be treated as ordinary finite energies: self heat samples diverge quickly. This is a meaningful sector-level experiment, not the Ozuch coefficient. The full coefficient still needs the singular ALE source normalization, Bianchi/gauge projection, AA/BB tensor sectors, rigorous heat tail estimates, frame-normalized equality of adjacent ordered coefficients, and matching into the t_i lambda_i + t_i^2 mu_i radial expansion with an o(t^3) remainder. Even a nonzero final local adjacent coefficient would only constrain a chosen symmetric desingularization branch and would not exclude unrelated Einstein metrics on #_3(S^2 x S^2), let alone solve the all-dimensional Yau--26 proposition.

A second, potentially more central development came from the session group: A02's Sambusetti/BCG suggestion for M=N#N, where N is a closed real-hyperbolic four-manifold. I could not retrieve Sambusetti's original Math. Ann./C.R. source, so the exact theorem remains a source-reading gap. However, local Knowledge contains contemporary and later secondary anchors saying Sambusetti used BCG entropy inequalities to obstruct Einstein metrics on connected sums of two real-hyperbolic four-manifolds. More importantly, the quantitative metric inequality can be derived directly from BCG for the degree-two map N#N -> N. For any metric g on M, BCG gives h(g)^4 Vol(g) >= 2*3^4 Vol(N). If Ric_g >= -3(1+eps)g, Bishop gives h(g)<=3 sqrt(1+eps), hence Vol(g)>=2 Vol(N)/(1+eps)^2. Under two-sided negative pinching -3(1+eps)g <= Ric_g <= -3(1-eps)g, Chern--Gauss--Bonnet gives an upper volume bound Vol(g)<=2 Vol(N)(1-1/chi(N))/(1-eps)^2, using the corrected identity s^2/24-|E|^2/2=s^2/6-|Ric|^2/2 and a box-minimum argument for eps<=1/3. The two inequalities are incompatible when eps < (1-sqrt(1-1/chi(N)))/(1+sqrt(1-1/chi(N))) ~ 1/(4 chi(N)), or absolute |Ric+3g| defect below about 3/(4 chi(N)). This does not construct a counterexample; it supplies a quantitative obstruction showing that an N#N negative-branch construction cannot beat an O(1/chi(N)) defect threshold. The hyperbolic connected-sum route is therefore a serious constant-scale problem for the negative branch, not an immediate solution.
