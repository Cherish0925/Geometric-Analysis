## Deep-Reasoning

This step resolved the two critical interface gaps identified by Step 42's Preceptor review and the Strategist: the terminal spectral density integral bound and the general-order unweighted T derivative estimate. Together with the previously established mechanisms, the restricted four-dimensional negative Ishida–LeBrun gap theorem now has exactly one remaining analytic hypothesis.

The terminal density derivation traces the chain from the pointwise LeBrun spectral density G = f² − (b₋)² through four independent inputs: (1) the algebraic identity f = −2q/3 giving ∫f² = (4/9)E; (2) the nonneg LeBrun conformal/Yamabe decomposition (4/9)Δ_a = D_spec + D_Yam + D_mono establishing D_spec = ∫G ≤ (4/9)Δ_a; (3) the Chern–Weil identity I − R = T_M and the algebraic bound K ≤ 3I/2 combined with the sign-averaged monopole p ≥ A to give Δ_a ≤ 108π²(R − q_top); (4) the identity Ric⁰ = E⁰ giving R ≤ Q/(8π²). The result is ∫G ≤ 6Q + 48π²(−q_top)₊. TTCS independently verified every step and found three minor corrections (zero-point characterization of G, notation disambiguation, explicit citation of the LeBrun generalized-Yamabe spectral lower bound), none affecting the terminal spectral determination. The full terminal density satisfies ∫𝔇 ≤ 119Q + 912π²(−q_top)₊ where 119 = 1+4+108+6 and 912 = 864+48. This simplified density removes the positive-part rescaled-monopole harmonic-form/eigenline apparatus from the load-bearing proof, making the terminal contradiction rely only on: smooth curvature convergence from local Shi estimates, pointwise spectral determination G = 0 ⟹ Spec(W⁺) = (−1/6, 1/12, 1/12), and the Derdziński projector contradiction.

The general-order unweighted T estimate extends the Step 40 §4A mechanism from m = 1 to all m ≥ 0 by induction. At each level m, the critical term χ·C_{m+2}·T·P(X_m) (where C_{m+2} = ∇^{m+2}χ is the only derivative exceeding the available level) is handled by integrating one derivative off C_{m+2}, producing three terms all controlled by the inductive hypothesis: (I) dχ·C_{m+1}·T·P(X_m) uses bounded gradient and established L^q norms; (II) χ·C_{m+1}·∇T·P(X_m) uses the previous T-level; (III) χ·C_{m+1}·T·∇P(X_m) is absorbed by the a-weighted dissipation via Cauchy–Schwarz with χ ≤ √a. Connection variation terms contribute linearly to the Grönwall coefficient. The closed energy estimate d/dt ||X_m||_p^p ≤ C_p(1 + ||X_m||_p^p) gives finite L^p control for all m and p.

With this, hypothesis (H-AO) is resolved by combining the Step 40 staggered (U_m, C_{m+1}) induction with the Step 43 T_m estimates: bounded |U| on [τ, T*) implies all derivatives of T, U, and χ are in L^p for every finite p, hence pointwise bounds by Sobolev, hence smooth extension. Hypothesis (H-UT) is resolved directly.

The sole remaining analytic hypothesis is (H-DT): transported-coefficient DeTurck short-time existence with ĥat_a = a ∘ φ_t^{-1}, ε-independent tame estimates, uniqueness, and restart. Step 37 adapts Yang Theorem 8.2 conceptually but the regularization passage (replacing a by a + ε, obtaining ε-independent Ck estimates, and passing to the limit) and uniqueness among gauge-fixed solutions remain to be written as complete proofs. This is a concrete PDE problem with a known model (Yang's local Ricci flow theory) and a specific perturbation structure (the normalization −2ĥat_a·ĝhat is a bounded zeroth-order perturbation in the linearized tame estimate).

The broader Yau 26 problem remains open: the positive branch in dimension 4, arbitrary 4-manifold topology, dimensions n ≥ 5, dimension-only constants, and Lean 4 formalization are all untouched by the current negative-family result.
