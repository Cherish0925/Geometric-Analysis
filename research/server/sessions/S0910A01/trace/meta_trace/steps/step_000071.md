## Deep-Reasoning

This Step restored mathematical continuity after the Step 70 handoff by treating the new Strategist corrections as concrete implications to repair, not as a reason to reopen the whole restricted Ishida--LeBrun construction. The important lesson is structural: the IL negative-sign theorem depends on a very specific weighted cutoff-flow hierarchy. Replacing it by a superficially simpler fixed-weight hierarchy changes the object controlled by the Moser iteration. With the original Step 62 objects \(J_p=\int\eta^2a^{p-2}|E|^p\) and \(z_p=\eta a^{(p-1)/2}|E|^{p/2}\), the large-\(p\) limit controls \(aE\) on the whole support, including the transition region where the flow velocity matters for \(V(T)=\int\|aE\|_\infty\). The Step 70 fixed \(a^2\) version would only see the plateau cleanly and would not justify the velocity bound needed by the coupled bootstrap. The time exponent has the same character: the time-integrated recurrence must retain the factor \(L_k^{3/2}\), which gives \(\mathcal S^{1/3}t^{-1/2}I_0^{1/6}\); substituting \(I_0\le CtJ_6(0)\) then recovers \(t^{-1/3}\). The final endpoint is not a choice between two methods, but the result of keeping the established weighted hierarchy and the full time-slab arithmetic. The support lemma repair is similarly structural rather than cosmetic: the earlier Noether/BMY wording gave lower bounds on \(c_2\), while the curvature budget needs an upper bound. Under the actual IL class, where the \(X_j\) are minimal complex surfaces of general type, Noether gives \(K^2\ge2p_g-4\) and hence \(c_2=12(1-q+p_g)-K^2\le5K^2+36\). This explicitly uses complex-surface geography and must not be exported to arbitrary symplectic blocks. I also separated the volume upper bound from the subsequent estimate \(q_i\le J_i\le C\delta_i^2A_i\), avoiding circular use of \(\operatorname{Vol}\le CA\). These repairs preserve the useful selected-support construction and the older weighted defect mechanism while reducing the risk that a local note drift becomes a false theorem-level interface.

The broader route judgment remains unchanged in substance and sharper in detail. The IL theorem, even once repaired, is a strong four-dimensional negative-sign result for a special Seiberg--Witten geography; it is not the all-dimensional, sign-unrestricted Yau 26 resolution. The full problem is controlled by the sign quantifiers: a proof may succeed by establishing one normalized branch strongly enough in every dimension, but a disproof must defeat the relevant positive and negative branches in the same dimension, possibly on different manifolds. The current negative counterexample landscape has a complementary obstruction: surface-bundle families provide \(\delta_-\to0\) but leave unrestricted Einstein existence open through the full-curvature energy attainment equivalence, while extended graph manifolds provide all-sign Einstein nonexistence but still lack a \(\delta\to0\) construction. The A03 reflection-core identity read in this Step sharpens the graph route: for exact \(\operatorname{Ric}=-3g\) cores with a totally geodesic \(T^3\) center, \(e^{2t}(H-3)=-3-\int_0^t e^{2s}|S-I|^2ds\), so exact reflection cores approach cusp mean curvature only exponentially and never reach it at finite distance; approximate cores require logarithmic length. This is not a no-go, but it changes the graph problem from finite-time matching to an elliptic boundary-value and exponential-approach construction problem. The best next work should first verify the bounded repairs just made, then move research energy back to a global uncertainty: either the surface-bundle Einstein-existence question via full-curvature energy attainment and degeneration, or the graph-manifold small-defect construction via the corrected reflection-core identity and elliptic BVP structure. Repeated restricted-theorem reconstruction should not become the main trajectory unless a fresh mathematical inconsistency is found.
