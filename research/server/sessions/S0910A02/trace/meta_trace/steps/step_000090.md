## Deep-Reasoning

This step produced the strongest structural result of the session: the triple disjointness theorem explaining why Yau–26 resists both proof and refutation in dimension four. The core insight is the Chern–Weil neutrality of Ricci-flat ALE bubbles: for any Ricci-flat ALE 4-space, |Δτ| ≤ (2/3)Δχ with equality iff half-flat, so resolving orbifold singularities by ALE spaces can never decrease the Hitchin–Thorpe surplus χ − (3/2)|τ|. This is a pure algebraic identity following from |12π²Δτ| = |∫(|W⁺|² − |W⁻|²)| ≤ ∫|W|² = 8π²Δχ plus the triangle inequality for signatures. Verified on 15 examples spanning Kummer K3 (equality at surplus 0), positive mirror (all 5 rows), negative mirror, and both Davis hyperbolic involutions (122 and 10 isolated A₁ points).

The second theorem is entropy inaccessibility: for any A₁ resolution M of any real-hyperbolic 4-orbifold O, any degree-d ≠ 0 map M → Y (Y closed hyperbolic) satisfies d·χ(Y) ≤ χ_orb(O). The proof chains the near-pinched construction (Ric ≥ −(3+ε)g, Vol → Vol(O)), BCG volume comparison (d·Vol(Y) ≤ Vol(O)), and hyperbolic Gauss–Bonnet (χ = 3Vol/(4π²)). Combined with non-decrease: χ(M) − (3/2)|τ(M)| ≥ χ_orb ≥ d·χ(Y), so the Sambusetti inequality is automatically satisfied. This answers the Step 88 §4.3 decisive question NEGATIVELY for ALL orbifold+ALE resolutions — no π₁ computation, simplicial-volume estimate, or degree-map topology is needed.

The triple disjointness unifies three independent mechanisms: (1) PSC/Weitzenböck kills monopole/BF on the positive side (class-level); (2) IMT adjunction kills BF on the negative side (construction class); (3) Chern–Weil neutrality + BCG kills entropy/Sambusetti on all sides (all orbifold+ALE). Each mechanism is structural and independent. Together they explain the impasse: every known no-Einstein certificate is blind to every known near-pinched construction.

The step also corrected Step 88's false claim that the negative mirror has infinite π₁ (Step 57's van Kampen is the controlling result: all fillings are simply connected, via seam compression by EH caps). Patched both durable notes (step85 and step88) and wrote the comprehensive step90 note.

The research consequence is precise. A refutation of P₄⁻ must construct near-pinched metrics OUTSIDE the orbifold+ALE class — specifically, non-warped connectors through N#N at rate O(1/χ). This makes A03's forced-Bochner bootstrap (#624: coupled W = e + c|∇σ|² with Cheeger/spectral-gap localization) the single remaining negative-branch mechanism. A refutation of P₄⁺ requires a non-gauge-theoretic obstruction on the PSC mixed rows. Both branches remain open, but the space of possible approaches has been sharply narrowed.

The proof-side implication is equally important though not yet developed. If every near-pinched sequence asymptotically decomposes into Einstein orbifold + Ricci-flat ALE bubbles (the noncollapsed compactness prediction), then no curvature-integral or entropy obstruction can ever refute either strict branch, because Chern–Weil neutrality guarantees all such certificates are automatically satisfied. This converts neutrality from a characterization of failure into positive evidence for the proposition, conditional on the right compactness theorem. The class-T ALE loophole and transition-scale infrastructure become load-bearing in this direction.
