Spotti’s nodal smoothing paper supplies the local model z1²+z2²+z3²=s with s(t)=Ct+O(t²), the Eguchi-Hanson scale relation δ²=sqrt(|s|), and an explicit potential gluing picture, but its stated theorem assumes finite automorphism group and does not by itself apply to X4^T, which has continuous automorphisms in the algebraic classifications. Thus KE existence on the all-same-chirality del Pezzo smoothing can give zeros only along its matched parameter path, not force an arbitrary reduced function to vanish. This preserves the Step 79 lesson that movable parameters and their chart matter. Two concrete algebraic assets were created. First, an explicit diagonal pencil of quadrics Q0=sum xi²=0 and Q1_eps=eps x0²-eps x1²+(1+kappa eps)x2²+(1-kappa eps)x3²+c x4² was analyzed near the four A1 nodes. At eps=0 the eigenvalues have pairs (0,0) and (1,1), giving four nodes; the A-pair nodes have leading local smoothing parameter 2 eps, while the B-pair nodes have 2 kappa eps. Hence |kappa|=1 gives equal leading local smoothing magnitudes, and Spotti’s scale relation then gives equal EH diameters up to matched-coordinate constants. This is not yet the KE path, but it is a reproducible local model for the parameter-matching lemma. Second, a chirality-control ledger showed that in one equal-scale radial projection the all-same row has coefficient s+d_same+2 c_same, whereas the opposite-corner mixed row has s+d_same+2 c_opposite. Therefore, if a matched all-same KE degeneration proves s+d_same+2 c_same=0 in the same normalization and at the same power-log layer, the mixed coefficient reduces to 2(c_opposite-c_same). This is a genuine simplification: common self-response and same-orbit diagonal same-chirality terms can cancel out, leaving a direct adjacent chirality-difference calculation using the full product resolvent. The same ledger also captured the symmetry distinction stressed by the Strategist: the single-factor antipodal L reverses orientation and is compatible with the mixed assignment after chirality exchange, but it does not preserve the all-same oriented assignment; hence all-same KE existence cannot confirm the A+J+L mixed construction as a whole. The next substantial mathematical object is now sharper: derive the matched all-same control and compute the adjacent projected difference c_opposite-c_same, including possible logarithmic terms, with the additive product spectral denominators and the scalar/shape/Bianchi contributions retained.
