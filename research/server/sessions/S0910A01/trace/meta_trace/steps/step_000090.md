## Deep-Reasoning

This step discovered and developed the K-symmetry K(z,w)=(bar z,w) — single-factor meridian reflection — for the product orbifold (S^2 x S^2)/Z_2. Unlike L (which moves nodes between A-orbits) or J (which preserves chirality), K fixes all four nodes while exchanging chirality at each one: it maps omega_2^+ to omega_2^- with positive sign. This is the missing comparison symmetry that directly relates the two EH insertion models being compared.

The construction yields three mathematical results that substantially simplify the positive-branch desingularization calculation:

(1) Cross-parity elimination. Since P commutes with K^* and the source/receiver even/odd decomposition has definite K-parity, all cross-parity interactions K_{ij}^{01} and K_{ij}^{10} vanish for every node pair, including diagonal. The proof uses the naturality of the linearized curvature operator under isometries: K^* exchanges L_i^+ and L_i^- because K exchanges omega_2^+ and omega_2^-. This is a geometric statement valid at all orders, not restricted to flat V_2 jets. It removes the problematic even-odd self-singularities that Step 89 could not eliminate without 'separate symmetry or matched subtraction.'

(2) Geometric mode annihilation. The negative shape tensor q = g_A - g_B is K-even because K preserves both factor metrics (it's an isometry). The four scalar Ozuch zero modes u_A g_A etc. are K-even because Z_A = cos(theta_A) is invariant under the meridian reflection x_1 -> -x_1. Therefore the K-odd chirality source is geometrically orthogonal to all five problematic modes — not just at the flat V_2 level (which was Step 89's result) but in the full Bianchi-corrected, cutoff, curved-parametrix construction. This completely resolves the negative-mode and zero-mode concern that the Strategist and TTCS raised in Steps 88-89.

(3) K-odd spectral positivity. Product spectral decomposition (confirmed by TTCS) shows P >= 1 on the K-odd quotient-descending TT sector, with the axial Killing-Killing mode achieving the bound. The argument: the only negative TT eigenvalue comes from q (P = -1), which is K-even; scalar zero modes are K-even; all K-odd sectors have P >= 1 from the product spectrum. This means the Green pseudo-inverse G_P^perp is well-defined and positive as a bilinear form on the K-odd sector.

Combining all four symmetries A, J, L, K with the algebraic diagonal cancellation eps_i^2 - 1 = 0, the relative mixed-minus-all-same interaction reduces to a single object: an off-diagonal sum of smooth Green kernel values at distinct nodes in the odd-odd sector. No self-singularities, no negative modes, no zero modes, no cross-parity terms.

The critical limitation remains: P >= 1 does not determine the sign of individual off-diagonal Green kernel values K_{ij}^{11} at separated nodes. An off-diagonal matrix with zero diagonal is not positive-semidefinite in general. The TTCS verification confirmed all core claims and added the receiver-mechanism proof via linearized curvature naturality, while noting that full K-equivariance of the specific Ozuch/Biquard chart choices should be explicitly checked (natural operators satisfy it automatically, but local chart/gauge data need verification). TTCS also warned that higher-order nonlinear terms have K^*g_eps = g_{-eps}, so the multi-source cross-chirality structure at higher order needs separate treatment.

For the full Yau-26 problem, this is a structural simplification of the positive-branch constructive test. It does not prove or disprove Einstein existence on the mixed filling, and it does not address the restricted IL negative branch or dimensions >= 5. The strongest Yau-26-relevant asset remains the restricted IL negative-gap theorem. The product-orbifold work provides complementary structural insight into how spectral geometry distinguishes topologically different fillings of the same orbifold singularity.
