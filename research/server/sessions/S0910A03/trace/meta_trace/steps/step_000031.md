## Deep-Reasoning

This step resolved the central analytic controversy of the four-dimensional negative-branch program. The A01 claim that the weighted Moser iteration has a fatal half-power gap was examined against the Strategist's correction. The key insight is structural: the parabolic Moser step combines sup_t K_p (energy control) with K_{2p} = ||Z_p||_4^4 (Sobolev on Z_p = a^{(p-1)/2}Φ^{p/2}), and then K_{3p/2} ≤ K_p^{1/2} K_{2p}^{1/2} by exact Cauchy-Schwarz. The alleged half-power gap concerns ||Z_p||_3^3, which has a-exponent 3(p-1)/2 = (3p-3)/2 versus K_{3p/2} needing (3p-4)/2, a gap of 1/2. But ||Z_p||_3^3 is never used by the iteration. The correct interpolation factors are a^{(p-2)/2}Φ^{p/2} and a^{(2p-2)/2}Φ^p, whose squares give exactly K_p and K_{2p}.

The deeper mechanism is that ∇Z_p decomposes into cutoff gradient (controlled by r^{-2}K_p since a^{p-3}|∇a|^2 = 4a^{p-2}|∇χ|^2) and curvature gradient (controlled by D_p = ∫a^{p-1}F^{p-2}|∇Rm|^2). The a-weighted dissipation from the energy inequality exactly matches the Z_p gradient. This was verified by three independent sources: TTCS confirmed the interpolation identities and gradient decomposition; the Codex independently derived the same weighted recurrence and rejected unweighted Moser with a concrete counterexample; the verification script passed all 8 checks.

The Codex counterexample is sharp: near a regular zero of χ with a = x^2, placing a triangular bump w_ε in [ε, 2ε] gives ||w_ε||_4^2/(∫a|∇w_ε|^2 + ||w_ε||_2^2) ∝ ε^{-1/2} → ∞. This proves that no degenerate Sobolev inequality of the needed form exists, so unweighted Moser on U = aT fails at the Sobolev step. The U-equation also has an unweighted reaction ∫|U|^{p+1} that weighted diffusion cannot absorb.

For the continuation, TTCS Step 30 V1 provided the complete coupled lemma. The correction to Wang's printed bookkeeping is that the Γ₁ term, after one integration by parts, reduces ∇^{m+2}χ only to ∇^{m+1}χ, not to ∇^mχ. The correct induction couples X_p = ||∇^mU||_p^p with Z_p = ||∇^{m+1}χ||_p^p via d/dt(X+Z) + cD ≤ C(1+X+Z), which closes by Grönwall without dividing by χ. The connection-change formula ∂_t(∇^{m+1}χ) = U*∇^{m+1}χ + ∇^mU*∇χ + lower has the correct derivative counting, confirmed by the verification script. The TTCS correction about the transition region {0 < a < 1/2} being active was incorporated: the flow IS active there, but the continuation needs only U-derivative bounds (for flow existence via ∂_tg = -2Ric(U)) and plateau regularity (where a = 1, so T = U directly).
