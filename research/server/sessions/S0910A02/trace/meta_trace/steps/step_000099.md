## Deep-Reasoning

This Agent3 Execute step independently audited the concrete A01 Step98 product-orbifold computation before allowing it to change the Gate-3 route. The active Yau--26 proposition remains unresolved: no strict branch has been proved or refuted, no global no-Einstein theorem is known for the mixed rows, and no Lean formalization of curvature/gluing exists. The value of this step is narrower but important: it corrects the local positive-branch laboratory from a phase-frozen determinant story to a trace/traceless inter-node table with a verified split between the two balanced rows.

The main result is that A01's raw spectral data survived an A02 audit. I wrote `Sessions/S0910A02/workspace/scripts/step99_a01_trace_obstruction_audit.py`, which parses A01's `step98_full_chirality_tensor_maps.json`, `step96_kernel_plane_ab_map.json`, and A02's pattern data, then independently recomputes compact trace/double-angle and P/Q decompositions. The script produced `Sessions/S0910A02/workspace/data/step99_a01_trace_obstruction_audit.json`. It verifies, at every available tensor rung `(tau,Lmax)=(0.08,80),(0.04,120),(0.02,180),(0.01,260)`, that the physical same-chirality maps `B++` and `B--` vanish in AA/BB tensor sectors, with cross-parity zero and `B+-=B-+`; the AB sector ladder and pair comparison also have same-chirality maps zero. I then wrote `Sessions/S0910A02/workspace/scripts/step99_selected_spectral_recompute.py` and reran selected A01 spectral computations from the scripts rather than trusting the stored JSON alone. The output `Sessions/S0910A02/workspace/data/step99_selected_spectral_recompute.json` reports `pass: true`, with max tensor and AB deltas versus A01 stored data both `0.0`. This makes A01's same-chirality vanishing a real cross-verified raw inter-node fact, not merely a note-level assertion.

The audit also corrects the meaning of the earlier coefficients. The old values `c_adj=-0.03144` and `c_diag=+0.01773` were parity components, not physical same-chirality pair coefficients. In the physical cross-chirality table at receiver NN, the AB sector is pure trace/isotropic: adjacent sources NS and SN have trace coefficient `+0.12576998928`, equivalently isotropic eigen coefficient `+0.06288499464`; the diagonal source SS has trace coefficient `-0.070907042884`, equivalently eigen coefficient `-0.035453521442`. The AA/BB tensor sectors are traceless: adjacent NS gives a Q-type coefficient `0.0080049973185`, adjacent SN gives a P-type coefficient of the same magnitude, and the stored diagonal tensor cross coefficient is zero. Thus the leading obstruction is not the old `|q_diag|` versus `|2q_adj+q_diag|` comparison. The trace/isotropic channel is separate from the traceless P/Q channel and dominates the diagonal-balanced raw equation.

Pattern-by-pattern, the positive mirror now splits. For the diagonal balanced pattern `eps=(+,-,-,+)`, each receiver sees two opposite-chirality adjacent sources and a same-chirality diagonal source. The same-chirality diagonal term vanishes at the checked order, and the raw leading inter-node matrix has the form `0.06288499464*(u1+u2) I_K + T` with `||T|| <= 0.0080049973185*(u1+u2)`. The margin `0.0548799973215` is positive, so the raw leading inter-node matrix is uniformly positive definite/invertible for all positive bubble scale-squares and all phases. This verifies A01's diagonal-balanced leading local obstruction candidate in a stronger unequal-scale form than its original equal-scale trace statement. For the adjacent balanced representative `eps=(+,+,-,-)`, however, each receiver sees one opposite-chirality adjacent source and one opposite-chirality diagonal source. The scalar part is `P=0.06288499464*u_adj - 0.035453521442*u_diag`, while the adjacent traceless bound is `|Q| <= 0.0080049973185*u_adj`. Hence `|P|=|Q|` is possible for positive ratios `u_diag/u_adj≈1.54794206864` or `1.99951906257`, with `P=0` near `1.77373056560`. The trace obstruction is therefore not uniform across all mixed rows; it supports a diagonal-balanced local obstruction candidate and leaves an adjacent-balanced scale-ratio window that could become constructive or could be killed only by further coupled LS terms.

I patched the durable A02 research state accordingly. `workspace/notes/step99_a01_trace_obstruction_audit.md` records the audit, raw coefficients, pattern consequences, and remaining caveats. I overwrote `workspace/notes/step96_corrected_selection_and_determinantal.md` and `workspace/notes/step94_three_gates_and_highdim_theorem.md` so they no longer present the false `|Q|>|P|` solvability claim or the old equal-phase determinant criterion as Gate 3. I patched `workspace/notes/step85_yau26_established_reduction.md` to include Step99's same-chirality vanishing, cross-chirality trace table, diagonal/adjacent balanced split, and evidence files. I also sent peer message #675 with the audit result so A01/A03 can align their route maps.

Because the determinant mistake has recurred several times, I made a small formal algebra certificate. `Sessions/S0910A02/workspace/proofs/Step99RealLinearPQ.lean` proves the real determinant identity for `z ↦ Pz+Q conjugate(z)` and proves that if `|P|^2 ≠ |Q|^2`, then the real kernel is trivial. `lean_check` accepted the file with no placeholders or source axioms, and the strict command `qiushi-yau26 lean ... --declaration Yau26Step99.pq_kernel_trivial_of_normsq_ne --output Sessions/S0910A02/workspace/proofs/Step99RealLinearPQ.check.json` dependency-checked it with only the allowed Mathlib axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`. This formalizes only finite-dimensional algebra; it does not formalize curvature, Ozuch gluing, or Yau--26.

The mathematical frontier after this audit is sharper. The compatible Kähler-smoothing calibration is now supported by sources and computation: Biquard--Rollin identifies smoothing periods with the kernel plane `K=omega^perp`, OSS/Tian--Yau provide an open KE degree-4 smoothing locus, and A01/A02 spectral data show physical same-chirality leading inter-node vanishing. But a local theorem still requires a calibrated slice-compatible Lyapunov--Schmidt reduction, full self/complement/frame/global term accounting, and a relative `o(t^3)` expansion including possible logarithmic terms. Even that would only obstruct an Einstein branch with this product-orbifold limit and chirality pattern; it would not prove global no-Einstein on `#_3(S^2×S^2)` or settle Yau--26. The most useful next construction work is to turn the verified leading table into a conditional local theorem with exact hypotheses and to identify which uncomputed LS terms could still cancel or modify the diagonal-balanced margin, while preserving the adjacent-balanced row as a live possible existence window.
