## Memo: mechanisms governing the transition at the ratio $k/A=1/3$ (the $\sigma=-1$ branch)

The task frames the decisive object correctly: as $p=k/A\to 1/3^+$ the mixed Seiberg–Witten inequality $\tfrac1{4\pi^2}\int(s^2/24+2|W^+|^2)\ge \tfrac23(a^+)^2\ge \tfrac{2A}{3}$ combined with $2\chi+3\tau(X\#k\overline{\mathbb{CP}}^2)=A-k$ yields only $\int|E|^2\,dV\ge 8\pi^2(k-\tfrac A3)$, whose right side vanishes. I want to reopen the space around this and give several genuinely different mechanisms, some of which point toward a discontinuous transition (a uniform gap, hence support for $Q_4$ on this family) and one of which is the true remaining opening for a counterexample. The key move is to separate two mutually exclusive ways a sequence $g_j$ on $M_j=X_j\#k_j\overline{\mathbb{CP}}^2$ with $\delta_-(g_j)\to 0$ (necessarily $A_j,k_j\to\infty$) can carry its growing topology, and to attack each separately.

### 1. Curvature concentration is forced to be Ricci-flat, and Ricci-flat 4d atoms cannot absorb a $\overline{\mathbb{CP}}^2$

This is the strongest structural mechanism and I think it decides the concentrating regime completely.

Suppose along the sequence $\lambda_j=\max|Rm(g_j)|\to\infty$. Rescale $\hat g_j=\lambda_j g_j$. Because the Ricci tensor is invariant under constant rescaling as a $(0,2)$-tensor, $\hat g_j^{-1}\mathrm{Ric}=\lambda_j^{-1}g_j^{-1}\mathrm{Ric}\to 0$: *every* Ricci eigenvalue of the rescaled metric tends to $0$, and $\delta_-(g_j)\to0$ makes both the trace and trace-free parts vanish in the limit. Hence every pointed noncollapsed rescaled limit is a complete **Ricci-flat** 4-manifold with $|Rm|\le 1$ and $|Rm|(p)=1$ — a nontrivial gravitational instanton. This is the same bubble picture as in the noncollapsed Einstein degeneration theory (Anderson; Bando–Kasue–Nakajima; Cheeger–Naber; Ozuch I/II), and it forbids the naive constructions: a scalar-flat Kähler Burns cap on $O(-1)\to\mathbb{CP}^1$ has $s=0$ but $\mathrm{Ric}\ne0$, so after rescaling to size $\varepsilon$ its $g^{-1}\mathrm{Ric}$ has size $\varepsilon^{-2}$ and $\delta_-\sim\varepsilon^{-2}\to\infty$. Only genuinely Ricci-flat atoms are compatible with $\delta_-\to0$.

Now the topology. A noncollapsed Ricci-flat ALE 4-manifold is (Kronheimer; Bando–Kasue–Nakajima; Nakajima) a crepant resolution of $\mathbb C^2/\Gamma$, $\Gamma\subset SU(2)$, whose second homology intersection form is the definite Cartan matrix of an ADE type — **all self-intersections equal $-2$, an even form**. The blown-up summands each contribute a class $E_j$ with $E_j^2=-1$, an **odd** self-intersection. A single $(-1)$-sphere never appears as a vanishing cycle of any Ricci-flat gravitational instanton. Consequently the $k_j$ exceptional classes cannot be absorbed into any collection of Ricci-flat bubbles; a parity/intersection-form invariant obstructs it. The exceptional homology would then have to reside in the noncollapsed regular limit (the smooth Einstein body with $\mathrm{Ric}=-g$), but that limit is a fixed finite-topology space (morally the minimal model $X$ with its Aubin–Yau metric), which cannot hold $b_2$ growing like $k_j$. This is a contradiction: **concentrating $\delta_-\to0$ sequences on $X\#k\overline{\mathbb{CP}}^2$ with $k\to\infty$ are topologically impossible.**

What must be checked to make this a theorem rather than a strong indication: (i) the collapsed concentration case, where the atoms could be ALF/ALG/ALH rather than ALE. All known ALF gravitational instantons ($A_k$, $D_k$) still carry even, definite forms (they are hyperkähler, deformations of the ADE fibres); ALG/ALH are elliptic-fibration models and their compactly supported intersection forms should also be even, but this needs verification against the Chen–Chen / Hein classification. If every 4d Ricci-flat atom (ALE, ALF, ALG, ALH) has even form, the parity obstruction is unconditional. (ii) The transition between concentration scales in a bubble tree — but the total absorbed homology is a direct sum of even forms, so the parity argument survives tree formation. This is exactly the reverse of the Ozuch/Biquard desingularization obstruction ("no Ricci-flat ALE with a single $(-1)$-curve to glue"), read in the blow-*down* direction; the dossier's desingularization references (Ozuch I/II, higher-order obstructions) are the right technical home for the Pohozaev/matching apparatus.

### 2. Reduction to bounded curvature, and why the pinching lower bound genuinely degenerates there

If §1 excludes concentration, the only surviving route is $|Rm(g_j)|\le C$ with growing topology, which for $\mathrm{Ric}\approx -g$ means volume and diameter growth (Myers gives nothing on this branch). Here the integral accounting is transparent and, importantly, does **not** produce a gap by itself. Gauss–Bonnet with $s\approx-4$, $|E|$ small gives $\chi(M)\approx\frac1{8\pi^2}\!\int(|W|^2+\tfrac23)$, so bounded $|W|$ forces $\mathrm{Vol}=\Theta(\chi)=\Theta(A)$ (using Bogomolov–Miyaoka–Yau $c_2(X)\ge A/3$). Then
$$
\delta_-^2\ \ge\ \frac{\int|E|^2}{4\,\mathrm{Vol}}\ \ge\ \frac{8\pi^2(k-A/3)}{4\cdot 12\pi^2\chi}=\frac{k-A/3}{6\,\chi},
$$
and since $k-A/3=o(A)$ while $\chi=\Theta(A)$ as $p\to1/3^+$, this permits $\delta_-\to0$. So the bounded-curvature regime is where a counterexample, if any, must live; the mixed inequality does not close it. The decisive object becomes: *does $X\#k\overline{\mathbb{CP}}^2$ carry a bounded-geometry metric with $\mathrm{Ric}\approx-g$ realizing $\int|E|^2\approx 8\pi^2(k-A/3)=o(A)$?* Geometrically this asks for a bounded-curvature $\mathrm{Ric}\approx-1$ cap on the $(-1)$-disk bundle $O(-1)\to\mathbb{CP}^1$ that glues to the Aubin–Yau base. The concrete test: whether $O(-1)\to\mathbb{CP}^1$ admits a complete or long-neck Einstein/asymptotically-hyperbolic-type metric with $\mathrm{Ric}=-g$ and bounded curvature. Complete Einstein metrics with $\mathrm{Ric}=-g$ on line-bundle disk bundles exist (Calabi/momentum ansatz) only for a window of bundle degrees relative to the base; the $(-1)$ case over $\mathbb{CP}^1$ is exactly the marginal one to settle. A cohomogeneity-one / Bianchi-type ODE computation on $O(-1)\to\mathbb{CP}^1$ (radial function $b(t)$ over the $\mathbb{CP}^1$, warping the $S^1$ Hopf direction) with the $\mathrm{Ric}=-g$ system is a low-cost decisive computation: either a smooth capped solution with bounded $|Rm|$ exists (opening the bounded-curvature route) or the ODE has no such trajectory (reinforcing a gap).

### 3. Kähler rigidity: the algebraic-geometric path drives $\delta_-$ *up*, not down

There is direct evidence, computable now, that the natural constructions move away from $\delta_-=0$. Within Kähler metrics on $X\#k\overline{\mathbb{CP}}^2$: $\mathrm{Ric}\propto g$ would be Kähler–Einstein, impossible because the blow-up is non-minimal ($K$ not nef; $(-1)$-curves), so no Kähler metric on this manifold is near-Einstein in a scale-free sense. What *does* exist is the Arezzo–Pacard(–Singer) family: since the base has no holomorphic vector fields (general type), blow-ups at points in general position carry constant-scalar-curvature Kähler metrics with $s<0$ for small exceptional scale $\varepsilon$. These are explicit representatives of $M=X\#k\overline{\mathbb{CP}}^2$, so their Ricci pinching is directly computable. On the base they are $\approx g_{KE}$, giving $\delta_-\approx0$; but near each exceptional divisor they are modeled on a scaled scalar-flat Kähler Burns bubble, on which $g^{-1}\mathrm{Ric}$ has magnitude $\sim\varepsilon^{-2}$, so $\delta_-(g_\varepsilon)\sim C\varepsilon^{-2}$. Thus $\delta_-$ **blows up** as $\varepsilon\to0$ (the blow-down / degenerate-regime direction), and only decreases toward $O(1)$ as $\varepsilon$ grows to the largest value where the gluing survives. The immediate research product is a genuine computation of $\inf_\varepsilon \delta_-(g_\varepsilon)$ over the Arezzo–Pacard family (normalized to $\mathrm{Ric}$ average $-1$): if this infimum is a positive $O(1)$ constant, it is a sharp data point that the Kähler moduli do not approach $\delta_-=0$ at all, and that any counterexample must be non-Kähler on a simply-connected algebraic surface — a strong tension, since these spaces are otherwise geometrically rigid and (when $X$ simply connected) cannot even support $\sec<0$ by Cartan–Hadamard, removing the Fine–Premoselli / Gromov–Thurston / Hamenstädt–Jäckel existence mechanisms that live on aspherical manifolds.

### 4. The multi-monopole refinement, and the one place it can beat $O(k-A/3)$

The single-class mixed bound uses one optimally-signed basic class. All $2^k$ classes $a=c_1(X)+\sum\varepsilon_j E_j$ are monopole classes (blow-up formula). For a fixed metric $g$ with self-dual harmonic space $H^+$, writing the projections $v_0=c_1(X)^+$, $v_j=E_j^+$, the sign-averaging identity gives
$$
\max_{\varepsilon\in\{\pm1\}^k}\ (a^+)^2=\max_\varepsilon\Big|v_0+\sum\varepsilon_j v_j\Big|^2\ \ge\ |v_0|^2+\sum_j|v_j|^2\ \ge\ A+\sum_j (E_j^+)^2 .
$$
So the refined SW estimate reads $\frac1{4\pi^2}\int(s^2/24+2|W^+|^2)\ge \frac23\big(A+\sum_j(E_j^+)^2\big)$, which would push $\int|E|^2\ge 8\pi^2\big(k-A/3+\tfrac23\sum_j(E_j^+)^2\big)$ and could dominate the vanishing $k-A/3$ term. The subtlety is that $\sum_j(E_j^+)^2$ is metric-dependent and is $\approx0$ precisely for Kähler-adapted metrics, where each exceptional class is primitive $(1,1)$ and essentially anti-self-dual ($(E_j^+)^2\to0$, $(E_j^-)^2\to-1$). The live question is therefore mechanistic: does $\delta_-\to0$ (near-Einstein Weyl structure) *force* a lower bound on $\sum_j(E_j^+)^2$? Near-Einstein couples $W^+$ to the SW spinor and to the harmonic self-dual forms; a near-Einstein metric that keeps all $E_j$ anti-self-dual is close to Kähler, which §3 says is impossible without KE. Quantifying "how anti-self-dual the $E_j$ can be while $\delta_-$ is small" — e.g., a lower bound $\sum_j(E_j^+)^2\ge c\,k\,\delta_-^{\,\alpha}$ or an inequality tying $\sum_j(E_j^+)^2$ to $\int|W^+|^2$ and $\int|E|^2$ — is the analytic heart of whether the multiclass refinement closes the gap. This is exactly bottleneck B1' sharpened into a concrete inequality to prove or disprove, and it dovetails with LeBrun's finer curvature estimates (the $\int(s-\sqrt6|W^+|)^2$ and full $\int|Rm|^2$ lower bounds of the "Ricci curvature, minimal volumes, and SW theory" circle), which give more than the single scalar inequality and should be re-derived here with the full basic-class set.

### 5. Decisive computations and observations to separate continuous vs discontinuous transition

Concrete, mostly low-cost items that would each move the judgment:

- **ODE cap test (mechanism §2).** Integrate the cohomogeneity-one $\mathrm{Ric}=-g$ system on $O(-1)\to\mathbb{CP}^1$ with smooth-closure conditions at the $\mathbb{CP}^1$ bolt and prescribed asymptotics matching the Aubin–Yau collar. Existence/nonexistence of a bounded-$|Rm|$ trajectory is the single most decisive fact for the surviving route. Bianchi-IX and $U(2)$-invariant ansätze are the tractable families; symbolic Ricci from the warped-product formulas in the dossier §9 plus interval-verified shooting is enough.

- **Arezzo–Pacard $\delta_-$ computation (mechanism §3).** Compute $\delta_-(g_\varepsilon)$ explicitly from the scaled Burns model near the exceptional divisor (its $g^{-1}\mathrm{Ric}$ profile is known in closed form) and produce $\inf_\varepsilon\delta_-$. A positive value is strong evidence for a gap.

- **Ricci-flat atom parity verification (mechanism §1).** Confirm from the gravitational-instanton classification (ALE Kronheimer; ALF; ALG/ALH via Chen–Chen, Hein) that every complete 4d Ricci-flat model has an even, definite compactly-supported intersection form, so no $(-1)$-class occurs. This turns §1 into an unconditional statement about the concentrating route.

- **Self-dual projection inequality (mechanism §4).** Attempt to prove or refute $\sum_j(E_j^+)^2\ge c\,k$ (or a $\delta_-$-weighted version) under $\delta_-\le\varepsilon$, using the Weitzenböck/SW coupling; a positive result forces a uniform gap, a refutation (a near-Einstein metric with all $E_j$ nearly anti-self-dual) is precisely what a counterexample construction would need and would tell the constructor where to aim.

- **GH bubble-tree bookkeeping.** For any hypothetical $\delta_-\to0$ sequence, track the exceptional homology under Gromov–Hausdorff/bubble-tree convergence and verify it must vanish into Ricci-flat atoms (impossible, §1) or spread into an infinite-topology bounded-curvature body (route §2). This organizes the two routes into a single dichotomy that a proof of $Q_4$-on-this-family would resolve.

### Net scientific reading

The concentrating route to $\delta_-\to0$ at $p\to1/3^+$ appears genuinely obstructed by a parity mismatch between the odd $(-1)$ self-intersection of $\overline{\mathbb{CP}}^2$ and the even ADE forms of Ricci-flat gravitational instantons, and the algebraic-geometric (cscK) path drives $\delta_-$ upward rather than to zero. Both point toward a **discontinuous** transition (a uniform positive gap on this family, consistent with $Q_4$). The one route not closed is bounded-curvature, volume-growing, non-Kähler near-Einstein metrics, where the integral accounting shows the pinching lower bound honestly degenerates; there the existence question reduces to a concrete $\mathrm{Ric}=-1$ cap on $O(-1)\to\mathbb{CP}^1$ and to the multiclass self-dual-projection inequality of §4. Those two are the highest-value targets, each with an explicit computation attached, and each capable of flipping the judgment either to a proved gap or to a precise construction target.
