## Deep-Reasoning

This Agent4 Execute step did not try to extend a construction; it tested whether Step 71 and the new Strategist note should change the research allocation for the whole Yau--26 project. The main conclusion is a sharper separation between positive-branch proof work and positive-branch refutation work. For the exact active target, one sign branch in a fixed dimension suffices to prove the dimension statement, whereas refutation in dimension four would need both positive and negative branches to fail. The positive four-dimensional branch is structurally asymmetric: if a closed 4-manifold carries a metric with operator norm |Ric_g - g| <= delta < 1, then Ric_g is positive definite, scalar curvature is positive, Myers gives finite fundamental group, Bochner gives b1=0, and the Step-4 Chern--Weil coefficient gives strict Hitchin--Thorpe for delta < 1/(1+sqrt(3)). Thus the positive hypothesis class automatically passes the classical Einstein topological obstruction and simultaneously destroys the main hypotheses of the known smooth-structure Einstein obstructions. Reading Kotschick/LeBrun/Ishida--LeBrun sources confirmed the pattern: strict Hitchin--Thorpe no-Einstein examples known in simply connected dimension four are produced by Seiberg--Witten, monopole-class, or Bauer--Furuta mechanisms; Witten/Kotschick vanishing says a non-torsion or generic monopole class obstructs positive scalar curvature, and a nontrivial Bauer--Furuta invariant also obstructs positive scalar curvature. Since positive Ricci pinching supplies PSC, these mechanisms cannot supply a positive-branch counterexample. Simplicial-volume and entropy obstructions are also void in the finite-fundamental-group/amenable setting. This explains retroactively why the mixed walls, SW-void classification, positive product quotients, and collapsed stress families kept evading all known no-Einstein tools: it is not an accident of those families but a consequence of the entire positive pinched hypothesis class. A positive-branch refutation would require inventing a new Einstein-nonexistence criterion compatible with PSC, finite pi1, b1=0, and strict HT; no such mechanism is currently in the record or in the checked source catalogue. Therefore the scientifically honest allocation is to stop building positive counterexample families with existing obstruction tools and to pursue P_4^+ as a proof target, while preserving the possibility that a genuinely new PSC-compatible nonexistence invariant would reverse this judgment. The negative IL/graph-neck peer work may produce valuable restricted negative results, but even complete success there cannot refute Yau--26 in dimension four while P_4^+ remains standing.

The chi=8, tau=0 row is therefore an arithmetic coincidence rather than a general collapse theorem. Its real value is diagnostic: pure half-flat ALE concentration cannot account for the chiral four-unit requirement, so any real collapse in that row must involve ALF/integer-quantum, non-half-flat Ricci-flat, transition/neck/diffuse, or nonstandard ALE behavior.
