## Deep-Reasoning

Step 75 sharpened the positive mixed-row direction into a more constructive mathematical object. The full Yau--26 goal still asks for a sign-unrestricted all-dimensional proof or disproof, and the present positive product-orbifold family is not an exhaustive model for that goal. Its value is different: it is one of the sharpest known places where near-positive-Einstein input metrics coexist with smooth four-manifolds whose Einstein status is genuinely open. The right question is therefore not whether every higher obstruction coefficient vanishes identically. The useful question is solvability of a finite, coupled obstruction map after all real parameters are included: relative Eguchi--Hanson scales, kernel directions, chirality pattern, and the genuine global deformation variables left after slicing pure gauge. Ozuch's equations show why this matters. At each node of \(\mathcal O=(S^2\times S^2)/\mathbb Z_2\), \(R_+=R_- = \mathrm{diag}(\Lambda,0,0)\), so \(\lambda_k^j(\zeta_j,0)=0\) for \(\zeta_j\in\ker R_{\epsilon_j}\), and the local rank-one determinant formula makes the bare second obstruction \(\mu_1\) vanish in the product tensor. This does not settle the gluing: the first nontrivial information must come from how the local zero modes interact with global scalar/gauge directions, orbifold-kernel projections \(\nu_l\), and multipole-to-second-jet transport between nodes. A nonzero value of this interaction at one prescribed configuration only excludes that configuration near this orbifold degeneration; it does not exclude nearby choices or far-away Einstein metrics on the same manifold. Conversely, a zero of the leading map would be valuable only if accompanied by the usual inverse, nonlinear remainder, and transversality control. This is a more promising contribution than another universal flux identity because it turns the positive branch into a Kuranishi-type solvability problem with variables that might cancel between nodes. The step also corrected the product-with-spheres statement: products with positive Einstein spheres propagate near-pinched input metrics to dimensions \(n\ge6\), but they do not prove that a four-dimensional positive theorem implies the full theorem in higher dimensions, nor do they transfer nonexistence of an Einstein metric from a factor to its product, since an arbitrary Einstein metric on the product need not split. Thus the mixed rows are demanding stress families for positive forcing, not a solved reduction of all noncollapsed positive degeneration. The mature restricted IL negative theorem and the graph-neck work remain useful, but this step supports moving the main construction effort toward the coupled obstruction map for the positive mixed rows.
