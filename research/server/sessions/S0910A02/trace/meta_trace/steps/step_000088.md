## Deep-Reasoning

This Execute step as Agent2/Constructor built three structural results that reshape the negative-branch landscape of Yau–26 in dimension four, completing the work that Steps 86–87 identified but did not have time to write.

**1. Warped-product waist barrier.** The Ricci endomorphism at a warped-product waist (f'=0, f''≥0) in the metric g=dr²+f(r)²g_F with Einstein fiber Ric_F=2kg_F has eigenvalues λ_r=-3α and λ_F=β-α where α=f''/f, β=2k/f². For k≥0 (flat T³ or round S³ fiber), minimizing max(|λ_r+3|,|λ_F+3|) over α≥0, β≥0 gives δ≥3/2 exactly, achieved only at k=0, α=3/2. For round S³ (k=1): δ=3/2+3/(2f₀²)>3/2 at every scale. Verified computationally in `workspace/data/step88_warped_waist_and_bcg_threshold.json`. The check cases — H⁴ equidistant from H³ (k=-1, zero defect) and the hyperbolic cusp (k=0, no waist, zero defect) — all confirm the formulas. This is a pointwise obstruction, not an energy obstruction: it cannot be amortized by large volume. It explains why A03's monotone-functional search found neither a construction nor an exclusion for the connector problem: a cusp-to-cusp turnaround through S³ or T³ is locally forbidden below δ=3/2, while the defect-free waist (f=cosh r over H³) is H⁴ itself — Einstein.

**2. Sambusetti/BCG comprehensive note.** The promised durable note was written covering: the Sambusetti all-sign no-Einstein theorem for N#N (Kotschick Corollary 3, source-pinned), the BCG metric-level extension under Ric≥-3g (BBCG Theorems 1.1/1.5, Corollary 1.7), the quantitative pinching threshold ε<1/(4χ(N)), and the additive certificate limitation. The CGB lower bound s²/6-|Ric|²/2=(3/2)[(Σx_i)²-3Σx_i²]≥6(1-ε)² was independently verified; A01's intermediate formula (3/2)[(Σx)²/3-Σx²] has a factor-of-3 error in the first term but their final answer 6(1-ε)² and threshold δ~3/(4χ) are correct. The Strategist's key observation — the certificate is additive (from χ(M)=2χ(N)-2 versus 2χ(N), a fixed deficit) — means no additive certificate produces a uniform δ₀. At the critical rate, ∫|W|²=O(1).

**3. Resolution/simplicial-volume disjointness analysis.** This is the deepest new structural finding. Armstrong's theorem gives π₁(resolution) = Γ/⟨⟨order-2 elements with fixed points⟩⟩ because the EH manifold has π₁=1 and boundary RP³ has π₁=ℤ/2, so the EH cap kills the peripheral torsion. For the positive mirror: Γ=ℤ/2 is entirely torsion, giving simply connected resolution, ‖M‖=0. For the negative mirror: the coinvariant quotient π₁(Σ₂)/(x~ι*(x)) has finite abelianization (ℤ/2)⁴ per factor because ι* acts as -I on H₁; the full group is infinite (quotient of free products of ℤ/2) but with no evident map to a real-hyperbolic target. For a general real-hyperbolic A₁ orbifold ℍ⁴/Γ: π₁(M)=Γ₀/(γ~σ*(γ)) where Γ₀ is the torsion-free finite-index subgroup, and the abelianization rank equals b₁⁺(σ*), the +1-eigenspace dimension of σ* on H₁(N;ℝ). If b₁⁺>0, the resolution has infinite first Betti number and may have ‖M‖>0; if b₁⁺=0, the abelianization is finite and entropy certificates are harder to access.

The decisive open question is now precisely framed: Is there a closed real-hyperbolic 4-orbifold with isolated A₁ points whose resolution retains positive simplicial volume and a nonzero-degree map to a hyperbolic manifold violating the Sambusetti inequality? An affirmative answer refutes P₄⁻ using only the existing A₁ construction (Theorem 5) plus Sambusetti (Theorem 8a.1). A negative answer gives a double disjointness: PSC/Weitzenböck separates the positive side, and entropy/homotopy separates the negative side, making every known no-Einstein certificate disjoint from every known near-pinchable construction — each for its own reason.
