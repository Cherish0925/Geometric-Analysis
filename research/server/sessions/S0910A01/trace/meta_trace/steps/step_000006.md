## Deep-Reasoning

This step rebuilt the route judgment after the Step 5 near-equality progress. The curvature-ratio remainder remains valuable, but its meaning is narrower and more structural than the prior handoff made it appear. The estimate D_a/A -> 0 is normalized by the topology/volume scale A; in varying LeBrun-type families it does not say that D_a is small on the whole manifold, nor that every pointed smooth limit saturates LeBrun’s estimate. Total volume and diameter can grow, so a compact smooth limit and a smooth pointed limit are different mathematical objects. The compact-limit Armstrong contradiction is still correct in its proper scope, but compactness of Armstrong’s theorem cannot be silently transferred to a noncompact regular set. This changes the negative route from a supposed near-complete rigidity argument into a sharper local-and-measure problem: identify which regions carry small normalized proof defects, establish enough tensor convergence there, and then apply a genuinely local harmonic-Weyl mechanism rather than a compact almost-Kähler theorem.

The most promising refinement found in this step is the Derdziński local replacement. On an Einstein four-manifold, W^+ is divergence-free. Modern sources around Derdziński’s theorem, especially Catino--Dameno’s conformal Kähler rigidity text and LeBrun--Ozuch’s desingularization paper, record that the harmonic self-dual Weyl condition plus a two-eigenvalue nonzero W^+ structure gives local conformal-Kähler geometry on the set where W^+ is nonzero. If a smooth Einstein pointed limit inherited the calibrated profile with two eigenvalues and constant |W^+|, the conformal factor would be constant, so the limit would be locally Kähler. But a four-dimensional Kähler metric with Ric = -g has the standard self-dual Weyl norm |W^+| = 2/sqrt(6), while the calibrated profile has |W^+| = 1/(2sqrt(6)). Thus the Derdziński route can reproduce the contradiction locally, without requiring compact Armstrong. It is not yet a theorem about the original sequence: the missing work is precisely to prove the tensorial two-eigenvalue profile and the regularity needed to pass it to a smooth Einstein pointed limit.

The tensor algebra clarified what must be proved. The norm remainder controls |W^+| but not by itself the lowest eigenvalue w, an eigenform, a spinor direction, or the almost-Kähler form. For a trace-free W^+ with ordered eigenvalues alpha >= beta >= gamma = w, the algebra gives |W^+|^2 - (3/2)w^2 = (alpha - beta)^2/2 after substituting gamma = -alpha - beta. Thus two-eigenvalue saturation at the calibrated negative-Kähler orientation is equivalent to the extra tensor defect alpha - beta tending to zero, or equivalently w + sqrt(2/3)|W^+| tending to zero. The script also computed the pointwise slack between the |W^+|-version and the lowest-eigenvalue version of LeBrun’s squared estimate, so the next mathematical task is to locate this slack inside the chain of nonnegative inequalities in LeBrun’s proof and show that near final equality drives it small on good regions. A useful correction emerged: the calibrated eigenvalues (1/12, 1/12, -1/6) are one quarter of the Ric = -g Kähler-Einstein eigenvalues (1/3, 1/3, -2/3), not one half. The norm ratio one quarter was right; any note stating half for the eigenvalues should be repaired.

The positive branch was also restored to its real difficulty. S0910A02’s all-dimensional architecture is valuable because positive pinching gives Myers diameter control, volume upper control, Bochner b_1 = 0, finite fundamental group, and Gromov-Hausdorff precompactness. However, the step-5 positive noncollapsed theorem as written is not supported by the sources read here. Anderson-type C^{1,alpha} compactness requires an injectivity-radius or harmonic-radius input; volume lower plus two-sided Ricci and diameter does not automatically give global harmonic radius. Cheeger--Naber gives codimension-four regularity and curvature estimates in noncollapsed bounded-Ricci settings, with stronger regularity scale conclusions for Einstein metrics or metrics with derivative control on Ricci, not for arbitrary small Ricci defect alone. Naber--Zhang’s collapsed regularity requires a maximal nilpotency-rank condition on the fibered local fundamental group and has constants depending on the lower-dimensional model; it is not automatic from positive Ricci pinching. Round lens spaces already show exact positive Einstein collapse with zero defect and unbounded fundamental-group order, so a dimension-only proof of P_n^+ must understand compatible collapsed topologies rather than exclude collapse. The positive route is still strategically important, but it has at least two missing links: noncollapsed singular/orbifold regularity for near-Einstein metrics, and collapsed topology.

Ozuch/Yao-type desingularization sources likewise have precise scope. They concern exact Einstein sequences with noncollapse, diameter and volume assumptions, finite energy or dimension-four replacements, compact orbifold limits with isolated singularities, smooth or controlled ALE bubble trees, and weighted Hölder proximity to gluing models. They do not automatically handle arbitrary singular sets or near-Einstein input metrics with only pointwise Ricci defect. For the current Yau 26 research, their value is as a model for what must be proved if the near-pinched sequence can first be brought into their geometric regime.

The resulting route judgment is that neither the negative tensor route nor the positive existence route is ready for Execute as a settled program. The best immediate investment is a construction-level Explore step that turns the Derdziński idea into a conditional local theorem and decomposes LeBrun’s proof into nonnegative tensor slacks. This can produce a transferable mechanism: on any region with smooth pointed convergence, local volume concentration, and vanishing normalized slacks, the calibrated profile contradicts local Kähler geometry; hence any surviving near-pinning sequence must place most of its normalized volume in regions with failed regularity, failed tensor saturation, or spatial escape. That would be a real advance beyond family-specific bookkeeping and could later be combined with positive-branch compactness or Ozuch-style degeneration theory.
