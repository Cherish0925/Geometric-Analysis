[Part 4/4 of: [2012.13316] Higher order obstructions to the desingularization of Einstein metrics]

Definition A. 4 ( $C_{\beta, *}^{k, \alpha}$-norm on 2-tensors). Let $h$ be a 2-tensor on $\left(M, g^{D}\right),\left(M_{o}, g_{o}\right)$ or $\left(N, g_{b}\right)$. We define its $C_{\beta, *}^{k, \alpha}$-norm by
$$\|h\|_{C_{\beta, *}^{k, \alpha}:=\inf _{h_{*}, H_{k}}\left\|h_{*}\right\|_{C_{\beta}^{k, \alpha}}+\sum_{k}\left|H_{k}\right|_{g_{e}},}$$
where the infimum is taken on the $\left(h_{*},\left(H_{k}\right)_{k}\right)$ satisfying $h=h_{*}+\sum_{k} \chi_{A_{k}\left(t, \epsilon_{0}\right)} H_{k}$ for $\left(M, g^{D}\right)$ or $h=h_{*}+\sum_{k} \chi_{B_{k}\left(\epsilon_{0}\right)} H_{k}$ for $\left(M_{o}, g_{o}\right)$ or $\left(N, g_{b}\right)$, where each $H_{k}$ is some constant and trace-free symmetric 2 -tensors on $\mathbb{R}^{4} / \Gamma_{k}$.
A.2. An application of the analysis on weighted spaces. Consider for $n \in \mathbb{N}$, for $\gamma \in \mathbb{N} \backslash\{3-n, \ldots,-1\}$ and $0<\beta<1$ the Fredholm operator
$$P_{\mathbf{e}}: r_{\mathbf{e}}^{\gamma} C_{-\beta}^{2, \alpha}\left(\mathbb{R}^{n} \backslash\{0\}\right) \mapsto r_{\mathbf{e}}^{\gamma-2} C_{-\beta}^{\alpha}\left(\mathbb{R}^{n} \backslash\{0\}\right),$$
where the norms $C_{-\beta}^{l, \alpha}\left(\mathbb{R}^{n} \backslash\{0\}\right)$ on symmetric 2-tensors denote the norm on $\mathbb{R}^{n} \backslash\{0\}$ seen as orbifold at 0 like in Definition A. 1 and ALE at infinity like in definition A.2. This
operator is Fredholm and its kernel is composed of homogeneous harmonic 2-tensors in $r_{\mathbf{e}}^{\gamma}$. Its $L^{2}$-cokernel is the kernel of
$$P_{\mathbf{e}}: r_{\mathbf{e}}^{n+2-\gamma} C_{\beta}^{2, \alpha}\left(\mathbb{R}^{n} \backslash\{0\}\right) \mapsto r_{\mathbf{e}}^{n-\gamma} C_{\beta}^{\alpha}\left(\mathbb{R}^{n} \backslash\{0\}\right),$$
which is reduced to $\{0\}$. As a direct consequence of the open mapping theorem, we have the following result.

Lemma A.5. Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, for $\gamma \in \mathbb{N} \backslash\{3-n, \ldots,-1\}$ and $0<\beta<1$. Then, there exists $C>0$ such that for any symmetric 2 -tensor $v \in r_{\mathbf{e}}^{\gamma-2} C_{-\beta}^{\alpha}\left(\mathbb{R}^{n} \backslash\{0\}\right)$, there exists a 2 -tensor $h$ satisfying $P_{\mathbf{e}} h=v$, with
$$\|h\|_{r_{\mathbf{e}}^{\gamma} C_{-\beta}^{2, \alpha}\left(\mathbb{R}^{n} \backslash\{0\}\right)} \leqslant C\|v\|_{r_{\mathbf{e}}^{\gamma-2} C_{-\beta}^{\alpha}\left(\mathbb{R}^{n} \backslash\{0\}\right)} .$$ \(\square\)
