\hypertarget{from-gauge-saturation-and-weyl-modes-to-exact-geometry}{%
\section{From gauge saturation and Weyl modes to exact
geometry}\label{from-gauge-saturation-and-weyl-modes-to-exact-geometry}}

23 September 2026. Mathematical continuation of the first-line charter.
This note states the new consequences and how their proofs connect. Full
arguments are in the four linked manuscripts; earlier sources remain
intact. The statements are manuscript theorems, not independent
certification of correctness or publication priority.

\hypertarget{the-central-realization-statement}{%
\subsection{1. The central realization
statement}\label{the-central-realization-statement}}

Consider closed connected oriented smooth four-manifolds with \[
 \pi_1(M)\text{ torsion-free},\qquad \pi_2(M)=0.              \tag{H}
\] This includes aspherical manifolds, but also connected sums of them
and additions of \(S^1\times S^3\). The proof of this enlargement is in
\href{20260923_topological_core_scope.md}{topological core scope}.

Let \(a\) be the first Chern class of an actual Spin\(^c\) structure
solving the unperturbed monopole equations for every metric in \([g]\).
Its selection may depend on \(g\); the same structure must work
throughout the conformal weights. Set \[
 p=(a_g^+)^2,
 \qquad\delta_-(g)=\|\operatorname{Ric}_g^\sharp+I\|_{\infty,\mathrm{op}},
\] \[
 q(g,a)=\frac1{V_g}\left[
 \frac32\int_M(s_g^2+12|W_g^+|^2)\,d\mu_g-72\pi^2p\right]\ge0,
                                                               \tag{1}
\] where Weyl norms are Hilbert--Schmidt curvature-operator norms.

\textbf{Realization theorem.} For fixed \(D,A_0>0\), there is
\(\varepsilon(D,A_0)>0\) such that \[
 \boxed{\begin{gathered}
 (H),\quad \operatorname{diam}(g)\le D,\quad p\ge A_0,\\
 \delta_-(g)+q(g,a)<\varepsilon(D,A_0)\\
 \Longrightarrow\ \exists(h,J):\operatorname{Ric}_h=-h,\\
 (h,J)\text{ Kähler on the same oriented smooth }M.
 \end{gathered}}
                                                               \tag{2}
\] If \(a^2>0\), its integral square gives \(p\ge1\); then one may use
the threshold \(\varepsilon(D,1)\). No prescribed complex structure,
cover, independent Weyl bound or negative-square cost is required.

\href{20260923_gauge_saturation_ke.md}{The complete proof} uses the
preceding rounds in three different roles:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  The classical scalar monopole inequality supplies noncollapse from
  \(p\ge A_0\), the pointwise Ricci bound and bounded diameter.
\item
  Parameter-two saturation supplies the missing critical concentration
  control. With \(\kappa=\fint|\operatorname{Ric}+g|^2+q\), every
  measurable set satisfies \[
  \int_E|W^+|^2\le\frac43\operatorname{Vol}(E)
                             +\frac23\kappa V.             \tag{3}
  \] Thus along a vanishing-defect sequence, no half-flat ALE bubble can
  survive the topological core obstruction. The earlier harmonic-radius
  argument gives compactness preserving the smooth type.
\item
  The inherited actual-form theorem supplies bounded global self-dual
  forms with vanishing covariant derivative and asymptotic norm
  \(\sqrt2\). On the compact limit they become a nonvanishing parallel
  form, hence an actual global Kähler structure. A fixed small-defect
  threshold follows by contradiction over changing manifolds.
\end{enumerate}

This closes a previously unproved connection from actual gauge supply to
exact realization in a specified regime. It does not claim that a single
small average derivative already implies parallelness without
compactness. Only first-order form control is used; the old Hessian
square-cost budget is not smuggled into (2).

\hypertarget{a-genuine-example-determines-the-limit-of-this-implication}{%
\subsection{2. A genuine example determines the limit of this
implication}\label{a-genuine-example-determines-the-limit-of-this-implication}}

The fixed nonzero-shear closed metrics from the second scientific line
satisfy, after the normalization \(g_\ell=3G_\ell\), \[
 \delta_-(g_\ell)\to0,
 \quad V_{g_\ell}=2\pi^2A,
 \quad a^2=2\chi+3\tau=A>0,
 \quad p_\ell\ge A.
\] Chern--Weil gives the new explicit saturation consequence \[
                 0\le q(g_\ell,a)
                         \le36\delta_-(g_\ell)(1+\delta_-(g_\ell))
                         \longrightarrow0.                 \tag{4}
\] These are fixed aspherical smooth manifolds with no Kähler structure
even on any finite cover. Full curvature and each fixed curvature
derivative remain bounded in the inherited construction. They therefore
\textbf{disprove the diameter-free version of (2)}, not just leave its
proof incomplete. The new theorem forces their diameters to diverge.

This is a direct connection between the first line's realization
theorem, the second line's actual construction and the third line's
virtual-Kähler obstruction. The example does not exclude a completely
non-Kähler Einstein metric on that same manifold, so it is not a Yau26
counterexample.

\hypertarget{critical-chiral-information-is-sufficient-for-compactness}{%
\subsection{3. Critical chiral information is sufficient for
compactness}\label{critical-chiral-information-is-sufficient-for-compactness}}

The \href{20260923_critical_chiral_threshold.md}{critical-threshold
theorem} provides a second route to realization, without gauge
saturation. In (H), fix two-sided Ricci bounds, a diameter upper bound,
a volume lower bound and a radius \(r_0>0\). A uniform small threshold
for \[
 \sup_x\min\left\{
        \int_{B_{r_0}(x)}|W^+|^2,
        \int_{B_{r_0}(x)}|W^-|^2\right\}                    \tag{5}
\] gives a uniform harmonic radius and topology-preserving compactness.
Adding sufficiently small \(\delta_-\) gives an exact negative Einstein
metric on the same smooth manifold. The small half may be different on
different balls. This replaces a supercritical \(L^q\), \(q>2\), or
vanishing equiintegrability assumption by a fixed critical local
threshold.

The corresponding degeneration statement is spatial, not just a global
energy inequality: a bubble must carry a definite amount of
\textbf{both} Weyl energies at the same concentration point. The proof
combines a classical Sobolev--Weitzenböck gap on the exact Ricci-flat
blowup with core exclusion in both orientations. The explicit threshold
and norm conversion are in the proof; no sharp constant is claimed.

Furthermore, the nondegenerate intersection pairing shows that any
finite- fundamental-group ALE core embedded in (H) must have zero
rational second homology. Thus any remaining smooth ALE bubble would be
a rational homology ball with both Weyl halves nonzero. The existence or
exclusion of these general mixed-chirality bubbles is not settled by the
half-flat classification used here.

\hypertarget{exact-allowed-mode-selection-not-another-approximate-estimate}{%
\subsection{4. Exact allowed-mode selection, not another approximate
estimate}\label{exact-allowed-mode-selection-not-another-approximate-estimate}}

For an exact \(\operatorname{Ric}=-g\) metric let \[
 \mathscr S(g)=\fint_M
       (\lambda_3(W^+)-\lambda_2(W^+))^2.
                                                               \tag{6}
\] The \href{20260923_allowed_mode_rigidity.md}{spectral no-bubbling
theorem} proves that, under (H), bounded diameter and positive volume
lower bound, small \(\mathscr S\) gives full curvature bounds and smooth
compactness. There is no pointwise relative pinching or Weyl-amplitude
assumption.

Its mechanism is different from simply improving the older constants. On
a hypothetical Ricci-flat ALE blowup, vanishing splitting gives the
spectrum \((-4t/3,2t/3,2t/3)\). The exact Weyl equation gives
\(\Delta t\le-4t^2\) for the nonnegative Laplacian, and ALE decay forces
\(t=0\). The bubble is therefore half-flat and topologically impossible.
The nonzero Kähler mode allowed at the negative Einstein scale cannot
remain as a nonzero mode at the Ricci-flat ALE scale.

This supplies two exact consequences:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  For every fixed \(D\), an Einstein metric on any
  \(\Sigma_g\times\Sigma_h\), \(g,h\ge2\), with diameter at most \(D\)
  and \(\mathscr S<\varepsilon(D)\), is \textbf{locally isometric to
  \(\mathbb H^2_{-1}\times\mathbb H^2_{-1}\)}. Compactness places every
  counterexample sequence near a zero-splitting limit; the verified
  complex-product characterization and unobstructed local Einstein
  moduli then force exact geometry. The finite-cover and eigenline
  issues are explicitly treated. This is a classification of the metric
  under test, not merely a new proof that products admit an Einstein
  metric.
\item
  On the specified nonzero-shear self-gluings, every exact negative
  Einstein metric with diameter at most \(D\) obeys
  \(\mathscr S\ge\eta(D)>0\). The previous relative pointwise pinching
  restriction has been removed. This remains a constraint on possible
  outputs, not their complete exclusion.
\end{itemize}

The general allowed-mode statement keeps unobstructedness at KE limits
as an explicit condition. It is checked for the product application, not
asserted for all negative KE surfaces. The ASD endpoint uses an
established local chiral-rigidity theorem. Classical local theorems are
credited rather than counted as new results.

\hypertarget{what-this-changes-in-the-first-line-programme}{%
\subsection{5. What this changes in the first-line
programme}\label{what-this-changes-in-the-first-line-programme}}

The original integral obstruction mechanism remains valid independently
of the new bounded-diameter regime. The present work adds a precise
realization connection and an exact allowed-mode application. Actual
gauge saturation, or critical chiral/spectral data, prevents specific
bubbles when combined with topology and the global geometric bounds.
Smooth-type compactness then becomes available. Global actual forms
yield KE realization; an exhausted local Einstein moduli germ yields
exact mode rigidity. These outputs have different hypotheses and must
not be collapsed into a single unconditional master formula.

The immediate mathematical choices are now sharper. In the compact
regime, the remaining concentration must survive the rational-ball and
paired-chiral constraints. In the large-diameter regime, the actual
shear example proves that saturation cannot alone enforce Kählerization.
For its unrestricted Einstein question one needs either a genuinely
non-Kähler exact realization or information governing every possible
output, not further smallness of the same saturation error. These are
specific unresolved alternatives, not a prediction that either will
resolve the entire original problem.

No complete sign branch of Yau26 is proved. What is now available is a
theorem-level connection among actual fields, topology, degeneration and
exact geometry, together with an actual sharpness construction
delimiting that connection.
