\hypertarget{gauge-supply-global-propagation-collapse-and-singular-realization}{%
\section{Gauge supply, global propagation, collapse, and singular
realization}\label{gauge-supply-global-propagation-collapse-and-singular-realization}}

24 September 2026. Current research entry after
\href{20260924_global_realization.md}{compact realization}. The four
proof notes below address different restrictions of that result. They
are not four independent claims of world-first discovery, nor a
partition of all Yau26 inputs. Their common question is which
information lets an approximate Einstein metric lead to a conclusion
about its original smooth manifold.

\hypertarget{gauge-input-need-not-live-on-the-manifold-carrying-the-output}{%
\subsection{1. Gauge input need not live on the manifold carrying the
output}\label{gauge-input-need-not-live-on-the-manifold-carrying-the-output}}

The \href{20260924_virtual_gauge_realization.md}{finite-cover theorem}
allows actual monopole inputs on a finite cover \(N\to M\), with no
bound on its degree \(d\). Under bounded base diameter, a per-sheet
projection floor \(p/d\ge A_0\), and bilateral saturation, it produces
an exact metric on the \textbf{base} with universal cover
\(\mathbb H^2\times\mathbb H^2\). For a nonorientable base, one actual
input on an oriented finite cover suffices. At the Miyaoka--Yau
numerical boundary, one actual input with \(p\ge d(2\chi+3\tau)(M)\)
gives a locally complex-hyperbolic metric directly from small pointwise
Ricci defect.

The mechanism is to descend the scalar distance of the Weyl operator
from its equality orbit, not the auxiliary form or Chern class. Degree
cancels in the normalized estimates. Compactness and smooth-type
preservation are then proved downstairs. There is no assertion that an
arbitrary Einstein metric found upstairs is invariant and descends.

A concrete diagonal quotient of two even-genus hyperbolic surfaces has
\(\chi+\tau\equiv2\pmod4\) and hence no almost-complex structure in
either orientation, but has the required cover inputs and an exact
product quotient metric. This is a classical equality example, not a new
manifold. It demonstrates why allowing cover supply genuinely enlarges
the input description, and why the conclusion is Riemannian rather than
global KE.

\hypertarget{large-volume-no-longer-forces-a-small-total-residual}{%
\subsection{2. Large volume no longer forces a small total
residual}\label{large-volume-no-longer-forces-a-small-total-residual}}

The preceding no-diameter repair theorem required small unnormalized
\(L^2\) Ricci residual. The
\href{20260924_uniformly_local_repair.md}{uniformly local repair
theorem} replaces that condition by an explicit propagation estimate. If
the full gauged Einstein operator has \textbf{positive quadratic-form
coercivity} \(\lambda\), and the actual manifold satisfies \[
 \operatorname{Vol}B_R(x)\le C_G(1+R)^d e^{HR},\qquad H^2<8\lambda,
\] then an exponentially weighted energy identity gives \[
 \|L_g^{-1}f\|_{C^{2,\alpha}_{\rm uloc}}
       \le C\|f\|_{C^{0,\alpha}_{\rm uloc}}.
\] With the stated local regularity hypotheses, pointwise small Ricci
defect therefore gives an exact negative Einstein metric, without
diameter, volume or global-residual smallness. The proof retains every
tensor mode.

Fixed cyclic covers of a closed hyperbolic manifold have uniform linear
ball growth and Koiso coercivity. Independent small perturbations in
each cell can have total residual growing like \(t\sqrt m\), yet satisfy
the uniform repair criterion. These manifolds already admit Einstein
metrics; the increment is a uniform repair neighbourhood for distributed
errors. The
\href{20260924_higher_dimensional_local_repair.md}{bounded-chart
corollary} states the analytic argument in every dimension \(n\ge3\),
including the cyclic-cover examples and their dimension-dependent
coercivity constants. The four-dimensional no-bubble theorem is one
supplier of its local charts, not part of the weighted inverse identity
itself.

\hypertarget{genuine-collapse-now-has-an-actual-form-connection}{%
\subsection{3. Genuine collapse now has an actual-form
connection}\label{genuine-collapse-now-has-an-actual-form-connection}}

The \href{20260924_collapsed_bilateral_bulk.md}{collapsed bulk theorem}
uses maximal local nilpotency rank in the Euclidean-model regime of
Naber--Zhang. For bilateral gauge deficit \(K=2r+q_++q_-\to0\) and
pointwise Ricci defect tending to zero, local universal-cover balls
approach \(\mathbb H^2\times\mathbb H^2\) outside an exceptional set
with \[
                         \operatorname{Vol}(\mathcal E)/V\le CK^{1/4}.
\] There is no diameter, base-ball volume, homotopy or total-volume
bound. The essential transfer is an estimate for \textbf{local averages}
whose constant is independent of covering multiplicity. It is false for
unnormalized integrals, as an explicit pinching-surface product shows.

This changes the local input regime to include genuine collapse. It does
not give a global repair theorem: maximal rank is not automatic, the
small exceptional set can carry topology, and transition maps and low
modes are not controlled by its volume alone.

\hypertarget{with-nonzero-second-homotopy-allow-the-singular-limit}{%
\subsection{4. With nonzero second homotopy, allow the singular
limit}\label{with-nonzero-second-homotopy-allow-the-singular-limit}}

The \href{20260924_orbifold_smoothing_bridge.md}{orbifold and smoothing
theorem} removes \(\pi_2=0\) from the \textbf{compactification}
conclusion. Bounded diameter, actual positive projection and
simultaneous small Ricci/gauge defects yield a negative KE orbifold with
a global orbifold complex structure. Smooth ALE blow-up limits are
anti-self-dual.

To obtain an exact metric on the original smooth type, the note gives a
separate theorem: a global Q-Gorenstein smoothing, together with
matching of each whole geometric filling to its Milnor fiber
\textbf{with its boundary marking}, gives an oriented diffeomorphism to
a smooth fiber. Canonical ampleness and Aubin--Yau then provide the
required exact metric. The Lee--Park logarithmic-tangent obstruction
criterion supplies a checkable global smoothing condition; nodal
hypersurfaces give an explicit classical example. Merely classifying a
terminal ALE bubble does not supply the whole filling or this matching.

This is an alternative to excluding every bubble. The raw geometric data
prove the orbifold conclusion; the additional smoothing and
marked-topology conditions remain additional, not hidden consequences of
saturation.

These results suggest two focused continuations, not another general
route list. One is to classify the global transition and low-mode data
of the product-type collapsed regions, where the positive-coercivity
repair theorem does not apply. The other is to determine when the
geometric fillings produced by actual near-saturation must meet the
relative Milnor criterion, or to construct a counterexample. Either can
change exact existence; merely shrinking an average error or classifying
one more terminal bubble cannot substitute for it.

The retained diameter-free integral obstruction theory remains
independent and valid under its own actual-input assumptions. None of
these additions proves the unrestricted obstruction/existence dichotomy
or completes a Yau26 sign branch. All proofs, classical dependencies,
exact model tests and local commits remain in this project; inherited
sources and live systems are unchanged.
