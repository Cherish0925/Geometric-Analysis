## Deep-Reasoning

This Execute step repaired a significant false inference in the product-orbifold mixed-chirality route. The Step 86 zero-mode boundary projection result remains useful: Ozuch's Proposition 2.10 correctly gives the Fredholm zero-mode coefficients nu_l as boundary pairings, and the product scalar-kernel candidates pair trivially with the H^4 multipoles by C_{11} orthogonality. What failed was the later leap from that zero-mode statement to a mode-by-mode vanishing of the full Green response. Product factorization does not force the tensor indices of an eigentensor to equal the derivative indices in its quadratic jet, so the rank-four bilinear chirality defect D_{ij,mn} found in Step 86 is not inert. It can be seen by genuine product eigentensors whose local Taylor terms involve four distinct indices.

The concrete missed channel is the mixed coexact one-form product h=(*du_A)⊙(*du_B) on S^2 x S^2, with u the height function on each unit sphere. On a unit sphere a Killing one-form alpha=*du satisfies nabla^*nabla alpha=alpha; hence on the product nabla^*nabla h=2h, and because product curvature has no AB curvature action, P h=(1/2)nabla^*nabla h - Rcirc h = h. Thus this is a legitimate low eigenvalue product tensor, not a zero mode and not a pure diffeomorphism artifact. It is trace-free and divergence-free. Near the NN node, in the Step 84--86 coordinates, alpha=x_1 dx_0-x_0 dx_1+O(r_A^3) and beta=x_3 dx_2-x_2 dx_3+O(r_B^3), so h contains (h_2)_{03}=-x_1x_2 and (h_2)_{12}=-x_0x_3. These are exactly the fully mixed tensor/derivative-index channels Step 86's diagonal D_{ij,ij} check skipped.

The source boundary calculation was made exact. Using H^4_+=-(1/2)sum_k(x^T C_{2k}x)C_{2k}, H^4_-=-(1/2)sum_k(x^T C_{k2}x)C_{k2}, the S^3 quartic moment, and the quotient factor for S^3/Z_2, the script computed B_epsilon(V_2)=3 int_{S^3/Z_2}<H^4_epsilon,V_2>. For the missed coexact Killing-Killing mode it gives B_+=pi^2/2, B_-=-pi^2/2, so B_+-B_-=pi^2. The same data record D:M=-8 and D_{03,12}=D_{12,03}=2 in the shared C_ab convention. The receiver calculation, using the flat linearized curvature formula from the full quadratic jet, gives delta R^+=diag(-2,1,1) and delta R^-=diag(2,-1,-1) in the script's Riemann convention; for zeta=omega_2 the local radial components are +1 and -1. This gives a real nonzero source-and-local-receiver channel at P-eigenvalue 1, enough to disprove the Step 86 assertion that every physical product mode lies in ker(D).

TTCS independently verified this correction and added important refinements. The quotient generator near a node should be understood as a simultaneous pi-rotation with local tangent action -I, not the ordinary antipodal map on each S^2. The wording that one-form factors are odd is only a local coefficient shorthand; full one-form pullback includes the sign of dx. TTCS also confirmed that the nonzero single-mode calculation cannot be promoted to the full Ozuch coefficient: the real object is the basis-independent, node-to-node, normalized and regularized L_i G_P^perp(S_j^+-S_j^-), with complete eigenspace projection, node-frame transport, radial receiver normalization, and zero/negative mode handling.

I extended the first calculation to the l=1 one-form product source space. The all coexact source script shows that in the NN axial frame, among the coexact l=1 products, only (*dZ_A)⊙(*dZ_B) gives a nonzero chirality source difference; X/Y coexact modes either have zero source difference in this source-side projection or have nonzero constant jets that require receiver frame/background-curvature treatment before they can be summed. The larger exact/coexact l=1 source matrix shows the quotient-descending nonzero source projections are dZ_A⊙dZ_B, with B_+=B_-=-pi^2/2 and no chirality difference, and (*dZ_A)⊙(*dZ_B), with B_+=pi^2/2 and B_-=-pi^2/2. A one-dimensional axial low-mode node-transport ledger, using norm ||h||^2=64pi^2/9 on the quotient and eigenvalue 1, gives a provisional rank-one contribution of magnitude 9/64 with signs determined by the N/S node signs. This is a controlled low-mode contribution and a unit test for a complete product-eigenspace engine, not the final geometric coefficient.

For the broader Yau-26 research, this step sharpens the positive mixed-row route rather than resolving it. The all-same del Pezzo Kähler-Einstein calibration cannot certify the mixed row through a vanished leading interaction; a genuine chirality-sensitive low eigenvalue channel exists. Whether complete eigenspace projection and node transport cancel or reinforce it remains open. The restricted Ishida-LeBrun negative route remains the strongest family-level result tied to the original problem, while the product-orbifold route remains a local positive-Einstein stress test whose outcome would still be narrower than the full sign-independent all-dimensional proposition.
