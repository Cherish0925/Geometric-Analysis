This Agent2 Execute step produced three interconnected structural results that reshape the noncollapsed positive-branch proof architecture for Yau–26, then corrected them under TTCS verification and peer challenges.

The first result is the ℓ-mode classification: for homogeneous degree-ℓ TT/radial-null modes on a transverse cone R^d/Γ with d≥4, the unweighted nuclear-norm mass integral is H_2 = log(R/b) for ℓ=2 and H_ℓ = b^{2-ℓ}/(ℓ-2) → ∞ for ℓ>2. Therefore ℓ=2 is the unique unweighted log-critical polynomial mode, giving δ ≥ c/L, while ℓ>2 gives δ ≥ c·b^{ℓ-2} → 0, which is power-scale and vanishes in the bubbling limit. This is dimension-general and TTCS-verified.

The second result combines the ℓ-classification with Ozuch's scaling hierarchy to establish a structural impossibility: no theorem based solely on C⁰ Ricci-endomorphism pinching δ→0 can force higher Biquard obstructions to vanish. Ozuch lines 142–147 show the k-th obstruction creates O(t^k) error in the (0,2)-form on the ALE core, hence O(t^{k-1}) in operator norm. Since FND:A1construction's dominant error is O(1/|log a|) and t~a^2, we have O(a^{2(k-1)}) << O(1/|log a|) for all k≥2. The Step-70 skeleton's step 3 ('force the whole obstruction tower to vanish from tight pinching') is therefore unachievable by any flux/pinching refinement. TTCS confirmed the mechanism but added precision: no concrete nonzero higher obstruction has been computed on the mixed rows, and the identification of cone degree ℓ with Biquard obstruction order k needs a separate theorem.

The third result is product dimension propagation: on M⁴ × S^{n-4}(Ric=σg) for n≥6, the product metric has δ(product) = δ(g_a) since Ricci is block-diagonal on products. A gap at n=5 arises because S¹ has Ric=0. However, both TTCS and A03 (#512) corrected a critical overclaim: this does NOT prove P₄⁺ ⟹ P_n⁺, because P_n⁺ quantifies over ALL closed n-manifolds, not just products. An Einstein metric on M⁴×S^{n-4} need not split. The correct statement is that 4D is a bottleneck (any P_n⁺ proof must handle these products) but not a universal reduction. The note has been corrected accordingly.

The combined consequence is a clear architecture with two named honest continuations for the noncollapsed branch. Route A (tower rigidity): prove that first-obstruction vanishing forces all higher obstructions to vanish; this is a strengthening of Ozuch's Question 0.8 and is testable on the mixed rows. Route B (global existence): find Einstein metrics by a method that doesn't go through nearby orbifold limits. The mixed-row Einstein question on (S²×S²)/Z₂ is now the crux of the noncollapsed P₄⁺ branch, not a bounded laboratory. Both outcomes of the second-obstruction computation are consequential: vanishing would produce the first positive Einstein metric on a s.c./PSC/SW-void/non-Kähler four-manifold; nonvanishing would prove the local route fails for these manifolds.

This is a useful specification for future library work.
