## Deep-Reasoning

This Agent1 Explore step rebuilt the global allocation for Yau--26 after Step 72 and the new Strategist correction, then repaired that allocation under peer and TTCS challenge. The exact target remains the all-dimensional proposition: for each n, a dimension-only two-sided Ricci interval should force existence of some Einstein metric on the same smooth closed connected manifold, or the proposition must be strictly refuted. The sign-normalized logic remains central: proving P_n^+ or P_n^- proves Q_n in that dimension, while refutation requires a closed n-manifold with no Einstein metric of any sign and near-pinched metrics defeating both sign alternatives in the same dimension. The main allocation insight is that positive-branch refutation is much more constrained than ordinary no-Einstein topology: tight positive pinching gives positive scalar/positive Ricci input, finite fundamental group, b_1=0, Myers diameter, and Bishop volume upper bounds in every dimension; in dimension four it also gives strict Hitchin--Thorpe under the corrected Chern--Gauss--Bonnet window. Thus the relevant counterexample reservoir is not merely PSC-compatible no-Einstein manifolds, since examples such as #_5 CP^2 may be PSC/no-Einstein but are excluded by Hitchin--Thorpe from tight positive pinching. The true falsifiability gates are narrower: either a new Einstein-nonexistence invariant compatible with the full positive-pinning class, or a concrete high-dimensional closed smooth manifold known not to admit any Einstein metric of any sign and plausibly compatible with a sign branch. The source checks performed here support the claim that no such high-dimensional reservoir is presently in the inspected record: Besse-style higher-dimensional surveys emphasize the lack of known general obstructions in n>=5, Case's six-dimensional conformal-invariant obstruction has no known finite/nonpositive closed examples, and PSC surveys mostly describe scalar-curvature obstructions/constructions rather than Einstein nonexistence compatible with the positive-pinning class. This does not prove counterexamples impossible; it changes research allocation by making P_n^+, not further family-building with existing no-Einstein tools, the best current proof-side target.

The second major insight is that the one genuinely all-dimensional proof-side mechanism already in the record is the annular flux identity, but it must be used at exactly its real scale. The manuscript's FND:flux is dimension-general in the transverse cone dimension d>=4 and gives the clean coefficient -(d+2)/2 for quadratic homogeneous TT/radial-null modes. I initially planned a general higher-mode extension with the same unweighted inverse-log lower bound. TTCS verifier branches and A01 #506 exposed the decisive scaling error: the Wronskian identity for a homogeneous degree ell mode is valid with dual o_ell=r^{-(d+2ell-2)}K_ell and coefficient -(d+2ell-2)/2, but the nuclear-norm mass is log-sized only for ell=2. For ell>2 the correct unweighted lower bound has denominator H_ell(b,R)=(b^{2-ell}-R^{2-ell})/(ell-2), hence scales like b^{ell-2}; only a weighted norm sup r^{2-ell}|D Ric| retains a logarithmic denominator. Since Yau--26 controls the unweighted Ricci endomorphism, the quadratic mode is the unique log-critical polynomial mode unless an additional sequence-level propagation or weighted-control theorem is proved. This repair is important because it prevents the positive proof route from smuggling a dimension-only obstruction out of an inverse-log estimate. Even for ell=2, c/L permits delta->0 when L->infty, as the manuscript's constant-scalar A1 examples with |log a| delta -> 1/6 demonstrate. Coefficient vanishing therefore requires an additional mechanism such as delta_i L_i ->0, bounded controlled obstruction annuli, iterated fixed-rescaled-annulus extraction, or a global rigidity/existence input. The next mathematical theorem should be a cone flux scale classification plus a quadratic first-obstruction necessity interface, not an overgeneral higher-obstruction necessity theorem.

The high-dimensional noncollapsed route is now clearer but still frontier-level. In total dimension N>=5 the singular set under bounded-Ricci noncollapse is not generally a finite set of points but codimension four. Step 53's recovered probe is therefore not an optional side note: it names the right object for the all-dimensional program, namely a bundle-valued pointwise/elliptic condition along a stratum, schematically R_normal,±(y) zeta(y) plus normal-connection and tangential-derivative terms, rather than a stratum-integral obstruction. The locality reason is dictated by the target: Yau--26 pinching is a pointwise operator-norm hypothesis, so a nonzero core obstruction at one stratum point cannot be canceled by averaging. But the stronger warning from TTCS is also essential: in a true stratum problem, tangential derivatives of the obstruction section and normal-connection curvature can cancel or modify the normal algebraic term, so one cannot assert pointwise R_normal zeta=0 without parallelism/slow-variation or an elliptic bundle estimate. The clean next step is therefore tiered: prove the isolated cone identity and corrected scale bounds, then formulate a product/parallel-section stratum result, then add quantified connection/tangential errors. Bubble trees must also be visible: LeBrun--Ozuch Section 9 explicitly excludes trees, and their later tree handling is conformally Kähler/admissible, not a general Yau--26 theorem. Any noncollapsed proof architecture must either assume no trees or run nested tower-subtraction/flux on all scales.

The collapse half remains the hardest part of P_n^+. The Step 71/72 ADE/class-T arithmetic should be treated as a diagnostic instrument, not a theorem: pure half-flat ALE chirality quanta block only sporadic four-dimensional targets such as the k=3 balanced row, and ALF/non-half-flat/transition/APS effects may dissolve that coincidence. Its finite next use is an atom table with chirality and APS eta terms, not another loose rod model. A01's graph-neck updates remain valuable for the negative branch and for understanding inverse-log/neck phenomena: their Yamabe construction repairs diagonal analytic Koiso center data on T^3, while their scale critique correctly forces the positive flux route to articulate how c/L becomes coefficient vanishing. But even a successful restricted negative graph or IL theorem cannot refute Q_4 while P_4^+ remains viable, and it does not alter the all-dimensional proof allocation. Finally, Lean work has been moved to a background Codex task because statement fidelity needs a real curvature interface; synthetic Ricci shells and arithmetic-only audits cannot satisfy the final Lean proof requirement.
