## Deep-Reasoning

This Step was a Preceptor audit after Step 79 and the new Strategist/peer corrections. The exact Yau--26 target remains unresolved: no n≥4 sign branch is proved or refuted, and no faithful Lean Ricci formalization exists. The main value of this step was to prevent two fresh but tempting misreadings from distorting the route. First, Step 79 correctly killed the magnitude version of the harmonic self-dual alignment obstruction, because Gursky/Gursky--LeBrun/Bishop give lower bounds on \(\int |W^\pm|^2\) while CGB leaves nonempty slack on the \(\chi=8,\tau=0\) mixed rows. But Step 79 closed the whole alignment idea too early. Its dismissal by neck-stretching used arbitrary degenerating non-Einstein metrics on connected sums; it does not answer the different question whether a normalized positive Einstein metric on one fixed mixed row can realize the required three-lump concentration of three independent harmonic self-dual forms. This category distinction is load-bearing for Yau--26 because the fixed positive-mirror mixed rows already carry noncollapsed near-pinched metrics \(\delta(g_a)=O(1/|\log a|)\to0\); if any one fixed row is proved to admit no Einstein metric of any sign, \(P_4^+\) is refuted. The alignment-rigidity version is therefore not solved: it is a plausible PSC-compatible nonexistence invariant precisely because it would use Einstein rigidity rather than ordinary topological obstruction. Source reading sharpened the available facts but did not provide the theorem: Taubes shows that a nonzero self-dual harmonic form is symplectic off its zero set and is symplectic if nowhere zero; generic/self-dual harmonic forms vanish along circles; Gerig and Baykur place this in near-symplectic/SW-Gromov theory; Baykur records that connected sums with positive b_+ remain near-symplectic while symplectic connected sums are obstructed by Taubes/SW vanishing. Thus on the nonsymplectic mixed rows every self-dual harmonic representative must vanish somewhere for every metric, but no read source converts this into a positive-Einstein contradiction. The correct frontier is to derive or refute an Einstein-specific rigidity theorem for the Gram matrix of harmonic self-dual forms, not to cite neck-stretching as a counterexample.

Second, Step 79's collapse theorem is real infrastructure but orthogonal to the fixed mixed-row crux. The log-Weyl/Eguchi--Hanson near-pinched families on the decisive rows converge to the fixed-volume orbifold \((S^2\times S^2)/\mathbb Z_2\) with four \(A_1\) bubbles; total volume does not tend to zero and the local bubble asymptotic group is order 2. Therefore the transition-scale/\(\epsilon\)-collapse/AVR machinery governs other hypothetical collapsing near-positive-Einstein sequences, not these fixed noncollapsed orbifold-bubbling families. It should be kept as general positive-branch infrastructure, but not allowed to create a productivity illusion by replacing the barred central front. I audited the TTCS correction and the sources: the Bishop--Gromov transition-scale definition, \(\rho_i\to0\), unit-scale noncollapse, and large-scale volume-ratio bound are correct; Ricci decay after rescaling requires the two-sided near-Einstein upper bound; the output is an RCD volume-transition limit unless additional regularity identifies a smooth Ricci-flat bubble. The hyper-Kähler ADE ALE exclusion is correct only in its qualified form. I wrote and ran `Sessions/S0910A02/workspace/scripts/step80_preceptor_audit_constants.py`, producing `Sessions/S0910A02/workspace/data/step80_preceptor_audit_constants.json`, which records exact chiral Chern--Weil budgets and the arithmetic scope. For \(\chi=8\), the collapse-limit budgets are \(\int |W^+|^2\to2\pi^2(2\chi+3\tau)\), \(\int |W^-|^2\to2\pi^2(2\chi-3\tau)\); hence for \(\tau=0\) each chirality has \(32\pi^2\), while \(\tau=\pm2\) gives \((20\pi^2,44\pi^2)\) up to orientation. The ADE cap remains: total \(Q\le8\) gives max ADE order 48, and balanced one-chirality \(Q\le4\) gives max ADE order 4. But the same script and the read sources show why this does not exclude all ALE: class-T/non-SU(2) Ricci-flat Kähler ALE smoothings have \(Q_T(d,n)=d-1/(dn^2)\), \(\chi=d\), and end order \(|\Gamma|=dn^2\), so for fixed \(d\le8\) (or \(d\le4\) in one balanced chirality) the end group can grow without violating the energy budget. Li's 2023 paper explicitly states that the general four-dimensional Ricci-flat ALE classification/hyper-Kähler conjecture remains open in Type III and proves only restricted Hermitian non-Kähler results; Şuvaina and Răsdeaconu--Şuvaina classify ALE Ricci-flat Kähler surfaces and include non-simply-connected/free-quotient/class-T examples. Thus Step 79's statement must remain: smooth Kronheimer hyper-Kähler ALE outermost models are excluded below the appropriate \(\epsilon\), but non-ADE Ricci-flat ALE, locally hyper-Kähler quotients, ALE orbifolds with internal singularities, and zero-AVR non-ALE transition geometries remain open.

I also read A01 Step82. Its TTCS-corrected low-order obstruction ledger is useful but not a Yau--26 decision: Ozuch integrability explains Biq13-level first-obstruction vanishing on Einstein orbifolds; A+J+L parity eliminates the currently tracked \(\lambda,\mu,\nu\) coefficients in the equivariant product-orbifold calculation; however Ozuch Proposition 2.19 is necessary, not sufficient, and the surviving radial reduced function \(F(t)\) can first appear at \(O(t^3)\) or \(O(t^3\log t)\), below the \(O(t^{5/2})\) necessary-equation resolution. A01's OSS/Tian same-chirality KE validation is a strong consistency check for the all-same-chirality \(\mathrm{Bl}_5\mathbb{CP}^2\) row, but by A01/A03's own correction it does not apply to the mixed diagonal \(\#_3(S^2\times S^2)\) row, which is not Fano. This leaves the same central fork: either prove mixed-row positive Einstein nonexistence by a new PSC-compatible invariant, or prove mixed-row Einstein existence by a genuine global/radial reduced-map identity. Collapse theory and same-chirality KE controls are supporting infrastructure, not substitutes for deciding the four fixed manifolds.
