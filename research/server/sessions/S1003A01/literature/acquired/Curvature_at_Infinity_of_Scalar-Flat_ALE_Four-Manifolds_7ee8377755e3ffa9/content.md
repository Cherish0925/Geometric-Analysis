## Where scalar-flat Kähler ALE surfaces enter 

Kähler ALE surfaces 

In real dimension four, a scalar-flat Kähler metric is anti-self-dual with respect to the Kähler orientation. Hence scalar-flat Kähler ALE surfaces are included in the self-dual / anti-self-dual branch of the Tian–Viaclovsky theorem. Starting point 

Tian–Viaclovsky gives 

gij = δij + O∞(r−τ ) for every τ < 2.

The decay is almost r−2, but the leading coefficient is still not determined.

## Known results 

Here are the known results most closely related to this talk: 

In 1989, Bando–Kasue–Nakajima constructed coordinates at infinity under fast curvature decay and maximal volume growth. 

In 1994, Cheeger–Tian proved the cone structure at infinity for Ricci-flat manifolds with Euclidean volume growth and quadratic curvature decay. 

In 2005–2008, Tian–Viaclovsky proved ALE structure and curvature decay for four-dimensional critical metrics, including the scalar-flat classes considered here. 

In 2023, Biquard–Hein related the leading asymptotics of Ricci-flat ALE spaces to renormalized volume. 

In 2025, Wang–Yin constructed preferred coordinates and a Weyl tensor at infinity for Ricci-flat ALE ends. 

In Kähler ALE geometry, Kronheimer, Calderbank–Singer, Lock–Viaclovsky, Han–Viaclovsky, Hein–Răsdeaconu–Şuvaina, and Hein–LeBrun provide the main examples, moduli results, and mass formula.
