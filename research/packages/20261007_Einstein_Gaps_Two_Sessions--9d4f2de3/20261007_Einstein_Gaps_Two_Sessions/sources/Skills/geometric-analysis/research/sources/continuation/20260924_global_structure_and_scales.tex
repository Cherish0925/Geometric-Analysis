\section{Global structure and the scales of exact
realization}\label{global-structure-and-the-scales-of-exact-realization}}

24 September 2026. Three continuations strengthen different parts of the
first-line allowed-geometry/realization mechanism. They extend the
\href{20260924_global_data_realization.md}{preceding results}, rather
than repeat the integral calibration or replace the earlier obstruction
theory. The four proof manuscripts below contain the arguments; this
entry states their changes in scope and their relationship to Yau26.

\hypertarget{fiber-thickness-becomes-a-conclusion-from-global-irreducibility}{%
\subsection{1. Fiber thickness becomes a conclusion from global
irreducibility}\label{fiber-thickness-becomes-a-conclusion-from-global-irreducibility}}

The \href{20260924_thin_monodromy_alternative.md}{thin-monodromy
theorem} removes the thick reference fiber from I32. Small actual
conformal deformation around based generators implies a global
alternative: \[
 \boxed{\text{finite monodromy}\quad\text{or}\quad
        \text{one common invariant essential multicurve}.}
\] The constant depends only on fiber genus, not on generator count. The
key point is simultaneous preservation: a gap among the finitely many
short hyperbolic curve lengths supplies a single multicurve preserved by
every generator. It is not an inference from individually reducible
generators to reducibility of the group.

For a minimal Riemannian surface submersion with base and fiber genera
at least two, let \[
 \delta=\|\operatorname{Ric}^{\sharp}+I\|_{\infty,\mathrm{op}},
 \quad \eta=\|(K_B+1)_+\|_\infty.
\] The complete implication is \[
 \begin{gathered}
 2\operatorname{diam}(B)\sqrt{\delta+\eta}<\mu_f,
 \qquad\text{irreducible monodromy}\\
 \Longrightarrow\quad
 \boxed{M\text{ admits an exact negative KE metric}.}
 \end{gathered}
\] Its universal cover is the hyperbolic bidisk. The output is on the
original smooth bundle, by Nielsen realization and surface-bundle
classification. The input does not assume a thick fiber, hyperbolic
intrinsic fiber metrics, a flat connection, or actual gauge classes. It
does assume the displayed global fibration and minimal submersion.

In the reducible case the multicurve has a genuine smooth realization
through the entire bundle. A bounded-degree base cover labels its curves
and sides and cuts the total space into surface bundles with boundary.
Component representations and annular twists remain in the problem; they
have not been shown to admit compatible Einstein matching.

The actual thin Dehn-twist family realizes this alternative, whereas
signature-zero bundles with infinite irreducible monodromy obey a new
thickness-independent base-scale lower bound. These are tests of the
stated input theorem, not unrestricted Einstein nonexistence assertions.

\hypertarget{full-nonlinear-slow-equations-connect-directly-to-actual-gauge-energy}{%
\subsection{2. Full nonlinear slow equations connect directly to actual
gauge
energy}\label{full-nonlinear-slow-equations-connect-directly-to-actual-gauge-energy}}

The \href{20260924_nonlinear_slow_moduli.md}{nonlinear slow-moduli
theorem} uses genuine horizontal hyperbolic fiber paths \(h_v\), rather
than linear tensors with an artificial quadratic amplitude error.
Normalize the actual path tangent \(k_v\) by \(\int_F|k_v|^2=1\), and
put \(G=\gamma+h_{v(x)}\). All Ricci blocks, including the normal
acceleration of the path, are computed. The slow equation is exactly \[
 \int_F\langle(\operatorname{Ric}_G+G)_{VV},k_v\rangle
                     =\tfrac12\Delta_\gamma v.
\] For hyperbolic base its \textbf{full}, not projected, energy is \[
 \boxed{\int_M|\operatorname{Ric}_G+G|^2
       =\tfrac14\int_B(\Delta v)^2+\int_B\Xi(v)|dv|^4,
       \qquad\Xi(v)\ge\frac1{16\operatorname{Area}(F)}.}
\] The manuscript also gives the complete square decomposition for
variable base curvature. Curvature adjustment cannot cancel the rank-one
trace-free stress. Every exact Einstein metric in this entire block
family has constant \(v\) and hyperbolic base.

The \href{20260924_moduli_energy_gauge.md}{actual-gauge companion} shows
that the two canonical classes are actual monopole inputs and, for
hyperbolic base, their harmonic forms are exactly
\(-(dA_B\pm dA_F)/(2\pi)\) at the representative level. There is no
period error or zero set. Writing \(r=\fint|\operatorname{Ric}+G|^2\)
and \(q_\pm=\mathfrak D_{2,\pm}/V\), it proves \[
 \boxed{q_+=q_-=\frac9{4V}\int_B|dv|^2+\frac92r
                 =\frac92\left(\fint|\nabla\omega_\pm|^2+r\right).}
\] Thus the first-line functional defect measures an actual global
moduli energy in this family. With variable base curvature the exact
formula retains both the base-curvature variance and the nonzero
negative projection of the canonical classes. In this family pointwise
negative Ricci pinching itself implies \(q_\pm\le54\delta+9\delta^2\);
no auxiliary-form existence assumption remains.

On genuine cyclic covers, fixed \(a>0\) and \(v_m=a\cos(2\pi z/m)\) give
\[
 \delta_-(G_m)=\Theta(m^{-2}),\quad
 \mathcal E(G_m)=\Theta(m^{-4}),\quad
 q_{\pm,m}=\Theta(m^{-2}),\quad
 \int|\operatorname{Ric}+G_m|^2=\Theta(m^{-3}),
\] while \(\operatorname{Var}(v_m)=a^2/2\) stays fixed. The metrics
remain a fixed distance from every fiber-preserving product output. The
scalar scale \(\lambda_1(B_m)\asymp m^{-2}\), not a local curvature
spike or missing gauge field, accounts for this behavior.

This extends the previous shrinking-amplitude test but has a different
scope: it does \textbf{not} prove fixed-distance separation from all
Einstein metrics and arbitrary diffeomorphisms. The smooth products
already have Einstein outputs. The complete scalar equation and retained
normal term, rather than that unproved stronger exclusion, are the new
nonlinear tool.

\hypertarget{original-defects-now-give-a-quantitative-test-for-whole-core-zeros}{%
\subsection{3. Original defects now give a quantitative test for
whole-core
zeros}\label{original-defects-now-give-a-quantitative-test-for-whole-core-zeros}}

The \href{20260924_harmonic_zero_scale.md}{harmonic-zero scale theorem}
replaces I34's qualitative nonvanishing premise by a quantitative
metric-scale test. For the actual self-dual harmonic class
representative \(H\), inherited form supply gives \[
 \int_M\bigl(|-2\pi H|-\sqrt2\bigr)^2
                    \le818(r+q)V.
\] On a coordinate ball of radius \(\rho\) with fixed rescaled
\(C^{1,\alpha}\) metric control, the first-order Hodge equation gives \[
 H(x)=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \int_{U_{x,\rho}}\bigl(|-2\pi H|-\sqrt2\bigr)^2\ge c\rho^4.
\] No derivative of Ricci or extra pointwise bound on \(H\) is assumed.
If every point of a \textbf{whole} singular filling has such a supplied
coordinate scale at least \(\rho_{\rm core}\), then \[
 (r+q)V<c_0\rho_{\rm core}^4
       \quad\Longrightarrow\quad H\text{ has no zeros on the core}.
\] Under the retained A1/Betti or rational-ball hypotheses this forces
the whole filling to be the appropriate \textbf{boundary-marked} Milnor
fiber; the rational-ball case forces Wahl type. A separate global
Q-Gorenstein smoothing then yields negative KE on the same smooth
manifold. The coordinate scale may tend to zero; the comparison uses
total defect, not merely its average.

A classical zero-circle birth, rescaled on a fixed negative KE product,
provides actual harmonic representatives of the same canonical class
with unchanged periods and arbitrarily small average form errors but
persisting zeros. It proves that those form data alone are insufficient.
Its pointwise Ricci defect is large, so it is not a counterexample to
automatic matching under the full near-Einstein equation. Whether that
equation itself forces the displayed core-scale ratio remains open.

\hypertarget{mathematical-position-and-retained-work}{%
\subsection{4. Mathematical position and retained
work}\label{mathematical-position-and-retained-work}}

The first result enlarges an exact existence input class. The second
solves a genuine nonlinear slow family and identifies its gauge energy
and sharp global scale. The third gives a raw-defect-to-marked-filling
criterion, with a model explaining why a scale is needed. These are
three result families, not one universal obstruction/existence theorem.

Earlier uniform integral obstacles, parameterized non-saturation,
two-chirality topology, and their actual gauge suppliers remain intact.
Neither these continuations nor the absence of an obstruction settles
general Einstein existence on the shear manifolds. They do not finish a
complete Yau26 sign branch. The next unresolved information is now
explicit: residual component/twist matching, non-adapted slow-mode
coverage, and the core defect/scale relation under the full Ricci
equation.
