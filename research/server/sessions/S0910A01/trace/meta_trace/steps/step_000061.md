## Deep-Reasoning

This step ran genuine gate audits (not confirmations) of the three load-bearing analytic modules for the restricted four-dimensional negative Ishida--LeBrun cutoff-flow theorem, following the Strategist's correction that prior audit assignments presupposed the verdict. The central finding is that the defect Moser endpoint ||aE(t)||_inf <= C*delta*(r^2/t)^{1/3} FAILED the gate: while the J_6 persistence via two-factor absorption is algebraically sound and genuinely independent of the cubic curvature moment Y, the subsequent Moser iteration from the J_6 seed to L^inf has two structural gaps. First, the general-p two-factor defect inequality needed for the Moser iteration is proved only for p=6 (Step 34 §5); the algebraic extension to general even p >= 6 using w_p = eta*a^{(p-1)/2}*s^{p/4} is natural but not written anywhere in the record. Second, the nested Moser recurrence (Step 34 eq 6.1) is cited from 'Step 32/A03 Step 34' without derivation; the seed H_{p_0} is never defined, and the exponent 1/3 vs 1/2 depends on whether H_{p_0} = J_{p_0} (yielding 1/3) or H_{p_0} = D_{p_0}^E (yielding 1/2). Without the defect endpoint, the metric drift V(T) = int_0^T ||aE||_inf dt cannot close, so the (Gamma,V) bootstrap has no V-leg and the first-exit interval cannot be closed. This is the single blocking gap. The curvature Moser endpoint ||aF_T(t)||_inf <= C*Gamma/t passed the gate conditionally: its internal logic is sound and compatible with the Step 55 (Gamma,V) repair (it carries a generic transport factor A that becomes e^{8V_*}), but the index-level tensor identities it consumes are unproved (pure-scaling checksum needed), the metric-drift term C_V||aE||_inf H must be inserted into the H-inequality display, and A = e^{8V_*} must be recorded explicitly. No structural gap exists in the curvature Moser machinery itself. The per-datum existence construction (A03 Step 56) also passed conditionally: the tame hierarchy, maximum principle, smooth limit, pullback, and autonomous splice are all verified (the splice correctly circumvents the uniqueness problem by time-jet matching on an autonomous equation), but the fixed-parameter parabolic existence is cited-standard without a named theorem, the time-derivative induction is asserted not displayed, the DeTurck decomposition identity needs explicit conventions, the derivative-reordering lemma is schematic, and the upstream first-exit estimates depend on the failed defect endpoint. The A03 Step 57 tensor calculation (which the curvature endpoint cites) exists and provides the term-by-term H_2 evolution with the correct shifted-curvature variable T = Rm + G, so the curvature endpoint's tensor premise is closer to proof level than the defect endpoint's. The graph-manifold route (A03 Step 63, corrected) is now a gap-theorem candidate rather than a counterexample route: T^3 cannot carry Ric=-2g, so the exact Einstein funnel exists only over hyperbolic 3-manifolds. The flat-slice barrier H^2 >= 9-6*delta remains valid. The IL theorem and the graph-manifold analysis sharpen each other: a completed IL gap excludes tightly pinched negative counterexamples on calibrated SW geographies; any refutation must then live where no such calibration budget exists. A03's quantifier normalization (message #440) correctly reduces Yau 26 to two independent single-sign questions, aligning with the session framework.
