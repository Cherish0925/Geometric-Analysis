## Deep-Reasoning

Step 76 constructed the reduced Ozuch obstruction map for Einstein desingularization of the positive product orbifold $(S^2 \times S^2)/\mathbb{Z}_2$ and discovered through TTCS-assisted verification that the problem is much more favorable than initially expected. The construction started from Step 75's handoff asking for a finite-dimensional reduced map with variables (relative EH scales, kernel directions, chirality patterns, orbifold deformations) and equations (local $\lambda_k^j$, $\mu_k^j$ at each node, and global $\nu_l$). Reading the full Ozuch source (arXiv 2012.13316), I extracted the exact formulas: $\lambda_k^j(\phi, 0) = c\langle R^{\pm}\phi, \phi_{(k)}\rangle$ from Proposition 2.2/eq.(24), $\mu_1^j = C\det(R_{22,23;32,33})$ from §2.1.4/Lemma 2.14, and $\nu_l = 3\sum_j \int_{S^3/\mathbb{Z}_2} \langle H^4, V_{l,2}^{(j)}\rangle$ from Proposition 2.10/eq.(36). For the product orbifold with $R^+ = R^- = \text{diag}(\Lambda, 0, 0)$ confirmed by direct sectional-curvature calculation, the first obstruction $\lambda_k^j$ vanishes automatically when the EH direction $\zeta_j$ lies in the 2-dimensional kernel of $R_{\pm}$, and the second obstruction $\mu_1^j = C \cdot 0 \cdot \Lambda = 0$ by the rank-1 cascade. TTCS verification then revealed the critical correction: $\mathbf{O}(g_o) = 0$ for the product orbifold, because the shape deformation $g_1 - g_2$ satisfies $P_g h = -\Lambda h \neq 0$ (unstable eigenvector, not a zero mode), the TT kernel is zero by product eigenvalue analysis, and conformal/Killing modes do not contribute. With $d = \dim \mathbf{O}(g_o) = 0$, the global $\nu_l$ system is empty—there are no global obstruction equations at all. TTCS also corrected: (i) the angular dependence of $H^4$ on the in-kernel direction is $C + A\cos 2\alpha + B\sin 2\alpha$ (quadratic, not linear), (ii) the Weyl eigenvalues are $(2\Lambda/3, -\Lambda/3, -\Lambda/3)$, not the half-values. The combined result is that ALL Ozuch obstructions through second order vanish for the product quotient with kernel-direction EH bubbles. The approximate metric is Einstein to order $O(t^{5/2})$. This makes $(S^2 \times S^2)/\mathbb{Z}_2$ the cleanest known test case for Ozuch's Question 0.8 and Conjecture 2.22, which ask whether first-obstruction vanishing suffices for Einstein desingularization. The surviving question is whether the higher-order Ozuch iteration converges. This is a well-posed mathematical problem: either the rank-1 cascade and orbifold rigidity force all higher-order obstructions to vanish (supporting construction of new positive Einstein metrics on $\#_r(S^2 \times S^2)$), or a structural higher-order obstruction is discovered. The original A02 claim of '4 non-TT scalar/gauge directions in ker P' was referring to a different operator or convention; in Ozuch's $L^2$-kernel sense, the orbifold is rigid.
