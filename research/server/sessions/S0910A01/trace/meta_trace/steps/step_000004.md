## Deep-Reasoning

This step repaired an important route distortion and produced a more faithful structure for the four-dimensional negative branch. The mixed Seiberg--Witten estimate remains a strong obstruction, but it does not turn the remaining problem into a free bolt optimization. The real mathematical object is now clearer: any no-Einstein sequence with negative Ricci pinching δ_-→0 near the ratio k/A→1/3 is forced toward near-equality in LeBrun's mixed estimate, and the source-level equality mechanism is almost-Kähler, not Weyl-only. LeBrun 2001 shows that the estimate is obtained through the Seiberg--Witten spinor Weitzenböck formula, a Weitzenböck formula for self-dual forms, a conformal minimization for s+3w, and norm inequalities; it does not rest on F_A^+ being harmonic. Equality forces an almost-Kähler form in the lowest W_+ eigenspace, s+s* constant, and compatibility of the almost-complex first Chern class with the relevant spin^c class. Exact equality on blow-ups is then blocked by the Taubes positive-area argument for exceptional classes. This makes the central opportunity a quantitative near-equality problem through collapse and curvature concentration: can the exact almost-Kähler/Taubes contradiction be made stable under δ_-→0, or can exceptional topology concentrate in regions that are invisible to the normalized analytic defects while keeping unweighted Ricci error small?

The algebraic near-equality forcing is now sharper. With I=(1/(4π²))∫(s²/24+2|W^+|²), J=(1/(4π²))∫|r̊|²/2, q=k-A/3, and I-J=A-k, the mixed-estimate deficit D_a=I-(2/3)(a^+)² satisfies the exact identity D_a=J-q-(2/3)((a^+)²-A). For LeBrun's sign-selected monopole class, (a^+)²≥A, so 0≤D_a≤J-q and J≥q. The TTCS verifier correctly tightened the volume step: J/A=O(δ²) follows for the minimal general-type blow-up families only after proving Vol(g)=O(A), for example by the Chern--Gauss--Bonnet lower bound using |s+4|≤4δ together with χ(M)/A=O(1), supplied by Noether-type bounds for complex surfaces and k/A bounded. Thus D_a/A→0 is a genuine consequence in the intended LeBrun boundary families, but it is not automatic for arbitrary monopole-class four-manifolds without comparable topology or volume control. The all-sign monopole average still gives, for actual Ricci-pinched metrics, Σ(E_j^+)²/A→0 when δ→0 and q/A→0; however this is a consequence of the metric and the Seiberg--Witten estimate, not an abstract intersection-form obstruction.

The cap mechanism was also corrected. A bare Ricci-flat ALE core has normalized defect 1 against the target Ric=-g, independent of scale, but that observation only excludes an uncorrected insertion. A core scaled by t² with an O(t²) relative C² correction can contribute at order one to the original-scale Ricci endomorphism, so the first-obstruction-zero corrected gluing route remains live. The relevant computation is therefore not a solitary residual infimum over a restricted U(2)-invariant ansatz. Exact Taub--Bolt--AdS fillings show that exceptional-handle-type topology can occur with Ric=-g for the interface data actually induced by those fillings. Any obstruction must come from matching these data to an exterior, solving the transition and linearized equations, or from the global near-equality Seiberg--Witten mechanism. This reorients the research toward two coupled tasks: source-grounded near-equality rigidity and interface Cauchy-data matching for corrected local models. The full Yau 26 proposition is still far from resolved, but the route is now more mathematically faithful and less vulnerable to false exclusions or unsupported reductions.
