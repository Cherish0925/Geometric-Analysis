## Deep-Reasoning

This Explore step restored the global research judgment around the restricted four-dimensional negative Ishida--LeBrun cutoff-flow route. The important correction is that Step 58's summary was stronger than its actual mathematical support: the useful continuation mechanisms remain, but the differentiated tensor hierarchy, the base layer, and the fixed-background conversion were not yet closed merely because several named modules existed. I therefore redirected the work from approving the route to repairing the current-pair induction ledger itself. The central mathematical point is that terms such as B_{m+2}*U_0 and B_2*U_m cannot be hidden inside a strict lower family. Expanding B_k=nabla^k(chi^2) shows that B_{m+2}*U_0 contains chi*C_{m+2}*U_0 and C_1*C_{m+1}*U_0, while B_2*U_m contains chi*C_2*U_m and C_1*C_1*U_m. These are precisely the top-cutoff, current-pair, and signed-Hessian terms that need their own estimates. The analogous extraction is needed in the unweighted T_m equation. The repaired normal form also clarified the source order of the base package: one starts with U_0 in L^infty and T_0 in finite L^q, then proves (U_1,C_2) in finite L^q, then T_1, then obtains pointwise T_0 by W^{1,p}->L^infty for p>4, and only then enters the general m>=2 induction. This prevents the Sobolev pointwise step from being used to prove its own derivative input. The fixed-background conversion became cleaner: from the exact ordered identity partial_t A=g^{-1}*U_1, one obtains partial_t A_m=g^{-1}*U_{m+1}+P_m(A_{<=m-1},U_{<=m},g^{-1}); no current A_m coefficient is needed. This strictly triangular structure is likely the right way to turn evolving-derivative bounds into fixed-background Sobolev regularity once the current-pair tower is genuinely bounded. I wrote the repaired ledger in workspace/notes/step59_induction_ledger_repair.md and a finite structural enumerator in workspace/scripts/step59_induction_ledger_verify.py, which produced workspace/data/step59_induction_ledger_verify.json with 11/11 ledger checks through m=64. This script is deliberately only a combinatorial ledger, not a PDE proof. TTCS verification at trace/ttcs/results/step59_ttcs01_verifier1_integration.md confirmed the two main repairs: current terms are now extracted correctly, and the background conversion is strictly triangular. TTCS also sharpened what remains: exact tensor-index evolution formulas, derivative-reordering lemmas showing that the highest cutoff jet C_{m+2} only pairs with U_0/T_0, complete energy estimates for all remainder families, a self-contained non-superseded base proof, the imported Moser endpoints, and the degenerate local existence module. I patched the stale Step 58 note, route assessment, and structural script so later agents do not inherit the old statement that only per-datum local existence remains. The patched Step 58 script now passes 34/34 while explicitly saying the base layer is imported and the dependency map is refined by Step 59. I also read A03 Step 38 and Step 39 to locate the best existing base material: Step 38 supplies the useful signed Hessian lemma, while Step 39 is superseded because it treated current cutoff levels as already controlled and incorrectly assumed all derivatives of a=chi^2 carry a chi factor. This makes the next mathematical target concrete: derive the m=1 base pair at index level and determine whether the apparent int chi|C_2||U_1|^p term is always a signed Hessian contraction that can be integrated by parts. I also read A02 Step 61's toric-cone positive-branch work. It gives valuable local information about opposite warping, Weyl eigenvalue reachability, and Bianchi/Cotton rate constraints, but it remains local and does not supply a closed Ricci-pinched non-Einstein manifold, a global curvature budget, or a terminal impossible Einstein density. Thus the broader Yau 26 problem remains open: completing the IL analytic bridge would be a substantial reusable smoothing theorem for a restricted negative four-dimensional class, but it would not provide the missing positive-sign, general-topology, higher-dimensional, or dimension-only ingredients.
