## Deep-Reasoning

This Execute step sharpened the positive-branch chi=8 balanced-row program by separating three objects that had been partially conflated: the mathematical Biquard correction h needed for a small-defect near-pinched certificate, the direct Ricci-endomorphism audit that certifies any proposed h independent of how it was produced, and the existence-oriented Einstein-DeTurck/variational flow experiment that can start from any smooth metric, including the naive glued metric. The active Yau-26 proposition is still completely unresolved: no dimension n>=4 branch has been proved or refuted, no no-Einstein theorem for the balanced rows has been obtained, and no Lean formalization exists. What changed is the experimental and analytic map of the current cheapest decision point.

The Kähler-potential check was the central scientific discriminator. The Strategist suggested that the exact computation Delta(r^2)=8 might reflect a scalar Kähler potential and collapse the tensor BVP. The actual symbolic substitution into Step-65's full four-dimensional EH linearized Ricci formulas gives the precise answer: in the Step-65 Bianchi-IX conventions a radial Kähler variation h_phi=A_phi(e0^2+e3^2)+B_phi(e1^2+e2^2) satisfies dRic_EH(h_phi)=-(1/2)i\partial\bar\partial(A_phi+B_phi). Thus the local scalar identity is real, including the factor -1/2, and it is not a numerical artifact. But the naive version is false: i\partial\bar\partial(r^2)=(4+4r^{-4})e0\wedge e3+(4-4r^{-4})e1\wedge e2, so it contains an anti-self-dual discrepancy and is not proportional to the EH Kähler form. The true end potential with q=phi'=2r^5/(r^4-1) gives A=B=4, hence i\partial\bar\partial phi=4omega on the punctured end, but phi=r^2+(1/2)log((r^2-1)/(r^2+1)) is logarithmically singular at the bolt. That singularity is not a removable numerical nuisance: it reflects the nontrivial bolt cohomology and explains why a smooth global scalar potential cannot solve dRic(h)=Lambda g_EH by itself. This identifies, rather than removes, the Biquard obstruction mechanism.

The direct audit infrastructure is now more important than BVP convergence. Step-66 built first-order and nonlinear radial Ricci-endomorphism auditors using Step-65's full-4D formulas. These audits show that raw EH and parallel/Kähler-class scaling retain order-one defect, h=r^2g is much worse because full dRic is not the Lichnerowicz rough term alone, and scalar cutoff profiles kill the source away from the bolt but leave an O(1)--O(10) compact core/cutoff defect. The nonlinear scaled-insert audit confirmed the same structure for g_epsilon=epsilon(EH+epsilon h): raw remains delta=1; scalar cutoff far-region median defects fall with epsilon but all-region defects remain large. Therefore the current scalar cutoff is diagnostic only; it does not discharge the near-pinching half of a P4+ refutation. But the audit interface is valuable because any future Biquard/Fredholm, Frobenius, or finite-element h can be judged by the same direct sup-norm Ricci-endomorphism certificate rather than by trusting solver convergence.

The ODE extraction also became sharper. Matrix extraction from the full Step-65 formulas shows that the highest-derivative system for (f1',f2'',f3'') has rank 2 with left null vector (-1,2,1), not a freely solvable three-equation 2+1 system. The compatibility constraint is Lambda r^6-(3r^4+1)f1+2(r^4+1)f2+2r^5f2'+(r^4-1)f3+r(r^4-1)f3'=0. TTCS verified the rank-two conclusion but corrected the interpretation: one should not say that f1' is chosen to propagate the constraint; the constraint propagates under the two second-order equations, while the remaining freedom is radial diffeomorphism gauge and must be fixed by a Bianchi gauge or equivalent condition. This is a real mathematical refinement of the Step-65 handoff and explains why naive boundary conditions at the bolt caused numerical resistance.

A finite-difference least-squares profile was tried because the Strategist emphasized certificate decoupling: fit a candidate h and audit it directly, rather than demanding a perfect BVP solve first. The LS fit reduced the interior linearized defect substantially (max about 0.0125, median about 5e-4 away from boundaries) but hit the 20000-iteration limit, had a huge all-region bolt defect about 4.7e3, and the nonlinear audit still showed r>1.2 max defects around 0.65 for epsilon from 1e-2 to 3e-4. This is not a near-pinched metric certificate. Its value is negative/diagnostic: it confirms that Frobenius bolt regularity, true ambient-H matching, and gauge fixing are load-bearing, not cosmetic.

Independent TTCS verification agreed with the scientific conclusions and added two corrections that must be inherited: Biquard's o_j are global smooth L2 decaying kernel/cokernel tensors on EH, while the compact support enters through the glued cutoff chi_t o_j; and the equation in the source is d_EH Ric(h)=Lambda EH+sum lambda_j o_j, not literally (1/3)sum lambda_j o_j, because the PDF extraction visually confused the summation notation. TTCS also emphasized that the scalar cohomology argument explains only a restricted fixed-complex-structure channel, not the full three-dimensional hyperkähler obstruction space, and that the actual next mathematical object is the ambient quadratic jet H at the product-quotient A1 point, put in compatible Bianchi gauge and decomposed into the relevant EH angular/tensor modes. Peer updates from A01/A03 improved the negative-branch landscape but did not change this positive-branch allocation: A01 reports the IL analytic bridge essentially closed except per-datum existence/limit passage, and A03 closed a graph/collar model class negatively, making the chi=8 positive rows even more central as the small four-dimensional counterexample territory.

The refined h export remains needed for any refutation direction because small delta is what converts a no-Einstein result into a Yau-26 counterexample. The existence direction is logically separate: a naive smooth glued metric is a valid start for Einstein-DeTurck or variational search, and convergence on a balanced row would be new positive evidence about Einstein existence on SW-void simply connected non-Kähler manifolds. A Codex-loop exec task was started to prototype the decoupled naive-start control-flow pipeline, with the r=0 del Pezzo row as control; no result has been collected yet and none should be inferred. The current best research posture is therefore two-track: continue the rigorous h/audit construction only through the actual ambient-H/gauge/Frobenius objects, and in parallel run controlled naive-start flow experiments whose convergence or failure is interpreted independently of near-pinching certification.
