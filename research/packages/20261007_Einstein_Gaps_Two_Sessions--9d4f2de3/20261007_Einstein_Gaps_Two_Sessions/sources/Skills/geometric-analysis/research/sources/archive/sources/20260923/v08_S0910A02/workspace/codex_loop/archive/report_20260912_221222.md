# Research Report: decoupled naive-start control-flow prototype

## Task and scientific context

The proposal asked for a bounded prototype of the positive-branch Einstein-flow experiment for the chi=8 positive-mirror rows. The immediate requirement was experimental discipline rather than a full four-dimensional solve: a known positive-Einstein control, a mixed-like start with four local defects, exact `Z2 x Z2`-type symmetry, a direct geometric defect audit at every saved time, and a pre-registered distinction among control failure, seam re-collapse, and interior concentration/divergence. The refined Biquard correction was explicitly out of scope.

I implemented and tested that pipeline as a genuine two-dimensional normalized Ricci flow. This substantially validates the orchestration, symmetry reduction, independent audit, and failure taxonomy, but it does not test the topology or tensor dynamics of the actual four-manifolds. It yields no proof or counterexample to Yau Problem 26.

## Work performed

The runnable module `Sessions/S0910A02/workspace/codex_loop/work/naive_start_v4_ricci_flow.py` evolves

\[
g=e^{2u}g_{S^2},\qquad
\partial_tu=\bar K-e^{-2u}(1-\Delta_{S^2}u)
\]

on a subdivided icosphere. The control is a smooth invariant perturbation of the round positive-Einstein metric. The mixed-like start has four equal conformal defect wells at a tetrahedral orbit. The mesh is quotient-reduced by the half-turn group `V4 = Z2 x Z2`: at production resolution, 642 vertex values become 162 orbit variables. Symmetry is therefore exact by construction.

The evolution uses a spherical mass matrix and cotangent Laplacian, with a sparse BDF solve. Its Liouville functional is recorded as a variational check. At each of 101 saved times, an independent routine conformally scales the edge lengths, reconstructs triangle angles, calculates Regge angle deficits and mixed Voronoi areas, and takes a direct curvature/Einstein sup norm. This audit does not reuse the curvature formula driving the ODE and is the model analogue of the Step-66 direct Ricci-endomorphism audit.

The JSON pre-registers the classifier and its thresholds. Classification precedence is numerical failure, invalid/degenerate discrete metric, seam re-collapse, interior concentration or divergence, convergence, then stalled/unresolved. The mixed result is forcibly relabeled `uninterpretable_control_failure` unless the control first passes. The file also states explicitly that nonconvergence does not imply nonexistence and that toy convergence has no four-dimensional existence implication.

I ran the experiment at 162, 642, and 2562 vertices, created a consolidated refinement audit, inspected the diagnostic plot, and added regression tests for mesh topology and mass, constants in the Laplacian nullspace, exact group reduction, Gauss–Bonnet/direct-audit calibration, and every failure-taxonomy branch.

## Main results

At the production resolution (642 vertices, 1280 faces, 162 quotient variables, final time 5), both solvers reached the requested final time. The control is classified `converged`, so the mixed-like run is interpretable under the registered gate; it is also classified `converged`.

| diagnostic | control: initial -> final | four-well: initial -> final |
|---|---:|---:|
| PDE/model defect sup | `1.915 -> 3.50e-9` | `10.146 -> 1.35e-9` |
| independently audited, mesh-calibrated defect sup | `1.920 -> 3.58e-9` | `9.735 -> 1.38e-9` |
| literal direct Einstein defect | `1.931 -> 7.16e-4` | `9.803 -> 7.16e-4` |
| minimum seam-cap area ratio | `0.986 -> 1.000` | `0.675 -> 1.000` |
| maximum direct `\|K\|` away from seams | `1.505 -> 1.006` | `2.079 -> 1.006` |
| final area relative error | `2.00e-8` | `2.18e-8` |
| maximum symmetry error | `0` | `0` |

The mixed direct defect crossed the registered `0.03` convergence threshold by time `0.65`. Its four local area wells recovered to the round value; no seam re-collapse occurred. Curvature away from the wells decreased rather than concentrated. The Liouville energy decreased from `0.698` to numerical zero in the control and from `2.05` to numerical zero in the mixed run, with no upward saved step exceeding `1e-8`.

All classifications are unchanged across the three mesh levels. The unavoidable literal pointwise Einstein-defect floor of the irregular round triangulation decreases from `2.15e-3` to `7.16e-4` to `2.15e-4`; the observed orders under edge halving are 1.59 and 1.73. The mesh-calibrated final direct defects are below `1e-7` in every run, exact `V4` symmetry holds at every saved time, and all refinement checks pass.

These are positive results about the numerical pipeline only. Convergence on `S^2` is expected and cannot be transferred to `#3(S2 x S2)`, `#3CP2#3CP2bar`, or any other chi=8 row. The four `(+,+,-,-)` seam labels are stored only as metadata because a scalar conformal model cannot encode Eguchi–Hanson orientation.

## Four-dimensional handoff and blockers

The detailed upgrade in `Sessions/S0910A02/workspace/codex_loop/work/technical_note.md` preserves the successful separation of concerns. It calls for a tagged four-dimensional simplicial complex with orbifold exterior, four EH cores, transition annuli, and bolt two-spheres; ten metric variables in a fixed frame or a positive-definite log-Cholesky parametrization; the harmonic-reduced Einstein–DeTurck operator relative to the naive glued metric; and an implicit Newton/Krylov solve. A direct high-order quadrature audit must compute the eigenvalues of

\[
g^{-1}(\operatorname{Ric}(g)-\Lambda g)
\]

independently of the nonlinear residual, alongside the DeTurck-vector norm, `L2` and sup curvature, volume, metric positivity, and the location/tag of curvature maxima. The toy seam-cap diagnostic should become the areas of the four tagged bolt spheres and volumes of the EH cores. The correct run order is an analytic round `S^4` manufactured control, the r=0 del Pezzo naive-start control, then the non-spin and spin balanced rows, with mesh/order/time-step and DeTurck-reference checks.

The current environment lacks `dolfinx`, `ufl`, `petsc4py`, `mpi4py`, and `gmsh`; more importantly, no conforming tagged four-dimensional glued mesh or atlas is present. Those are the immediate execution blockers for the true PDE. The refined Biquard correction is not a blocker for the existence-oriented naive-start flow and can later replace only the initial-data constructor. No outcome here should be upgraded to an Einstein existence/nonexistence claim.
