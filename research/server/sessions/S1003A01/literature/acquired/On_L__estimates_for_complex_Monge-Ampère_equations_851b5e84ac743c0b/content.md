# On L∞ estimates for complex Monge-Ampère equations 

Journal: Annals of Mathematics (2023) 

Authors: Bin Guo, Duong Phong, Freid Tong 

Citation: This paper presents a PDE-based approach to L∞ esti-mates for the complex Monge-Ampère equation, covering both fixed and degenerating metrics. The method, enhancing fully non-linear equation analysis, simplifies existing pluripotential theory-based tech-niques. 

Summary: 

Auxiliary complex Monge-Ampère equations in nonlinear PDE estimates: a survey 1

Abstract: We review recent developments in analytic estimates for solutions to fully nonlinear partial differential equations on Kähler manifolds. The comparison approach with auxiliary complex Monge-Ampère equations, introduced by the author along with Phong and Tong, has been pivotal in these developments. We also discuss the applications of these estimates in the study of the geometry of Kähler metrics. 

1. Introduction 

Nonlinear partial differential equations (PDEs) have attracted atten-tion from mathematicians over the past decades, especially those with back-grounds in geometry and physics. On a Kähler manifold, the solutions to these geometric PDEs give rise to canonical metrics, such as the Calabi-Yau and Kähler-Einstein (KE) metrics. To illustrate the impor-tance of L∞ estimates, let us consider the complex Monge-Ampère equation, which has a uniquely storied history. Early on, they were recognized as the defining difficulty in the problem of finding KE metrics. Yau’s introduc-tion [ 35 ] of Moser iteration in 1978 marked a crucial step in his solution of the Calabi conjecture, and ushered in a new era for complex MA equa-tions. Subsequently, Kołodziej [ 28 ] obtained sharp L∞ estimates for complex Monge-Ampère equations, which even hold for a family of Monge-Ampère equations with degenerating Kähler classes [ 8, 11 ]. Alternatively, a non-sharp L∞ estimate was derived by Blocki [ 2] who applied the Aleksandrov-Bakelman-Pucci (ABP) maximum principle from PDE theory. Kolodziej’s method of proof relies on the pluripotential theory developed in the late 1970’s by Bedford and Taylor [ 1], which is unique and however confined to complex Monge-Ampère equations only. In their work [ 23 ], the author, Phong and Tong have introduced a noval comparison method which yields sharp L∞ estimates for a broad family of fully nonlinear PDEs on Kähler manifolds, including the complex MA and Hessian equations. This new approach is purely PDE-based, with one of the advantages being its applicability to equations with degenerating reference metrics and even non-Kähler metrics [ 16 ]. Furthermore, these methods provide a resolution to the conjectured geometric diameter bounds and uniform Sobolev inequalities of Kähler metrics, with conditions solely imposed on the integral of volume forms (c.f. [ 20, 21 ]).
