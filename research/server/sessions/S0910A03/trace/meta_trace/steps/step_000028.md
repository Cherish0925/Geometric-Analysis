## Deep-Reasoning

This review sharply separated the sound structure of the four-dimensional negative-branch program from the two analytic lemmas that still prevent a self-contained theorem. The completed Codex run confirms the shifted-curvature mechanism for normalized cutoff flow: with G=(g∧g)/(2(n−1)), T=Rm+G, and U=aT, one has Ric(G)=g, Ric(U)=a(Ric+g), and ∂ₜg=−2Ric(U). Thus normalization is absorbed into the same curvature-contraction structure that drives Wang's cutoff-jet induction; the dangerous ∇^(m+2)a term is real, but one integration by parts lowers it to the current ∇^(m+1)χ level, while ∂ₜ∇^(m+1)χ is driven by ∇^mU. The Codex bookkeeping and executable verification support this mechanism, including the G-terms and transported-coefficient DeTurck restart. Nevertheless, the displayed proof still imports the hardest grouped integration and finite staggered-exponent estimates from Wang rather than reproducing them term by term. The low-order weighted-energy architecture is substantially stronger than earlier versions. The correct critical quantity is the active energy β=S(∫suppχ F²)^(1/2), excluding the stationary collar. The q=2 estimate propagates this active energy and produces spacetime H₃; the separate q=3 inequality transports a good time slice to the terminal time, yielding S²H₃(t)≤Cβ³/t. The homogeneous global Q and fixed J₆ estimates preserve the initial Ricci-defect powers, and three-factor Hölder on positive-time slabs yields the volume-independent endpoints |aRm|≤Cβ/t and |aE|≤Cδ(r²/t)^(1/3). The latter is integrable at zero and closes metric and Sobolev comparison, while the former is used only near a positive terminal time for continuation. A precise defect remains in the written general-p derivation: cD_p alone controls ∥∇Z_p∥² only with a p^−2 coefficient. A uniform coefficient follows only if the p(p−2) scalar-gradient dissipation from differentiating Φ^p is retained explicitly; alternatively a polynomially deteriorating coefficient is acceptable because the Moser product still converges. This must be established for every tensor, moving-norm, measure, Hessian-cutoff, and regularized-defect term rather than inferred from representative terms. The downstream geometry is conceptually much cleaner than collapse classification. A maximal separated subset of the positive-volume maximal-function good set, combined with Bishop–Gromov comparison, gives disjoint buffered supports whose inner balls retain a fixed volume proportion. Bilipschitz control transfers that proportion to the terminal metric and gives a fixed terminal geodesic buffer. On such a plateau, positive-time interior estimates supply all curvature derivatives. The exponential-pullback averaging lemma is genuinely robust under collapse and arbitrary exponential-map multiplicity: Rauch comparison gives a fixed pullback-volume lower bound, and Liouville invariance controls averages without sheet-count loss. Rebuilding the Seiberg–Witten data for the same terminal metric, using r=(sqrt(6)|W⁺|−s)⁺ and the positive-semidefinite operator A=W⁺+sqrt(2/3)|W⁺|I, produces a joint nonnegative density controlling Einstein defect, calibrated modulus, harmonic-form amplitude, and lowest-eigenline error. Its vanishing pullback average yields a smooth Einstein germ with a nonzero harmonic self-dual form and spectrum (−1/6,1/12,1/12); the Step 6 projector argument forces ∇W⁺=0, whereas the Einstein–Weyl identity gives |∇W⁺|²=1/16. This terminal contradiction is sound for fixed topology once the flow platform and positive-part monopole lemma are written rigorously. The full Yau 26 problem remains far beyond this line: even a completed fixed-manifold gap would exclude one proposed negative counterexample mechanism rather than settle either normalized sign branch, arbitrary topology, dimensions at least five, dimension-only constants, or the requested Lean formalization. A02's corrected positive-collapse Bochner result likewise controls exact Killing central directions but leaves horizontal twist and almost-Killing realization open. The most valuable immediate work is therefore to finish the exact two analytic lemmas and terminal-density bridge, obtain a definitive fixed-topology theorem or discover a genuine failure, and then reassess whether this mechanism can be made topology-uniform or should inform a broader route.
