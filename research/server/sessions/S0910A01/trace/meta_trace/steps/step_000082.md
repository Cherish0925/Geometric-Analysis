Step 82 produced a significant structural synthesis connecting three independent mathematical frameworks to the positive mixed-row product-orbifold construction, and discovered a decisive consistency check that validates the entire symmetry reduction while sharply identifying the remaining open question.

The deepest insight of this step concerns the relationship between the Ozuch obstruction hierarchy and known Kähler-Einstein existence results. The product orbifold (P¹×P¹)/Z₂ ≅ X₄ᵀ is classified by Odaka-Spotti-Sun as a degree-4 canonical del Pezzo surface in their complete KE moduli classification. Its Q-Gorenstein smoothing gives smooth Bl₅P², which admits KE by Tian's theorem. Crucially, this algebraic smoothing corresponds to the ALL-SAME-CHIRALITY Biquard-Ozuch desingularization: all 4 EH insertions with the same orientation give a resolution with b⁺=1, b⁻=5, τ=-4, which is topologically Bl₅P². Our obstruction analysis (Steps 77-81: λ_k=0 by in-kernel insertion, μ₁=0 by rank-one determinant, μ₂=μ₃=0 by J-parity, ν_l=0 by A-parity) correctly predicts this — it is NECESSARY for the known KE metric to exist. This provides independent validation of the entire A+J+L symmetry reduction framework.

The DIAGONAL chirality pattern (2+, 2−, opposite corners) gives a DIFFERENT smooth manifold: #₃(S²×S²) with b⁺=3, b⁻=3, τ=0. This is NOT Fano (-K is nef and big but not ample due to mixed-chirality (-2)-curves contributing to both b⁺ and b⁻), so neither Tian nor OSS applies. The obstruction COEFFICIENTS (λ, μ at Ozuch's tracked orders) are identical for all chirality patterns because R⁺=R⁻=diag(Λ,0,0) on the product. But the complete reduced function F(t) involves inter-node Green response that distinguishes same-chirality from mixed-chirality source-receiver pairs. For same-chirality, F(t)=0 is guaranteed by Tian/OSS. For mixed-chirality, F(t)=? is the genuine open question.

The TTCS verification was essential: it correctly identified that my initial existence claim was premature. Ozuch Proposition 2.19 gives NECESSARY conditions (existence → obstructions bounded), not sufficient. The low-order vanishing 0=O(t^{5/2}) is uninformative — it is satisfied by any F(t)=ct³+... with c≠0, which would have no small positive zero. The complete radial function's first nonzero layer at O(t³) or O(t³ log t) sits below Ozuch's error and cannot be resolved by the published framework. TTCS also corrected the O(g_o)={0} claim: the four scalar kernel modes u_ig_j lie in P's full L² kernel, making O(g_o) at least 4-dimensional. The A-parity argument eliminates their projections within the equivariant family, which is the correct mechanism rather than trivial kernel.

The Integrability paper connection (Ozuch arXiv:2105.13193) provides a conceptual explanation for WHY first-order obstructions vanish on Einstein orbifolds: Proposition A.2 uses bilinearity and known Einstein examples (S²×S² explicitly cited for the mixed-chirality term H_{W+}∘H_{W-}). But the paper explicitly excludes the higher-order [Ozu20] obstructions (line 563), and Question 0.8 asks whether the preserved-quantity framework can recover them — this is open in the field.

For the broader Yau-26 goal, this step clarifies the research landscape: the positive mixed-row route is well-understood structurally but resolves to a specific technical question (mixed-chirality Green coefficient) whose answer produces either a new Einstein metric on #₃(S²×S²) (interesting but not Yau-26) or a local obstruction (also not Yau-26). The restricted IL negative theorem remains the strongest scope-relevant result. A02's alignment closure (#548) confirms no topological barrier to positive Einstein on χ=8 mixed rows. A03's PE scattering correction (#553) excludes ASD metrics as graph-neck reflected cores but sharpens the non-ASD backward-evolution question.

The most promising mathematical opportunities are: (1) determine whether chirality-dependence of the inter-node Green response is detectable from the product spectral structure, which would settle F(t) for #₃(S²×S²); (2) reassess whether the restricted IL theorem or a strengthened version is closer to Lean-formalizable modular form; (3) explore whether the validated framework and the chirality-topology connection suggest new families of Einstein 4-manifolds beyond the known del Pezzo examples.
