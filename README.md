# Einstein Geometry on Four-Manifolds: Gaps, Curvature Extremals, and Realization

<strong>Shuxing Yang, Junyao Wu, Rui Zhao, Hongsheng Chen<sup>&#42;</sup>, Yihao Yang<sup>&#42;</sup></strong>  
College of Information Science and Electronic Engineering, Zhejiang University  
Qiushi Engine Team

<sup>&#42;</sup> Corresponding authors: [Hongsheng Chen](mailto:hansomchen@zju.edu.cn) and [Yihao Yang](mailto:yangyihao@zju.edu.cn).

We study Einstein metrics on four-manifolds through curvature inequalities, variational problems, and geometric constructions. For a closed oriented smooth four-manifold, a central invariant is

```math
P_*(M)=\inf_{g\in\mathcal G(M)}\int_M\left|\mathrm{Ric}_g-\frac{s_g}{4}g\right|_g^2\,dV_g,
```

where the infimum ranges over all smooth Riemannian metrics. The questions are when this infimum is positive, how its value depends on topology, whether it is attained, and how minimizing sequences degenerate. Normalized curvature functionals give related Einstein gap problems.

The analysis combines the Seiberg–Witten equations with curvature identities. Blow-up and orbifold resolution constructions examine the interaction between inserted metrics and background curvature. Further results concern the correction of approximate Einstein metrics and the integrability of compatible almost-Kähler structures.
