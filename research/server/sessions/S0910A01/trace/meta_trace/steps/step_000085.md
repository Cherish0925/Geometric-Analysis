This recovers the four scalar zero modes u_i g_j and the negative constant trace/shape-scale directions, and it matches the corrected mixed one-form gap at ell_1=ell_2=1. Thus the computation no longer treats the nine C_ab matrices as a single TT sector with one denominator.

These numbers are not Ozuch coefficients. The numerical facts from Step 84 remain: T preserves the special pencil, G=<T,S0,S2> is transitive on the four nodes, and the invariant holomorphic vector-field subspace is zero. The mistaken part was labeling S0 and S2 as anti-holomorphic. They are complex-linear projective sign changes in CP^4, hence holomorphic automorphisms of the del Pezzo fibers.
