This step achieved a structurally complete resolution of the leading-order chirality question for the product-orbifold mixed-row desingularization, through three independent and mutually reinforcing mathematical mechanisms.

The deepest insight is the connection between Ozuch's boundary-pairing formula (Proposition 2.10) and the SD/ASD algebra of the product orbifold. The TTCS verification established that the Fredholm kernel projection nu_l vanishes for all four candidate kernel modes and both chiralities, because the kernel modes' V_{l,2} components are proportional to C_{11} (the shape direction g_A - g_B), which is orthogonal to all H^4 source multipoles C_{2k} and C_{k2} by the exact trace identity tr(C_{ab}C_{cd}) = 4 delta_{ac} delta_{bd}. This is a clean structural result: it follows from the product curvature having rank-1 SD/ASD structure with kernel in the off-diagonal C_{ab} channels, while Einstein deformation modes live in the diagonal C_{11} channel.

Since nu_l = 0, the inter-node coupling is mediated entirely by the non-kernel Green function of P on the compact orbifold. The correct formula is a double-boundary spectral sum: c_{ij} = sum_alpha R_i(psi_alpha) S_j(psi_alpha) / lambda_alpha, where both source and receiver act through Ozuch's boundary pairing (3 int_{S^3/Z_2} <H^4, (psi)_2>). This is exact and frame-invariant, resolving the frame sensitivity of the prior proxy computations.

The chirality dependence enters only through the source multipole H^4(zeta^+) vs H^4(zeta^-). Using the S^3 quartic moment formula int (x^T A x)(x_i x_j) = (pi^2/12) A_{ij} for trace-free A, the boundary pairing factorizes into products of C_{ab} entries. The chirality difference in the spectral sum is controlled by the bilinear defect tensor D_{ij,mn} = sum_k [C_{1k,ij}C_{1k,mn} - C_{k1,ij}C_{k1,mn}].

The computation revealed a subtle layered structure: (1) The SQUARED identity sum_k C_{ak,ij}^2 = sum_k C_{ka,ij}^2 holds exactly for ALL (a,i,j) — this is verified numerically with zero deviation. (2) The BILINEAR identity fails with max deviation 2.0 — the defect tensor D has rank 4 with eigenvalues +/-4 (multiplicity 2 each), and its nonzero entries are exactly at 'fully mixed' index quartets (0,1,2,3)-type, reflecting the orientation tensor epsilon. (3) Despite D being nonzero, ALL physical product eigenmodes give zero chirality difference, because their tensor/angular indices are always matched by the product factorization — AB modes pair (iA,jB) tensor with (iA,jB) angular, so the contraction with D involves D_{iA,jB,iA,jB} which has repeated indices and vanishes; AA/BB modes have all indices within one factor, while D requires all four directions.

The complete vanishing theorem c_opp - c_same = 0 at leading Ozuch order is therefore not a cancellation miracle but a structural consequence of three independent mechanisms: kernel orthogonality (nu_l = 0), orientation-sector confinement of D, and product-mode index matching. Any chirality difference must arise at higher order: nonlinear Q^(3) terms, H_4^4 logarithmic corrections, v-zeta coupling, or orbifold-deformation response.

For the broader Yau-26 problem, this result has important strategic implications. If the all-same-chirality KE degeneration can be matched to the Ozuch gluing chart (establishing F_same(t) = 0 at leading order), then F_mixed(t) = 0 also at this order. The mixed-chirality obstruction, if it exists, is a HIGHER-ORDER effect. This makes the constructive existence route more promising than previously assumed: the leading-order equation is the same for both chiralities, so the mixed smoothing to #_3(S^2 x S^2) faces the same leading obstruction landscape as the same-chirality smoothing to Bl_5 P^2.

The restricted IL negative theorem remains the strongest scope-relevant result tied to the original all-dimensional problem. The positive mixed-row work is a demanding local test that has now been sharpened to a higher-order question. The graph-manifold route (A03 #573, #577) has its own first-physical-layer difficulties.
