## Deep-Reasoning

This step resolved the two remaining analytic inputs for the restricted four-dimensional negative Ishida–LeBrun defect gap theorem: the exact weighted shifted-curvature evolution (H-WU) and the transported DeTurck start/restart (H-DT).

The H-WU resolution rests on a structural observation about how the cutoff weight propagates through the weighted evolution equation. The weighted shifted curvature is U = χ²T where T = Rm+G. Since χ is a fixed scalar function, ∂_t U = a∂_t T, and taking m covariant derivatives yields ∂_t U_m = ∇^m(a∂_t T) + [∂_t, ∇^m]U. The dangerous cutoff jet C_{m+2} = ∇^{m+2}χ can only arise from the Hessian-a part of the Riemann variation: ∇^m(a · (∇²a)*E) contains ∇^{m+2}a contracted with aE. Since ∇^{m+2}a = ∇^{m+2}(χ²) has top single jet 2χC_{m+2} by the product-rule identity, this source has coefficient proportional to χ — not bare. TTCS independently verified the derivation and identified one correction: in the parabolic form (∂_t - a∆)U_m, extracting a∆U from a²∆T produces an additional term -(∆a)U whose m-th derivative also contains C_{m+2}, but again with the χ weight from ∇^{m+2}(χ²). This correction was incorporated. The commutator [∂_t, ∇^m]U was shown to produce at most C_m-level jets, and all other terms (G-variation, normalization, reaction, gradient-a drift) produce at most C_{m+1}. Thus every C_{m+2} source carries χ, which is exactly what A03's staggered energy mechanism needs: after integration by parts, the remaining χ pairs with the weighted dissipation ∫χ²|∇U_m|²|U_m|^{p-2}. Without χ, the Cauchy-Schwarz absorption would require χ² ≥ 1 in the transition region, which fails.

A03 Step 45 independently confirmed this weight structure using a coordinate-time convention (∂_t throughout, not metric-compatible D_t) with coefficient regularization b = χ² + ε². Their construction retains the B-notation B_{r+1}*B_{s+1}*T_t before expanding B = ∇b, which correctly tracks the χ² factor: B₁*B₁*T_m = 4(χ²/b)(C₁*C₁)*[U_m - lower], giving a uniformly bounded coefficient. Two independent TTCS verifiers confirmed closure uniform in ε after four minor corrections, with 110/110 algebraic checks. The coordinate-level construction is the more complete derivation because it handles spatial commutators (bRm = U - bG), time commutators (using (1.3)-(1.5)), and the m=2 signed-Hessian endpoint explicitly.

For H-DT, the assessment compared two independent routes: (1) A03 Step 44's direct Yang adaptation, which shows the normalization is a zeroth-order smooth-tame perturbation preserving Yang's Theorem 8.2, and (2) A03 Step 45's coefficient-regularized construction, which uses fixed-ε transported DeTurck for each ε > 0, geometric first-exit energies for a common per-datum time, and fixed-background connection-difference induction for C^∞ compactness as ε → 0. The second route is cleaner because it avoids importing Yang's compressed tame inverse proof; it obtains existence/restart directly from the coefficient-regularized flows. Uniqueness of the degenerate geometric flow is not supplied (and not needed for the per-datum platform).

The per-datum flow platform is now complete: existence, continuation (bounded |U| implies smooth extension via the staggered T₀ → (U₁,C₂) → T₁ → ⋯ schedule), and restart. The remaining task, explicitly identified by A03 #365, is an independent review of the assembled first-exit/support/terminal chain. This is the chain from support selection (volume-average maximal function, gradient cutoffs, global a = Σχ_α², inner centers) through first-exit bootstrap (K₂ → Y → J₆ → Moser → weighted defect endpoints) through global Q propagation (disjoint buffered supports) through terminal recalibration (SW on evolved metric with q ≥ 0) through exponential pullback and Derdziński contradiction. Each piece has been individually checked in earlier steps, but the end-to-end coherence on one common flow has not been verified. This is Agent4 (Preceptor) work.
