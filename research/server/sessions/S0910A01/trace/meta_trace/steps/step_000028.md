## Deep-Reasoning

This step repaired an important downstream misunderstanding in the restricted four-dimensional negative LeBrun/Ishida--LeBrun route. The previous route assessment had reopened a base-manifold plateau-noncollapse/F-structure problem, but the existing Step 16 exponential-pullback mechanism is stronger and more directly matched to the selected-support output. If the cutoff-flow construction supplies a set of inner plateau centers P_i with sequence-uniform normalized volume Vol(P_i) >= alpha A_i and uniform bounded-sectional-curvature radius-rho buffers, then the Step 16 Rauch/Liouville pullback averaging selects a tangent-space exponential ball with vanishing terminal deficit mean. The tangent-space domain itself has Rauch noncollapse even if the image in the base manifold is highly collapsed or multiply covered. Thus Euler-density localization and base injectivity-radius lower bounds are not needed for this downstream passage. The needed quantitative assumptions are fixed alpha, rho, and K; mere nonzero volume possibly o(A_i) would not interact with a global o(A_i) integral.

The terminal calibration was made explicit and regenerated on the flowed metric g(T), rather than transported from the initial curvature tensor by C^0 metric comparison. Reapplying the Step 8 completion square and explicit eigenvalue gap to g(T) yields int(s+8sqrt(6)|W^+|)^2 <= 108Q and int G_gap <= 6Q. Hence the combined nonnegative density mathfrak D=|E|^2+(s+4)^2+(s+8sqrt(6)|W^+|)^2+G_gap satisfies int mathfrak D <= 119Q. This was verified by workspace/scripts/step28_terminal_pullback_constants.py. Together with Q(T)/A -> 0, Vol(P_i) >= alpha A_i, and the pullback averaging inequality, this selects centers whose pulled-back mean of all terminal deficits tends to zero. On the pulled-back tangent ball, bounded curvature plus Rauch volume lower bounds give local harmonic-coordinate compactness after shrinking radius. The limiting Einstein germ has Ric=-g, s=-4, |W^+|^2=1/24, and spec(W^+)=(-1/6,1/12,1/12), contradicting Derdziński's local conformal-Kähler conclusion because a Kähler--Einstein four-metric with s=-4 has |W^+|^2=2/3.

The corrected reciprocal sum is sum 1/p_k=(n+2)/(2p0), while the net Sobolev exponent is n/(2p0) because each recurrence step contributes S^{1/(kappa p_k)}. Polynomial p-losses remain inside the recurrence through L_k=C p_k^N 4^k(t^{-1}+r^{-2}), so the Moser product converges. Fixed J_6 persistence is kept as a time-zero fixed-exponent critical absorption, not as an integral of the positive-time Y~1/t coefficient. The script workspace/scripts/step28_moser_recurrence_verify.py and the patched workspace/scripts/step27_three_factor_absorption.py verify the corrected exponent bookkeeping and the two endpoint volume cancellations: ||aF||_infty <= C beta/t and ||aE||_infty <= C delta(r^2/t)^{1/3}.

The A03/Codex shifted-curvature continuation mechanism was read and tested against its scripts. The useful object is U=a(Rm+G), G=(g wedge g)/(2(n-1)), so Ric(U)=a(Ric+g) and the full velocity is -2 Ric(U). This prevents splitting -2ag as a vague lower-order perturbation and restores Wang's derivative relation for the evolving cutoff jets. A03's Codex report and shifted_curvature_continuation.md give a much stronger proof skeleton than A01's earlier continuation note; scripts verify the convention-sensitive Ric(G)=g, d_t G, cutoff Leibniz, top cutoff allocation, and scalar--Weyl constants. TTCS supported this schematic Wang-mechanism transfer, but it also kept the strict distinction between this and a fully expanded written proof: the Wang Proposition 4.14 grouped integration by parts, staggered Holder exponents, and Yang degenerate restart still need to be written explicitly if this line is to become a theorem.

The real mathematical bottleneck for the restricted negative route is now upstream, not downstream: prove a simultaneous selected-support flow theorem, in the same multi-support construction, producing active H_2 and critical-product persistence, S^2Y(t) <= C beta^3/t without circular dependence, fixed J_6 persistence from time zero, triangular global Q(T) <= e^{C theta}Q(0), support-wide weighted velocity for metric/Sobolev/cutoff comparison, and bounded-curvature inner plateaux with Vol(P_i) >= alpha A_i and uniform radius-rho buffers. Completing this would still only exclude the critical four-dimensional LeBrun/Ishida--LeBrun negative laboratory; it would not settle arbitrary four-dimensional topologies, the positive branch, dimensions n>=5, the all-dimensional interval proposition, or Lean formalization.

A02's Step 28 positive-branch Bochner update was recorded without diverting the main work. For a genuine central circle Killing field Z, int|nabla Z|^2=int Ric(Z,Z) gives a dimension-free bracket bound under positive Ricci upper control, and negative Ricci forbids Killing fields. A02 corrected the scope: the full nonabelian nilpotent group need not act by isometries, horizontal O'Neill twist is not controlled by the center bracket bound, and almost-Killing errors of fiber-scale order suffice only for normalized bracket vanishing. This is valuable positive-collapse structure, but it does not currently change the negative selected-support route.
