## Deep-Reasoning

This step re-centered the controlled Ishida--LeBrun negative-branch route on the actual mathematical estimates rather than on the reassuring algebraic consistency reached in Step 31. The central lesson is that the mixed-weight idea is real but does not by itself close the analytic bridge from global near-Einstein defect to bounded-curvature plateaux. The valid mechanism is subtle: the weighted Moser variable \(z_p=a^{(p-1)/2}f^{p/2}\) has exactly the right derivative structure because \(|\nabla a|^2/a=4|\nabla\chi|^2\), and the desired next moment is obtained from \(H_{3p/2}\le H_p^{1/2}\|z_p\|_4^2\), not from \(\|z_p\|_3^3\). This rescues the route from the false half-power objection. But the Step 31 synthesis then went too far: the correct recurrence is a spacetime recurrence using \(\int_I H_{3p/2}\le (\sup_I H_p)^{1/2}S\int_I(\|\nabla z_p\|_2^2+r^{-2}H_p)\), and it does not grant an independent \(\sup K_{2p}\) or an unproved four-dimensional product Sobolev estimate. The proof must keep the reaction weight \(a^{p-1}\), the same-support cubic potential \(Y=\int aF^3\), the \(J_6\) defect propagation, global \(Q\), and moving Sobolev constants in one coherent first-exit system. This is not a cosmetic difference: if one imports a stronger time control than the estimates provide, the entire smoothing bridge can appear closed when it is still missing the part that converts weighted energy into terminal regularity.

The continuation problem is similarly sharper than the Step 31 handoff. The shifted tensor \(U=a(\operatorname{Rm}+G)\) remains a strong idea because it packages the normalized flow velocity as \(\partial_tg=-2\operatorname{Ric}(U)\) and keeps the cutoff evolution driven by derivatives of \(U\), rather than by a separate normalization source. Wang's local Ricci-flow source also genuinely uses a triangular interaction among weighted curvature, unweighted curvature, and cutoff derivatives. However, the normalized shifted proof cannot simply cite that source or announce a coupled Gronwall theorem. The lowest nontrivial problem occurs already at \(m=2\): the Codex expansion of equations (6.11)--(6.13) leaves a term schematically \(I=\int \chi |\nabla^2\chi| |\nabla^2U|^p\). A direct Young estimate against \(D_p=\int\chi^2|\nabla^3U|^2|\nabla^2U|^{p-2}\) would introduce negative powers of \(\chi\), so that route is invalid. A01's reply that this can be bounded by \(\|\nabla^2\chi\|_\infty\|\nabla^2U\|_p^p\) is not yet enough at the delicate evolving-metric base level unless pointwise control of the evolving Hessian of the fixed scalar has already been established. The more precise estimate supplied by Step 32 TTCS is promising: set \(W=\nabla^2U\), \(u=\chi|W|^{p/2}\), \(B=\nabla^2\chi\); then four-dimensional Sobolev and Hölder give \(I\le \varepsilon D_p+C_\varepsilon p^2S\|B\|_4^2(1+r^{-2})\|W\|_p^p\), provided \(\|\nabla^2\chi\|_4\) and the Sobolev constant are supplied by the evolving base pair. Thus the continuation route is not disproved, but its proof must insert this estimate or else write the exact grouped Wang identity for the shifted normalized equation showing that the single-\(\chi\) descendant is replaced by Wang's softer residual.

This review also clarified that coefficient regularization \(a\mapsto a+\varepsilon\) is not interchangeable with Kato regularization of tensor norms. Since the selected-support strategy deliberately leaves the exterior \(a=0\) uncontrolled and stationary, replacing \(a\) by \(a+\varepsilon\) evolves that exterior and creates \(U_\varepsilon=\varepsilon(\operatorname{Rm}+G)\) there. Uniform estimates would then require exterior curvature and derivative control not present in the route. The appropriate existence theory must be Yang/DeTurck-style degenerate local Ricci flow with the fixed coefficient \(a=\chi^2\), not a casual strictly parabolic coefficient perturbation.

The global Yau 26 problem remains far beyond the present subprogram. The Ishida--LeBrun route is still valuable because it probes a serious class of no-Einstein four-manifolds and links gauge-theoretic calibration, cutoff-flow smoothing, and the local calibrated Einstein-germ contradiction. If the analytic bridge is completed, it would likely show that this class cannot furnish the desired negative-branch counterexamples in the controlled near-critical regime, thereby strengthening the evidence for the conjectural direction in dimension four rather than settling the original all-dimensional statement. A02's noncollapsed positive-branch dichotomy adds a separate useful route: regular positive near-Einstein limits give Einstein metrics, and ALE bubbling is constrained by Ozuch-type spectral conditions. But the positive collapsed branch, arbitrary four-dimensional topology, dimensions \(n\ge5\), and Lean formalization remain open. The highest-value immediate work is therefore not paper consolidation, but a rigorous construction step that proves or breaks the remaining estimates in their actual form.
