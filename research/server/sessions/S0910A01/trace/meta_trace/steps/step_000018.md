But this does not prove the tensor theorem. TTCS and A03 both sharpened the status: the coupled local L2 energy system is a first-exit lemma under provisional metric, Sobolev, finite-neighbor, cutoff, and local-energy controls, not an autonomous theorem. The supported scale mechanism is that in dimension four A_U ||Rm||_2 ~ sqrt(K0) r^2, so collapse volume cancels in the absorption of int a |Rm| |E|^2 and analogous curvature reactions. This also generalizes formally in dimension n at the critical L^{n/2} curvature norm, but the present LeBrun route has such an input only in dimension four via Chern-Weil/Noether. The remaining pointwise regularity is now sharply isolated: from small normalized critical energy under the degenerate operator partial_t-aDelta, prove a weighted curvature potential such as |a Rm| <= C/t or A_U^4 int a|Rm|^3 <= C/t, with constants independent of collapse. This is better than relying on a defect maximum principle with C/t coefficient, because int_0^T dt/t diverges. The one-dimensional and constant-diffusivity models support the plausibility of the weighted target, and the Whitney scaling computation gives a concrete route: at chi(x0)=chi0>0, work on radius ell=c r chi0 where a/a0, drift, and potential coefficients are uniform after rescaling; the diffusion length stays inside the Whitney ball for theta <= c^2. This suggests a real proof strategy for degenerate weighted Moser/mean-value estimates, not a completed proof.
