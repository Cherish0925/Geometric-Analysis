## Deep-Reasoning

This step developed the static circle reduction of W⁺ for Einstein four-manifolds with Ric = -g, discovered and corrected a significant formula error through TTCS verification, and established a clean fiber survival dichotomy that reshapes the negative-branch collapse program.

The central mathematical construction is the static circle reduction: for g = g_B + ℓ²dψ² with Ric = -g, the self-dual Weyl operator satisfies Spec(W⁺) = {1/3 - η_i} where η_i are eigenvalues of h = Hess(ℓ)/ℓ with tr(h) = 1. The initial derivation used an incorrect factor (the '2J(h - I/3)' formula), which was caught by the TTCS verifier through a careful index-by-index recomputation using the three-dimensional Riemann-Ricci identity and the trace constraint. The corrected formula—confirmed against constant curvature (gives W⁺ = 0) and Taub-NUT-AdS (static part + twist = 0)—changes the calibrated Hessian eigenvalues from (3/8, 3/8, 1/4) to (1/2, 1/4, 1/4). The cohomogeneity-one incompatibility strengthens: the Einstein ODE system gives a'' = a/4 from the radial equation but a'' = a/2 from differentiating the angular constraint, with relative gap 1/2.

Two additional corrections matter. First, the twist sign: the modified Laplacian Δ_B ℓ + (ℓ³/4)|F|² = ℓ gives tr(h) = 1 + ℓ²|F|²/4 > 1, not < 1. Second and more importantly: near a smooth NUT where ℓ → 0, both h and the twist correction diverge individually (h_ang ~ 1/(8νx), ℓ²|F|² ~ 1/(2νx)) while canceling in the smooth total curvature. The earlier claim that 'the static formula dominates near the NUT, forcing W⁺ toward 0' is therefore false. In principle, a NUT with appropriate twist cancellation could maintain any W⁺ eigenvalue structure.

These corrections refine but do not destroy the collapse obstruction program. The valid structural result is the fiber survival dichotomy from the TTCS generator analysis. On the calibrated good set, either (S) the circle fiber survives at a fixed buffer scale—in which case a buffered normal cover unwraps it, the mean transport carries the calibrated deficit, and the forbidden Einstein germ (Steps 5-6) gives a contradiction—or (D) the fiber dies within every fixed buffer scale, which requires persistent high twist ℓ²|F|² ≥ c > 0 to maintain the calibrated W⁺ anisotropy against the static incompatibility. The generator further showed that ℓ(p) can be arbitrarily small at a good point (a multiplicative rescaling ℓ → εℓ doesn't change h), so the curvature-mean bound controls h, not ℓ itself. But in the non-degenerate case, Harnack for the positive eigenfunction equation Δ_B ℓ = (1 + τ/4)ℓ prevents ℓ from dying within a controlled neighborhood.

The Strategist's correction about the Pontryagin density is fully validated: for any Einstein metric with Ric = -g, the Levi-Civita connection on Λ²₊ has self-dual curvature, giving |ι_V F|² = |F|²/2 automatically. The isotropic scalar contribution s²/24 provides 88.9% of the calibrated characteristic density. The distinguishing feature is not the density magnitude but the W⁺ anisotropy (|W⁺|² = 1/24 vs 0).

S0910A02's toric orthogonal transitivity result connects directly to our dichotomy: their conserved twist constants vanish at degeneration edges, forcing static structure near bolts/edges. If this extends to approximate settings, it would push case (D) back toward case (S) near fiber death points. S0910A01's rank-one Cartan structure analysis supports the diffuse mixed-curvature picture. The three sessions now have complementary results that should be combined to attack the remaining high-twist case.

The positive branch, higher dimensions, and Lean formalization remain substantially open. The current work provides a significantly refined but still incomplete negative-branch analysis.
