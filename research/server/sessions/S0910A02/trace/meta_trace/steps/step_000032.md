## Deep-Reasoning

Step 32 was an execution audit that corrected the immediate Route-A handoff before it could turn into a misleading model experiment. The active target remains the full Yau-26 proposition: dimension-only two-sided Ricci pinching on every closed connected smooth n-manifold should imply, or fail to imply, existence of some Einstein metric on the same smooth manifold, with no proximity, sign, symmetry, noncollapse, or Kähler assumption. The current work still concerns only the four-dimensional positive noncollapsed subproblem, so it cannot be mistaken for a proof or disproof of Yau-26.

The main conceptual correction is that the proposed Page-metric annular-flux experiment is not scientifically probative. The Page metric is a single rigid positive Einstein metric on \(\mathbb{CP}^2\#\overline{\mathbb{CP}}{}^2\), produced by blowing up a smooth point in the rational-surface topology; an Eguchi--Hanson resolution of an \(A_1=\mathbb C^2/\{\pm1\}\) singularity instead inserts a \((-2)\)-sphere. The audit script checked the intersection-form signal: \(\mathbb{CP}^2\#\overline{\mathbb{CP}}{}^2\) with form \(\langle1\rangle\oplus\langle-1\rangle\) has no integral class of square \(-2\) in the searched range, and in fact parity gives the structural reason. More importantly, Page is not a degenerating family with a bubble scale \(t\to0\), so there is no balanced neck on which a nonzero obstruction could be detected or refuted. Any flux on an exact rigid Einstein metric would vanish as a consistency check. The real relevance of Page, and likewise Chen--LeBrun--Weber, is opposite: these are known Einstein metrics on rational/Fano surfaces without Kähler--Einstein metrics in the relevant complex structure, proving again that no Kähler--Einstein metric is not the same as no Riemannian Einstein metric. This prevents an erroneous counterexample search based on KE nonexistence.

The second correction reconnects the noncollapsed-bubble bridge to the Step-1/Step-7 obstruction-amplitude principle rather than to a new exact-family experiment. In the Morteza--Viaclovsky/Calabi normal form one has schematically \(\operatorname{Ric}_{g_t}-\Lambda g_t-t\lambda\chi_t o=O_{g_{cal}}(t^2\rho^2)\) and \(g_t\sim t g_{cal}\) on the core. Raising an index with \(g_t^{-1}\) converts the non-scalar obstruction term \(t\lambda o\) into an order-\(|\lambda|\) Ricci-endomorphism defect, while the remainder becomes \(O(t\rho^2)\), hence small on the core and at most \(O(t^{1/2})\) at the Step-1 transition scale \(\rho\sim t^{-1/4}\). The missing theorem is not numerical detection; it is ansatz-independence: an arbitrary \(C^0\)-Ricci-pinched noncollapsed degeneration must be put into a neck/gluing expansion, or an equivalent local projection theory, in which the same unsuppressed obstruction coefficient appears.

The third correction is the order accounting for flux. The Step-30 cosmological Einstein tensor identity remains useful as a \(C^0\)-Ricci-safe divergence identity, but it is linear in the defect and cannot itself see Ozuch's Taub/Wronskian cross term \(H_2\)-\(H^4\). The relevant bilinear obstruction has local sizes \(|H_2|\sim r^2\), \(|t^4H^4|\sim t^4r^{-4}\), with boundary area \(r^3\), so \(r^3(H_2\partial H^4-H^4\partial H_2)\sim t^4\), independent of radius. The natural balanced radius is \(r=t^{2/3}\), where both pieces have size \(t^{4/3}\). The audit computes that a localized balanced-shell pairing of a \(\delta\)-small Ricci residual with the natural modes scales like \(\delta r^6=\delta t^4\), so the exponent is \(a=6\) in the strategist's notation and, at least on a localized balanced dyadic shell, no rate assumption stronger than \(\delta_i\to0\) is suggested. By contrast, the older vector-field \(\mathcal E_g\) flux with \(Z\sim r\), \(\mathcal L_Zg\sim1\) has error \(\delta r^4=\delta t^{8/3}\) at the balance scale, larger than a \(t^4\) signal unless \(\delta\ll t^{4/3}\), and is the wrong object for the bilinear obstruction anyway. This gives the next construction a sharper target: a bilinear Taub/Wronskian identity localized near the balance annulus, or a genuinely conserved version controlling the sum over a long neck, not a Page metric test and not a linear \(\mathcal E_g\) detection experiment.
