# Research Project Proposal

## Research Objective
Rigorously determine whether Wang's smooth-extension induction for local Ricci flow transfers to the normalized fixed-cutoff flow
\[
\partial_t g=-2a(\operatorname{Ric}+g),\qquad a=\chi^2,
\]
under the sole terminal-time hypothesis \(\sup_{M\times[0,T)}|a\operatorname{Rm}|<\infty\), with constants allowed to depend on the individual initial smooth metric and cutoff.

## Current Progress
The session has built a conditional four-dimensional negative-branch Ricci-pinching gap argument whose last analytic obstruction is this extension theorem. The previous specification incorrectly treated the added \(-2ag\) term as harmless without tracking the highest derivatives of the cutoff. The full curvature variation contains Hessian-cutoff terms. A promising correction is to introduce the parallel constant-curvature tensor
\[
G(g)=\frac1{n-1}(g\owedge g)/2
\]
(with the convention chosen so \(\operatorname{Ric}(G)=g\)) and shifted curvature \(\widetilde R=\operatorname{Rm}+G\), so \(\operatorname{Ric}(\widetilde R)=E=\operatorname{Ric}+g\) and \(\nabla\widetilde R=\nabla\operatorname{Rm}\). This may make the normalized flow exactly Wang's tensor mechanism with \(R\) replaced by \(\widetilde R\), plus controlled algebraic \(G\)-terms.

Do not omit the terms involving \(\nabla^{m+2}a\). Determine whether the Hessian terms are exactly \((\nabla^2a)*\operatorname{Ric}(\widetilde R)\), with first derivative terms \(da*\nabla\operatorname{Ric}(\widetilde R)\), plus algebraic terms.
3. Derive the evolution of \(\nabla^m(a\widetilde R)\), and compare its highest-order dependency graph with Wang Lemmas 4.4--4.6. Explicitly show where \(\nabla^{m+2}\chi\), \(\nabla^{m+1}(a\widetilde R)\), unweighted \(\nabla^m\widetilde R\), and \(\nabla^{m+1}\chi\) enter.

7. Produce symbolic/index bookkeeping checks where useful, but distinguish them from proof.
