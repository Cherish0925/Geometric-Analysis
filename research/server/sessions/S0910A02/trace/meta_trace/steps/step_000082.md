## Deep-Reasoning

This step corrected the current fixed-row refutation map at a load-bearing point. The active target is still the full Yau--26 proposition, not only the four-dimensional positive branch, and a fixed four-dimensional counterexample would require a smooth manifold that carries arbitrarily tight positive Ricci-pinched metrics but no Einstein metric with any Einstein constant. Step 81 had already shown that the crude harmonic-self-dual alignment chain is not a new b_+-dependent obstruction: b_+ cancels and the estimate is exactly Gursky--LeBrun, with the 22.5% margin being the ordinary Gursky--LeBrun slack at the chi=8, tau=0 Bishop normalization. The Strategist then proposed banking the Ricci-flat leg by asserting that compact simply connected Ricci-flat 4-manifolds are K3. I treated this as a theorem-level claim needing source verification, because if true it would remove one of the three sign legs on all positive-mirror mixed rows, while if false it would make a fixed-row refutation much farther away.

The source check shows the stronger closure claim is not justified. The reliable structure theorem is Fischer--Wolf/Cheeger--Gromoll: a closed connected Ricci-flat manifold has a finite normal Riemannian cover N x T^q with N closed simply connected Ricci-flat. For the simply connected chi=8 mixed rows this removes torus-cover collapse and reduces the unknown Ricci-flat case to a compact simply connected irreducible Ricci-flat 4-manifold. Special-holonomy subcases are excluded by topology: flat holonomy gives chi=0; reducible products in dimensions 1--3 are flat/nonexistent in the relevant compact simply connected setting; SU(2)/hyperkähler compact 4-manifolds are flat tori or K3 surfaces, and the simply connected nonflat case is K3 with chi=24, not chi=8. But Berger's holonomy list also leaves generic SO(4) holonomy for irreducible Ricci-flat metrics, and the sources explicitly state that whether compact Ricci-flat manifolds with generic SO(n) holonomy exist is open in dimensions at least 4. Degen's K3 moduli note says classification of closed Ricci-flat 4-manifolds is unsolved and that the only known examples are finitely covered by T^4 or K3; Tony's Ricci-flat/parallel-spinor paper states the Ricci-flat-implies-parallel-spinor/special-holonomy converse is open in general and records the same open generic-holonomy question. Kotschick--Wehrheim likewise frame 'all closed Ricci-flat four-manifolds are covered by K3 or T^4' as a conjectural motivation, not a theorem. Therefore lambda=0 is not banked; only the known/special-holonomy Ricci-flat mechanisms are excluded on the chi=8 rows. This is a negative but important resolution: the fixed-row counterexample route is not one theorem away, even after a hypothetical positive-Einstein alignment theorem.

The step also repaired a sign error in the Step 81 note. In dimension four, for any Einstein metric Ric=lambda g, the Chern--Weil identities depend on lambda squared: int |W|^2 = 8 pi^2 chi - (2/3) lambda^2 Vol and int |W^pm|^2 = 2 pi^2(2 chi +/- 3 tau) - (1/3) lambda^2 Vol. Thus a negative Einstein metric after normalization has the same Weyl-budget form as a positive one with the same |lambda|; the earlier text saying lambda<0 gives int |W|^2 > 64 pi^2 on chi=8 was wrong and has been replaced. This correction matters because CGB alone gives no sign-specific obstruction to negative Einstein metrics on these rows. The alignment engine is sign-locked to lambda>0 because its basic inequality uses the positive scalar term in the self-dual harmonic-form Weitzenbock formula; it becomes vacuous for s<=0. The negative leg remains essentially without a compatible obstruction: the rows are simply connected, positive-scalar-curvature, SW-void by the known mechanisms, non-Kähler and nonsymplectic in both orientations, and strict Hitchin--Thorpe. Existing monopole/Bauer--Furuta/nonexistence tools require precisely the monopole or symplectic/algebraic input that the mixed rows lack.

Therefore no Gram-route computation from this script should be used as evidence yet; it is only a nearly ready artifact requiring a trivial serialization repair before rerun.
