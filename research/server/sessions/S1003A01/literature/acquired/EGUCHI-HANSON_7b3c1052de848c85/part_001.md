TheEguchi-Hansonspace is dieomorphic to the cotangent bundle of the two-sphere and asymptotic to the at cone R4/Z2. It is the simplest example of a Ricci at asymptotically locally euclidean (ALE) manifold and in the physics literature known as a gravitational instanton.
