## Abstract

We show that a compact almost-Kähler four-manifold (M,g,ω) with harmonic self-dual Weyl curvature and constant scalar curvature is Kähler if c1⋅\[ω\]≥0. We also prove an integral curvature inequality for compact almost-Kähler four-manifolds with harmonic self-dual Weyl curvature.

## Introduction

Let (M,ω) be a symplectic four-manifold. Then (M,ω) admits a compatible almost-complex structure _J_, that is, ω(X,Y)=ω(JX,JY) and ω(v,Jv)>0 for any nonzero v∈TM \[12\]. If we define a metric by g(X,Y):=ω(X,JY), then (M,g,ω,J) is called an almost-Kähler structure. (M,ω) has a well-defined first Chern class c1(M,ω). When _J_ is integrable, _ω_ is parallel and this defines a Kähler structure.

If W−=0, then _g_ is called to be self-dual. If ric0=0, then _g_ is called to be Einstein. From the intersection pairing of the de Rham cohomology H2(M,R) on a smooth, compact four-manifold _M_, we get the diagonal matrix. We denote by b± the number of (±1)'s of the diagonal matrix. Then we have b2=b++b− and τ=b+−b−, where b2 is the second betti number of _M_ and _τ_ is the signature of _M_.

A compact almost-Kähler Einstein four-manifold with nonnegative scalar curvature is Kähler \[14\]. More generally, it was shown in \[8\] that a compact almost-Kähler four-manifold with δW+=0 and nonnegative scalar curvature is Kähler with constant scalar curvature.

Using methods in proofs in \[8\], we prove two results. First, we show that a compact almost-Kähler four-manifold with δW+=0 and constant scalar curvature is Kähler if c1⋅\[ω\]≥0. This shows in particular a compact almost-Kähler Einstein four-manifold with c1⋅\[ω\]≥0 is Kähler-Einstein.

This result is not new for Goldberg's conjecture since there is a more general result in \[4\]. On the other hand, our hypothesis δW+=0 is weaker than an Einstein condition.
