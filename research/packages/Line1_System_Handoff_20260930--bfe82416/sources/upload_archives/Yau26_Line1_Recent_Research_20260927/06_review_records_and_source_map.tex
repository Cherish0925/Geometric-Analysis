The bounded-spinor Liouville shortcut was rejected. On the standard
Eguchi--Hanson space X=O(-2), the canonical section s\_Z of E=O(Z), with
\textbar s\_Z\textbar\^{}2=(r\^{}2-a\^{}2)/(r\^{}2+a\^{}2), has an ASD

and twisted-mode background is
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.08453}{Franchetti--Krasnov}.

annuli of radii r\_i where
\textbar Phi\textbar\textgreater=m\textgreater0, put
w\_i=Vol(A\_i)/r\_i\^{}4, f\_i=int\_Ai\textbar F\_\{S+\}\textbar\^{}2
and q\_i=int\_Ai\textbar nabla Phi\textbar\^{}4. Coarea and Holder give
suitable boundary radii with total absolute flux at most C(sum
w\_i)\^{}(1/4){[}m\^{}(-1)(sum f\_i)\^{}(1/2)(sum q\_i)\^{}(1/4)
+m\^{}(-3)(sum q\_i)\^{}(3/4){]}. Thus O(Ah) annular volume-ratio and
curvature budgets plus o(Ah) quartic budget suffice for o(Ah) flux. The
hypotheses were not derived from arbitrary near-Einstein input and are
not silently inserted into I40. The actual proof instead closes the
global spinor budget.
