## 1. The problem and the unit of progress

The working Yau26 statement asks whether sufficiently tight **pointwise**
Ricci pinching, with a threshold depending only on dimension in at least one
sign branch, forces some Einstein metric on the **same smooth manifold**.
The output need not resemble the input or have its sign, symmetry or
complex structure. The original problem source and the project's precise
quantifier convention remain separate source objects.

A successful research task produces a usable theorem, sharpness result or
counterexample, exact realization theorem, or a proved connection between
previously separate mechanisms. Necessary lemmas are legitimate work when
they support such a statement. Neither the number of lemmas nor the number
of rounds measures the scientific increment.

Do not assume that the absence of a known obstruction implies existence.
Do not require a local perturbation theorem as the only path to existence:
compactness preserving smooth type can also prove existence.

## 2. The smallest shared analytic mechanism

The inherited first-line engine has distinct, composable inputs:

1. actual solvability for the selected gauge class, with the required
   auxiliary positive weights and chamber/polarization quantifiers;
2. a self-dual projection budget and the appropriate square/negative-square
   cost, uniform over the selected family;
3. quantitative near-saturation of the relevant curvature functional;
4. bounded actual fields, covariant second-order control, a smooth
   polynomial Weyl surrogate, and weak-Bianchi self-testing.

The last stage can exclude incompatible modes or control allowed modes.
These are different conclusions. The classical inequalities, new estimates,
class-supply arguments and topological applications should stay separately
callable even if one paper organizes them together. A proposed "master
theory" is an organizing aspiration, not a newly proved universal theorem.

[parameterized functional](../mathematics/current/line1_functional_pencil.md),
[actual form composition](../mathematics/current/line1_composed_application.md),
[Round 21 analytic criterion](../mathematics/current/round21_analytic.md).

## 3. Class supply is an independent mathematical module

Ordinary basic classes, actual monopole classes, special classes,
metricwise polarized inputs, Pin-minus twisted inputs and Bauer–Furuta
inputs must not be conflated. Convex-hull vectors do not automatically have
weighted monopole solutions. Multiple classes cannot be replaced by one
formal averaged class without a solvability theorem.

The post-charter baseline already contains a concrete
[Bauer–Furuta application](../mathematics/current/line1_bauer_furuta_geography.md):
connected-sum manifolds with vanishing ordinary SW invariants and no almost
complex structure can still supply actual classes and enter the two-chirality
gap. Thus BF is no longer merely a literature suggestion. The classical
BF/geography input and the project's quantitative application have different
attributions. Broader class systems remain a real research direction.

## 4. Feasibility regions must gain independent information

The lower energy envelope, the opposite-chirality upper budget, and the
later compactified-pencil upper energy wall give a coherent restricted
feasibility problem. They do not classify all Einstein four-manifolds.

Sources: [energy envelope](../mathematics/current/line1_energy_envelope_gap.md),
[two-chirality gap](../mathematics/current/line1_two_chirality_topology.md),
[compactified pencil](../mathematics/current/line1_compactified_pencil.md).

New entropy, representation-volume, Gram, period or characteristic-class
information is useful when it changes the admissible region or the possible
geometry. The existing [rank-one entropy comparison](../mathematics/current/line1_rank_one_entropy_scope.md)
already explains why the ordinary degree/entropy comparison does not supply
the missing independent budget in its stated classical-base family. It does
not rule out all entropy methods or non-Kähler source categories.

## 5. Sharpness and the full equality set

Use actual models: KE, unequal-factor cscK products, complex hyperbolic
surfaces, reducible locally symmetric spaces, orbifold/ALE degenerations,
the genuine shear constructions, dilution examples and collapsing families.
Examples calibrate a precise statement; they are not decorations.

The LeBrun cscK functional cannot unconditionally control traceless Ricci
energy: its complete equality set contains non-Einstein metrics. The
Kähler energy decomposition identifies the anti-self-dual canonical-class
contribution, but that identity is not automatically valid for an arbitrary
monopole class on a non-Kähler manifold. Parameter-dependent constants must
respect real KE equality tests. Sampled numerical maxima are not certified
continuous suprema; a necessary degeneration rate need not be optimal.

## 6. Allowed modes are a separate geometric question

For the specified rank-one surrogate the allowed roots are
\(t=0,-\sigma/2\). This is not a classification of the Weyl spectra of all
Einstein metrics. Near the KE root, improving a form estimate does not itself
produce an obstruction. One needs global integrability, topology,
deformation/realization, or another independent incompatibility.

Retain zeros, eigenline monodromy and the possible double cover in applying
Derdziński-type conclusions. Do not reverse a maximum-eigenvalue theorem
into a minimum-eigenvalue theorem by a change of words. The established
[harmonic-Weyl structure estimate](../mathematics/current/line1_signed_harmonic_weyl.md)
uses a genuine differential equation; general near-Einstein inputs do not
automatically satisfy it.

## 7. From average information to global geometry

Average near-parallelism, harmonic representative proximity, curvature-model
proximity, and a global parallel/Kähler structure are distinct levels of
information. Promising missing data include holonomy, periods, eigenbundle
topology, zero sets, quantitative integrability and global deformation
response. No particular list of tools is compulsory.

The recent [aspherical ALE-core exclusion](../mathematics/current/line1_aspherical_no_bubbles.md)
adds a concrete local-to-global mechanism: a genuine compact core of a
geometric limit cannot embed in the original topology. It treats rational
ball quotients as well as negative-definite cores. It does not assume a
limit fundamental group automatically injects into the original group.

## 8. Compactness and collapse have different present statuses

The integral obstruction mechanism does not assume noncollapse. Its success
does not mean every later existence argument is also collapse-independent.
The exact aspherical no-bubbling theorem controls bounded-diameter,
one-half-Weyl-bounded Einstein families. The new
[compact-regime realization proof](../continuation/20260923_compact_realization.md)
instead handles approximate Ricci equations through harmonic coordinates.

This continuation supplies an explicit theorem-level connection to
Jiang–Naber and Bando–Kasue–Nakajima. It retains diameter, volume and
supercritical one-half-Weyl hypotheses. A dimension-only threshold, arbitrary
collapse, diameter escape, and unrestricted critical curvature concentration
remain outside it. These are possible places for new mathematics, not
conclusions already hidden in the compact theorem.

## 9. Flow remains an optional module

Study defect evolution, propagation of saturation and regularization only
with the actual flow equation and its existence/control hypotheses. Failure
of the flow from one chosen input says nothing by itself about Einstein
existence elsewhere on the manifold. A flow estimate earns a place here when
it completes an obstruction, structure or realization theorem.

## 10. Exact realization is now a concrete program

Gluing, DeTurck, Kuranishi/Lyapunov–Schmidt, KE existence, algebraic smoothing,
moduli compactness and posteriori validation are alternative routes, not one
automatic pipeline. Preserve the second and third lines' actual matching and
global-existence work; the first line need not duplicate it.

The compact-regime theorem is a qualitative exact-existence statement on the
same smooth type, not a constructive Newton algorithm, a universal closeness
estimate, or the full Yau26 dichotomy. It gives a specific positive branch of
the charter's realization program with explicit additional hypotheses.

## 11. Higher-dimensional transfer is a question, not notation

Weak self-testing, polynomial surrogates, actual-field supply, topology versus
defect, two-sided envelopes and equality-mode selection may transfer.
Four-dimensional SW and Weyl chirality do not. A higher-dimensional theorem
requires a real replacement field/equation, a controllable critical budget,
and a topological or geometric positive quantity. Spinors, Yang–Mills,
harmonic tensors and special-holonomy forms are possible inputs only after
those properties are proved.

## 12. Failures change the research choice

Keep the actual counterexample or failed inference with its scope and
correction. The Fable W gluing failure, cscK/KE distinction, failure of
unconditional relative-integral replacement, Yamabe normalization issue,
and budget barriers should prevent repetition, not become endless new
investigations of the same failed proposal. A new mechanism can reopen a
question; a renamed old assumption cannot.

## 13. Maintain three live questions, not three quotas

**A — Obstruction.** Which actual gauge/topology inputs force a uniform
pointwise-input barrier, via a valid integral theorem or another mechanism?

**B — Allowed geometry.** What real structures survive the equations and
near-saturation, and what geometric freedom remains invisible to the present
quantities?

**C — Realization.** Which allowed configurations imply an exact metric on
the same smooth manifold, and which produce a rigorous counterexample?

Choose work by the mathematical judgment it can change. Keep stable papers
stable while exploration proceeds. Do not require a new topology every round,
and do not count a renamed lemma as an expanded theorem. The next research
decision should use the proved reach and genuine escape mechanisms of the
new compact theorem alongside the independent obstruction results—not
another general direction review.
