## Deep-Reasoning

This step resolved the question of whether the 4D Sobolev-energy cancellation extends from the coupled L² energy system to the curvature-potential mechanism needed for closing the cutoff-flow first-exit. The answer is structurally yes: Wang's critical parameter β = A₀²·‖Rm‖_{L^{n/2}} is volume-independent in every dimension (β = C²r²K₀^{2/n}), and in 4D the L² energy IS the critical L^{n/2} exponent, so the available Chern-Weil/maximal-function data directly supplies β = C√ε_Li small. This means the 4D volume cancellation is not a separate miracle but the same mechanism operating at the curvature-potential level rather than the defect-energy level.

The second important finding is the p > 4 threshold for connection and cutoff-Hessian drift integrability. If the weighted defect satisfies |χ²E(t)| ≤ C_pδ(r²/t)^{2/p}, then the companion derivative estimate |χ∇(χ²E)(t)| ≤ C_pδt^{-1/2}(r²/t)^{2/p} is time-integrable if and only if p > 4. The metric drift requires only p > 2; the derivative/connection drift requires the strictly stronger p > 4 condition. For p = 6, both metric drift ~ δθ^{2/3}r² and connection drift ~ δθ^{1/6} are simultaneously O(δ) with dimension-only constants. This separates two genuinely different analytic demands and shows that a single fixed p > 4 (e.g., p = 6) gives simultaneous control of all first-exit quantities.

Independent TTCS verification confirmed both proved results and identified two important corrections. First, the volume cancellation of β is dimension-general, not 4D-specific; what IS 4D-specific is that L² is the critical exponent and Chern-Gauss-Bonnet bounds the mean curvature. Second, Wang's Theorem 6.3 does NOT localize to the finite-neighbor degenerate system by simple restriction: the localization requires an independent treatment of two localization cutoff scales (flow weight χ² and Moser test function η), level-dependent neighbor coupling at each Moser exponent, and evolving measure terms. Li's iteration is local but non-degenerate; Wang's is degenerate but global; the needed hybrid is local AND degenerate.

The TTCS also sharpened the treatment of the normalized-flow correction. The term ∇²(χ²)⊙g from h₂ = -2χ²g is an Rm-independent additive forcing, not merely a lower-order term. For the defect E, the homogeneity C_a(0) = 0 removes this source, but for Rm (needed as curvature potential), the source persists and is controlled only as an O(θK₀V) additive energy over the flow time—absorbed for small θ but requiring explicit treatment.

The current architecture: if both PDE targets (localized degenerate curvature ε-regularity and localized weighted tensor Bernstein) can be proved, the first-exit closes with no circularity. The proof order would be: small energy → Wang/Yang curvature decay → Li curvature potential → defect pointwise estimate → weighted Bernstein derivative → integration at p = 6 → all drift O(δ) → first-exit improved. The volume-independent β removes the collapsed-volume obstruction from the curvature-potential step; the remaining difficulty is purely PDE-localization.

This does NOT solve Yau 26. Even if both targets are proved and the first-exit closes, the result would be: fixed-topology (or Noether-uniform across the standard subclass) negative-pinching gap for critical Ishida-LeBrun 4-manifolds. The unrestricted negative branch, positive branch, dimensions n ≥ 5, dimension-only quantifiers, and Lean formalization remain entirely open.
