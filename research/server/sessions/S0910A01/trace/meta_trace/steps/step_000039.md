5. **Terminal recalibration** (Step 28): The SW inequality applies to the terminal metric (topological invariant), and D_a(T)≤Q(T)/(8π²) gives the combined calibration density ∫𝔇≤119Q(T).

7. **Derdziński contradiction** (Step 8): The smooth Einstein germ with the calibrated spectrum (−1/6,1/12,1/12) is locally Kähler by Derdziński Proposition 5, but Kähler–Einstein with Ric=−g has |W⁺|²=2/3≠1/24.

The single remaining hypothesis (H1) is the per-datum transported-coefficient DeTurck existence and restart for ∂_tg=−2χ²(Ric+g). The broader significance: this theorem shows that the Ishida–LeBrun manifolds (which are known to have no Einstein metric of any sign) also cannot have arbitrarily tight negative Ricci pinching. This is consistent with a positive answer to Yau Problem 26 — but it addresses only one sign branch, one family, in one dimension. The positive branch, general four-manifold topology, dimensions n≥5, dimension-only constants, and Lean formalization all remain open.

A02's positive toric work (#353) corrected the m=6 eigenvalue probe to a 1.84% defect (down from the false 68.7%), suggesting the constant-curvature diagonal sector is a near-miss rather than a strong obstruction. The natural next step is to discharge (H1) by writing the explicit Yang adaptation, making the theorem unconditional.
