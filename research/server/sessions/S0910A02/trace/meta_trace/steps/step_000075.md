## Deep-Reasoning

This Agent3 Execute step corrected the meaning of the mixed-row computation and turned the next obstruction calculation from an open-ended Green-machinery program into a smaller symmetry- and weight-filtered problem. The exact Yau--26 target remains unchanged: a full resolution must prove or refute the dimension-only Ricci-pinching-to-Einstein proposition for every closed connected smooth manifold in every dimension, and none of the current four-dimensional local models can be upgraded to that statement without controlling far-away Einstein metrics and collapse. The Step-74 tower-impossibility theorem is therefore a guardrail: since higher Biquard obstructions are subcritical in the Ricci operator norm, small \(\delta\) cannot force their vanishing. Thus a computed second obstruction on the \((S^2\times S^2)/\mathbb Z_2\) mixed rows has real but narrower decision value: vanishing is evidence that the local desingularization route remains viable for this product geometry, while nonvanishing says this local nearby-orbifold route cannot decide these manifolds and a genuinely global existence mechanism is needed. It would not prove tower rigidity, and it would not produce a Yau--26 counterexample, because Einstein nonexistence on these PSC, finite-\(\pi_1\), strict-Hitchin--Thorpe manifolds is not supplied by any obstruction in the record.

The main new structural content is the finite symmetry and representation ledger for the four \(A_1\) nodes. The nodes form a \(2\times2\) pole grid, and the orientation-preserving row flip, column flip, and factor swap generate a dihedral group of order 8. Composing an orientation-preserving symmetry with the orientation-reversing chirality-exchange reflection \(c(z,w)=(\bar z,w)\) gives a true pattern symmetry exactly when the orientation-preserving part carries the reversed-node set to its complement. The executable check confirms the Strategist's correction: no such complementer exists for \(r=1\) or \(r=3\) by cardinality, while both balanced \(r=2\) classes are self-conjugate. The adjacent balanced class has stabilizer size 2; the diagonal balanced class has stabilizer size 4, giving an extra order-four bug-check and a smaller unknown system. This means the obstruction computation should treat exactly three independent pattern cases: \(r=1\sim r=3\), balanced adjacent, and balanced diagonal. The older single \(r=2\) bucket is mathematically too coarse.

The product spin-lift calculation gives a plausible resolution of the Step-64 open bit: in the canonical product spin lift of the half-turn \(\rho(z,w)=(-z,-w)\), the fixed-point signs are the product of the north/south lift signs on the two factors, hence the distinguished opposite-sign pair is diagonal up to global lift convention. Combined with the Step-64 seam rule, this identifies the diagonal balanced pattern as the spin/Freedman \(\#_3(S^2\times S^2)\) row and the adjacent balanced pattern as the non-spin \(\#_3\mathbb{CP}^2\#3\overline{\mathbb{CP}}{}^2\) row. This is important because it attaches the collapse stress-family topology to the diagonal obstruction system. However, because this uses a product spin-lift normalization and boundary spin convention rather than a full Kirby/source-pinned proof, it should be audited before it becomes load-bearing final topology.

The second structural filter is local \(T^2\)-isotropy at poles. A pole value or second jet can only see spherical/spin-weight channels with \(|m_a|\le2\) in each \(S^2\) factor, and the quotient half-turn imposes even parity \(m_1+m_2\equiv0\pmod2\). More sharply, the EH kernel-product block \(\ker R_+\otimes\ker R_-\) has characters exactly \((\pm2,0)\) and \((0,\pm2)\). Thus the multipole-to-second-jet Green computation is not a generic tensor-harmonic sum over all components: the scalar value channel and mixed odd channels are excluded from the load-bearing EH block except insofar as trace/gauge pieces enter the four non-TT scalar directions that must be sliced. The rule is not a finite \(l\)-truncation: infinitely many harmonics with those few characters remain. But it changes the next computation from a sprawling tensor program into a few equivariant channel Green problems with strict symmetry tests.

The collapse half was also sharpened, but not closed. Positive pinching with \(\chi>0\) and \(\operatorname{Vol}\to0\) forces curvature concentration, yet a bare volume-collapse argument does not rule out inner ALE bubbles: after rescaling at curvature scale \(r_i=|\mathrm{Rm}|^{-1/2}\), fixed-radius ALE balls have Euclidean volume ratio in the rescaled metric while their original volumes are \(O(r_i^4)\), compatible with total volume tending to zero. Bishop--Gromov allows the volume ratio to fall on larger scales. What collapse should force is a volume-collapsed outermost concentration or transition model unless there is a controlled annulus preserving positive four-dimensional volume ratio from the ALE end to the ambient scale. Therefore the ADE quantum gate remains an inner-bubble diagnostic, not the collapse theorem; the sharper collapsed-front target is an outermost ALF/ALG/ALH/APS transition ledger or a proof that any ALE pieces are children inside a volume-collapsed parent model. This prevents skipping the collapse half while making its next analytic obligation more precise.
