Pure finite-volume real-hyperbolic four-pieces have positive Euler characteristic, with Vol₋₁(P)=4π²χ(P)/3, and gluing along flat three-manifolds preserves the sum of their Euler characteristics. Imposing the closed χ=0 formula on the connector deleted precisely this hyperbolic contribution. The value 3(√3−1)/2 was already the Step 2/64 lower bound for closed four-manifolds with nonpositive Euler characteristic, and equating separate scalar and trace-free Ricci estimates did not establish geometric attainment or sharpness.

The useful replacement is an exact relative Chern–Gauss–Bonnet identity. If N is the union of regions replacing exact hyperbolic cusp tails of total volume Vrem, the complement remains sectional-curvature −1, VN=Vol(N), and E=Ric+3g with e=tr E, then
2∫N|W|²+12(VN−Vrem)=∫N(|E°|²+2e−e²/12).
For Ricci eigenvalue errors xi∈[−δ,δ], the quadratic part is Σxi²−(Σxi)²/3, whose absolute value is at most 4δ², while |2Σxi|≤8δ. Hence the absolute left side is at most (8δ+4δ²)VN. A vanishing-defect sequence must therefore satisfy Vrem−VN=(1/6)∫N|W|²+o(VN). This is a genuine global compatibility relation: volume deficit pays for Weyl energy. It is not automatically obstructive because VN may be smaller than Vrem, and satisfying it does not solve the horizontal, shape, or charge equations.

An explicit pure-piece laboratory was identified from the Ratcliffe–Tschantz one-cusped 24-cell manifolds. The orientable double cover 24cdc1.1 has one T³ cusp, χ=2, hyperbolic volume 8π²/3, maximum-cusp volume 16, b₁=1, and b₃=0. Thus the cusp-section inclusion has positive rational H₁ image, closing the old Step 70 peripheral-rank question for this piece. A primitive character nonzero on the cusp group yields, for degrees N coprime to its peripheral multiplicity, one-cusp cyclic covers with peripheral type (N,1,1). Two such pieces can be paired through an orientation-compatible affine torus gluing to form the intended pure-piece graph family, subject to direct verification of the precise Di Cerbo hypotheses and gluing data.

For each fixed dimensionless connector profile, a degree-N cusp cover truncated at T=log N+T₀ has interface three-volume and removed-tail volume proportional to N⁻². The rank-two quotient of the charged Koiso connector is locally isometric before quotienting and likewise multiplies connector volume, Weyl energy, and every local defect integral by N⁻². The executable calculation verifies this common scaling to relative spread below 2.3×10⁻¹⁵. Consequently lattice collapse alone creates neither a contradiction nor a construction: after division by connector volume, the relative Chern–Gauss–Bonnet equation is independent of N. In the actual vanishing-defect regime the half-length R must also tend to infinity; isotropic endpoint expansion changes the matching depth to T=log N−R+O(1), so one must choose N much larger than e^R and retain the R-dependent dimensionless volumes. This joint asymptotic relation is the next global refinement.

The corrected global bridge strengthens the research without replacing the unresolved center-to-expansion problem. It supplies a precise observable that can distinguish a persistent geometric mismatch from a poor interpolant, while the current evidence shows no such mismatch. The faithful full-matrix construction still needs a resolution-continuable transition with complete Ricci defect tending to zero, and the positive branch and Lean formalization remain independent unfinished parts of the highest goal.
