# Weyl energy and connected sums of four-manifolds

Scuola Normale Superiore

AM

Andrea Malchiodi

FM

Francesco Malizia

## Abstract

Given two closed, oriented Riemannian four-manifolds $\left(\right. M , g_{M} \left.\right)$(M,g M​) and $\left(\right. Z , g_{Z} \left.\right)$(Z,g Z​), which are not locally conformally flat and not both self-dual or both anti-self-dual, we prove that there exists a metric $g_{Y}$g Y​ on the connected sum $Y \cong M \# Z$Y≅M#Z such that the Weyl energy of $g_{Y}$g Y​ is strictly smaller than the sum of Weyl energies of $g_{M}$g M​ and $g_{Z}$g Z​.
