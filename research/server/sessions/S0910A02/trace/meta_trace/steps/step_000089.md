## Deep-Reasoning

This Agent4 Execute step functioned as an adversarial audit of the new negative-branch Sambusetti/BCG and A1-hyperbolic-orbifold route. The important correction is that the route is more delicate, and also more alive, than Step 88 recorded. Step 88 correctly established the BCG/CGB additive threshold for N#N and the warped-product waist barrier, but its §4 topological shortcut was wrong: it treated the negative mirror resolution as an infinite coinvariant quotient, contradicting the older Step 57 van Kampen proof that every sign-pattern filling of (Σ2×Σ2)/<ι×ι> is simply connected. The older result is the one to keep. The A1 cap kills the RP3 peripheral Z/2; with 36 fixed points one is not merely taking a single coinvariant quotient, but imposing the normal closures of all point stabilizers. In the Kummer-like situation these extra relations annihilate the remaining H1/2H1 data. Therefore the negative mirror is entropy-invisible not because its resolution has a mysterious infinite group without a hyperbolic target, but because the resolution collapses the relevant fundamental group completely. This is not a minor cleanup: the same mistake would make any future simplicial-volume argument falsely look like an instant refutation of P4-. The correct lesson is that positive relative simplicial volume in the outside orbifold piece can be destroyed by compressible amenable RP3 seams; additivity/superadditivity needs the correct compatibility or π1-injectivity hypotheses, and A1 fillings violate exactly those hypotheses by killing the boundary group. This seam-compression mechanism is now a standing caution for all negative A1-orbifold work.

The second major insight is that the hyperbolic-orbifold existence question has split into two independent layers. The purely local/geometric question — whether a closed hyperbolic 4-manifold can have an orientation-preserving isometric involution whose fixed set is finite and nonempty, with no 2-dimensional fixed components — appears to be answered yes by the Davis hyperbolic 4-manifold sources. Ratcliffe--Tschantz's Ĝ-index paper says the orientation-preserving isometry group of the Davis manifold has many classes with only isolated fixed points; specifically the antipodal involution represented by diag(-1,-1,-1,-1,1) has 122 isolated fixed points, and the involution ασ has 10 isolated fixed points, while other involutions such as σ and diag(1,1,-1,-1,1) have totally geodesic genus-4 surface fixed sets. This means closed real-hyperbolic Einstein 4-orbifolds with only A1 point singularities are not hypothetical. The near-pinched construction theorem should therefore apply to their A1 resolutions after normalizing Ric=-g. However, this does not refute P4- yet, because the Sambusetti certificate requires a nonzero-degree map from the resolved smooth manifold to a closed real-hyperbolic 4-manifold and a violation of the entropy/Euler-signature inequality. The quotient map runs from the hyperbolic manifold to the orbifold, not from the resolution to the hyperbolic cover; after resolving the fixed points, Armstrong/van Kampen replaces the orbifold group by the semidirect product quotient modulo all fixed-point stabilizers. With many isolated fixed points, this quotient may again collapse enough of the lattice to destroy any degree map or positive simplicial volume. Thus the sharp frontier is no longer existence of A1 hyperbolic orbifolds, but the topology of their resolutions: compute π1, H1, simplicial volume proxies, and possible nonzero-degree maps for the Davis involutions and for lower-node arithmetic examples if available.

The local fixed-set structure also became clearer. An orientation-preserving order-2 linear isotropy in SO(4) has eigenvalue type either (-1,-1,-1,-1), giving an isolated A1 point, or (-1,-1,+1,+1), giving a codimension-2 fixed surface; both occur in explicit hyperbolic 4-manifold symmetries. Hence a search for point symmetries is not enough: one must exclude all elements in the chosen quotient group whose fixed set contributes codimension-2 strata, or work with a specific manifold/involution pair where the source already confirms only isolated points. The Davis α and ασ examples satisfy this local requirement, but their many fixed points are precisely the regime where the stabilizer relations may be too strong. The Strategist's single-fixed-point arithmetic ideal remains valuable because fewer nodes means fewer extra relations; nevertheless no source or construction proving a one-node example was obtained in this step.

This step did not solve, refute, or formalize Yau--26. It corrected a load-bearing false criterion, produced a small reproducible audit file for the corrected group and local-linear bookkeeping, and found a concrete source-supported hyperbolic A1-orbifold test family. The negative branch now has three complementary fronts: (i) N#N has all-sign no-Einstein by Sambusetti but only an additive O(1/χ) pinching obstruction and warped/nonwarped connector difficulties; (ii) known A1 constructions give δ→0 near-negative metrics, and hyperbolic A1 orbifolds now exist, but their resolutions may lose the entropy certificate through stabilizer compression; (iii) A03's exact/forced OT Bochner program, newly corrected in message #621, blocks exact reflected OT connectors and leaves non-warped forced connectors as a separate analytic frontier. The positive branch is untouched by these entropy mechanisms and remains tied to the mixed-row/Ozuch-desingularization program, where A01's AB-sector sign is useful but not yet a global Einstein existence or nonexistence theorem.
