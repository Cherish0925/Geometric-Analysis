## Deep-Reasoning

This step changed the smoothing route from an announced closure into a precise and substantially stronger analytic program. The Step 13 inner/outer energy inequality was not closed: an estimate of the form \(Y'_{\rm inner}\le Cr^{-2}Y_{\rm outer}\) does not yield Gronwall control of \(Y_{\rm inner}\), and persistence of the small normalized curvature energy during the flow had not been established. The previous numerical absorption factors tested only algebra. Those statements were explicitly superseded in the research record.

Ye Li's Corollary 3.5 supplies the correct defect-propagation mechanism once its dynamic hypotheses are available. For the normalized Einstein defect \(E=\operatorname{Ric}+g\), the exact evolution remains
\[
\partial_tE=\Delta_LE-2E,\qquad
(\partial_t-\Delta)|E|^2=-2|\nabla E|^2+4\operatorname{Rm}(E,E).
\]
Regularizing \(|E|\) by \(u_\eta=(|E|^2+\eta)^{1/2}\) gives
\[
(\partial_t-\Delta)u_\eta\le2|\operatorname{Rm}|u_\eta.
\]
After the exact conversion from normalized to unnormalized Ricci flow, Corollary 3.5 applies to the corresponding tensor \(H=\operatorname{Ric}_{\bar g}+(1+2s)^{-1}\bar g\). Its four-dimensional Sobolev coefficient satisfies \(A\lesssim r^2/\operatorname{Vol}(B_r)^{1/2}\). This cancels the volume both in the critical curvature-energy input and in the initial \(L^3\) defect term, yielding the sharp conditional estimate
\[
|E(x,t)|\le C\delta r^{4/3}t^{-2/3}=C\delta(r^2/t)^{2/3}.
\]
The singularity is integrable, so the normalized metric drift is at most \(C\delta r^{4/3}t^{1/3}\), hence \(O(\delta r^2)\) at time \(t=\theta r^2\). This is collapse-uniform and avoids the divergent pointwise \(C/t\) Gronwall argument.

The source scope is now clear. Li's Theorem 3.1 explicitly assumes small critical energy for every centre on the complete manifold, and Theorem 3.6 inherits that quantifier when it restates the theorem using normalized energy. Li's proof propagates curvature energy, Sobolev inequalities, metric equivalence, and an \(L^3\) curvature-potential estimate through a supremum over centres and bounded-overlap coverings. A maximal-function good point supplies the initial all-centres condition only inside a buffered region. Thus the defect estimate is proved under Li's global setting or under an independently constructed local flow carrying the same dynamic structure, but the published theorem does not by itself construct that collapsed local flow. The printed \(\phi^2\) in Corollary 3.5 is incompatible with its undefined notation, Theorem 2.5, and Li's subsequent use for ordinary Ricci flow; the source-consistent version is the ordinary subsolution inequality without that factor.

A normalized cutoff flow on the original closed manifold now provides the most coherent localization interface. For \(a=\chi^2\) and \(\partial_tg=-2aE\), direct variation gives
\[
\partial_tE=a(\Delta_LE-2E)+\mathcal C_a(E),
\]
where every term of \(\mathcal C_a(E)\) contains \(E\), \(\operatorname{tr}E\), or their first derivatives. Therefore an exact negative Einstein metric is stationary even in the transition annulus; no collar source independent of \(E\) appears. In the integrated defect energy, contracted Bianchi and integration by parts remove all Hessians of \(a\). The identity \(|d(\chi^2)|^2/\chi^2=4|d\chi|^2\) prevents a \(1/\chi\) loss, leaving only multiplicative transition terms such as \(|d\chi|^2|E|^2\). This homogeneity was checked symbolically in `workspace/scripts/verify_cutoff_defect_energy.py` with exit code 0.

Yang-Wang local Ricci flow theory confirms that compactly supported equations \(\partial_tg=-2\chi^2\operatorname{Ric}\) exist and possess critical curvature-energy estimates, while Fei He's work states that parallel normalized arguments use \(\operatorname{Ric}-\lambda g\). These sources support the architecture but do not yet furnish the exact theorem needed here: Wang assumes a global Sobolev coefficient and global small \(L^{n/2}\) energy on the cutoff support, while He assumes pointwise full-curvature scales and initial derivatives of the Ricci defect. The present sequence instead has local normalized energy on buffered good regions and no initial full-curvature or derivative bound. The remaining analytic object is consequently a bounded-overlap, collapse-uniform curvature-energy bootstrap for \(\partial_tg=-2\chi^2(\operatorname{Ric}+g)\), not further numerical testing on exact Einstein examples.

Localization and calibrated Weyl information must remain coupled. A cutoff flow on the original closed topology is preferable to completion or doubling because a global \(O(\delta_i)\) defect estimate lets the Ishida-LeBrun Chern-Weil and monopole near-saturation argument be rerun on the evolved closed metric without additive collar error. Yet global small calibrated deficit alone does not guarantee low normalized deficit in one preselected collapsed ball. A successful construction should smooth a positive-volume portion of the joint curvature/deficit good set, with bounded overlap and homogeneous global defect propagation, so the global deficit can select a bounded-curvature region with low local normalized deficit. After that, collapsed bounded-curvature reconstruction remains a separate geometric problem; bounded magnetic Weyl data does not control all quotient coefficients, and bounded sectional curvature alone does not create a noncollapsed harmonic chart. The complete Yau Problem 26, the positive branch, dimensions at least five, and Lean formalization remain far beyond this four-dimensional critical negative-branch advance.
