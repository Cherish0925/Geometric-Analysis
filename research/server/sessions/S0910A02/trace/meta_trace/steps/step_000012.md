## Deep-Reasoning

Step 12 redirected the Execute work from the non-executable Li-smoothing check to the toric determinant/orbit-space mechanism identified by the Strategist. The central insight from Step 11 was that the opposite-warping T²-invariant ansatz is not only a Calderón-Zygmund regularity counterexample; it is also the natural reduced laboratory for the four-dimensional positive volume-gap conjecture behind Proposition A. I therefore performed exact symbolic jet computations for general local T²-invariant four-metrics of the form g = g_B + h_IJ(dphi^I + A^I)(dphi^J + A^J), with orbit-area density w = sqrt(det h). The computation materially sharpened the proposed mechanism. In a gauge with base geodesic orthonormal coordinates, h = I, and connection curvature F^I = f_I dx∧dy, the vertical trace satisfies tr_h Ric^V = -Δ_B w / w + |F|_h²/2. Thus Ric = lambda g implies Δ_B w = (|F|²/2 - 2 lambda) w. The clean equation Δw = -2w is exactly the positive Einstein, twist-free case; twisting does not cancel automatically but contributes a nonnegative vertical-curvature term. A second symbolic mixed-Ricci calculation in the gauge A_x = 0, A_y = f x gave Ric_xI = 1/2 ∂_y(w h_IJ f_J) and Ric_yI = -1/2 ∂_x(w h_IJ f_J), i.e. the conserved twist equation d(w h_IJ *_B F^J)=0. This identifies the real bridge: for smooth compact Einstein four-manifolds with T² fixed-point/edge degeneration, smooth closure should force the conserved twist constants to vanish, hence orthogonal transitivity and F=0 on the regular orbit space. In free collapse there is no degeneration boundary, so this forcing disappears; this matches the lens/Sasaki escape mechanism and explains why simple connectivity/fixed points matter. For C⁰ pinched metrics, the same equations become approximate equations, so the hard object is a quantitative approximate-transitivity and rod-boundary stability theorem, not pointwise curvature regularity alone.

In the twist-free diagonal reduction g = g_B + p² dtheta² + q² dphi², the standard equations give Δ(pq) = -2 lambda pq; for Ric = g, w = pq is a positive first Dirichlet eigenfunction of the orbit space with eigenvalue 2 and boundary determined by circle degeneration. The divergence equation also gives div(w grad v)=0 for p = exp(v) sqrt(w), q = exp(-v) sqrt(w), showing that the Step 11 opposite-warping freedom is not an exact Einstein deformation: it is a C⁰-Ricci regularity counterexample because it uses Hessian cancellation without satisfying the full exact reduced Einstein system. I tested the simplest rectangular separated rod model p = (Lx/pi) sin(pi x/Lx), q = (Ly/pi) sin(pi y/Ly). The eigenvalue condition pi²/Lx² + pi²/Ly² = 2 pins the scale, and AM-GM gives Vol_4 ≥ 16 pi², equality at the product S²×S². Aspect-ratio degeneration increases volume rather than producing collapse. This is strong evidence that the determinant-eigenfunction mechanism sees the volume gap in the invariant class, but it is not a proof for arbitrary orbit spaces. A symbolic edge expansion near a p=0 edge showed the remaining freedom: with base metric dr² + a(r,s)² ds² and a = 1 + a2(s) r²/2 + ..., the leading equations give p3 = -a2/3 - 1/6, q2 = a2 Q/2, and Q'' + (1+2a2)Q = 0. The edge curvature coefficient a2 is a Weyl/sectional degree of freedom, not controlled by Ricci alone. Large rod curvature could potentially make transverse circle lengths turn over rapidly, so the full invariant volume gap still requires either a global rod-geometry estimate or a reduced counterexample exploiting this freedom.

The step also repaired a cross-session characteristic-class normalization before the negative Taub-NUT/Pontryagin work is used. For M = X#k CPbar² with A = c1²(X) = 2 chi(X) + 3 tau(X), the tangent Pontryagin integral is ∫p1(TM)=3 tau(M)=A - 2 chi(X) - 3k, typically negative near k ~ A/3. But the calibrated LeBrun/Derdzinski Chern-Weil density used by A01/A03 is for Λ²_+, whose integral is 2 chi(M)+3 tau(M)=A-k ~ 2A/3, positive in the chosen orientation. Thus the magnitude of the diffuse charge remains, but the sign and bundle must be named explicitly; residue comparisons using an unnamed p1 are unsafe. Peer messages #252-#254 also made clear that standard Taub-NUT-AdS is not a calibrated finite-residue bulk model: core-containing balls have divergent mean curvature energy, smaller annular covers unwrap and limit to hyperbolic space with macroscopic calibrated defect, and the surviving negative-branch target is diffuse non-basic mixed Λ²_+ curvature rather than counting O(A) NUT residues.

This step did not prove P_4^+, refute it, or touch the all-dimensional theorem directly. It produced a sharper exact reduced system and evidence that positive scale pinning is a genuine geometric mechanism in fixed-point torus collapse, distinct from Ricci-flat semi-flat collapse and free lens/Sasaki collapse. The most valuable next work is to verify the symbolic reductions independently, prove the orthogonal-transitivity lemma from the conserved twist equation and rod smoothness, derive a stable C⁰-pinched version, and then attack the edge/rod volume lower bound or construct a reduced counterexample. Literature retrieval for toric Einstein classification/reduction was attempted but returned no usable new object; a grep found related local rigidity, Calderbank-Pedersen selfdual Einstein torus-symmetry, Page/Chen-LeBrun-Weber, and ambitoric/Hermitian references, but no source-grade theorem was read strongly enough to cite as closing the present reduced problem.
