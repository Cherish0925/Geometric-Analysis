[Part 4/4 of: CRITICAL METRICS ON CONNECTED SUMS OF EINSTEIN FOUR-MANIFOLDS]

Lemma 12.2. Fix an orthonormal basis $\left\{v_{1}, \ldots, v_{4}\right\}$ of $V$, and let $W_{i k j \ell}$ (resp., $\widetilde{W}_{i \ell j k}$ ) denote the components of $W$ (resp., $\widetilde{W}$ ) with respect to this basis. Then
$$\begin{aligned}
W_{i k j \ell} \widetilde{W}_{i \ell j k} & =2\left\{\lambda \tilde{\lambda}+\mu \tilde{\mu}+\nu \tilde{\nu}+\lambda^{-} \tilde{\lambda}^{-}+\mu^{-} \tilde{\mu}^{-}+\nu^{-} \tilde{\nu}^{-}\right\} \\
& =2\langle W, \widetilde{W}\rangle=\frac{1}{2} W_{i k j \ell} \widetilde{W}_{i k j \ell} .
\end{aligned}$$
In particular, the answer is independent of the choice of basis.
Proof. By (12.32),
$$\begin{aligned}
4 W_{i k j \ell} \widetilde{W}_{i \ell j k}= & \left\{\lambda \omega_{i k} \omega_{j \ell}+\mu \eta_{i k} \eta_{j \ell}+\nu \theta_{i k} \theta_{j \ell}\right. \\
& \left.+\lambda^{-} \omega_{i k}^{-} \omega_{j \ell}^{-}+\mu^{-} \eta_{i k}^{-} \eta_{j \ell}^{-}+\nu^{-} \theta_{i k}^{-} \theta_{j \ell}^{-}\right\} \\
\times & \left\{\tilde{\lambda} \omega_{i \ell} \omega_{j k}+\tilde{\mu} \eta_{i \ell} \eta_{j k}+\tilde{\nu} \theta_{i \ell} \theta_{j k}\right. \\
& \left.+\tilde{\lambda}^{-} \omega_{i \ell}^{-} \omega_{j k}^{-}+\tilde{\mu}^{-} \eta_{i \ell}^{-} \eta_{j k}^{-}+\tilde{\nu}^{-} \theta_{i \ell}^{-} \theta_{j k}^{-}\right\} .
\end{aligned}$$
As we multiply and distribute we see that there are six kinds of terms, which we represent schematically as
$$\begin{array}{ll}
\left(A_{i k} A_{j \ell}\right) \cdot\left(A_{i \ell} A_{j k}\right), & \left(A_{i k}^{-} A_{j \ell}^{-} \cdot\left(A_{i \ell}^{-} A_{j k}^{-}\right)\right. \\
\left(A_{i k} A_{j \ell}\right) \cdot\left(B_{i \ell} B_{j k}\right), & \left(A_{i k}^{-} A_{j \ell}^{-}\right) \cdot\left(B_{i \ell}^{-} B_{j k}^{-}\right) \\
\left(A_{i k} A_{j \ell}\right) \cdot\left(C_{i \ell}^{-} C_{j k}^{-}\right), & \left(A_{i k}^{-} A_{j \ell}^{-}\right) \cdot\left(C_{i \ell} C_{j k}\right),
\end{array}$$
where $A, B, C$ are self-dual and $A^{-}, B^{-}$, and $C^{-}$are anti-self-dual. Using the multiplication rules in (12.33) and (12.34), we find
$$\begin{array}{cl}
\left(A_{i k} A_{j \ell}\right) \cdot\left(A_{i \ell} A_{j k}\right)=4, & \left(A_{i k}^{-} A_{j \ell}^{-} \cdot\left(A_{i \ell}^{-} A_{j k}^{-}\right)=4,\right. \\
\left(A_{i k} A_{j \ell}\right) \cdot\left(B_{i \ell} B_{j k}\right)=-4, & \left(A_{i k}^{-} A_{j \ell}^{-}\right) \cdot\left(B_{\ell \ell}^{-} B_{j k}^{-}\right)=-4, \\
\left(A_{i k} A_{j \ell}\right) \cdot\left(C_{i \ell}^{-} C_{j k}^{-}\right)=-4, & \left(A_{i k}^{-} A_{j \ell}^{-}\right) \cdot\left(C_{i \ell} C_{j k}\right)=-4 .
\end{array}$$
Therefore, after multiplying out and collecting all the terms in (12.37), we find
$$\begin{aligned}
4 W_{i k j \ell} \widetilde{W}_{i \ell j k} & =4 \lambda(\tilde{\lambda}-\tilde{\mu}-\tilde{\nu})+4 \lambda\left(-\tilde{\lambda}^{-}-\tilde{\mu}^{-}-\tilde{\nu}^{-}\right) \\
& +4 \mu(-\tilde{\lambda}+\tilde{\mu}-\tilde{\nu})+4 \mu\left(-\tilde{\lambda}^{-}-\tilde{\mu}^{-}-\tilde{\nu}^{-}\right) \\
& +4 \nu(-\tilde{\lambda}-\tilde{\mu}+\tilde{\nu})+4 \nu\left(-\tilde{\lambda}^{-}-\tilde{\mu}^{-}-\tilde{\nu}^{-}\right) \\
& +4 \lambda^{-}(-\tilde{\lambda}-\tilde{\mu}-\tilde{\nu})+4 \lambda^{-}\left(\tilde{\lambda}^{-}-\tilde{\mu}^{-}-\tilde{\nu}^{-}\right) \\
& +4 \mu^{-}(-\tilde{\lambda}-\tilde{\mu}-\tilde{\nu})+4 \mu^{-}\left(-\lambda^{-}+\mu^{-}-\nu^{-}\right) \\
& +4 \nu^{-}(-\tilde{\lambda}-\tilde{\mu}-\tilde{\nu})+4 \nu^{-}\left(-\tilde{\lambda}^{-}-\tilde{\mu}^{-}+\tilde{\nu}^{-}\right) .
\end{aligned}$$
By (12.31), this gives
$$\begin{aligned}
4 W_{i k j \ell} \widetilde{W}_{i \ell j k} & =8\left\{\lambda \tilde{\lambda}+\mu \tilde{\mu}+\nu \tilde{\nu}+\lambda^{-} \tilde{\lambda}^{-}+\mu^{-} \tilde{\mu}^{-}+\nu^{-} \tilde{\nu}^{-}\right\} \\
& =8\langle W, \widetilde{W}\rangle
\end{aligned}$$
and (12.36) follows. \(\square\)

To compute the values of $t_{0}$, we note that in the coordinate system $\left\{z^{i}\right\}$ given in Section 2, letting
$$\begin{aligned}
& \omega^{ \pm}=e^{1} \wedge e^{2} \pm e^{3} \wedge e^{4} \\
& \eta^{ \pm}=e^{1} \wedge e^{3} \mp e^{2} \wedge e^{4} \\

\end{aligned}$$
with $\left(e^{1}, e^{3}, e^{3}, e^{4}\right)=\left(d z^{1}, d z^{2}, d z^{3}, d z^{4}\right)$, we have
$$\begin{aligned}
W^{+}\left(g_{F S}\right) & =\operatorname{diag}(R / 6,-R / 12,-R / 12)=\operatorname{diag}(4,-2,-2), \\
W^{-}\left(g_{F S}\right) & =\operatorname{diag}(0,0,0), \\
W^{+}\left(g_{S^{2} \times S^{2}}\right) & =\operatorname{diag}(R / 6,-R / 12,-R / 12)=\operatorname{diag}(2 / 3,-1 / 3,-1 / 3), \\
W^{-}\left(g_{S^{2} \times S^{2}}\right) & =\operatorname{diag}(2 / 3,-1 / 3,-1 / 3) .
\end{aligned}$$
In case (i), since $\operatorname{mass}\left(\hat{g}_{F S}\right)=2$,
$$t_{0}=\frac{-1}{6 \cdot 24 \cdot 2} 4(16+4+4)=-\frac{1}{3} .$$
In case (ii) with a Burns metric attached, we have
$$t_{0}=\frac{-1}{6 \cdot 4 \cdot 2} 4(4 \cdot(2 / 3)+2 \cdot(1 / 3)+2 \cdot(1 / 3))=-\frac{1}{3} .$$
Case (v) has the same value as this.
In case (ii) with a Green's function $S^{2} \times S^{2}$ attached,
$$\begin{aligned}
t_{0} & =\frac{-1}{6 \cdot 24 \cdot \operatorname{mass}\left(\hat{g}_{S^{2} \times S^{2}}\right)} 4((2 / 3) \cdot 4+(1 / 3) \cdot 2+(1 / 3) \cdot 2) \\
& =-\frac{1}{9 \cdot \operatorname{mass}\left(\hat{g}_{S^{2} \times S^{2}}\right)}

In case (iii),
$$\begin{aligned}
t_{0} & =\frac{-1}{6 \cdot 4 \cdot \operatorname{mass}\left(\hat{g}_{S^{2} \times S^{2}}\right)}\left((2 / 3)^{2}+(1 / 3)^{2}+(1 / 3)^{2}+(2 / 3)^{2}+(1 / 3)^{2}+(1 / 3)^{2}\right) \\
& =-\frac{2}{9 \cdot \operatorname{mass}\left(\hat{g}_{S^{2} \times S^{2}}\right)}
\end{aligned}$$
Case (iv) has the same value of $t_{0}$ as does case (iii).
All other cases are computed similarly as the above cases, so it is not necessary to write every case here. We only need mention the fact that in all non-orientable cases, the answer does not depend on choice of local orientation.

\section*{Appendix A. Proof of Proposition 8.5}

To prove the proposition, we use the expansion of the metric in AF coordinates,
$$g_{\mu \nu}=\delta_{\mu \nu}-\frac{1}{3} R_{\mu k \nu \ell}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k} x^{\ell}}{|x|^{4}}+\frac{2 A}{|x|^{2}} \delta_{\mu \nu}+O\left(|x|^{-3}\right) .$$

In terms of the Christoffel symbols, the Ricci tensor is given by
$$\begin{aligned}
R_{i j} & =\partial_{m} \Gamma_{i j}^{m}-\partial_{i} \Gamma_{j m}^{m}+\Gamma * \Gamma \\
& =-\frac{1}{2} g^{m p}\left\{\partial_{m} \partial_{p} g_{i j}-\partial_{m} \partial_{i} g_{j p}-\partial_{m} \partial_{j} g_{i p}+\partial_{i} \partial_{j} g_{m p}\right\}+\partial g * \partial g
\end{aligned}$$
By the expansion (A.1),
$$g^{m p}=\delta_{m p}+O\left(|x|^{-2}\right),$$
hence
$$\begin{aligned}
R_{i j} & =-\frac{1}{2}\left\{\delta_{m p}+O\left(|x|^{-2}\right)\right\}\left\{\partial_{m} \partial_{p} g_{i j}-\partial_{m} \partial_{i} g_{j p}-\partial_{m} \partial_{j} g_{i p}+\partial_{i} \partial_{j} g_{m p}\right\}+\partial g * \partial g \\
& =-\frac{1}{2}\left\{\partial_{m} \partial_{m} g_{i j}-\partial_{m} \partial_{i} g_{j m}-\partial_{m} \partial_{j} g_{i m}+\partial_{i} \partial_{j} g_{m m}\right\}+\left(\partial^{2} g\right) *\left(|x|^{-2}\right)+\partial g * \partial g
\end{aligned}$$
In AF coordinates, $\partial g=O\left(|x|^{-3}\right), \partial^{2} g=O\left(|x|^{-4}\right)$; hence
$$R_{i j}=-\frac{1}{2}\left\{\partial_{m} \partial_{m} g_{i j}-\partial_{m} \partial_{i} g_{j m}-\partial_{m} \partial_{j} g_{i m}+\partial_{i} \partial_{j} g_{m m}\right\}+O\left(|x|^{-6}\right) .$$

By (A.1),
$$\begin{aligned}
\partial_{\beta} g_{\mu \nu}=- & \frac{1}{3} R_{\mu \beta \nu k}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k}}{|x|^{4}}-\frac{1}{3} R_{\mu k \nu \beta}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k}}{|x|^{4}} \\
& +\frac{4}{3} R_{\mu k \nu \ell}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k} x^{\ell} x^{\beta}}{|x|^{6}}-\frac{4 A}{|x|^{4}} x^{\beta} \delta_{\mu \nu}+O\left(|x|^{-4}\right),
\end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned}
\partial_{\alpha} \partial_{\beta} g_{\mu \nu}= & -\frac{1}{3} R_{\mu \beta \nu \alpha}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{1}{|x|^{4}}-\frac{1}{3} R_{\mu \alpha \nu \beta}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{1}{|x|^{4}} \\
& +\frac{4}{3} R_{\mu \beta \nu k}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k} x^{\alpha}}{|x|^{6}}+\frac{4}{3} R_{\mu k \nu \beta}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k} x^{\alpha}}{|x|^{6}} \\
& +\frac{4}{3} R_{\mu \alpha \nu k}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k} x^{\beta}}{|x|^{6}}+\frac{4}{3} R_{\mu k \nu \alpha}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k} x^{\beta}}{|x|^{6}} \\
& +\frac{4}{3} R_{\mu k \nu \ell}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k} x^{\ell}}{|x|^{6}} \delta_{\alpha \beta}-8 R_{\mu k \nu \ell}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k} x^{\ell} x^{\alpha} x^{\beta}}{|x|^{8}} \\
& -\frac{4 A}{|x|^{4}} \delta_{\alpha \beta} \delta_{\mu \nu}+\frac{16 A}{|x|^{6}} x^{\alpha} x^{\beta} \delta_{\mu \nu}+O\left(|x|^{-5}\right) .
\end{aligned}$$

Consequently, the first term in (A.5) is
$$\begin{aligned}
\partial_{m} \partial_{m} g_{i j}= & -\frac{1}{3} R_{i m j m}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{1}{|x|^{4}}-\frac{1}{3} R_{i m j m}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{1}{|x|^{4}} \\
& +\frac{4}{3} R_{i m j k}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k} x^{m}}{|x|^{6}}+\frac{4}{3} R_{i k j m}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k} x^{m}}{|x|^{6}} \\
& +\frac{4}{3} R_{i m j k}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k} x^{m}}{|x|^{6}}+\frac{4}{3} R_{i k j m}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k} x^{m}}{|x|^{6}} \\
& +\frac{4}{3} R_{i k j \ell}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k} x^{\ell}}{|x|^{6}} \delta_{m m}-8 R_{i k j \ell}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k} x^{\ell} x^{m} x^{m}}{|x|^{8}} \\
& -\frac{4 A}{|x|^{4}} \delta_{m m} \delta_{i j}+\frac{16 A}{|x|^{6}} x^{m} x^{m} \delta_{i j}+O\left(|x|^{-5}\right) .
\end{aligned}$$
The first two terms combine to give a Ricci curvature term, while the third through the eighth terms are all the same (though with different coefficients); adding up we get
$$\partial_{m} \partial_{m} g_{i j}=-\frac{2}{3} R_{i j}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{1}{|x|^{4}}+\frac{8}{3} R_{i k j \ell}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k} x^{\ell}}{|x|^{6}}+O\left(|x|^{-5}\right) .$$
The second term is
$$\begin{aligned}
\partial_{m} \partial_{i} g_{j m}= & -\frac{1}{3} R_{j i m m}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{1}{|x|^{4}}-\frac{1}{3} R_{j m m i}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{1}{|x|^{4}} \\
& +\frac{4}{3} R_{j i m k}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k} x^{m}}{|x|^{6}}+\frac{4}{3} R_{j k m i}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k} x^{m}}{|x|^{6}} \\
& +\frac{4}{3} R_{j m m k}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k} x^{i}}{|x|^{6}}+\frac{4}{3} R_{j k m m}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k} x^{i}}{|x|^{6}} \\
& +\frac{4}{3} R_{j k m \ell}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k} x^{\ell}}{|x|^{6}} \delta_{m i}-8 R_{j k m \ell}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k} x^{\ell} x^{m} x^{i}}{|x|^{8}} \\
& -\frac{4 A}{|x|^{4}} \delta_{m i} \delta_{j m}+\frac{16 A}{|x|^{6}} x^{m} x^{i} \delta_{j m}+O\left(|x|^{-5}\right),
\end{aligned}$$
notice that the fourth and seventh terms cancel each other, while the first, sixth, and eight terms vanish because of the skew-symmetry of the curvature tensor. Also, the second and fifth terms are traces. Therefore,
$$\partial_{m} \partial_{i} g_{j m}=\frac{1}{3} R_{i j}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{1}{|x|^{4}}-\frac{4}{3} R_{j k}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{i} x^{k}}{|x|^{6}}-\frac{4 A}{|x|^{4}} \delta_{i j}+\frac{16 A}{|x|^{6}} x^{i} x^{j}+O\left(|x|^{-5}\right),$$
while the third term is
$$\partial_{m} \partial_{j} g_{i m}=\frac{1}{3} R_{i j}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{1}{|x|^{4}}-\frac{4}{3} R_{i k}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{j} x^{k}}{|x|^{6}}-\frac{4 A}{|x|^{4}} \delta_{i j}+\frac{16 A}{|x|^{6}} x^{i} x^{j}+O\left(|x|^{-5}\right) .$$

The last term in (A.5) is
$$\begin{aligned}
\partial_{i} \partial_{j} g_{m m}= & -\frac{1}{3} R_{m j m i}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{1}{|x|^{4}}-\frac{1}{3} R_{m i m j}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{1}{|x|^{4}} \\
& +\frac{4}{3} R_{m j m k}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k} x^{i}}{|x|^{6}}+\frac{4}{3} R_{m k m j}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k} x^{i}}{|x|^{6}} \\
& +\frac{4}{3} R_{m i m k}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k} x^{j}}{|x|^{6}}+\frac{4}{3} R_{m k m i}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k} x^{j}}{|x|^{6}} \\
& +\frac{4}{3} R_{m k m \ell}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k} x^{\ell}}{|x|^{6}} \delta_{i j}-8 R_{m k m \ell}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k} x^{\ell} x^{i} x^{j}}{|x|^{8}} \\
& -\frac{4 A}{|x|^{4}} \delta_{i j} \delta_{m m}+\frac{16 A}{|x|^{6}} x^{i} x^{j} \delta_{m m}+O\left(|x|^{-5}\right) .
\end{aligned}$$
In this case all the curvature terms involve traces, so we get
$$\begin{aligned}
\partial_{i} \partial_{j} g_{m m}= & -\frac{2}{3} R_{i j}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{1}{|x|^{4}}+\frac{8}{3} R_{j k}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{i} x^{k}}{|x|^{6}}+\frac{8}{3} R_{i k}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{j} x^{k}}{|x|^{6}} \\
& +\frac{4}{3} R_{k \ell}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k} x^{\ell}}{|x|^{6}} \delta_{i j}-8 R_{k \ell}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k} x^{\ell} x^{i} x^{j}}{|x|^{8}} \\
& -\frac{16 A}{|x|^{4}} \delta_{i j}+\frac{64 A}{|x|^{4}} x^{i} x^{j}+O\left(|x|^{-5}\right) .
\end{aligned}$$
Combining (A.9)-(A.14),
$$\begin{aligned}
& \partial_{m} \partial_{m} g_{i j}-\partial_{m} \partial_{i} g_{j m}-\partial_{m} \partial_{j} g_{i m}+\partial_{i} \partial_{j} g_{m m}= \\
& \quad \frac{8}{3} R_{i k j \ell}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k} x^{\ell}}{|x|^{6}}-2 R_{i j}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{1}{|x|^{4}}+4 R_{j k}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{i} x^{k}}{|x|^{6}}+4 R_{i k}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{j} x^{k}}{|x|^{6}} \\
& \quad \quad+\frac{4}{3} R_{k \ell}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k} x^{\ell}}{|x|^{6}} \delta_{i j}-8 R_{k \ell}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k} x^{\ell} x^{i} x^{j}}{|x|^{8}}-\frac{8 A}{|x|^{4}} \delta_{i j}+\frac{32 A}{|x|^{6}} x^{i} x^{j}+O\left(|x|^{-5}\right) .
\end{aligned}$$
We now use the fact that $\left(Y, g_{Y}\right)$ is Einstein, and that $\left\{y^{i}\right\}$ are normal coordinates centered at $y_{0}$ :
$$\begin{aligned}
R_{i j}\left(y_{0}\right) & =\frac{1}{4} R\left(y_{0}\right) \delta_{i j} \\
R_{i j k \ell}\left(y_{0}\right) & =W_{i j k \ell}\left(y_{0}\right)+\frac{1}{12} R\left(y_{0}\right)\left(\delta_{i k} \delta_{j \ell}-\delta_{i \ell} \delta_{j k}\right) .
\end{aligned}$$
Substituting these gives
$$\begin{aligned}
\partial_{m} \partial_{m} g_{i j}-\partial_{m} \partial_{i} g_{j m} & -\partial_{m} \partial_{j} g_{i m}+\partial_{i} \partial_{j} g_{m m}= \\
& \frac{8}{3} W_{i k j \ell}\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{k} x^{\ell}}{|x|^{6}}+\frac{1}{18} R\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{1}{|x|^{4}} \delta_{i j}-\frac{2}{9} R\left(y_{0}\right) \frac{x^{i} x^{j}}{|x|^{6}} \\
& -\frac{8 A}{|x|^{4}} \delta_{i j}+\frac{32 A}{|x|^{6}} x^{i} x^{j}+O\left(|x|^{-5}\right)
\end{aligned}$$
and (8.43) follows.

All non-simply-connected possiblities with more than one bubble are listed in Table B.1. The approximate metric in each case is obtained by using the first factor as the compact manifold, with the AF space clear from the latter factors. The first two cases are analogous to Cases (iv) and (v). The third and fourth are analogous to Cases (vi) and (vii). The fifth and six are analogous to Cases (vii) and (ix). The last four cases require a short explanation. In the case of $S^{2} \times S^{2} / \mathbb{Z}_{2}$, there are two fixed points: the equivalence classes of $(n, n)$ and $(n, s)$. The diagonal symmetry descends to the quotient, and fixes both of these points. The symmetry of reflection in a horizontal line descends to the quotient, and this interchanges the fixed points, and we again call this invariance bilateral symmetry. We may therefore glue on the same AF space at each fixed point, and require bilateral symmetry, which yields the last four cases in Table B.1.
