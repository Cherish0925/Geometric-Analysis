(2) The linearized Ricci of the Weyl-direction jet W at x=0 is zero (verified symbolically), confirming W is in the kernel of the linearized Ricci operator. (3) All drift terms vanish at x=0 because W is quadratic in x and dW is linear — the O(A') and O(A'') corrections only appear at O(r) away from center, scaling as O(delta) at the outer edge for |A'|,|A''| <= delta. The drift channel is universally available, not specific to special orbifold geometries. For the product-quotient orbifold (Steps 55-57), the Weyl block is already degenerate, consistent with the automatic first-obstruction vanishing there.

The net implication is that the rate-limited H4 necessity theorem (either R_pm*zeta=0 or t <= exp(-c/delta)) is SHARP at the rate delta*log(1/t). The exponential escape channel t ~ exp(-c/delta) is a genuine geometric possibility at the 2-jet level, not merely a failure of the proof technique. This does NOT construct a counterexample: global realization requires solving a geometric matching problem on an exponential neck, ensuring smoothness at all singular loci, and confirming the underlying smooth manifold carries no Einstein metric (the smooth-category fidelity condition). The product-quotient mixed-wall family is the natural candidate, but no closed drift neck has been constructed on any mixed row.

Both are substantial; the positive collapse branch may be more tractable because it does not require solving the full matching problem.
