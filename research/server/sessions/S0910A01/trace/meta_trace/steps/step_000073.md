## Deep-Reasoning

This step developed three structural results for the graph-neck construction and then subjected them to rigorous TTCS verification, which found five genuine errors — all repaired. The corrected picture reveals a deeper understanding of the graph-manifold route's viability and, more importantly, redirects the research toward the positive branch.

The Preissmann obstruction is the cleanest result: for any Riemannian metric h on T^3, max |Ric_h + 2h|_op > 2/3. The proof uses the 3D sectional curvature identity K_{ij} = (r_i + r_j - r_k)/2 to show that |r_i + 2| < 2/3 forces K < 0, contradicting Preissmann for pi_1 = Z^3. At c = 2/3, the flat torus theorem forces flatness but flat has |Ric_h + 2h| = 2 > 2/3. TTCS corrected the boundary argument: the fixed triple (-4/3, -4/3, -8/3) does not follow from the bound; the flat torus theorem is the correct closure. The consequence is quantitative: every isotropic warped collar dt^2 + cosh^2(t) h on T^3 has center defect > 2/3, independently of fiber metric and collar length.

The Koiso center conditions were verified algebraically. At a totally geodesic center S(0) = 0 of a Gaussian collar, Einstein forces S'(0) = Ric_h^sharp + 3I with R_h = -6. Isotropic S' = I requires Ric_h = -2h (constant negative curvature), impossible on T^3. For the T^2-invariant diagonal ansatz with a_0 = 0, the fiber constraint R_h = -6 becomes u^2 + v^2 + uv + u' + v' = 3, and the Koiso accelerations are a_tt = uv, b_tt = v^2 + v', c_tt = u^2 + u'. These were confirmed to machine precision.

The anisotropy decay analysis had two important TTCS corrections. First, the unforced ODE rate is sech(3t) (from H' = 9 - H^2 giving H = 3 tanh(3t)), not sech(sqrt(3) t) which came from the two-direction a=0 gauge. Second, and more fundamentally, the simplified ODE s_i' + H s_i = 3 cannot produce Koiso anisotropy because delta_ij(0) = 0. The correct equation is s_i' + H s_i = 3 + r_i(h_t), with fiber Ricci forcing. The Duhamel representation from zero initial difference gives delta_ij(t) = exp(-int H) int_0^t exp(int H)(r_i - r_j) dtau. With fiber Ricci scaling as e^{-2t} in the cusp regime, the forced decay rate is e^{-2t}, confirmed numerically (fitted rate 2.002). This is slower than the unforced e^{-3t} but still exponential. The TTCS also correctly identified that the higher Fourier mode stability claim was unjustified from the principal part alone.

The a = 0 gauge analysis was corrected: the full PDE gives b_t c_t + b_theta c_theta = 0, not b_t c_t = 0. The rank-1 conclusion holds only for the theta-homogeneous ODE. The Koiso solution immediately leaves a = 0 because a_tt(0) = uv != 0 at generic theta.

The most consequential finding is the fiber periodicity gap. The Riccati shooting with v = B sin(theta) produces periodic u(theta) with R = -6 pointwise, but the mean int(u)/(2pi) is strictly positive and the branch terminates at B ~ 2.25 with mean ~ 0.88. Zero-mean periodic fibers on T^3 are NOT obtained in the T^2-invariant diagonal form with this parametrization. The Kazdan-Warner theorem guarantees R = -6 metrics exist on T^3, but not in the restricted diagonal form.

This fiber gap does not close the graph route entirely — more general ansätze or Fourier/Galerkin methods might find zero-mean solutions — but it shows that the simplest computational handle is inadequate. Combined with the fact that even a successful negative-branch graph construction would not refute the sign-unrestricted Yau 26, this redirects research energy toward the positive branch.

A02's message #503 opens a complementary and arguably more promising direction: proving P_n^+ all-dimensionally using the manuscript's dimension-general annular flux proposition with coefficient -(d+2)/2. The key observation is that positive-branch counterexamples require PSC-compatible no-Einstein manifolds, but known no-Einstein examples in dim 4 are monopole-driven (non-PSC), and no concrete closed no-Einstein manifold is known in n >= 5. This makes the positive branch a proof problem rather than a counterexample problem. A02 proposes a cone/stratum-aware higher-obstruction flux necessity lemma separating transverse cone dimension d from total dimension N, building on Step 53's stratum object.

The global research structure is now clearer than at any earlier point. The restricted IL negative theorem is stable after Step 71 repairs. The graph-neck route has interesting structure (Preissmann, Koiso, exponential decay) but faces the fiber periodicity gap. The positive branch, opened by A02, is the most promising direction for the full all-dimensional Yau 26 because it is not blocked by known counterexamples in any dimension. The next agent should evaluate whether to pursue the positive-branch flux mechanism, continue the graph route with a more general fiber ansatz, or develop the restricted IL toward publication level.
