What improved is the mathematical control of the current four-node \((S^2\times S^2)/\mathbb Z_2\) laboratory and its relation to near-Einstein input metrics.

The first structural improvement is that the local orientation-independence asserted in Step 67 is now based on an actual isometry rather than on a quadratic-jet inference. The reflection \(c(z,w)=(\overline z,w)\) is an orientation-reversing isometry of the product metric, commutes with \(\rho(z,w)=(-z,-w)\), descends to the orbifold, and fixes all four \(A_1\) nodes. It exchanges \(\Lambda^+\) and \(\Lambda^-\), so compatible and reversed single-node EH data are conjugate at the level of the whole metric germ, all gauges and all obstruction equations, up to orthogonal changes of parameter and obstruction bases. This is exactly the correct strength: local zero sets, ranks, scalar invariants and obstruction towers agree, but coefficient arrays in unrelated bases need not literally match, and the map reverses all nodes simultaneously in a multi-node configuration. Globally, it sends the reversed set \(S\) to \(S^c\), so \(r=1\) and \(r=3\) systems must have equivalent solvability, \(r=0\) and \(r=4\) are equivalent once the known pure-row Einstein smoothing is supplied, and \(r=2\) remains the self-complementary balanced case with possible adjacent/diagonal seam distinctions.

The second improvement is a corrected spectral and Green-object standard. Reading Ozuch, LeBrun--Ozuch, Kröncke and the 2026 stability survey fixed the convention: Ozuch's gauged operator is \(P=\frac12\nabla^*\nabla-\mathring R\), while Kröncke's \(\Delta_E=\nabla^*\nabla-2\mathring R=2P\). However, the full gauged \(P\)-kernel has four quotient-invariant scalar directions generated by the axial \(l=1\) harmonic on either factor times either factor metric. These are not TT, but Ozuch's full obstruction formalism projects against the full kernel unless a slice removes them. Therefore the favorable conclusion is not unrestricted invertibility; it is: no small TT eigenvalue amplification, but a faithful Ozuch implementation must explicitly fix or include the four scalar full-kernel variables/projections.
