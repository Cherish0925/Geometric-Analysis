# Step 72 addendum — orbifold-bulk route after A02 Theorem 87.O

A02 message #868 sharply narrows the Ricci-flat-ALE resolution route proposed after Lemma 86.S.

For a compact complex-hyperbolic ball quotient $X$, $A=c_1^2(X)$, and
$$
V_m=X\#m(S^4/\mathbb Z_2),
$$
the optimal orbifold Weyl deficit over all orbifold metrics is reported as
$$
\sup d_{\rm orb}=\sup(8\pi^2\chi_{\rm orb}-\mathcal W)
=4\pi^2(A/3-2m).
$$
The upper bound is the signature inequality $\mathcal W\ge12\pi^2\tau_{\rm orb}$ with
$\chi_{\rm orb}=A/3-m$, $\tau_{\rm orb}=A/9$ and zero $A_1$ eta correction. The Kobayashi sum of the CH metric with round $S^4/\mathbb Z_2$ realizes the bound in the limiting sense. Eguchi--Hanson resolution conserves the deficit: its Weyl energy $12\pi^2$ equals $8\pi^2$ times the Euler increment. Thus this additive ball-quotient channel lands exactly on the familiar $h=1/3$ wall and cannot beat it.

This excludes the easiest compact orbifold bulks. Positive self-dual Einstein/quaternionic orbifolds are also unsuitable for resolving to negative-Yamabe general-type blowups: standard-cutoff resolution would contradict the Yamabe inequality; any absorbing Einstein orbifold must have negative Einstein constant. The remaining high-value search is therefore precise:

> Find a compact negative-Einstein or quantitatively near-Einstein non-Kähler orbifold with isolated $A_1$ or Wahl points, whose smooth Ricci-flat-ALE resolution has the required general-type blowup topology and whose Weyl deficit is strictly larger than the ball-quotient/signature-saturating value; or prove that topology/field compatibility forbids such a bulk.

The general signature inequality alone leaves room, but a candidate must improve on saturation through genuinely non-Kähler negative geometry or non-additive global assembly. Local Ricci-flat caps and CH-collared $S^4/\mathbb Z_2$ cells cannot supply the improvement.
