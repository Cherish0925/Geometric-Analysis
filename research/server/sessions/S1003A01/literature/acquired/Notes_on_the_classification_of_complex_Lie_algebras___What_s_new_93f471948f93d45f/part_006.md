**[Terence Tao](http://www.math.ucla.edu/~tao)**

![Terence Tao's avatar](https://1.gravatar.com/avatar/d7f0e4a42bbbf58ffa656c92d4a32b2b6752e802dfa4cb919e5774dcfda006c0?s=32&d=identicon&r=PG)

I am [outsourcing this question to an AI](https://claude.ai/share/93c7d6ce-b071-4fc5-8191-6a8d714800e7), which interprets the Jacobi identity as a “fossil” of the associative law for algebras (or for groups).

Another analogy is with closed or exact 2-forms. Much as a Lie bracket ![[X,Y]](https://s0.wp.com/latex.php?latex=%5BX%2CY%5D&bg=ffffff&fg=545454&s=0&c=20201002) is supposed to be abstraction of the notion of a commutator ![\nabla_X Y - \nabla_Y X](https://s0.wp.com/latex.php?latex=%5Cnabla_X+Y+-+%5Cnabla_Y+X&bg=ffffff&fg=545454&s=0&c=20201002) of vector fields, the notion of a closed 2-form ![\omega](https://s0.wp.com/latex.php?latex=%5Comega&bg=ffffff&fg=545454&s=0&c=20201002) can be thought of as the abstraction of the notion of an exact 2-form, i.e., a differential ![\omega = d\theta](https://s0.wp.com/latex.php?latex=%5Comega+%3D+d%5Ctheta&bg=ffffff&fg=545454&s=0&c=20201002) of a ![1](https://s0.wp.com/latex.php?latex=1&bg=ffffff&fg=545454&s=0&c=20201002)-form. Compare for instance the Jacobi identity ![[X,[Y,Z]] + [Y,[Z,X]] + [Z,[X,Y]] = 0](https://s0.wp.com/latex.php?latex=%5BX%2C%5BY%2CZ%5D%5D+%2B+%5BY%2C%5BZ%2CX%5D%5D+%2B+%5BZ%2C%5BX%2CY%5D%5D+%3D+0&bg=ffffff&fg=545454&s=0&c=20201002) with the closure property ![d\omega=0](https://s0.wp.com/latex.php?latex=d%5Comega%3D0&bg=ffffff&fg=545454&s=0&c=20201002) (or in coordinates, ![\nabla_\alpha \omega_{\beta \gamma} + \nabla_\beta \omega_{\gamma \alpha} + \nabla_\gamma \omega_{\alpha \beta} = 0](https://s0.wp.com/latex.php?latex=%5Cnabla_%5Calpha+%5Comega_%7B%5Cbeta+%5Cgamma%7D+%2B+%5Cnabla_%5Cbeta+%5Comega_%7B%5Cgamma+%5Calpha%7D+%2B+%5Cnabla_%5Cgamma+%5Comega_%7B%5Calpha+%5Cbeta%7D+%3D+0&bg=ffffff&fg=545454&s=0&c=20201002)), and the commutator property ![[X,Y] = \nabla_X Y - \nabla_Y X](https://s0.wp.com/latex.php?latex=%5BX%2CY%5D+%3D+%5Cnabla_X+Y+-+%5Cnabla_Y+X&bg=ffffff&fg=545454&s=0&c=20201002) (or in coordinates, ![[X,Y]^\beta = X^\alpha \nabla_\alpha Y^\beta - Y^\alpha \nabla_\alpha X^\beta](https://s0.wp.com/latex.php?latex=%5BX%2CY%5D%5E%5Cbeta+%3D+X%5E%5Calpha+%5Cnabla_%5Calpha+Y%5E%5Cbeta+-+Y%5E%5Calpha+%5Cnabla_%5Calpha+X%5E%5Cbeta&bg=ffffff&fg=545454&s=0&c=20201002)) with the exact propety ![\omega = d\theta](https://s0.wp.com/latex.php?latex=%5Comega+%3D+d%5Ctheta&bg=ffffff&fg=545454&s=0&c=20201002) (or in coordinates, ![\omega_{\alpha \beta} = \partial_\alpha \theta_\beta - \partial_\beta \theta_\alpha](https://s0.wp.com/latex.php?latex=%5Comega_%7B%5Calpha+%5Cbeta%7D+%3D+%5Cpartial_%5Calpha+%5Ctheta_%5Cbeta+-+%5Cpartial_%5Cbeta+%5Ctheta_%5Calpha&bg=ffffff&fg=545454&s=0&c=20201002)).

For what it is worth, [here is an AI comment](https://claude.ai/share/93c7d6ce-b071-4fc5-8191-6a8d714800e7) on the above relation.
