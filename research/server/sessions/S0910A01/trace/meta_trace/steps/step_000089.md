## Deep-Reasoning

This step changed the mathematical status of the product-orbifold branch by converting Step 88's attractive but unsupported positivity narrative into a sharper and safer finite calculation problem. The key lesson is that the axial low mode remains a real structural signal, but it is not a theorem about the full Green interaction or about Einstein desingularization. The governing Yau--26 problem is still the sign-unrestricted, all-dimensional interval proposition, and the product-orbifold work is only a local positive-branch stress test. Its value is that it exposes how topologically different fillings of the same Einstein orbifold can be distinguished by spectral transport, but it cannot replace either a full positive forcing theorem or a sign-complete counterexample.

The central correction is spectral and analytic. On the unit product, the parallel shape tensor q=g_A-g_B is trace-free, divergence-free, descends through the quotient, and satisfies Pq=-q for P=\frac12\nabla^*\nabla-\mathring R. Therefore P is not positive on all TT tensors. Any argument based on a sum of nonnegative spectral terms over TT modes was structurally unsound unless it first proves that the geometric source has no component in negative directions and that the inverse is taken in a slice where the relevant sector is positive. At the flat principal V_2 level this source does annihilate q and the four scalar Ozuch zero modes, but that is only a principal jet fact; the full gluing source includes cutoff, Bianchi correction, curved-parametrix terms, constant-jet/frame terms, and Ozuch radial normalization. This distinction matters because a single algebraic identity for second jets does not by itself determine the geometric finite-dimensional equation.

The second correction is that point-supported quadrupole sources do not define ordinary finite self-energies. A heat-regularized Green matrix can be nonnegative before singular self-terms are removed, but deleting or subtracting those singular diagonal terms does not preserve positivity. The mathematically better object is a matched relative mixed-minus-all-same response computed with the same regularization and local normalization. For the odd--odd sector, the relative coefficient has factor \epsilon_i\epsilon_j-1, so diagonal terms vanish because \epsilon_i^2=1 before taking the regularization limit. This cancellation makes a finite inter-node comparison plausible, but its sign must be computed from the smooth off-diagonal Green kernel; it is not inherited from the positivity of an unrenormalized self-pairing. The even--odd and odd--even terms also remain unless an actual symmetry or matched local subtraction removes them.

The flat algebra did become stronger after repair. With H_{ijpq}=\partial_p\partial_qh_{ij}(0) and V_2=\frac12H_{ijpq}x_px_q, the correct fixed-frame identity is S_{\rm odd}(V_2)=\frac{\pi^2}{2}R_{\rm odd}(H), equivalently D:H=-16R_{\rm odd}. The factor differs from Step 88 because that script fed Hessian variables into a source formula written for polynomial V_2 coefficients. Exact basis verification shows that only four independent fully mixed non-diagonal components enter: D:H=-8H_{01,23}+8H_{03,12}+8H_{12,03}-8H_{23,01}, while R_{\rm odd}=\frac12(H_{01,23}-H_{03,12}-H_{12,03}+H_{23,01}). For the axial Killing--Killing mode, this gives S_{\rm odd}/\pi^2=1/2 and R_{\rm odd}=1, consistent with the exact Step 87 boundary values B^+=\pi^2/2 and B^-=-\pi^2/2 in the unaveraged symmetric product convention. This identity is a promising principal adjoint relation between the singular source and radial receiver, but it still has to be lifted through the full Ozuch/Biquard gluing construction.

The order bookkeeping also sharpened the target. Ozuch Proposition 2.19 gives a necessary estimate t_i\lambda_i+t_i^2\mu_i=O(t_{\max}^{5/2}) for a nearby Einstein desingularization. An inter-node source H^4(t_j\phi_j)=t_j^2H^4(\phi_j) changes the i-th receiver by \delta\lambda_i=O(t_j^2), hence enters the radial variable as t_i t_j^2=O(t^3) for comparable scales. Since t^3=o(t^{5/2}), the published necessary estimate cannot decide this layer; one needs a sharper projected expansion after the t^2 terms have been removed. This prevents interpreting the axial term as a current desingularization obstruction, but it gives a precise construction target: derive the matched O(t^3) relative inter-node response, including the correct receiver weight a_i=t_i and source weight b_j=t_j^2 rather than an energy-style w_iw_j expression.

Thus the positive product branch remains alive in a more disciplined form. The axial sign coincidence s=(+1,-1,-1,+1) still gives a robust low-mode distinction between diagonal mixed chirality and equal-scale all-same chirality. The corrected flat identity suggests a real source--receiver duality at the principal symbol level. But the geometric consequence depends on a full Bianchi-compatible source, a slice treatment of q and scalar modes, regularized off-diagonal kernel limits, even/odd sector separation, node transport, and Ozuch radial normalization. The restricted Ishida--LeBrun negative branch remains the most mature Yau--26-relevant asset, while this product-orbifold work supplies complementary structure rather than completion of the original problem.
