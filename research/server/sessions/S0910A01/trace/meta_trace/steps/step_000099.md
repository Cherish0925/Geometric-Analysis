## Deep-Reasoning

This step converted the Step 98 product-orbifold result from an overstrong local obstruction narrative into a sharper and more reliable mathematical object. The central distinction is now clear: the Step 98 computation is an implemented inter-node spectral response using the Step 96 homogeneous H^4 boundary functional and selected AB/AA/BB product-mode families; it is not yet the complete Ozuch coefficient and not a perturbative desingularization theorem. This matters for the full Yau-26 goal because a local product-orbifold mechanism can only become useful if every bridge from the model calculation to the true gluing equation is made explicit: complete eigentensor jets, singular source identity, quotient and frame normalizations, self and deformation terms, complement correction, and remainder estimates. A positive inter-node matrix is scientifically valuable only if it survives those allowed adjustments; chirality-independence of a self term would not by itself force that term to vanish or prevent balancing.

The main new evidence concerns whether Step 98 omitted parts of the geometric tensor modes. Direct support tests showed why the Strategist warning was real: arbitrary same-factor second jets H_ab,pq are invisible to the receiver but can be seen by the Step 96 source functional, so receiver sparsity alone cannot delete them. I then computed the actual omitted same-factor partial jets for the standard electric and full-Lie magnetic m=2 tensor families on S^2 in normal coordinates, using E=Hess(psi)+(mu/2)psi g and M=L_{J grad psi}g. For these actual tensor families, the omitted partial jets contract to zero with both the source and receiver functionals. A later exact-coefficient calculation strengthened this: Step 96 coefficient arrays were regenerated directly from the self-dual/anti-self-dual form algebra, agreed with the prior floating arrays with max difference 0.0, and gave exact zero contractions for the actual partial jets. Thus the most obvious same-factor tensor-jet omission does not alter the implemented pair map, while arbitrary same-factor support remains a warning that this was a real mathematical check rather than a formal reassurance.

The same-chirality zero also became better understood. A first support-level computation showed that source and receiver matrices have large nonzero same-chirality max-products before signed contraction, so the zero is not a support-level absence of individual modes. A signed grouped computation showed that cancellation occurs after summing the implemented polarizations at fixed degree pair, before the heat summation. An exact finite-dimensional degree-pair script, then an exact coefficient/low-mode script including tested ell_s=1 and constant-slice cases, found no same-chirality failures in the implemented model. This moves the phenomenon from numerical tail behavior toward a finite-dimensional algebraic identity, but the all-degree structural proof is still missing. The best conceptual lead remains the Kähler curvature identity: on a Kähler-Einstein surface in complex orientation, the self-dual curvature block has rank one and annihilates the real (2,0)+(0,2) kernel plane, so a genuine Kähler-Einstein variation has zero kernel-to-kernel curvature variation after allowing the plane to move. Showing that the same-chirality singular Green field belongs to this linearized Kähler sector would explain the cancellation geometrically; this membership has not been proved.

The broader problem remains open. The product branch is a local positive-Einstein desingularization laboratory, not a global Einstein-existence statement for the smooth filling and not a solution of Yau Problem 26. The correct invariants for #_3(S^2 x S^2) are chi=8 and tau=0, so Hitchin-Thorpe does not obstruct it; the manifold has positive scalar curvature, so ordinary Seiberg-Witten invariants are not a direct exclusion of positive Einstein metrics; and a local failure near this product orbifold would not exclude a non-perturbative Einstein metric elsewhere, nor would it address unrestricted Einstein constant sign. The restricted IL negative-pinching program, hyperbolic/graph connector problem, higher-dimensional gap, and Lean formalization remain separate central tasks.
