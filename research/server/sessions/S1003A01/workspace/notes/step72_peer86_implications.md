# Step 72 addendum — implications of A02 Step 86

Peer message #865 supplies two corrections/results relevant to the Einstein-gap programme.

1. The human defect $\mathscr D=(4P+L_s)/R$, $R=\int|\mathrm{Ric}|^2$, is invariant under finite orbifold covers. Any quotient argument may halve extensive curvature integrals, but not the dimensionless defect. The current A01 certified balanced-cell transfer does not use a factor $1/2$ from quotienting the defect; nevertheless older construction notes should be searched before reusing any equivariant comparison.

2. Ricci-flat ALE resolution of an orbifold point changes the bulk defect by $o(1)$ at the gluing scale. Thus a nonzero-Einstein orbifold bulk transfers $\alpha=0$ to a smooth resolution without a separate scalar-variance hypothesis. By contrast, scalar-flat ALE/AE bubbles with positive finite $P$ act by a Möbius transformation on the defect; Burns bubbles recover $4k/(A+k)$ over a negative Kähler–Einstein bulk. This cleanly separates Ricci-flat desingularization from blow-up cost.

A02 asks whether a compact non-Kähler orbifold with $A_1$ or Wahl points, resolving to $X\#k\overline{\mathbf{CP}}^2$, can carry an orbifold metric with Weyl energy below $8\pi^2\chi_{\rm orb}$. Such a metric would yield $\alpha<1$ after Ricci-flat ALE resolution and could bypass the current packing-limited cell construction. No such example is presently known in this Session. The natural search must compare actual compact Einstein or near-Einstein orbifolds, their orientation-sensitive Weyl energy, and the topology of their minimal resolutions; a local ALE model alone cannot provide the bulk Weyl deficit.
