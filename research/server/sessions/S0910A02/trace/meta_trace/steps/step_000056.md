## Deep-Reasoning

This Explore step audited the Step-55 negative bi-Kähler mixed-row wall against the full Yau--26 target. The active target remains unchanged: a genuine resolution must prove or refute the all-dimensional dimension-only Ricci-pinching proposition for closed connected smooth manifolds, with the output Einstein metric unrelated to the input metric and with Lean formalization only after a real mathematical result exists. Nothing in this step proves or refutes any branch. The value of the step is sharper route control: it separates an explicit near-negative-Einstein construction on a finite four-dimensional family from the much harder claim that any member lacks an Einstein metric, and it identifies which obstruction mechanisms are actually still live rather than merely absent from the current notes.

The Step-55 construction ledger survived its basic arithmetic but needed two interpretive corrections. I reran `Sessions/S0910A02/workspace/scripts/step55_negative_bikahler_mixed_wall.py`, so `Sessions/S0910A02/workspace/data/step55_negative_bikahler_mixed_wall.json` now includes the patched double-cover smoothing section. The model remains \(O=(Σ_2×Σ_2)/⟨ι×ι⟩\), where each genus-2 factor has curvature \(-1\) and \(ι\) is hyperelliptic. There are \(6⋅6=36\) isolated fixed points, each locally \(-I_4\), hence \(A_1\) singularities. The product Einstein curvature has \(R_+=R_-=\mathrm{diag}(-1,0,0)\), so for either chirality the Ozuch/Biquard vector first obstruction can be killed by choosing the Eguchi--Hanson parameter \(ζ\) in the two-dimensional kernel; this is stronger and more precise than merely saying \(\det R_\pm=0\). Combined with Steps 49--50, the explicit construction half remains: after all first obstructions are killed, refined EH replacement gives \(\| \mathrm{Ric}+g\|_{\mathrm{op}}	o0\) on each realized sign-pattern filling. This is a sufficiency statement for explicit glued metrics, not the unproved H4-dual necessity theorem for arbitrary pinched degenerations.

The new orientation-reversing symmetry materially changes the family. If \(Σ_2\) is realized over the reals, complex conjugation on one factor commutes with the hyperelliptic involution and is orientation-reversing; \(c×\mathrm{id}\) descends to the quotient orbifold. On a node-pattern \(S\), orientation reversal sends reversed nodes to the complement of \(c(S)\). Thus at the row-arithmetic level \(r\) pairs with \(36-r\), and representatives reduce to \(r=0,\ldots,18\), with a caveat that smooth type may still depend on the actual subset, not only its cardinality. The endpoint \(r=0\) is the holomorphic/all-compatible double-cover row; \(r=36\) is its orientation reverse and therefore carries the same Einstein metric as an unoriented smooth manifold. This corrects any slogan that reversed bubbling is intrinsically Einstein-hostile. The live rows are \(1\le r\le18\) up to orientation, not because they are known non-Einstein, but because the endpoints are known Einstein controls.

I encoded this corrected ledger in `Sessions/S0910A02/workspace/scripts/step56_negative_bikahler_audit.py`, producing `Sessions/S0910A02/workspace/data/step56_negative_bikahler_audit.json`. It records \(\chi=56\), \(	au=-36+2r\), \(b_+=9+r\), \(b_-=45-r\), strict Hitchin--Thorpe for all rows, and the orientation pairing \(r↔36-r\). It also records the Noether parity test: a complex surface in the original orientation would have \(c_1^2+c_2=60+6r\), and in the reversed orientation \(276-6r\); for odd \(r\) neither is divisible by 12, so odd live rows admit no complex structure in either orientation. These odd rows are therefore outside ordinary Kähler/algebraic existence machines. Even rows are only numerically compatible with complex surfaces; they are not thereby known to be complex or Einstein. The source `Six-Lectures-on-Four-4-Manifolds--.../object.md` lines 103--107 supports the general double-cover \(H(m,n)\) geography, and lines 650--658 warn that non-lattice or non-complex smooth four-manifolds remain a genuine botany frontier.

The double-cover smoothing interpretation for \(M_0\) is now source-supported and should be kept: the quotient is a double cover of \(\mathbb P^1×\mathbb P^1\) branched over six vertical plus six horizontal fibres, a \((6,6)\) normal-crossing divisor with 36 crossings; smoothing in \(|\mathcal O(6,6)|\) gives a smooth double cover with branch genus 25, \(\chi=56\), \(K=\pi^*\mathcal O(1,1)\), \(K^2=4\), and \(	au=-36\), hence a negative KE metric by Aubin--Yau. The local cyclic-cover source `On-Einstein-Metrics-on-4-Manifolds-with-Finite-Cyclic--.../object.md` lines 170--208 supports the \(A_1\) minimal-resolution/smoothing diffeomorphism, and lines 210--219 give a Catanese-type simply-connected cyclic-cover criterion for smooth flexible branch divisors. This reinforces the target-fidelity principle already learned in Steps 54--55: complex-structure KE nonexistence on a singular/resolved central model does not imply smooth Einstein nonexistence for Yau--26.

A more important audit correction concerns the Gromov-norm/Sambusetti door. The strategist framed simplicial-volume and BCG/Sambusetti obstructions as a live route to refutation. The source checks show this is only true when the topology has the right fundamental-group content. `Simplicial-volume-Manifold-Atlas--.../object.md` lines 338--355 state that oriented closed manifolds with amenable fundamental group, including trivial fundamental group, have zero simplicial volume; LeBrun’s `Ricci-curvature-minimal-volumes-and-Seiberg-Witten-theory--.../object.md` lines 365--370 explicitly says Gromov’s simplicial-volume obstruction works only in the presence of infinite fundamental group. Kotschick’s entropy source `Entropies-Volumes-and-Einstein-Metrics--.../object.md` lines 47--50 and 206--216 gives the strengthened Gromov-Hitchin-Thorpe and Sambusetti inequalities, but again their force comes from nonzero simplicial volume, entropy, or maps to hyperbolic manifolds. For this specific mixed \(Σ_2×Σ_2\) family, I found a strong van Kampen proof sketch that all sign-pattern fillings should have the same fundamental group: the all-compatible filling \(M_0\) is simply connected by the smooth flexible double-cover model, both \(±2\) disk-bundle fillings are simply connected with boundary \(\mathbb{RP}^3\), and the boundary \(\pi_1=\mathbb Z/2\) is killed in either filling. If this sketch is written cleanly, all mixed rows are simply connected and the simplicial-volume/Sambusetti route is closed for this family. Because I did not write the full topology proof in this step, the correct status is not yet “proved simply connected,” but it is much closer to a closure of the Gromov-norm door than to a refutation route.

The monopole/Bauer--Furuta door remains live but narrowed. The source `Constraints-on-embedded-spheres-and-real-projective-planes-in-4-manifold--.../object.md` lines 27--48 gives the standard adjunction inequality for simple-type \(b_+>1\) manifolds with nonzero numerical Seiberg--Witten invariant and explains the handle-addition argument for a sphere class: if a non-torsion class is represented by a sphere, then the \(g=1\) case gives \(|⟨c,[S]⟩|+[S]^2≤0\). A \(+2\)-sphere is therefore incompatible with any nonzero ordinary numerical SW basic class under those hypotheses. Since for \(1≤r≤35\) a row has \(+2\)-spheres in both orientations after applying the orientation symmetry, the ordinary SW input used by LeBrun/Ishida--LeBrun estimates is killed or at least unavailable in both orientations. But Kotschick’s `Monopole-classes-and-Einstein-metrics--.../object.md` lines 80--123 sharply distinguishes special, generic, torsion, numerical, and Bauer--Furuta-detected monopole classes; it also records that Bauer--Furuta classes can survive connected sums and yield generic monopole classes of positive expected dimension. The present sources do not prove that a \(+2\)-sphere kills every generic monopole or stable-cohomotopy class. This is the one real obstruction door I would not close: if such a class survives on an odd or mixed row and satisfies a LeBrun/Kotschick Einstein estimate, it could transform the wall into a genuine \(P_4^-\) refutation.

Peer updates were incorporated conservatively. A01 #395 reports a Lefschetz tensor-character scan: the BCGP \(K^2=2\) row supports the earlier Einstein-existence bridge, while scanned \(K^2=4,6\) rows have positive obstruction dimension \(d>0\). This should be read only as failure of A01’s \(H^2=0\) sufficient smoothing route, not no-smoothing and not no-Einstein. A03 #396 initially claimed a direct nonlinear flow construction, but A03 #398 withdraws certification of the claimed theorem because of a derivative-splitting gap and unresolved high/low commutator/Galerkin bootstrap issues. A02 should therefore not cite the negative flow start as proved, nor return to the withdrawn \(A^{-1/2}\) impossibility claim. The flow line remains conditional and separate from the present noncollapsed mixed-bubble wall.

The resulting review judgment is: the negative bi-Kähler mixed family is a useful organizing wall but not a counterexample. Its construction half is concrete and should be written as a conditional reduction theorem for actual sign-pattern fillings: if \(P_4^-\) holds, every such near-\(-g\)-pinched smooth filling must carry some Einstein metric. Its topology half has promising symmetry, parity, endpoint Einstein controls, and likely simply connectivity for all sign patterns, making odd rows especially outside complex/Kähler existence machines but also probably outside simplicial-volume obstructions. Its obstruction half is weaker than the construction half: ordinary numerical SW/LeBrun tools are dead on mixed rows, Gromov-norm likely dies if \(\pi_1=1\), and only Bauer--Furuta/generic monopole phenomena or a completely different smooth obstruction remain plausible. This is four-dimensional noncollapsed route control only. It does not address positive collapse, negative collapse/diameter escape, non-\(SU(2)\) ALE bubbles, dimensions \(n≥5\), the unproved H4-dual necessity bridge, or the final Lean obligation.
