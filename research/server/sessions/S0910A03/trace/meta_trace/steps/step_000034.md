## Deep-Reasoning

This step restored the mathematical state after Step 33 had redirected the group from two unsupported conclusions. The Sobolev issue is a convention error, not a new collapse obstruction. With the Sobolev embedding written in the squared form actually used by the energy estimates, \(\|u\|_4^2\le \mathcal S(\|\nabla u\|_2^2+r^{-2}\|u\|_2^2)\), Maheux--Saloff-Coste gives \(\mathcal S\asymp r^2/\sqrt V\). At \(p=2\), \(K_3\le K_2^{1/2}\|\chi F\|_4^2\le C\mathcal S K_2^{1/2}(D_2+r^{-2}K_2)\), so the absorption number is \(\gamma=\mathcal S K_2^{1/2}\). On a matched good support, \(K_2\le K_0V\), hence \(\gamma\le Cr^2\sqrt{K_0}\), with collapse volume cancelling exactly. Step 33 used a symbol having the scaling of \(\mathcal S\) and then squared it again. The fixed-manifold rescue was also invalid: Ricci lower bounds and total-volume bounds do not control diameter or fixed-radius ball volumes, even on one smooth topology. Products \(\Sigma\times\Sigma\) of closed hyperbolic surfaces, with one factor degenerating by pinching a geodesic, have \(\operatorname{Ric}=-g\), fixed topology, fixed volume, diameter tending to infinity, and vanishing local ball volumes in thin regions. This refutes the compactness inference, not the Ishida--LeBrun geometric obstruction. Peer messages #332 and #333 corrected A03 message #330 and asked the group not to use the Step 33 per-manifold conclusion.

The restored state is subtler and more valuable: the weighted route remains alive, but only through the actual spacetime estimates. I wrote `workspace/notes/step34_restored_weighted_flow_state_and_exact_obligations.md` to fix notation and preserve the exact open work. A short script `workspace/scripts/verify_step34_scaling_and_time_powers.py` checked only algebraic scalings: \(\gamma=r^2\sqrt{K_0}\), the defect endpoint \(\delta(r^2/t)^{1/3}\), the drift integral \((3/2)\delta\theta^{2/3}r^2\), and the curvature endpoint \(\gamma/t\); unlike the Step 33 script, it does not encode geometric or PDE premises as automatic truths. TTCS verification then found one needed repair to the first version of the note: the recurrence coefficient cannot use a mere time average \(|I|^{-1}\|\mathcal S^2Y\|_{L^1(I)}\), since the energy contains \(\int_I(\mathcal S^2Y)K_p\). The correct direct energy recurrence uses \(\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_I\mathcal S^2Y\), or a different argument controlling correlation. The existing fixed \(q=2,3\) machinery supplies the needed dyadic pointwise input \(\mathcal S^2Y(s)\le C\gamma^3/s\), so on \([t/2,t]\) the recurrence has the coefficient \(t^{-1}+r^{-2}+1+\gamma^3/t\). This retains the true temporal cost \(t^{-3/p_0}\). Thus the supported curvature endpoint from the \(p_0=3\) seed is \(\|aF(t)\|_\infty\lesssim \gamma/t\), not a time-uniform bound near zero. If \(J_6(t)\le C\delta^6V\) holds from time zero, the defect endpoint is \(\|aE(t)\|_\infty\lesssim\delta(r^2/t)^{1/3}\), whose time integral is finite and gives the required sequence-uniform metric drift. The research has therefore returned to the real hard point: prove the regularized \(J_6\) persistence without first using the nonintegrable curvature pointwise estimate, and carry the moving Sobolev/support comparison in one first-exit argument.

To advance that hard point rather than only describe it, I wrote `workspace/notes/step34_exact_defect_J6_lemma.md`. It isolates the desired regularized defect moment inequality for \(\Phi_\varepsilon=(|E|^2+\varepsilon^2)^{1/2}\): \(J_{m,\varepsilon}'+c_mX_{m,\varepsilon}\le C_m(1+r^{-2})J_{m,\varepsilon}+C_m\int a^{m-1}F\Phi_\varepsilon^m\), with \(J_{m,\varepsilon}=\int\eta^2a^{m-2}\Phi_\varepsilon^m\) and \(X_{m,\varepsilon}=\int\eta^2a^{m-1}\Phi_\varepsilon^{m-2}|\nabla E|^2\). The note uses the exact defect equation \(\partial_tE=a(\Delta_LE-2E)+\mathcal C_a(E)\), the integrated Hessian-\(a\) identity from A01 Codex, the regular identity \(|da|^2/a=4|d\chi|^2\), and full tensor-gradient coercivity. The potential term is then \(\int Fz_{m,\varepsilon}^2\), which Sobolev--Hölder bounds by \(C_m\gamma(X_{m,\varepsilon}+r^{-2}J_{m,\varepsilon})\). For \(m=6\), small \(\gamma\) would give \(J_6(t)\le e^{C(1+r^{-2})t}J_6(0)\) without invoking \(\|aF\|_\infty\). The note is close to a proof but remains to be finalized by writing the epsilon-dependent remainder explicitly and aligning the support, stationary collar, and moving-metric comparison factors.

For continuation, the TTCS result `trace/ttcs/results/step34_ttcs01_verifier2_integration.md` gives useful independent support for the shifted-curvature order structure. Under a terminal hypothesis \(\sup|U|<\infty\), finite datum-dependent control can proceed through the pair \((\nabla U,\nabla^2\chi)\), then finite unweighted \(\nabla T\) moments, then the pair \((\nabla^2U,\nabla^3\chi)\). The first truly delicate term is still \(\int\chi|\nabla^2\chi||\nabla^2U|^p\). It is handled, after \(\nabla^2\chi\in L^4\) is supplied by the base pair, by \(I\le\varepsilon D_{2,p}+C_{\varepsilon,p}[1+\mathcal S\|\nabla^2\chi\|_4^2(1+r^{-2})]\|\nabla^2U\|_p^p\). This does not follow from bilipschitz comparison or initial smoothness alone; it requires the coupled connection-evolution estimate. Coefficient regularization \(a\mapsto a+\varepsilon\) remains excluded because it activates uncontrolled exterior regions. A02 message #334 was also incorporated: the positive/noncollapsed Route A and its ALF collapsed front are complementary evidence that orbifold-bubble and collapsed mechanisms must not be conflated, but they do not replace the cutoff-flow estimates.
