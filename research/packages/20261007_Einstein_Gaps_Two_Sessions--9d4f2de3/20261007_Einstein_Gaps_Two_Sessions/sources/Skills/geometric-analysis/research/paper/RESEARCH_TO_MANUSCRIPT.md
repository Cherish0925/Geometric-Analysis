## The remaining mathematical conclusions

1. **Density-independent weak zero-Weyl endpoint:** every fixed finite
   `chi/A <= B` theorem is complete. What remains is unbounded density,
   where the exterior and core may degenerate together. The positive-
   potential route needs retained fifth-line mass and its full weak
   equation for the required resolvent test; current radial estimates
   do not yet supply both. A closed actual counterexample must satisfy
   the original supply and class-square quantifiers. The target is a
   volume-scale conclusion, not a local positive cost.
2. **Balanced optimal first-order cost:** a lower bound against every
   genuine near-minimizing geometry, matched by a recovery construction.
   The compensated target is `Z >= 8 pi^2 (Q-N) - o(Q)` with
   `P = 8 pi^2 N + Z`. Linear order is already established; coefficient
   four is not assumed. Critical integral near-stationary/near-stable
   selection is now available. The missing conclusion is a global
   signed compensation or complete topology payment, not merely the
   existence of a minimizing sequence or a legal variational test.
3. **New actual scope:** a natural supplier or family genuinely outside
   the previously covered range that satisfies the weaker single-solution
   hypothesis. Rephrasing the same supplier is not such an application.
4. **Full BF classical budget:** a bound on all possible monopole classes
   of the BF connected-sum examples at the required precision. The
   known BF sign-set calculation is complete. The web increment also
   completes the full ordinary surgery-family budget; these are
   different smooth families and the latter does not close the former.
5. **Independent global existence tests:** diameter-free realization on
   the specified Chern boundary, all Einstein outputs on the fixed bundle,
   and the remaining general-input matching and repair questions. They
   are not unproved lemmas of the integral coercivity theorem.

## Scope of the month-wide integration

The October 1 integration preserves the complete incoming ZIP, both web
sessions' nested original packages, all source versions, computation files
and receipts in `../archive/sources/20261001-web-increment/`. Its contents
are linked to manuscript sections in
`../catalog/web_increment_20261001.json`. English mathematical sections
are translated or merged by proof dependency; metadata, repeated
formats and numerical receipts remain in the archive, not in the paper.
Earlier attempts are retained even when their current endpoint is
superseded. They do not override the current theorem statements.

The original three-line program has not been declared solved. This paper
centers the first line and includes the older constructions and analytical
modules that enter its proofs, limitations, or realization questions.
Independent second- and third-line programs remain in the original
research tree; they are not silently relabeled as first-line theorems.
In particular, the independent A1/log-Weyl construction program is not
replaced by the short Ricci-small curvature-spike example included here.

The continuous source and its compilation are a usable mathematical
handoff, not a claim that every statement in the month-long archive has
been independently reproved. The original arguments, theorem hypotheses,
and the explicit open conclusions remain available for detailed reading.
