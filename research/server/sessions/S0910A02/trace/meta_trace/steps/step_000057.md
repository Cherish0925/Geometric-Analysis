This step completed the four-dimensional noncollapsed wall into a statable dichotomy. The central construction is: tight operator-norm Ricci pinching through codimension-four ALE bubble insertion in dimension four drives the output manifold precisely into the unique region where all known Einstein existence and nonexistence tools for simply connected b₊>1 manifolds are simultaneously void. The mechanism is that compatible (holomorphic) ALE bubbling preserves K² and lands on manifolds where Aubin–Yau or del Pezzo existence already supplies an Einstein metric, while reversed ALE bubbling inserts a non-torsion +2-sphere that annihilates all ordinary SW basic classes (adjunction with handle, b₊>1, Taubes) and therefore kills every Kähler/symplectic structure in both orientations. This removes the input for all known nonexistence tools (LeBrun/Ishida–LeBrun/Kotschick require nonzero monopole classes) and all known existence tools (Aubin–Yau/Kähler–Einstein/Tian require Kähler structure). The Kähler exclusion argument is clean and strictly strengthens the earlier Noether parity exclusion, which only reached odd rows.

The van Kampen proof for π₁ = 1 on all sign-pattern fillings is straightforward once noticed: both ±2 disk bundles over S² are simply connected with boundary RP³, and both kill the boundary π₁(RP³) = Z/2 image. Since M₀ is simply connected by the smooth double-cover model, all M_r share π₁ = 1. This closes the simplicial-volume/Sambusetti/BCG door for the entire family. The Bauer–Furuta scope check comes back empty: all known nonvanishing inputs run through Kähler summands, and mixed rows are not Kähler and do not split along their +2-spheres (boundary RP³, not S³).

The higher-genus generalization (Σ_h×Σ_h)/⟨ι×ι⟩ was checked and shows strict Hitchin–Thorpe margin 4(h-1)²+6·min(r,N-r) for all h≥2 and all r. No enlargement produces a topological contradiction.

The multi-bubble estimate source gap is precisely identified: Ozuch cites Biquard 2013 (Invent. Math. 192), Section 14, for the EH-region estimate. This session failed to retrieve the Biquard paper. The reduction theorem should be stated as conditional on this cited estimate plus disjoint non-interaction of finitely many simultaneous EH insertions.

The endpoint correlation is structural, not accidental: the two rows r=0 and r=N that retain monopole classes in one orientation are exactly the two rows known to be Einstein. This holds for both the positive (S²×S²)/Z₂ mirror (Steps 49-50) and the negative (Σ₂×Σ₂)/⟨ι×ι⟩ mirror (Steps 55-57).

The result: Yau Problem 26 in dimension four (noncollapsed) is at least as hard as producing Einstein metrics on simply connected closed 4-manifolds with b₊≥2 and no monopole classes. This is a genuine structural insight about the problem's difficulty, but it is emphatically not progress toward resolution. It shows that proving the proposition requires either new Einstein existence machinery beyond Kähler geometry, or new nonexistence obstructions beyond monopole/SW theory, or an argument that bypasses noncollapsed bubbling entirely.

The untouched open directions remain decisive: positive collapse (n=4), negative collapse/diameter escape (n=4), non-SU(2) ALE bubbles, all n≥5, the H4-dual normalized-flux necessity bridge, and the Lean obligation. The Strategist correctly identifies that the next arc should be chosen from these rather than from a third variant of the noncollapsed wall. Positive collapse is arguably the most important remaining four-dimensional direction because the finite-type theorem (Step 9) shows collapsing must occur for #_k(S²×S²) stress families, and the almost-Killing-field bridge (Steps 28-30) is the identified missing theorem. Higher dimensions remain wide open with no candidate obstruction or existence family.
