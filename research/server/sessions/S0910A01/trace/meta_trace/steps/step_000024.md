## Deep-Reasoning

This step repaired an important mismatch between the Step 23 cooperative graph lemma and the actual selected-good-support construction in the four-dimensional negative LeBrun/Ishida-LeBrun route. The dissipation-locality insight from Step 23 remains mathematically useful: when the Sobolev variable is supported in one energy domain, the gradient term absorbed by Sobolev is the same domain's dissipation, not a neighboring dissipation. But the maximal-net repair introduced a new all-center smallness assumption. A maximal r-net over all of M includes centers outside the maximal-function good set; the global Chern-Weil curvature budget gives only total L2 curvature control and does not make A_alpha^2 ||F||_{L2(U_alpha)} small at every such center. Therefore the Step 23 graph theorem is now recorded as conditional on an all-center smallness family, not as an available input for the real LeBrun sequence.

The new formulation returns to domains where the initial hypotheses genuinely hold. On a single selected maximal-function good support, the physical flow cutoff chi and the auxiliary energy cutoff eta are separated: supp chi is compactly contained in {eta=1}, and supp grad eta lies in an open collar where chi=0. Thus a=chi^2 satisfies a|grad eta|^2=0, grad a dot grad eta=0, and supp a is contained in {eta=1}. The collar is exactly stationary because the flow coefficient a is zero there; this is a support identity, not a finite-propagation assertion. The active transition of chi is fully inside the energy domain, so transition terms are included rather than hidden.

This support-local setup removes the bad-center obstruction at the energy level. The maximal-function condition at the selected center p gives H2(0)=int eta^2 F^2 <= (1+K0) Vol(Omega_eta), and the four-dimensional relative Sobolev coefficient satisfies A_Omega^2 <= C(L_eta) r^2 Vol(Omega_eta)^(-1/2). Hence beta0=A_Omega^2 H2(0)^(1/2) <= C(L_eta) r^2 sqrt(1+K0), with collapsed volume cancelling on the selected support itself. The script step24_buffered_support_algebra.py shows this scaling and also shows that a trial r=0.003 is too large for sample constants, forcing the conceptual point that r must be chosen after the support ratios and tensor constants; it computes a sample repaired radius r=1.71322e-3 with beta=0.025 and absorption ratio 0.5.

The weighted moment bookkeeping was sharpened. For q=2, the actual curvature reaction is int a F^3 = int (chi F)^2 F, absorbed by the active first-exit parameter beta_a(t)=A_Omega^2 ||F(t)||_{L2(supp chi)} through int aF^3 <= C beta_a(D2+r^{-2}H2). This yields H2 persistence and integrated D2 control, provided the exact tensor inequality H2'+cD2 <= C int aF^3 + C r^{-2}H2 is proved. Integrating the same absorption gives the correctly weighted spacetime cubic A_Omega^4 int int aF^3 <= C beta^3. This is not interchangeable with a differently weighted parabolic-Sobolev moment such as a^{3/2}F^3.

TTCS then found and repaired a real error in my first q=3 section. The previous Riccati-style estimate for I4=int a^2F^4 was not supported by the cutoff algebra. The right q=3 Sobolev variable is u=aF^{3/2}, not chi F^{3/2}. With u=aF^{3/2}, one gets I4=int F u^2 <= ||F||_2 ||u||_4^2 and ||grad u||_2^2 <= C D3 + C int |grad a|^2 F^3 <= C D3 + C r^{-2}Y, since |grad a|^2=4a|grad chi|^2. Thus I4 <= C beta_a(D3+r^{-2}Y), giving a linear propagation inequality Y' <= C r^{-2}Y after absorption. The time average from A^4 int Y <= C beta^3 then produces an intermediate s0 in [t/2,t], and linear forward propagation gives A^4Y(t) <= C beta^3/t. The new script step24_support_tensor_monomials.py verifies the exponents: chi F is the q=2 variable, aF^{3/2} is the q=3 variable, chi E is the defect variable, and the wrong chi F^{3/2} factors aF^4 rather than a^2F^4 while producing an unweighted |grad chi|^2 F^3 term.

The triangular defect structure was also reorganized on the same support. The equation for E=Ric+g under partial_t g=-2aE is homogeneous in E and grad E; if E=0, the velocity is zero and no independent curvature-only or Hessian-a source can remain after Ricci and normalization are combined. The target energy inequality is Q'+cX <= C r^{-2}Q + C int aF|E|^2, and the potential is absorbed by chi E: int aF|E|^2 <= beta_a(X+r^{-2}Q). This keeps the needed unweighted defect propagation multiplicative rather than additive.

Finally, I added the static selected-support packing/coloring component. The script step24_packing_coloring.py shows that a maximal disjoint B_r family inside the good set, followed by a bounded-degree coloring of fixed-multiple supports B_{L_eta r}, retains a fixed sequence-independent plateau volume fraction. With illustrative constants r=0.0017 and L_eta=8, the retained fraction is tiny, about 6.74e-10 if Vol(G)>=0.5 Vol(M), but it is independent of collapse and topology scale. This is enough for a later averaging/Derdzinski route if the PDE estimates provide bounded-curvature plateaux and small tensor defects there.

The hard analytic content is now sharply isolated: exact tensor inequalities for q=2, q=3, and Q; uniform evolving Sobolev and cutoff-gradient control; uniform smooth existence and metric comparability through theta r^2; and a bridge from selected smoothed plateau mass to the Step 8 calibrated tensor-defect Derdzinski contradiction. The current work still concerns only the critical four-dimensional negative LeBrun/Ishida-LeBrun laboratory and does not solve the positive branch, arbitrary four-dimensional topologies, dimensions n>=5, the full Yau 26 proposition, or Lean formalization.
