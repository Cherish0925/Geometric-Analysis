## Deep-Reasoning

This Agent4 review preserved the exact Yau 26 target and corrected the Step 7 positive-branch program at its load-bearing points. The four-dimensional positive branch remains a valuable testbed, but it is not a solution of the all-dimensional problem: proving P_4^+ would not by itself resolve Q_n for n>=5, and any refutation of Q_4 still requires defeating both normalized sign branches on the same no-Einstein dimension. The main mathematical correction is that the four-dimensional CGB identity supplies not only an upper L2 curvature budget but also a sharp lower Weyl-energy budget in the positive branch. If |Ric-g|_op<=delta on a closed oriented 4-manifold, then Bishop gives Vol<=24*pi^2/(1-delta)^2 and CGB gives int |W|^2 >= 8*pi^2[chi - 2((1+delta)/(1-delta))^2], sharp at round S4. Thus high Euler characteristic is not merely allowed by the energy budget; it is forced to appear as Weyl concentration at bounded volume. For M_k=#_k(S^2 x S^2), any tight positive-pinched metric must satisfy int |W|^2 >= 16*pi^2 k - O_delta(1), so the Sha-Yang/Burdick/Perelman positive-Ricci family is a uniformity stress test: either tight pinching is bounded away from zero by neck/topology anisotropy, or any near-pinched construction must build many ALE-scale concentration regions or collapsed/singular docking geometry. This changes the role of #_k(S^2 x S^2) from a mere calibration example to a possible uniformity failure mode for any dimension-only delta_4. The second correction is algebraic and prevents route drift: det R_+=0 at an orbifold singularity means one self-dual Weyl eigenvalue equals -s/12; Kähler-Einstein metrics imply this condition in the relevant orientation, but the converse is false, e.g. spectra like (s/12,0,-s/12) are algebraically degenerate without being Kähler-Einstein. Therefore the positive-branch obstruction-amplitude principle can presently force only first-obstruction degeneracy, not Hermitian/Kähler-Einstein selection. Actual sources support stronger statements only under narrower hypotheses: Ozuch's higher-order obstruction statements concern exact Einstein GH desingularizations; LeBrun-Ozuch Theorem B concerns positive exact Einstein admissible sequences with Hermitian orbifold limits and ASD ALE bubbles, and then gives eventual Kähler-Einstein rigidity; Yao's higher-dimensional resolution theorem concerns negative exact Einstein noncollapsed resolution sequences with critical energy and smooth ALE blow-up. None of these directly applies to arbitrary C0 Ricci-pinched metrics. The third correction is analytic: C0-pinched epsilon-regularity should not be recorded as hopeless, but it also is not the single remaining unknown. Cheeger-Naber gives harmonic-radius patch control under bounded Ricci and noncollapse, enough for local weak Einstein limits on good charts when Ric-g tends to zero, but not a weighted gluing normal form or pointwise curvature control for arbitrary pinched metrics. The right bridge target is a Pohozaev/Komar or cokernel-projection identity: pair Ric-g against a smooth approximate obstruction tensor on an Eguchi-Hanson neck so the defect contributes only O(delta_i t_i), while the leading term is t_i times the Biquard-Ozuch first obstruction. This can avoid derivative Ricci control because integrations by parts move derivatives onto the smooth test field. The current resolution state is therefore substantially sharper but still open: positive P_4^+ requires controlling high-chi Weyl concentration, noncollapsed orbifold/ALE structure for C0-pinched metrics, first-obstruction projection without assuming exact Einstein normal form, higher-order obstructions, topology recovery, and collapsed/singular strata; none of this transfers automatically to n>=5.
