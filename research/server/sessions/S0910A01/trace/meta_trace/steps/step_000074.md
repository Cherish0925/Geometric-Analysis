## Deep-Reasoning

This Explore step corrected the route comparison at the current decision point and produced two reusable mathematical assets. The first correction concerns the graph-neck negative branch. Step 73 had found a real failure of the fixed diagonal Riccati family with v=B sin(theta): the periodic u-branch did not satisfy the zero-mean condition needed for a periodic torus fiber. The Strategist pointed out, and this step developed, a much larger construction that bypasses that finite ansatz. For any nonconstant analytic periodic f, the diagonal T^2-invariant metric h0=dtheta^2+e^{2f(theta)}dx^2+e^{-2f(theta)}dy^2 on T^3 has scalar curvature R(h0)=-2(f')^2 and volume density one, hence negative Yamabe class. The Yamabe minimizer normalized to scalar -6 is unique in the negative class, so T^2 symmetry is preserved; writing h=psi^4 h0 gives the analytic periodic ODE -8 psi''-2(f')^2 psi=-6 psi^5. In arclength gauge ds=psi^2 dtheta, this becomes a diagonal periodic metric h=ds^2+e^{2b(s)}dx^2+e^{2c(s)}dy^2 with R_h=-6. For a Gaussian Ric=-3g collar with totally geodesic center S(0)=0, the constraints reduce exactly to R_h=-6 and the first acceleration is S'(0)=Ric_h^#+3I. Thus the construction gives valid analytic Koiso center data; it does not prove long-time extension, asymptotic cusp matching, or a small-defect graph-manifold metric. I wrote and ran `workspace/scripts/step74_torus_yamabe_center.py` for f=sin(theta). It found a positive solution with psi in [0.6228147217,0.6583452279], arclength period 2.5788610778, independent FFT second-derivative residual about 1.5e-5, scalar curvature in arclength gauge between -6.0001469 and -5.9998782, and strongly anisotropic Koiso acceleration with maximum deviation from I about 7.15. The numerical residual computed by substituting the ODE right-hand side was correctly re-labelled as algebraic self-consistency rather than independent evidence. The second correction concerns the positive branch. A02's all-dimensional positive proof allocation is valuable, but the annular flux mechanism has inverse-logarithmic scale. The manuscript's FND:flux gives only delta >= c/L for a quadratic TT obstruction on an annulus of logarithmic length L, and the constant-scalar A1 model explicitly has s=4 and |log a| delta_+ -> 1/6. TTCS independently verified that this scale criticism is correct and sharp: a nonzero obstruction coefficient is compatible with delta -> 0 when L -> infinity unless an additional sequence-level mechanism supplies bounded-length capture, delta L -> 0, fixed-rescaled-annulus tightness with iterated limits, or a different rigidity/existence mechanism. After A02 message #509, this was sharpened further: ell=2 is the unique unweighted log-critical mode for the Yau--26 C^0 Ricci hypothesis; ell>2 modes are power-subcritical, and higher Biquard--Ozuch obstructions contribute O(t^{k-1}) in operator norm once the first obstruction vanishes, which is much smaller than the dominant 1/|log a| defect in near-pinched A1 constructions. Thus a positive proof cannot be a simple all-mode flux necessity tower. The real noncollapsed positive crux is now the mixed-row positive Einstein question for resolutions of (S^2 x S^2)/Z2: either first-obstruction vanishing is accompanied by tower rigidity and transversality, yielding new positive Einstein metrics on currently open smooth types, or a global existence method is required beyond nearby orbifold gluing. A02 also observed that products with positively Einstein spheres propagate the four-dimensional near-pinched input issue to n>=6, while n=5 remains separate. A03 message #510 added a serious boundary obstruction to the T^2-invariant reflected graph route: even though Yamabe center data exist, the conformal-gauge anisotropy d=b-c obeys a drift-Laplacian and nonconstant center anisotropy is amplified toward a cusp, while a flat cusp has constant orbit anisotropy. I have not independently rebuilt A03's full proof in this step, but it changes the graph route from a center-data problem to a boundary-anisotropy problem in the symmetric reflected class. I saved the consolidated note `workspace/notes/step74_graph_center_and_positive_flux_rebalance.md`, route assessment `workspace/plans/route_assessment_step74.md`, sent peer messages #506 and #507, and incorporated TTCS verification at `trace/ttcs/results/step74_ttcs01_verifier1_integration.md`. The active direction is now clearer: keep the restricted IL theorem as a mature negative-branch asset; treat the graph neck as a constrained negative laboratory; put main scope-relevant effort into the four-dimensional positive mixed-row Einstein problem and its implications for P_4^+ and n>=6.
