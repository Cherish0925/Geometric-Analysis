The new useful structure is the fixed-p weighted defect energy J_p=int eta^2 a^{p-2}|E|^p. This addresses the concern that a gauge-fixed construction might silently solve a transported-cutoff equation. For the normalized flow, the hatted equation must include the lower-order term -2(chi o phi^{-1})^2 hat g, which should not change the principal parabolic mechanism but must be written explicitly. Yang's uniform-time theorem, lines 835-878, is not mere short-time existence; it is a first-exit argument carrying Sobolev, metric comparison, current curvature energy, and a weighted pointwise curvature bound. Wang's later extension result, source lines 396-412 in the pseudolocality record, extends local Ricci flow under support-wide weighted curvature control |chi^2 Rm|, so a common time theta r^2 still requires a simultaneous weighted curvature and weighted velocity bootstrap.

The strongest new match is with Yang Appendix B. Lines 1127-1136 give an estimate whose output is |chi^2 f| from spacetime input int chi^{2p0-n} f^{p0} and a potential condition (int chi^{q-n}u^{q/2})^{2/q}. Setting n=4, p0=q=6, f=|E|, and u=F gives exactly the p=6 weighted defect input int chi^8|E|^6 and the q=3 curvature potential (int chi^2 F^3)^{1/3}. TTCS verified the repair and did not find a Hardy-type obstruction in this weighted framework; the earlier Hardy failure applies to unweighted critical Nash shortcuts, not to the Yang weighted powers. The remaining mathematical substance is now well localized: prove the p=6 defect inequality with full tensor/Kato detail, prove or instantiate the Yang B.1 weighted Moser step for |E| using a time-slab q=3 curvature potential, complete support-wide weighted curvature sufficient for Wang extension, and write the normalized DeTurck/Yang adaptation.
