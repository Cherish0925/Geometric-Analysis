## Deep-Reasoning

Step 30 reoriented the research after Steps 28--29 without changing the Yau-26 target. The exact proposition remains all-dimensional and all closed connected smooth manifolds, with output an Einstein metric on the same smooth manifold and no proximity, sign, symmetry, noncollapse, or curvature-operator assumptions. The positive-collapse Bochner work remains valuable, but Step 29 and A03 reply #322 made clear that its missing bridge -- obtaining scale-correct central almost-Killing fields from C^0 Ricci pinching without bounded Rm -- cannot be supplied by Cheeger--Fukaya--Gromov or by the peer negative Ricci-flow smoothing route. I therefore reopened the four-dimensional positive noncollapsed bubble route that had been largely dormant since Steps 7--10. The main conceptual refinement is that noncollapsed P_4^+ splits into a genuinely closed regular alternative and a still-open bubbling alternative. If a positive-pinched sequence with a uniform lower volume bound has no harmonic-radius degeneration, Myers plus Bishop--Gromov gives global noncollapse, Cheeger--Naber harmonic-coordinate compactness gives C^{1,alpha}\cap W^{2,p} convergence, the weak limit satisfies Ric=g on the entire smooth limit, elliptic regularity makes it smooth Einstein, and Cheeger--Gromov diffeomorphism recovery puts that Einstein metric on the original smooth M_i. Thus any noncollapsed no-Einstein counterexample must bubble. This is a stronger and cleaner view than the earlier phrase that regular-part convergence merely falls short of existence. But the singular alternative is not yet a proven finite ALE bubble-tree theorem for C^0 Ricci-pinched metrics: Ozuch/Anderson--BKN source theorems are exact-Einstein statements, and Cheeger--Naber bounded-Ricci theory alone does not provide the weighted two-scale orbifold/ALE normal form or neck decay needed to read Ozuch's obstruction. The decisive mathematical obstruction is therefore not the formal boundary identity itself but the missing scale-sensitive expansion for merely L^infty-near-Einstein metrics.

The source and support work sharpened what the noncollapsed bubble route can and cannot promise. A knowledge request retrieved Ozuch's 2021 paper `Integrability of Einstein deformations and desingularizations` as `Knowledge/objects/papers/Integrability-of-Einstein-deformations-and-desingularizations--8e63541ce32f--b5af64ba3e90/object.md` with citation key `ozuch2021integrability`. Reading it together with the existing Ozuch noncollapsed-degeneration source clarified that the relevant exact-Einstein obstruction is a Taub/Pohozaev preserved quantity: for an orbifold expansion g_o=e+H_2+O(r^3) and ALE expansion g_b=e+H^4+O(r^{-5}), Theorem 4.6 gives vanishing of boundary integrals of Ric_e^{(2)}(H_2,H^4) against cone rotations and r partial_r; Corollary 4.10 specializes the Eguchi--Hanson case H^4=-theta_1^- circ omega_1^+/(2r^4) to the vector condition R_+ v=0 for the EH orientation v. Only after allowing the EH orientation to vary does this become det R_+=0, and det R_+=0 remains much weaker than Kähler--Einstein. I wrote and ran `workspace/scripts/step30_noncollapsed_bubble_variational_audit.py`, producing `workspace/data/step30_noncollapsed_bubble_variational_audit.json`. It verified the Eguchi--Hanson volume-gauge leading term: in x=r^{-4}, the relative coefficients are -x/2,-x/2,+x/2,+x/2 at order r^{-4}, so the trace and ordinary mass/volume flux vanish at leading order; the detectable datum is the anisotropic H^4 paired with the orbifold H_2 curvature. The same script checked the linear obstruction algebra for sample R_+ matrices and recorded the annular scaling warning eta^2 r^4 << t^4 iff eta << t^2/r^2, explaining why naive cone-field transplantation is too fragile and motivating variational/harmonic extensions with prescribed asymptotics.

The most robust new technical object from this step is the divergence-free cosmological Einstein tensor pairing. For positive normalization in dimension n, set Ecal_g = Ric_g - (1/2)s_g g + ((n-2)/2)g. If A=Ric_g-g and ||A||_op<=delta, then Ecal_g=A-(1/2)(tr A)g, hence Ecal_g vanishes for Ric=g, is O_n(delta) pointwise, and is divergence-free by contracted Bianchi. Ozuch's Lemma 1.4 gives the exact identity int_{partial Omega} Ecal_g(Z,nu) = (1/2) int_Omega <Ecal_g, L_Z g>, with no derivative of the Ricci defect. This is the correct C^0-safe bulk pairing, in contrast with Ric-g whose divergence contains ds. TTCS confirmed this and gave the explicit four-dimensional estimate |int_{partial Omega} Ecal_g(Z,nu)| <= 2 delta Vol(Omega)^{1/2} J(Z)^{1/2} for J=int |L_Zg|^2. TTCS also corrected and sharpened the variational-field proposal: for the radial channel r partial_r one must use the conformal-Killing operator K_g Z=(L_Z g)^circ and energy int |K_g Z|^2, because r partial_r is conformal Killing and raw int |L_Zg|^2 has an irrelevant R^4 volume term even on the flat cone. The minimizer satisfies delta K Z=0, while Ozuch's ALE fields are harmonic vector fields satisfying vector Laplace equations; these coincide only after a Weitzenbock/model analysis, so the variational minimizer is not yet a drop-in replacement for Ozuch's fields on an arbitrary pinched neck.

The route judgment after TTCS is intentionally more demanding than my first note. Route A, the noncollapsed four-dimensional positive bubble route via a variational Pohozaev/Komar projection, is now the highest-value next construction because it directly targets the only noncollapsed obstruction to putting an Einstein metric on the same smooth topology: regular noncollapse is already conceptually settled, while bubbling is exactly where Ozuch/Biquard obstruction theory lives. But Route A is not close to completion. To get from C^0 Ricci pinching to R_+v=0, one must either prove a new weighted orbifold/ALE two-scale normal form for L^infty-near-Einstein sequences, or build a weak projection theory that identifies the H_2-H^4 cross term without such a normal form. Existing exact-Einstein sources use harmonic curvature and neck decay estimates unavailable under Ricci-only C^0 control; Step 11's CZ spike warns that pointwise Rm regularity cannot be smuggled in. Even if first-obstruction vanishing is obtained, that is only a necessary local condition. Higher-order Ozuch obstructions, global cokernels, multiple bubbles, nondegeneracy, ALE type/orientation, and diffeomorphism recovery remain independent obligations. TTCS emphasized a serious possibility: higher-order obstructions may block exact Einstein gluing while producing only O(epsilon) operator Ricci defect after scale correction, so C^0 pinching may detect the first obstruction but not all exact solvability obstructions. Therefore Step 30 changes route weighting and produces tools, but it does not prove or refute any branch of Yau-26. The positive-collapse Bochner route remains live as a secondary path with the variational almost-Killing scale-selection idea; the toric fixed-strata alpha-family remains a collapse stress test; and the A01/A03 negative Ishida--LeBrun selected-support program should be monitored as a restricted four-dimensional negative side theorem, especially after their messages #323--#325 about weighted Moser corrections, but it does not settle the full proposition.
