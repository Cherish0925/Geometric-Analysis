## Deep-Reasoning

Step 90 revised the free-T² direction at its decisive unresolved point. Exact Maxwell conservation removes twist from an exact orthogonally transitive open end only when the metric is Einstein throughout the connected region. For an approximately Einstein connector, mixed Ricci defect sources the charge, so Step 89's constant-Q resolvent curve cannot close the forced problem. The three quantities Q_-=inf Q, Q_+=sup Q, and the separator average Q_C govern different parts of the geometry and cannot be merged: Q_- enters the curvature-dimension estimate, Q_+ enters tension and flux size, and Q_C raises the minimal-separator energy floor. Exact OT ends imply Q_-=0, leaving the uniform curvature parameter at 3+δ rather than improving it.

The twist energy has a stronger internal structure than an arbitrary source. For Q=(1/2)e^{-3σ}q^T M^{-1}q on H², symbolic calculation established Hess_H Q=Qh and |grad_H Q|²=Q², while the vertical traceless equation gives S_F=-grad_H Q. Consequently <dU,D S_F>=-Qe+3X+R_q, with |X|≤|grad σ|Q√e and |R_q|≤√(2Qe)|b|. The -Qe term cancels pointwise with the +Qe supplied by the Einstein-solved horizontal drift curvature. Thus twist is not simply favorable: after expansion, its leading linear reaction disappears and the remaining area-charge exchange and charge derivative carry the difficulty. The mixed equation has the exact normalization dq=±2ρ*E_mix and |b|²=4|E_mix|²_HS, so ||E||op≤δ gives |b|≤2√2δ. On a round expanding cusp the physical amplitude w=e^{-(3σ+α)/2}q obeys w'+3w=b; sustained small forcing permits w=O(δ), while coordinate charge may change at order δe^{3r}.

Combining the orbit, charge, and area equations without differentiating E singles out W=e+2Q+3|grad σ|². Its first-order exchanges cancel and its flux is O(δ√W), but its zero-order part is not coercive for large Q: at a common critical point compatible with exact second jets, the nonflux contribution is 54-2Q². This explains structurally why neither the constant-mass resolvent nor a local maximum argument controls a concentrated charged core. A TTCS suggestion that an average Q=O(δ²) could close the argument was not adopted because it first assumes the unavailable e ceiling and does not convert global average control into the kernel-weighted control required by the resolvent.

The execution then exposed a concrete alternative mechanism. In the radial reduction γ=dr²+ψ²dθ², M=diag(e^α,e^{-α}), q=(q0,0), the exact constant-charge equations are u'=6+Q-u²-Hu, v'=Q-Hv-uv, K=-3+Q+Hu, and H'=-K-H². Smooth-center data have u=((6+Q0)/2)r+O(r³), v=(Q0/2)r+O(r³), H=r^{-1}-(Q0/2)r+O(r³), and K(0)=3Q0/2. The unused horizontal equation C=E_rr propagates exactly by C'=-2(u+H)C, so a regular center with C=0 solves the full reduced Einstein system. Numerical continuations for Q0=0.1,1,3,10,30,100 all reached r=4 and approached u=2, v=0, H=1, K=-1, Q=0; for Q0=100, Q fell below 10^{-2} by r≈0.982 and below 10^{-4} by r≈1.720. These runs establish the mechanism over the sampled range, while the local regular family permits every finite Q0.

After switching when w=δ, a forced collar with b=-A sin²(πs/L) was shot to w(L)=0. Across Q0=1,10,100, δ=0.1,0.03,0.01, and L=0.5,1,2, all 27 runs succeeded. The required amplitude was max|b|=C(L)δ, with C≈1.75–1.85, 0.365–0.395, and 0.0266–0.0293 respectively. The remaining horizontal constraint satisfies C'=-2(u+H)C-wb and was O(δ²). In this rank-one model the only defect blocks are C and the mixed component |b|/2, hence every sampled collar had ||Ric+3g||op<0.925δ. This rules out a local principle asserting that one exact OT end and small mixed Ricci defect force small interior Q.

The same constraint law isolates the next obstruction. A monotone discharge with w≥0 and b≤0 necessarily leaves C(L)>0 because C(L)=-e^{-2I(L)}∫e^{2I}wb. Endpoint smoothing alone cannot produce an exact Einstein attachment. A sign-changing two-mode pilot imposed w(L)=C(L)=0 algebraically, but required max|b|≈26δ for Q0=10, δ=0.03, L=1; this demonstrates controllability in one inefficient basis, not small-defect feasibility or a lower bound. The radial quotient is a disk rather than the two-ended cylinder required by the minimal-separator theorem, although the fibre T² action remains free. Generalized Einstein Dehn filling supplies a closely related exact benchmark: the toral black-hole metric on D²×T² is Einstein and asymptotic to a hyperbolic cusp. It confirms the charged-cap geometry but caps one cusp and produces an Einstein-bearing topology, so it does not supply the desired no-Einstein graph connector.

The negative free-T² frontier has therefore moved from an incorrectly presumed resolvent closure to a global two-end matching problem. The strongest next object is a cylindrical charged throat joining asymptotics (u,H,v,w)=(-2,-1,0,0) and (2,1,0,0), with flat endpoint jets and full operator Ricci defect tending to zero. Either such a throat can exploit charge redshift while satisfying both Maxwell and horizontal moment equations, or the two-end topology and minimal separator enforce a new global identity. This remains only a symmetry-class problem; diffuse non-OT S³ necks remain untouched. The positive branch is independently at the corrected tensor-sector determinantal response problem communicated by A02: the missing quantities are the Q-channel responses q_diag and q_adj, not the superseded scalar mixed-chirality interpretation. No result in this Step proves or disproves the full sign-unrestricted, all-dimensional Yau 26 statement, and no final Lean formalization is warranted.
