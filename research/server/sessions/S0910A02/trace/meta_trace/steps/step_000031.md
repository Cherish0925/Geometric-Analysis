## Deep-Reasoning

Step 31 constructed the mathematical core of Route A: the four-dimensional noncollapsed positive dichotomy theorem. The construction has three layers of increasing conditionality, and the Kähler/non-Kähler structural partition is the central new insight.

The unconditional layer is Theorem A (Regular Limit): if a noncollapsed near-Einstein 4-sequence has no codimension-4 singular set in the GH limit, then the limit is smooth exact Einstein and Cheeger–Gromov diffeomorphism recovery gives an Einstein metric on the original smooth manifold M. The proof chain is standard (Myers → Bishop–Gromov → Cheeger–Naber C^{1,α} ∩ W^{2,q} → Ricci convergence → elliptic regularity → smooth Einstein → diffeomorphism recovery) and each link was source-verified against Cheeger–Naber 2014 and Ozuch 2021. This result is sharp: the ONLY noncollapsed obstruction to Einstein existence is the nonemptiness of the singular set.

The spectral layer computes the self-dual curvature operator R_+ = W_+ + (1/3)Id on Λ²_+ for all standard positive Einstein 4-manifolds. For Eguchi–Hanson bubbles, the first Ozuch obstruction requires det R_+(p) = 0 at each orbifold singular point (equivalently, W_+ has eigenvalue −1/3). Verified numerically: S⁴ round has det R_+ = 1/27 ≠ 0 (no EH bubbling), while CP² and S²×S² both have det R_+ = 0 (EH compatible, both Kähler-Einstein). The decisive structural observation is that ALL Kähler-Einstein 4-manifolds have W_+ eigenvalue −Λ/3 at every point (the anti-Kähler directions), so det R_+ = 0 identically and EH bubbling is always first-order compatible. For generic non-Kähler positive Einstein orbifolds, det R_+ ≠ 0 is codimension-1 open, so the first obstruction excludes EH bubbling at all points. This creates a clean partition:

• Non-Kähler generic: det R_+ ≠ 0 everywhere → no EH bubbling possible → regular limit → Einstein on M.
• Kähler-Einstein: det R_+ = 0 everywhere → EH bubbling first-order compatible → must examine higher obstructions.

The conditional layer is Theorem B (Dichotomy): under assumptions A1 (bubble-tree finiteness for near-Einstein, expected from CGB) and A2 (near-Einstein degeneration inherits exact-Einstein Ozuch obstruction), either (a) M carries Einstein, or (b) every singular point of the orbifold limit has W_+ eigenvalue −1/3. Assumption A2 is the main open bridge. The Strategist's 'exactness of both ends' argument—that the orbifold limit is exact Einstein and the ALE blow-up is exact Ricci-flat, so the obstruction should transfer through a compactness/limiting argument—is conceptually natural but not yet rigorous. The Step-30 cosmological Einstein tensor E_g is a first-order identity (detects O(δ) defect) while the Ozuch obstruction is second-order (curvature 2-jet matching), so the direct annular flux route requires either a refined second-variation identity or a different limiting argument. This distinction was sharpened in the note.

The higher-order obstruction analysis used a new Knowledge retrieval of Ozuch 2020 (cite{ozuch2020higher}, arxiv:2012.13316). This paper computes explicit polynomial obstruction equations for T⁴/Z₂ desingularization: 57 constraints for general Einstein, 84 for stable Ricci-flat. The decisive finding is that a 14-dimensional chessboard (8+8) family of EH configurations SATISFIES all 84 identified constraints. This means the known obstructions are NOT sufficient to exclude all bubble configurations—higher obstructions exist in principle (Ozuch Question 0.8) but have not been computed. Ozuch raises the question of whether every compact Einstein 4-orbifold that is a GH limit of EH-bubbling Einstein metrics must be Kähler; an affirmative answer would severely constrain counterexamples.

For the positive branch of Yau-26: the noncollapsed case is now cleanly structured. The exclusion direction (contrapositive of Ozuch obstruction) gives Einstein existence whenever the orbifold limit is conformally flat or has generic non-Kähler W_+. The hard case is the Kähler-Einstein boundary, where det R_+ = 0 everywhere and the 14-dimensional surviving family on T⁴/Z₂ shows that first-generation obstructions do not close. The tight-pinching vs. integral-nonexistence mutual exclusion (Steps 3–4) further constrains the four-dimensional refutation locus.

A complete positive-branch theorem needs BOTH noncollapsed (this step) and collapsed (Bochner center bound + almost-Killing bridge, Steps 28–29).
