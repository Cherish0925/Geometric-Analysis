By Arzelà-Ascoli on compact sets, subsequences converge to a solution f of P_eh f = Lambda eh + sum lambda_k o_k. The obstruction coefficients lambda_k are determined by the large-rho behavior f ~ H_2 + O(rho^{-2}), where H_2 is the orbifold quadratic expansion. By Ozuch's uniqueness, this gives lambda_k = c<R_pm(H_2)zeta, zeta_k>, hence R_pm zeta = 0 if the matching holds.

The gap is precisely the two-scale matching: propagating the rate h_i = O(t_i) from the matching radius rho ~ eta/sqrt(t_i) to compact sets rho = O(1). The indicial analysis on EH confirms the (2,-4) pair for the obstruction modes, with R-independent Wronskian (-6pi^2). The naive iterative interior estimate fails because elliptic constants accumulate as C^k ~ t^{-c/2} over k ~ |log t|/2 doubling scales. The exact Einstein framework avoids this via weighted Hölder norms (Biquard Section 4, Ozuch Sections 1-2), but in the pinched case, the residual w_i = h_i - t_i underline_h_2 has a cosmological source Lambda t_i^2 underline_h_2 that grows as t_i^2 rho^2, requiring absorption by the obstruction tower (underline_h_4 etc.) with uncontrolled error.

The result is a clean dichotomy: either (A) the weighted matching works, giving a counterexample classification theorem forcing all noncollapsed positive counterexample bubble points into finite degeneracy loci, or (B) it fails and wild bubbling sequences exist that bypass the obstruction classification.

The Strategist's point about the wall between Einstein endpoints was also verified and incorporated: the margin 2chi - 3|tau| is minimal at the endpoints (which are known Einstein) and increases for interior rows, so no (chi, tau, ||M||, degree)-monotone obstruction can exclude interior rows. This is structural and holds for both mirrors.

The important allocation consequence: the H4-dual, even if proved, only classifies noncollapsed counterexamples and does not resolve Yau-26. The branches that could actually decide the proposition have been untouched for ~30 steps: positive collapse (blocked at almost-Killing production since Step 29), negative collapse/diameter escape, and n >= 5. The next highest-value work should move to one of these. The Strategist specifically recommends the adversarial positive-collapse construction: try to globalize the Step-29 local twist-spike into a closed collapsing near-pinched family. Either it succeeds (producing a new counterexample channel) or the obstruction encountered becomes the proof strategy.
