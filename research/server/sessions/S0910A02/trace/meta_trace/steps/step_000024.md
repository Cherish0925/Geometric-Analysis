## Deep-Reasoning

This Explore step converted the Step-23 all-dimensional route judgment into a concrete positive-branch collapse architecture and, importantly, corrected a tempting but false generalization of the Step-6 circle-collapse obstruction. The exact Yau-26 target remains unchanged: for each dimension \(n\ge2\), decide whether some dimension-only Ricci interval forces the same smooth closed connected manifold to admit an Einstein metric; equivalently, outside the interval-containing-zero degeneracy, prove one normalized branch \(P_n^+\) or \(P_n^-\) for each \(n\), or refute by finding a fixed dimension where both signs fail on no-Einstein underlying manifolds. No proof, refutation, or Lean formalization was obtained.

The main mathematical update is the Kaluza--Klein circle-collapse correction. Step 6 had shown that a constant-length free principal \(S^1\)-connection metric over a closed 3-dimensional base cannot be exact positive Einstein: writing \(\phi=tF=*b\), the equations force \(b\) to be harmonic and simultaneously give \(\operatorname{Ric}_B(b,b)>0\), contradicting Bochner; quantitatively this obstructed the model for \(\delta<1/(1+\sqrt2)\). The strategist suggested testing whether the same obstruction extends to all odd-dimensional bases because a two-form on an odd-dimensional base has a kernel. Direct algebra shows that this extension is false. In the constant-fiber connection setting, with circle period \(2\pi\), \(d\alpha=\pi^*F\), \(U=t^{-1}\xi\), \(\phi=tF\), and the two-form norm \(|F|^2=\sum_{i<j}F_{ij}^2\), O'Neill/Kaluza--Klein gives
\[
\operatorname{Ric}_M^H=\operatorname{Ric}_B-	frac12(\phi\circ\phi),\qquad
\operatorname{Ric}_M(U,U)=	frac12|\phi|^2,\qquad
\operatorname{Ric}_M^{mix}=\pm	frac12d_B^*\phi .
\]
Thus \(\operatorname{Ric}_M=g_M\) in this constant-length class is equivalent to \(|\phi|^2=2\), \(d_B^*\phi=0\), and \(\operatorname{Ric}_B=g_B+	frac12\phi\circ\phi\), with \(d\phi=0\) automatic from connection closure rather than an extra Einstein equation. The product base \(B=S^2_{\operatorname{Ric}=2}	imes N^d_{\operatorname{Ric}=1}\), \(\phi=\sqrt2\,\omega_{S^2}\), satisfies these equations for every closed normalized positive Einstein \(N^d\), \(d\ge2\). For \(d=1\), i.e. total dimension four, the missing equation is \(\operatorname{Ric}_{S^1}=g_{S^1}\), impossible; Step 6 in fact proves the stronger obstruction for any closed 3-dimensional base in this model. Hence codimension-one positive Einstein collapse exists in \(n=3\) and every \(n\ge5\), but the Step-6 Bochner mechanism is genuinely a three-base/four-total-dimensional phenomenon.
