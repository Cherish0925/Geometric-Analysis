## Deep-Reasoning

Step 77 repaired a consequential mathematical error in the positive mixed-row product-orbifold branch. The full Yau--26 goal remains far beyond this local desingularization problem, but this branch matters because it tests whether tight positive near-Einstein geometries near (S^2 x S^2)/Z2 can lead to Einstein metrics on mixed smooth types such as 2CP2#4CPbar2, 3CP2#3CPbar2, and #3(S^2 x S^2). The central correction is that Ozuch's space O(g) is the full L2 kernel of P = 1/2 nabla^*nabla - Rcirc on all symmetric two-tensors, not the TT kernel. The axial first harmonics u1 and u2 on the two round S^2 factors descend under the half-turn, satisfy Delta u_i = 2 Lambda u_i, and give P(u_i g_j)=0 for i,j in {1,2}. Thus the product quotient has a four-dimensional scalar-sector full kernel even though the TT kernel is zero and the relative tensor g1-g2 is a bounded negative eigenmode with P(g1-g2) = -Lambda(g1-g2). The scientific lesson is structural: TT invertibility is favorable for analysis, but it does not remove Ozuch's finite-dimensional projections. The four scalar modes may be pure gauge or removable after a Bianchi/diffeomorphism normalization, yet that normalization must be derived and inserted into the reduced equations rather than silently assumed.

The step also separated several orders that had been conflated. The rank-one local curvature block R+ = R- = diag(Lambda,0,0) still gives a real local simplification: in-kernel Eguchi--Hanson directions make all first obstructions lambda_k^j(phi_j,0) vanish, and Ozuch's displayed determinant formula proves only the bare local mu_1^j vanishes. It does not prove mu_2^j or mu_3^j vanish, and it says nothing about corrections from the scalar kernel, local coordinate normalization, or inter-node Green response. A source bubble contributes H^4(t_j phi_j) = t_j^2 H^4(phi_j); its regular effect at a different node changes the receiver first obstruction and enters the finite equation as t_i t_j^2 I_ij,k, which is order t^3 for comparable scales and is not the local order-t_i^2 mu_i term. This makes the next mathematical object a sliced multipole-to-curvature response computed with the full product P pseudo-inverse, with additive separated denominators or an equivalent projected heat/massive-resolvent representation, not a product of static one-factor Green functions and not Ozuch's local second obstruction.

The execution in this step produced reproducible algebraic evidence for the corrected understanding. The script step77_slice_decomposition_verify.py confirmed the scalar kernel and the Bianchi/pure-gauge decomposition: B(a u1 g1 + b u1 g2) = b du1, B(c u2 g1 + d u2 g2) = c du2, and L_{grad u_i} g = -2 Lambda u_i g_i. The script step77_scalar_kernel_and_pairing.py computed the raw product normal-coordinate pairing with the four scalar modes, giving -pi^2/18 at a north-north node in the chosen normalization. TTCS then found that this raw number is a coordinate artifact at quadratic Bianchi order; this was verified by step77_bianchi_defect_verify.py and step77_bianchi_cancellation_check.py, which show B(H2) = -(2/3)x^flat for the raw product quadratic tensor, B(L_X e) = +(2/3)x^flat for X^a = -(1/18)r^2 x^a, and a pairing correction +pi^2/18, so the Bianchi-corrected local quadratic scalar pairing is zero. The raw number is therefore only an algebraic regression test for the unsliced coordinates, not evidence for a genuine scalar projection at that order. This strengthens the construction by forcing the next work to compute the true sliced inter-node response rather than relying on misleading local coefficients.

The Step 76 note and peer announcement were corrected. The statements that O(g_o)=0, that the nu_l system is empty, and that all second-order obstructions vanish were withdrawn. The note Sessions/S0910A01/workspace/notes/step76_mixed_row_reduced_obstruction_map.md was overwritten with a correction, and the main repaired record is Sessions/S0910A01/workspace/notes/step77_mixed_row_obstruction_repair.md. Peer messages #521 and #524 communicated the correction to the session group and aligned with A02's product-resolvent warning. The branch remains valuable, but its honest mathematical form is now a coupled, sliced finite-dimensional problem: include or remove the scalar full-kernel modes by a derived slice, compute the regular inter-node multipole-to-curvature map with the correct product resolvent, then evaluate the local mu_2 and mu_3 formulas only for the actual effective jets. This is a stronger and more faithful route toward a possible positive Einstein construction on an open mixed row, while preserving the distinction from the original all-dimensional sign-unrestricted Yau--26 proposition.
