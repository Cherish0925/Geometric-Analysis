A massive one-factor reduction is valid only after proving one factor has genuinely been projected to a fixed eigenspace.

Therefore the next coefficient calculation must use closed forms, heat-kernel/Ewald/Abel summation, or certified tail bounds; a raw truncated harmonic sum cannot decide vanishing or nonvanishing.

They must either be included as finite-dimensional equations or removed by an explicitly derived reduced slice with its compatibility conditions. Second, the inter-node regular jet is not simply Ozuch's local mu. At equal scales this is order t^3, not the local t_j^2 mu_{j,k}. The next object should therefore be called a sliced multipole-to-curvature interaction/Green-response map, with explicit order bookkeeping.
