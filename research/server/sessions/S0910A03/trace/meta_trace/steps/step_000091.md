Using the O'Neill/Koszul formula for the horizontal-vertical connection, the twist energy Q contributes exactly Q to |II|² through the off-diagonal terms II(e_T,e_α)=(ε/2)√G_{αα}f^α. Combined with the second variation φ=1 on the stable minimal separator, this gives ⟨|II|²⟩≤3+δ and hence Q_C≤3+δ. The separator floor becomes ⟨e_s⟩≥12−8δ+2Q_C, with the coefficient 2 exact.

TTCS corrected this: the ceiling B(δ) was derived under Q=0 (exact OT), and with Q>0 the modified ceiling grows faster than the floor. The derivative of the twisted ceiling with respect to Q+ is ~8.86 near (δ*,0), while the floor derivative with respect to Q_C is only 2. So the resolvent-floor combination provides no extra Q_C constraint beyond stability. This is an important negative result that prevents a false closure.

Starting from dr²+f²dφ²+a²(dx²+dy²) and substituting φ=θ+kx reproduces the charged radial ansatz with G_xx=k²f²+a², A_θ=kf²/(k²f²+a²). At large cusp distance, α→(1/2)log(1+k²)=const, so the orbit anisotropy is just the rotated torus identification.

The curvature-energy tradeoff is a new structural observation: R(q_r)=−2σ_zz−(3/2)σ_z²−(1/2)e_s^(z), confirmed symbolically to match the Step 88 identity exactly. At a turning point H_r=0, the contracted Gauss requires R(q_r)≥6−4δ>0, but orbit energy pushes R negative. This means the flat-slice barrier and the Bochner analysis are structurally coupled: z-variation that makes slices positively curved (needed at the turning point) competes with the orbit energy that the separator floor demands. The turning point and separator are at different r-values, so this is a redistribution constraint rather than a pointwise contradiction.

A01's positive-branch result (#668) is significant: universal B_same=0 and mixed-diagonal trace obstruction Δ_tr=0.503t²>0. If the convergence ladder confirms, the mixed desingularization is perturbatively obstructed. This does not yet prove global Einstein nonexistence (non-perturbative metrics may exist), but it identifies candidate smooth 4-manifolds for the positive branch. The gap from perturbative obstruction to global nonexistence requires either a moduli-space argument or a topological exclusion.

The current negative-branch frontier is genuinely z-dependent geometry on the free cylindrical base. The established constraints — stability (Q_C≤3+δ), floor (⟨e_s⟩≥12−8δ+2Q_C), Bochner (exact OT obstruction), curvature-energy tradeoff (R(q_r) requires area concavity) — are all verified structural results, but none individually closes the gap for non-OT metrics. The decisive next question is whether the coupled (area, orbit, Maxwell, Bochner) system permits sufficient energy redistribution between the turning region and the separator for all constraints to hold simultaneously.
