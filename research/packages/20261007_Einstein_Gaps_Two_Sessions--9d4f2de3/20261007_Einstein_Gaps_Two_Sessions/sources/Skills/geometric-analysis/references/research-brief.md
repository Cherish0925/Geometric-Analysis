# Scientific scope

Prepared from the owner's 3 October 2026 direction and the accumulated
research texts. The subject is **Geometric Analysis**, not a project number
or the name of one historical research line.

> Curvature-equation realizability and its quantitative structure:
> topological constraints, actual fields, and extremal geometry.

Given a smooth manifold with topological, field, and boundary data, study
which curvature conditions a metric can satisfy, which it can approach,
the optimum of the relevant curvature functionals, and the geometry of
equality and limiting sequences. Four-dimensional Einstein geometry is
the principal developed setting; self-dual forms, gauge fields, and
simultaneous actual classes provide different entrances to the analysis.

Topology gives global relations. Actual fields realize these relations as
geometric objects that can be differentiated and integrated. The metric,
its curvature, and the covariant derivatives of the fields must satisfy
coupled identities. Their simultaneous compatibility can force a cost
that numerical curvature or characteristic-number inequalities alone do
not detect. The same problem also requires extremal constructions,
variational analysis, degeneration, and exact existence.

The purpose of retaining the complete research corpus is to continue this
mathematics without losing earlier ideas or repeatedly reconstructing
proved intermediate results. The current endpoint and optimal-cost
questions are important tests, not definitions of the whole subject.
New natural inputs, different proofs, and new geometric realizations are
welcome when they change a concrete mathematical conclusion.

Rigidity is a conclusion to establish. Optimality concerns all competitors
in the stated class. Existence requires a construction or a sufficient
global analytical argument. These three roles can interact without being
identified with one another.

The portable library therefore retains the complete current manuscript,
original mathematical sources, earlier constructions and corrections,
both web research contributions, and literature connections. Working
instructions are short; extensive source material is retrieved as needed.
