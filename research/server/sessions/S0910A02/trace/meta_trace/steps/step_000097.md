The main structural advance is the variational reframing. The reduced obstruction equation in an Einstein gluing problem should come from a Lyapunov--Schmidt reduction of the normalized total scalar curvature / Einstein-Hilbert functional: solve the complement equation, obtain a finite-dimensional function of the kernel parameters, and set its gradient to zero. This is not merely a philosophical point. It imposes algebraic constraints on the P/Q response map developed in Steps 95--96. The quartic leading potential has the form, schematically,
\[f_4=\sum_{i<j} c_{ij}|\xi_i|^2|\xi_j|^2+\operatorname{Re}(q_{ij}\xi_i^2\overline{\xi_j}^2)+\cdots,\]
so the reduced equation is a gradient, \(\partial f_4/\partial\overline{\xi_j}=P_j\xi_j+Q_j\overline{\xi_j}\), up to convention factors. This immediately gives cheap but essential diagnostics: the isotropic coefficients must be real and symmetric in a common variational normalization, anisotropic pair coefficients must satisfy reciprocity \(q_{ji}=\overline{q_{ij}}\) after frame transport, and Euler homogeneity implies that a nonzero critical point of the quartic leading functional must lie on the zero level \(f_4=0\). These are stronger and more global constraints than a single receiver-node determinant.

This reframing corrects a significant weakness in the Step-96 handoff. The expression \(|q_{\mathrm{diag}}|\) versus \(|2q_{\mathrm{adj}}+q_{\mathrm{diag}}|\) is not the general Gate-3 decision; it silently fixes equal scales and a special phase convention. The kernel-plane phases are actual gluing parameters, modulo only the effective torus action and frame identifications. Thus their Q contributions can sweep a disk of magnitudes rather than a single fixed value. This makes the finite-dimensional question better posed as: does the real quartic potential, including scale, phase, and ambient deformation variables, have a zero critical point on the physical configuration space? That question can lean toward solvability rather than obstruction, especially because mixed patterns have more phase-tunable Q channels than the compatible KE row.
