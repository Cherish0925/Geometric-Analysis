## Deep-Reasoning

This step was a substantive mathematical review and repair of the restricted four-dimensional negative Ishida–LeBrun cutoff-flow route, centered on the common-time continuation interface from Step 56. The main scientific correction is that the raw overlap uniqueness argument in Step 56 cannot be used: the ungauged Ricci linearization contains second-order diffeomorphism terms, so the metric difference is not governed by a coercive weighted tensor heat operator. I patched `workspace/notes/step56_common_time_and_terminal_compactness.md`, `workspace/plans/route_assessment_step56.md`, and `workspace/scripts/step56_common_time_terminal_verify.py` so they no longer present raw ungauged L2 uniqueness as a proof mechanism. The corrected Step 56 structural script now passes 22/22 with the statement that raw uniqueness is not used.

I developed a replacement in `workspace/notes/step57_preceptor_deTurck_uniqueness_and_scope_review.md`. In the transported-cutoff DeTurck variables, comparing two smooth pairs `(G_i, beta_i)` with differences `H=G_1-G_2` and `b=beta_1-beta_2` gives a plausible coupled energy using the averaged principal coefficient `bar A^{ab}=1/2(beta_1^2 G_1^{ab}+beta_2^2 G_2^{ab})` and dissipation `int (beta_1^2+beta_2^2)|DH|^2`. TTCS verified the key point: the metric Lie-cross term and scalar transport coupling can be handled on a smooth per-datum overlap, and the averaged cutoff weight is essential. TTCS also found a hidden gap: pair uniqueness alone does not automatically give branch-preserving uniqueness for arbitrary geometric solutions unless a reverse geometric-to-pair gauge lemma is proved. I therefore rewrote the continuation interface to avoid needing that uniqueness: one can use existence-style restart from a smooth preterminal slice, or, more strongly, A03 Step 60’s all-order route giving a smooth endpoint `g_*` and a uniqueness-free splice by autonomous time-jet matching.

I read A03 Step 60’s `step60_all_order_continuation_and_terminal_chart.md`, its enumeration script, and its data. This was important new peer evidence. A03 independently rejected the same ungauged uniqueness shortcut by an explicit principal-symbol counterexample and proposed a stronger all-order continuation route. The final A03 enumeration records 252,256 derivative-allocation checks through order 64 with zero failures after adding coefficient-before-commutator and time-reordering subfamilies. TTCS verified the two main algebraic conversions, `(B_1*B_1)T=4(C_1*C_1)U` and `aT_m=U_m-sum_{q=1}^m B_q*T_{m-q}`, as genuine ways to remove dangerous unweighted current terms. It also verified that the Liouville pullback selection, strong `W^{2,q}` convergence, `L^1` density passage, and Derdziński forbidden-germ endpoint are suitable for terminal contradiction. I nevertheless preserved the remaining mathematical work: A03 Step 60 should not be used unchanged until the base `T,U` equations remove independent nonzero forcing terms such as `a*1` or `a^2` (since `T=U=0` is stationary), the induction starts from the already established base layer `T_1,U_1,C_2 in L^q` rather than applying the same residual estimate at `m=1`, the lower commutator remainders are written as strict lower-order polynomial families, and the fixed-background `A=nabla^g-barnabla` Sobolev induction is written with its product estimates.

A second correction concerned theorem scope. Step 56 had temporarily regressed toward a topology-dependent threshold, and A03 Step 59 had revived the previously withdrawn shrinking-with-topology loss. I corrected the A01 notes to preserve the actual strength of the assembled IL terminal argument: `int Phi_i <= C delta_i^2 A_i` and `Vol(P_i) >= c_0 A_i` select a pullback chart with mean density `O(delta_i^2)`, so the factor `A_i` cancels. Conditional on uniform support, flow, chart, and terminal constants over the stated IL class, the result is a class-uniform negative pinching gap for input metrics. It is not a statement that the topology lacks a negative Einstein output metric, and it is not a dimension-only Yau-26 theorem. The positive branch, other four-dimensional topologies, dimensions `n>=5`, and Lean formalization remain unsolved.
