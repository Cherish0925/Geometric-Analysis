## Deep-Reasoning

Step 52 corrected the analytic direction after the Strategist note showed that Step 51's conclusion could not be carried forward. The key mathematical lesson is that the normalization term in the cutoff flow has two distinct effects that must not be conflated. Constant homothetic scaling gives the formal coefficient (2p-n) in an unshifted L^p curvature energy, and this coefficient vanishes at p=n/2. But the cutoff coefficient is spatially nonconstant, and the conformal variation creates the source g\owedge Hess(a). This source is not a curvature-multiple term and cannot be made homogeneous in the unshifted critical energy by the integration by parts used in Step 51. The explicit flat-torus test makes this decisive: from flat T^4 and nonconstant chi, the normalized flow immediately creates curvature, so any homogeneous Gronwall statement for E(t)=int |Rm|^2 would force E identically zero and is false. The script `workspace/scripts/step52_flat_cutoff_forcing_test.py` verifies the exact coefficient for chi=(1+cos x1)/2 on period-2pi T^4: |g\owedge Hess(a)|^2=12(a'')^2, int_0^{2pi}(a'')^2=pi/2, hence int_{T^4}|Rm(g(t))|^2=48*pi^4 t^2+O(t^3). This exposes the true failure in Step 51: after one integration by parts, absorption by a F^{p-2}|nabla Rm|^2 leaves (|da|^2/a)F^{p-2}, not F^p; a further integration by parts restores Delta_g a, whose control is precisely part of the evolving cutoff-jet problem.

The step then tried to replace the invalid energy proof by using Guoyi Xu's detailed proof of Yang's local Ricci-flow start. This was scientifically useful because it separated arbitrary smooth per-datum existence from Yang's later small-critical-energy smoothing theorem. Xu's Section 2, downloaded as `staging/downloads/0907.5604.pdf` and converted to `staging/processing/xu_local_ricci_flow.txt`, gives a fixed-point proof for the degenerate local Ricci-DeTurck equation. In the frozen strictly parabolic map, adding the algebraic reaction -2 chi^2 V really is harmless: it does not change the principal symbol, introduces no derivatives of V, is independent of epsilon, preserves the eigenvalue/positivity estimates after reducing time, adds only bounded terms to fixed-background Sobolev energies, and changes the contraction inequality only by an extra bounded coefficient. TTCS independently confirmed these fixed-coordinate estimates and the flat-torus rejection of Step 51.

However, TTCS also found a decisive error in the attempted geometric conclusion. A fixed Eulerian cutoff in the gauge-fixed metric equation does not pull back to the same fixed cutoff: phi^*(a ghat)=(a o phi) phi^*ghat, not a phi^*ghat. Thus the metric-only Xu fixed-point repair gives a flow with transported cutoff in geometric coordinates, not the desired fixed-cutoff equation partial_t g=-2a(Ric+g). This restores the central start problem, but in a sharper form. The algebraic reaction is not the obstacle; the obstacle is the transported coefficient needed for exact pullback. The correct unknowns should include ahat=a o phi^{-1}, with a transport equation partial_t ahat = ahat W(ahat) when the DeTurck vector is -ahat W, coupled to the metric equation partial_t ghat=-2 ahat(Ric_ghat+ghat)+L_{ahat W}ghat. Pulling this coupled system back gives the fixed coefficient a in the geometric flow. The remaining work is to prove existence for this coupled metric-coefficient system, either by adapting Yang's transported-pair smooth-tame inverse or by extending Xu's fixed-point method to the transported scalar coefficient. The positive-time shifted T/U/C continuation from Step 50 remains conceptually valuable but cannot be used until this exact start exists.

A02 message #380 was incorporated as positive-branch context: mixed product-quotient rows remain a positive-branch hardness wall, not a refutation; Q-Gorenstein smoothing via Aubin-Yau applies to negative/general-type product quotients but not directly to the positive S2xS2 mixed rows. This does not alter the negative-flow start problem pursued here, but it reinforces that the full Yau 26 problem remains much broader than the restricted IL route.
