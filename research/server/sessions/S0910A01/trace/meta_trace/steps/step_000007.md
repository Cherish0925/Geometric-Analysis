## Deep-Reasoning

This step produced a significant structural advance and then a critical self-correction. The advance is a complete 9-step chain excluding noncollapsed smooth regions from any hypothetical near-boundary negative-pinching sequence on LeBrun blow-ups.

The step began by developing three analytic tools from the Strategist's observations: (1) the Cheeger-Naber harmonic-radius bridge under bounded Ricci alone (Remark 1.11 and Theorem 1.13 give measure-theoretic harmonic-radius control on noncollapsed balls without Einstein or derivative-Ricci assumptions); (2) W^{2,p} convergence on harmonic charts, exploiting the specific structure of the Ricci tensor in harmonic coordinates as a quasilinear elliptic system; (3) the volume-random basepoint averaging argument that eliminates spatial escape for noncollapsed regions.

The first analysis concluded that the Derdziński local conformal-Kähler mechanism fails on noncompact pointed limits because the conformal factor f satisfying the scalar curvature matching equation Δf = −f/2 can be nonconstant. This led to the (wrong) conclusion that the negative-branch argument is inherently global, requiring compact Armstrong/Stokes mechanisms.

The TTCS verifier corrected this with a deeper insight: the Derdziński conformal factor is NOT an arbitrary solution of the scalar equation. It is uniquely determined by the Kähler integrability condition as g_K = C|w|^{2/3}g, where w is the lowest eigenvalue of W⁺. When the eigenvalues are constant (which is forced by near-equality: ξ = w + √(2/3)|W⁺| → 0 and |W⁺| = 1/(2√6) constant on the smooth limit), the factor |w|^{1/3} is constant. Therefore g is locally Kähler, and Kähler-Einstein with Ric = −g forces |W⁺|² = 2/3, contradicting the calibrated 1/24. This IS a local contradiction, not a global one.

The TTCS verifier also corrected the Bochner formula coefficients from (s/3, −4) to (s/2, −6), changing the calibrated |∇W⁺|² from 1/24 to 1/16. S0910A03 independently confirmed these coefficients. The qualitative conclusions are unchanged: the calibrated profile is Bochner-consistent but not parallel.

The complete noncollapsed-exclusion chain is: near-equality → tensor slack ∫ξ ≤ 4π²D_a → volume-random basepoint selects balls with vanishing ξ → Cheeger-Naber harmonic radius on noncollapsed balls → W^{2,p} → smooth Einstein limit with ξ = 0 and constant |W⁺| → Derdziński factor constant → Kähler → |W⁺|² = 2/3 ≠ 1/24. Every noncollapsed smooth region is contradicted.

The remaining gap for the negative branch is whether a near-pinching LeBrun sequence can be entirely collapsed. The Weyl profile filter excludes all known collapse models (product surfaces have |W⁺| = 2/√6, free S¹ has χ = 0 versus χ ≈ 2A/3 for the LeBrun manifolds). But unknown collapse modes with the calibrated Weyl profile are not yet excluded.

A critical caveat: the claim g_K = C|w|^{2/3}g requires primary-source verification against Derdziński's 1983 Compositio paper. The Numdam issue page was accessible in Step 6 but the paper was not downloaded. If the conformal factor is not |w|^{1/3}, the local argument fails and the route reverts to the global Armstrong mechanism (which still works on compact manifolds but cannot be applied to noncompact pointed limits). This source-verification is the single most consequential next action.

For the positive branch: S0910A02 retracted the overly strong noncollapsed theorem but confirmed Cheeger-Naber measure-theoretic regularity and developed a Bochner obstruction for constant-fiber S¹ collapse. S0910A03 confirmed the Weyl profile filter on collapse models. The positive-branch remaining gaps are curvature concentration and general collapse, with rewound noncollapse on universal covers as the most promising strategy.
