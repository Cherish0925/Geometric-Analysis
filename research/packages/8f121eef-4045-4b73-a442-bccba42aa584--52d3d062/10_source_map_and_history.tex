\hypertarget{september-continuation-the-interior-need-not-have-a-normal-form}{%
\subsection{28 September continuation: the interior need not have a
normal
form}\label{september-continuation-the-interior-need-not-have-a-normal-form}}

The
\href{../continuation/20260928_localization_and_coupled_filling.md}{localized
coefficient theorem} uses the full relative moments before optimizing
the Einstein constant. Weighted Cauchy retains an exact nonnegative
remainder. This proves coefficient four for arbitrary interior metrics
confined to a vanishing Q-scale amount of CH background and
characterizes equality by whole-region volume, scalar and positive-Weyl
energy. Subtracting closed identities removes the need to identify
individual cores. An intrinsic bulk integral criterion and a
non-Einstein exterior version further isolate the missing selection
problem. No selection from unrestricted near-minimizers is claimed.

Continuing the actual construction test gives a global radial moment of
the full Ricci residual. Its exact orthogonal-square decomposition rules
out every diagonal almost-Kahler one-bolt cell without a conformal
change, not just small anisotropy or favorable pointwise signs. The
conformal case is reduced to a quantified signed gradient window. A
fixed improvement requires inverse-bolt-parameter net gradient
concentration; it has not been shown that the full equations prohibit or
realize that window.

The \href{../continuation/20260928_signed_quartic_flux.md}{global
torsion calculation} also moved past the prior principal-symbol
obstacle: the complete signed flux admits a derivative-free
curvature-square completion. Stokes transfers its cubic form into an
explicit bulk coupling, whose calibrated curvature has a computable
non-small quadratic contribution. A further exact central-curvature
Stokes cancellation and weighted Lichnerowicz identity remove the
determinant and scalar derivative payments altogether. Only the
trace-free Ricci interaction remains, but the original positive
characteristic budget cancels too. The resulting square difference
requires a new strict signed estimate using actual field provenance;
smallness of the Ricci payment alone would not settle the endpoint.

After I53 settled shrinking C0 tubes, repeating a fixed finite surgery

\href{../continuation/20260928_relative_cell_cost.md}{cell proof} rules
out the full conformally Kähler and biaxial routes, not just

specified nonconformal triaxial class. General cell budgets and the
absence of smooth fixed-collar stationary points identify what an
unrestricted construction still has to overcome. They do not determine
its infimum.

In parallel, the

calculation} showed that the cubic torsion already fixes the
almost-complex bracket; the current terms cancel without assuming
closure. Its curvature forcing is only trace-free Ricci. Following the
identity through the adjoint test, however, retains a symmetric torsion
Hessian. A reproducible Dirac jet checks that this particular symbol is
not identically zero. The next question is its global signed absorption,
not another derivation of the ideal bracket. No general endpoint or
all-metric coefficient is claimed.

\hypertarget{september-continuation-the-failed-gauge-is-bypassed-by-matched-caps}{%
\subsection{28 September continuation: the failed gauge is bypassed by
matched
caps}\label{september-continuation-the-failed-gauge-is-bypassed-by-matched-caps}}

The negative exact-slice result was not the stopping point. The
\href{../continuation/20260928_intrinsic_caps_and_stability.md}{I53
proof} instead modifies only a filling metric. Localized DeTurck
smoothing retains the actual outer collar and controls critical
curvature energy, producing a smooth cap with cost \(C(E_0+a^2)\).
Euler's identity supplies the global curvature budget without scalar
normalization. Selecting bands by energy and amplitude, then retaining
the bad cores instead of pretending to fill them, gives an explicit
partial-blowdown inequality.

Consequently the leading infimum is four in every shrinking C0 tube
around selected actual recoveries, even modulo arbitrary orientation-
preserving diffeomorphisms. The result tolerates a vanishing fraction of
unstructured cores and mean-square annular amplitudes. It therefore
changes the variational scope, not just the description of I52. The
unresolved step is now extraction of enough marked annuli from
unrestricted near-minimizers, or construction of geometry outside this
scope. No universal decomposition is presumed.

\hypertarget{september-a-marked-variable-cannot-be-removed-by-a-fixed-exact-slice}{%
\subsection{28 September: a marked variable cannot be removed by a fixed
exact
slice}\label{september-a-marked-variable-cannot-be-removed-by-a-fixed-exact-slice}}

The attempt to remove I52's gauge assumption produced a genuine
geometric obstruction,
\href{../continuation/20260928_marked_centers_and_harmonic_slices.md}{X09},
not simply an unavailable elliptic estimate. Move the CH background by a
local isometry near a retained exceptional point, keep the same marked
shrinking donor, and normalize. The resulting metrics are C0-near and
both have recovery coefficient four. A bounded-distortion exact Bianchi
map would limit to the unique harmonic map moving that point; the
shrinking exceptional class forbids the move.

The continuation did not stop at this negative result. Relative
normalization and the prior conformal Hessian estimate show that the
same examples have small actual gauge residual and remain covered by
I52's approximate criterion. Moving-center references absorb them
exactly, and the uniform estimate extends over compact configuration
families and their conformal/diffeomorphism saturations. These changes
identify the right distinction between an exact normal form and a usable
stability estimate. They do not determine the all-metric infimum.

In parallel, the next opposite-spinor Ricci equation was derived and
tested. Its scalar phase comparison cancels rather than creates a radial
coercive term; a derivative-free transgression retains a
curvature-gradient interaction. This calculation is preserved as a
continuation point, not a new endpoint theorem.

\hypertarget{the-initial-separation-obstruction-input-and-unrestricted-output}{%
\subsection{1. The initial separation: obstruction, input, and
unrestricted
output}\label{the-initial-separation-obstruction-input-and-unrestricted-output}}

The early research already had several genuinely different centers.
Exact Einstein topology was constrained by monopole curvature
inequalities and a uniform near-equality contradiction. Marked \(A_1\)
orbifold resolutions used corrected cores and logarithmic Weyl matching.
Fixed Dehn-twist bundles used slow interpolation and balanced collars.
Global work examined \(E_D\) attainment, hyperbolic graph-manifold
connectors, algebraic smoothing, positive torus models, and
higher-dimensional propagation.

The organizing distinction was mathematical: a pointwise-small input is
not an exact solution, and failure of one repair is not absence of all
exact outputs on the smooth manifold. The older
\href{../mathematics/current/skill/references/research-map.md}{three-line
map} retains the individual Session provenance and detailed branches.

The Fable supplement strengthened verification and supplied OT
critical-point and finite-end topology analysis, explicit two-pole
smooth fillings, and a negative GH-separation application. It also
exposed a false global inference: flattening an AH end does not match
its growing physical sections to fixed cusp blocks. The retained growing
core violates the fixed-topology volume budget. This corrected the
proposed W-to-closed-counterexample implication, not the independent
\(A_1\), \(E_D\) or rigidity results.

\hypertarget{intrinsic-integral-rigidity-replaced-one-missing-geometric-bridge}{%
\subsection{2. Intrinsic integral rigidity replaced one missing
geometric
bridge}\label{intrinsic-integral-rigidity-replaced-one-missing-geometric-bridge}}

The first fundamental change in the subsequent research was a different
proof architecture. Rather than first recover uniformly controlled
charts from arbitrarily collapsed approximate metrics, actual weighted
monopoles provide bounded fields directly on the original metrics. A
covariant Hessian estimate and polynomial Weyl model make a weak-Bianchi
self-test legitimate. The critical model gives incompatible integral
evaluations.

This produces the all-sign relative integral gap on specified topology,
without a uniform Sobolev, noncollapse or full-curvature assumption. The
special-class formulation records which part of the gauge input pays for
the determinant-connection energy. Formal cohomology vectors cannot
replace actual solutions. See
\href{../mathematics/current/round4_rigidity.md}{Round 4},
\href{../mathematics/current/round4_all_sign_gap.md}{all signs}, and
\href{../mathematics/current/round5_rigidity.md}{Round 5}.

The former local-flow/common-time and unfolding problems remain
meaningful analysis, but they no longer block this particular monopole
application. The proof change is lost if the latest theory is described
as only an improved exact-Einstein threshold.

\hypertarget{complete-models-repair-obstructions-and-new-closed-topology}{%
\subsection{3. Complete models, repair obstructions, and new closed
topology}\label{complete-models-repair-obstructions-and-new-closed-topology}}

The construction line developed a complete charged double-cylinder
Einstein family, retaining boundary response and nonlinear charge.
Independently, whole-fibre receivers enlarged the \(E_D\) repair
exclusion to a linear-size ball allowing every tensor mode. These are
not opposite answers to one question: their spaces, asymptotics and
correction classes differ.

The next major input construction separated two sources of topology.
Long collars carry mapping-class variation; distinct collapsed throats
carry integer shear. This produced actual fixed closed small-defect
manifolds, including self-gluings that are not surface bundles and have
no Kähler finite cover. Both orientation-sensitive gauge data and global
group structure could then be studied on the \textbf{same objects as the
input metrics}.

The accompanying global theory calculated ordinary basic-class sets and
variational values, and forced any Einstein output to have no nonzero
local Killing germ. It did not forbid a small nonsymmetric correction.
The \href{../mathematics/current/SYNTHESIS.md}{Rounds 1--12 synthesis}
preserves the full progression and the exact distinctions among complete
models, approximate noncompact ends and closed assemblies.

At the same time, the exact free-circle conformal-boundary theorem
settled a broader connector class than the old OT analysis. Explicit
approximate two-ended families showed why exact exclusion need not imply
a uniform approximate gap under degeneration. This is a scientific
contrast between equations, not a contradiction.

On the gauge side,

On the repair side, an actual waist mode explained inverse degeneration.
Optimized Maxwell profiles gave smaller pointwise residuals, but also
retained signed waist and fibre forces. A full-tensor repair exclusion
and componentwise Einstein-volume separation gave different
obstructions, both local to their stated regimes. Exact neck and
exterior responses then made the missing matching conditions explicit.

Integral rank-two balance and Schwarz-P rank-three balance supplied
legitimate periodic shear data. Their cancellation concerns a fibre TT
projection, not the waist equation or the entire Einstein system. These
arguments remain in \href{../mathematics/current/round16.md}{Rounds

program.

At monopole ratio one, genuine hyperbolic products exist. Consequently
the old forbidden-profile argument could not simply be reused with a

structure near a sharp volume endpoint. For exact candidates it led to
bounded-curvature degeneration, expanding regions of product geometry,
and exclusion of irreducible Hilbert modular cusp cores by an actual
embedding argument.

Reducible product limits remained; and metrics at other volumes
remained. This scope is essential to the meaning of
\href{../mathematics/current/round16_rigidity.md}{product propagation}
and \href{../mathematics/current/round16_global.md}{cusp-core
exclusion}.

The subsequent independent directions prevented this one model from
absorbing all three lines:

The fixed-piece hyperbolic-cover construction gave no-Einstein manifolds

Marked higher-ADE chirality checks and the already known

\hypertarget{a-common-analytic-compatibility-theorem-became-explicit}{%
\subsection{7. A common analytic compatibility theorem became

\href{../mathematics/current/round21_analytic.md}{Round 21} separated
the analytic theorem from its spinor supplier. For bounded smooth
two-forms it selected the allowable rank-one Weyl amplitudes, using the

The
\href{../mathematics/current/line1_integral_rigidity_paper.md}{closed

applications and the Ricci-flat branch. The
\href{../mathematics/current/round21_comparison.md}{LeBrun comparison}
is an exact-obstruction-to-quantitative-stability strengthening in its

The cscK equality correction was decisive: a LeBrun functional can be
exactly saturated while trace-free Ricci is nonzero. The
\href{../mathematics/current/line1_kahler_energy.md}{Kähler energy

separately. The later
\href{../mathematics/current/line1_composed_application.md}{actual-form

\hypertarget{two-independent-developments-now-produce-new-global-consequences}{%
\subsection{8. Two independent developments now produce new global
consequences}\label{two-independent-developments-now-produce-new-global-consequences}}

First, the
\href{../mathematics/current/line1_functional_pencil.md}{functional
pencil} made different curvature combinations available. Optimizing it
produced an
\href{../mathematics/current/line1_energy_envelope_gap.md}{energy lower
envelope}. Using the other Weyl half supplied an independent upper
budget, creating a
\href{../mathematics/current/line1_two_chirality_topology.md}{two-chirality
gap} on realized topologies outside the older band. Its
\href{../mathematics/current/line1_bauer_furuta_geography.md}{Bauer--Furuta
application} then enlarged the actual gauge/topological category;
ordinary SW vanishing and absence of almost-complex structures do not
remove these refined inputs. The
\href{../mathematics/current/line1_compactified_pencil.md}{compactified
parameter theorem} also tightens the upper energy boundary, with
explicit necessary endpoint decay.

Second, the equation itself supplied derivative budgets for allowed Weyl
geometry. The initial exact-output theorem allowed diameter growth or
opposite-Weyl concentration. The
\href{../mathematics/current/line1_aspherical_no_bubbles.md}{aspherical
core obstruction} handled both sphere-carrying ALE cores and
rational-ball quotients; it removed that concentration escape on the
actual shear family. Under the retained relative spectral condition,
splitting tending to zero now forces diameter divergence. This is not an
unrestricted Einstein nonexistence theorem.

These developments illustrate two different ways to raise the theory's
level: an independent budget can change the feasible topology region; or
global topology can remove a degeneration mechanism. Neither requires
erasing the older successful constructions or declaring all unresolved
arrows proved.

The \href{../continuation/20260923_compact_realization.md}{new
compact-regime continuation} uses this latter topology in an actual
realization theorem. It passes from pointwise almost-Einstein metrics,
not just exact sequences, to a smooth exact output on the same smooth
manifold. A diameter bound, volume lower bound and supercritical
one-sided Weyl bound remain explicit. The new regularity argument
normalizes harmonic radius, obtains full energy from Jiang--Naber, and
forces any blowup to be an impossible embedded ASD ALE core. This is a
concrete obstruction-to-existence bridge in a restricted compact regime,
not a general obstruction/existence dichotomy. It belongs to the current
library continuation rather than the immutable earlier baseline.

\hypertarget{the-allowed-branch-now-reaches-exact-geometry-in-a-bounded-regime}{%
\subsection{9. The allowed branch now reaches exact geometry in a
bounded
regime}\label{the-allowed-branch-now-reaches-exact-geometry-in-a-bounded-regime}}

The \href{../continuation/20260923_realization_and_rigidity.md}{new
continuation} does not restart the integral calibration or add another
general roadmap. It uses the actual-form supply and the last compactness
theorem together: gauge saturation rules out critical concentration;
topology forbids the half-flat bubble; global almost-parallel forms
become a global parallel form on the smooth limit. This yields a
fixed-threshold negative KE realization theorem on the original smooth
type.

Two separate improvements support and broaden this step. The topology
needs only torsion-free \(\pi_1\) and \(\pi_2=0\). The critical energy
theorem permits the controlled chirality to vary spatially, and forces
paired concentration for any remaining bubble. In the exact Einstein
setting, zero splitting at the Ricci-flat blowup scale forces the
self-dual Weyl tensor to vanish. Consequently small averaged splitting
already supplies compactness, and classical exhausted local moduli yield
exact local-product rigidity on the genuine product family.

The earlier shear construction now serves a different purpose:
\(\mathfrak D_2/V\le36\delta_-(1+\delta_-)\) follows from its exact
volume and actual canonical class. This produces an actual
counterexample to diameter-free KE realization, not a failure to find a
proof. It also explains why the new compact-regime theorem and those
older approximate inputs are consistent. It leaves possible non-Kähler
Einstein outputs open.

\hypertarget{a-stronger-existing-topological-theorem-shortens-the-existence-route}{%
\subsection{10. A stronger existing topological theorem shortens the
existence
route}\label{a-stronger-existing-topological-theorem-shortens-the-existence-route}}

The \href{../continuation/20260924_global_realization.md}{24 September
development} changes the role of saturation. Rather than continue
estimating the same Weyl model, a search for the precise remaining ALE
embedding problem identified Zhang's nonembedding theorem and
Bamler--Chen's explicit noncompact extension. A finite-cover lift
applies them under \(\pi_2=0\), even with torsion in \(\pi_1\). All
nonflat ALE bubbles are excluded, including the mixed-chirality
rational-ball case left unresolved by the previous half-flat argument.

This removes both Weyl control and gauge saturation from general
Einstein realization in the bounded-diameter, noncollapsed regime; both
signs work. Actual monopoles can now play the smaller role of supplying
volume. Their stronger form information is still useful for identifying
KE or locally symmetric outputs. Two independent actual form systems
give a bidisk metric without homotopy assumptions. At the Miyaoka--Yau
numerical boundary, classical identities force saturation directly from
Ricci defect and give complex-hyperbolic realization. The two deductions
identify different allowable geometries.

Localizing the same no-bubble argument removes diameter from local
regularity. With an explicit global tensor inverse and mixed residual
control, it also supports a classical diameter-free repair theorem. This
is not a proof that arbitrary large-scale degenerations have such an
inverse.

The strengthened noembedding result is credited to its original authors.
The project advance is the expanded and more direct mathematical
connection; it is not a claim to have invented the classical topological
obstruction.

\hypertarget{different-missing-information-leads-to-different-new-bridges}{%
\subsection{11. Different missing information leads to different new
bridges}\label{different-missing-information-leads-to-different-new-bridges}}

The \href{../continuation/20260924_frontier_bridges.md}{next
development} no longer requires the gauge class to live downstairs: its
scalar spectral consequences can descend through finite covers of
unbounded degree, while exact geometry is produced by compactness of the
original base. This avoids the false inference that an arbitrary
upstairs Einstein metric descends.

For large diameter, a weighted coercivity estimate was combined with
slow ball growth on the actual manifold. This converts an inverse
previously requiring total residual smallness into a uniformly local
inverse. Cyclic hyperbolic covers with independently distributed
perturbations test the distinction: the total residual diverges but
local repair stays uniform.

For collapse, the actual-form estimates were connected to Naber--Zhang's
maximal-rank local regularity using an explicit covering-average lemma.
The output is local product geometry off a small-volume bad set, not a
global metric. For nonzero second homotopy, the proof instead accepts a
KE orbifold limit and makes algebraic smoothing and the marked filling
criterion explicit. These are two different responses to degeneration,
not interchangeable versions of smooth compactness.

\hypertarget{local-allowed-geometry-acquires-explicit-global-data}{%

\hypertarget{scope-distinctions-that-prevent-false-global-conclusions}{%
\subsection{2. Scope distinctions that prevent false global
conclusions}\label{scope-distinctions-that-prevent-false-global-conclusions}}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Sharp Weyl payment is not a refutation of the balanced
  coefficient.}
  \href{../continuation/20260927_signed_balance_and_conformal_flow.md}{X06}
  uses actual ordinary SW solutions on h=0,U=3 products to show that
  \(\mathscr S\le cF+CP\), \(c<2\), fails universally. The balanced
  h\textgreater0 family has additional topology; its target retains
  \((\mathscr S-2F)_+=o(Ah)\) and remains open.
\item
  \textbf{Finite conformal-flow time does not identify the singularity.}
  I49 derives a uniform upper bound from all-metric budgets and genuine
  near-minimizing initial data. It neither presumes attainment nor uses
  failure of this flow as a proof that every Einstein output is absent.
  Conformal Ricci flow and normalized Ricci flow are different.
\item
  \textbf{An extracted metric core need not retain the actual calibrated
  field.} Exact stationary noncollapsed blow-up supplies terminal smooth
  ALE truncations under absolute error control. The norm-preserving
  field amplitude error acquires a factor \(r^{-4}\); global absolute
  vanishing alone does not make it small. The stationary/Sobolev
  zero-end theorem also retains its hypotheses and is not a theorem for
  all minimizers' approximating sequences.
\item
  \textbf{Local, neighborhood and all-output statements are different.}
  A conformal-class exclusion, a shrinking \(C^2\) repair ball, or a
  selected degeneration obstruction does not exclude distant Einstein
  metrics. The
  \href{../mathematics/current/round14_repair_radius.md}{all-mode repair
  theorem} retains its shrinking radius; the
  \href{../mathematics/current/round13_global.md}{volume-component
  result} leaves infinitely many components with accumulating volumes as
  a possibility.
\item
  \textbf{No local Killing fields does not give a positive perturbation
  distance.} Arbitrarily small nonsymmetric perturbations may destroy
  every continuous local symmetry.
  \href{../mathematics/current/round12_symmetry.md}{Round 12 symmetry}
  is an all-output structural restriction, not a no-repair theorem.
\item
  \textbf{A realizable curvature orbit is not a forbidden model.}
  Hyperbolic products attain the product orbit and the KE root. More
  accurate supply of their auxiliary forms need not create a
  contradiction. The rank-one Weyl model is not a classification of
  every Einstein Weyl tensor.
\item
  \textbf{Average structure is not exact structure.} Small
  \(\fint|\nabla\omega|^2\), harmonic alignment, or curvature-model
  distance alone does not create a global parallel form, complex
  structure or exact Einstein metric. The new
  \href{../continuation/20260923_gauge_saturation_ke.md}{gauge
  realization theorem} does prove this implication with its actual gauge
  supply, topology and diameter hypotheses, via topology-preserving
  compactness. Its Section 7.2 gives a real counterexample if diameter
  is deleted.
\item
  \textbf{Scalar and Ricci nonattainment do not settle full-curvature
  attainment.} On the specified \(E_D\) and shear objects, the last is
  equivalent to Einstein existence; the first two are already
  established separately.
\item
  \textbf{The product-boundary results have a volume-endpoint
  hypothesis.} \href{../mathematics/current/round16.md}{Rounds 15--16}
  do not describe arbitrary exact metrics at other volumes. The later
  diameter-only theorem replaces those hypotheses only in its different,
  explicitly spectrally pinched class.
\item
  \textbf{Complete noncompact models are not closed counterexamples.}
  Double cylinders, free-circle conformal ends, flat-torus ends,
  one-ended capped spaces, and fixed closed product-block assemblies are
  different topological and asymptotic problems.
\item
  \textbf{Actual marking and smooth type matter.} \(A_1\) chirality
  freedom does not automatically extend to higher ADE cones. A
  homeomorphic KE surface does not supply a metric on the chosen
  standard smooth resolution. See
  \href{../mathematics/current/round19_positive_bridge.md}{Round 19} and
  \href{../mathematics/current/round20_global.md}{Round 20}.
\item
  \textbf{Finite spectra do not settle full matching.} The old
  multi-node coefficients retain truncation/tail errors; same-order
  self, deformation, inter-node and complement terms belong in the same
  obstruction equation.
\item
  \textbf{No general flow shortcut has been proved.} Failure of the
  chosen Ricci flow to converge is not an all-metrics obstruction. The
  old common-time flow interface is not established merely by
  fixed-order estimates.
\end{itemize}

The project distinguishes a new local deduction from a new theorem in
the literature. Bauer--Furuta nonvanishing, symplectic geography, cscK
blowups, Derdziński classification, Gursky--LeBrun gaps, Einstein
compactness, ALE classification and causal boundary noncommunication
remain credited external inputs. Applying them to an actual object with
verified hypotheses can be a substantive project result; it does not
rediscover the source theorem.
