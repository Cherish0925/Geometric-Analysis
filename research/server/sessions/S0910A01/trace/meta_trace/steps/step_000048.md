## Deep-Reasoning

This Explore review sharpened the research picture around a structural distinction that now matters for the whole Yau 26 problem: producing arbitrarily accurate Einstein input metrics and proving exact Einstein existence or nonexistence are different mathematical questions. A local gluing obstruction to exact Einstein metrics does not by itself stop the input side of Yau 26, because the proposition only asks whether some unrelated Einstein metric exists on the same smooth manifold. Conversely, a family of near-Einstein input metrics is not a counterexample unless every possible Einstein sign on the same smooth manifold is excluded. The A02 positive-branch correction and the Strategist's product-surface sign comparison expose this distinction sharply. On a product of two oriented constant-curvature surfaces with equal Einstein constant \(\Lambda\), the curvature operator on both chiral blocks has the same form \(R_+=R_-=\operatorname{diag}(\Lambda,0,0)\). The two-dimensional kernel used to kill the first Eguchi--Hanson obstruction is therefore not a positive-curvature accident; it persists after changing from \(S^2\times S^2\) to products of hyperbolic curves. This creates a plausible common local construction principle for both signs: at \(A_1\) singularities of product orbifolds, kernel-directed Eguchi--Hanson replacements can plausibly make the raised-index Ricci defect tend to zero once the first obstruction vanishes. The real unresolved questions are then global: which smooth manifolds arise from the mixed resolutions, whether the four-dimensional core-and-neck estimates really give the desired operator-norm bound, and whether the resulting smooth manifolds carry any exact Einstein metric of any sign.

The source reading supports this reallocation without making it a finished route. Morteza--Viaclovsky's Calabi estimate gives, after subtracting the first obstruction term, \(\nabla^k(\operatorname{Ric}_{g_t}-\Lambda g_t-t\lambda\chi_t o)=O(t^2\rho^{2-k})\) in the Calabi norm on the bubble/damage region, while Ozuch's obstruction tower records \(t_j\lambda_k^j+t_j^2\mu_k^j=O(t_{max}^{5/2})\) for exact Einstein desingularizations. This is consistent with A02's operator-norm scaling: the first obstruction remains order one after raising an index in the bubble metric, but second and higher obstruction residuals vanish in the Yau-26 input norm. Thus higher exact-gluing obstructions cannot rescue a proof of positive pinching compactness once the first obstruction is killed. But this same fact weakens counterexample ambitions: the mixed rows need an Einstein obstruction compatible with positive scalar curvature, not merely absence of a known gluing theorem. Positive scalar curvature makes ordinary Seiberg--Witten and spin-index obstructions ineffective in their usual form; entropy and infinite-fundamental-group mechanisms also do not apply to the positive product-quotient rows if they are simply connected or finite-fundamental-group types. The smooth-type computation and the search for a sign-compatible exact Einstein obstruction are therefore the central positive-front tasks.

The negative product comparison is an especially useful stress test for the restricted Ishida--LeBrun mechanism. Product-quotient surfaces \(S\to(C_1\times C_2)/G\) with curves of genus at least two and isolated \(A_1\) rational double points are a natural source of negative product orbifolds. The product-quotient sources read in this step state that the minimal resolution preserves the quotient fundamental group, that canonical cyclic quotient singularities include the rational double points, and that in the \(p_g=q=0\), rational-double-point classification the quotient has only nodes with number \(8-K_S^2\) for \(K_S^2=2,4,6\). These are exactly the kind of finite singular product models where the sign-symmetric local gluing principle could be tested. However, the retrieval also found the decisive missing data: no acquired source computes Seiberg--Witten monopole sign families for these specific resolutions, no source verifies the Ishida--LeBrun hypotheses for them, and no source decides exact Einstein existence or nonexistence for the relevant smooth manifolds. Therefore the possible overlap with the IL uniform negative gap is not established. If a named product-quotient resolution both satisfied the explicit IL monopole-family and \(q\ge0\) hypotheses and also admitted a valid near-negative Eguchi--Hanson input construction, the two lines would conflict and force a real mathematical repair. At present that overlap is only a valuable test to compute, not evidence against either line.

The A03 normalized cutoff-flow status was also corrected. The Step 49 nonlinear \(b_\varepsilon=\chi^2+\varepsilon^2\) start cannot be inherited: metric equivalence alone does not control the evolving connection or covariant cutoff jets, and finite \(L^p\) curvature bounds do not yield pointwise curvature. I read A03's Step 50 replacement and checked it against Yang's actual lines 803--816. Yang's transported-coefficient DeTurck system has displayed lower-order \(D h+E\psi\) slots; the added normalized term varies as \(-2\widehat a h+4\widehat\chi\,d\widehat\chi(\zeta)\widehat g\), introducing no spatial derivative of the unknown and fitting those slots. This makes the direct normalized Yang perturbation a plausible source-level reduction, while still importing Yang's compressed smooth-tame inverse rather than reconstructing it from first principles. The continuation side must use the shifted variables \(T=\operatorname{Rm}+G\), \(U=\chi^2T\), evolving cutoff jets \(C_j=\nabla^j\chi\), and the current-pair propagation \((U_m,C_{m+1})\) followed by \(T_m\). The restricted IL theorem should therefore be reread on the Step 50 start/continuation interface before being promoted beyond a strong restricted theorem architecture. This step did not complete that full rereading, nor did it patch the Step 47 route note before the runtime close.

A small reusable script was created at `Sessions/S0910A01/workspace/scripts/step48_product_surface_blocks.py` to verify two finite-dimensional pieces: the sign-independent product curvature blocks \(R_+=R_-=\operatorname{diag}(\Lambda,0,0)\), and the \(p_g=q=0\) rational-double-point product-quotient arithmetic \((K^2,\#A_1,e,\tau,b_+,b_-)=(2,6,10,-6,1,7),(4,4,8,-4,1,5),(6,2,6,-2,1,3)\). Because the runtime requested immediate closure, I did not run the script or write the intended Step 48 synthesis note. The research state is nevertheless clearer: there are now two sign-linked construction fronts sharing the first-obstruction kernel geometry, a corrected entropy/global-volume route that remains target-dependent, and a restricted IL negative theorem whose analytic flow module has a better source-level start but still needs an integrated independent rereading after the Step 50 correction. None of these resolves the all-dimensional proposition or the Lean formalization requirement, but they expose concrete mathematical quantities whose resolution would strongly change the direction of the project.
