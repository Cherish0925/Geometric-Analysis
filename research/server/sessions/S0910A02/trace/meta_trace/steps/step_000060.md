The main mathematical correction is that Step 59's nonresonant weighted matching lemma was misweighted. In the ALE frame for an A1/Eguchi--Hanson bubble with metric scale t_i, the Ricci defect has size <= t_i delta_i. The l=2 radial operator has roots 2 and -4 and a scale-independent Wronskian. Relative to the ambient H2 signal O(t_i), the error is O(delta_i log(1/t_i)). Thus the old fixed-annulus iterated-limit removal of the delta log(1/t) condition in Steps 35--36/42 was logically circular: it assumed the cross-neck O(t_i) matching rate it needed to prove. Step 34's resonance insight was structurally right, though its notation used a different scale convention.

The correct status is not an unconditional H4 necessity theorem. TTCS verified the scaling and resonance but emphasized that a geometric theorem still requires a full tensor flux identity, compatible annular Bianchi gauges/projection, subtraction of the ambient H2/H4 tower, nonlinear/gauge/tower errors o(t_i), inner EH-flux normalization or vanishing, and outer curvature identification. Under those still-open analytic pieces, delta_i log(1/t_i)->0 would force the first vector obstruction R_pm(p) zeta=0. Without that rate, the named failure channel is exponential neck scale: t_i approximately exp(-a/delta_i), or in the repaired local model t_i=delta_i exp(-2a/delta_i), where C^0 Ricci pinching permits O(1) l=2 drift through the free Cotton/trace-free-Hessian channel.

The second substantive result is constructive pressure on that failure channel. The first local warping model u=t r^2 A(log r) cos(2theta) correctly showed Delta u=t(A''+4A')cos(2theta), but TTCS caught that with fixed outer radius eta=1 the exact doubly-warped metric has |du|^2~t at the outer scale, too large compared with the allowed t delta. I then wrote and ran an exact symbolic Ricci checker for g=dr^2+r^2dtheta^2+e^{2u}dphi^2+e^{-2u}dpsi^2, confirming Ric_base=-2 du⊗du and fiber components +/-Delta_B u. Using the repaired scale eta_i^2~delta_i, R_i=eta_i/sqrt(t_i)=exp(a/delta_i), hence t_i=delta_i exp(-2a/delta_i), the full exact local Ricci tensor is O(t_i delta_i) while the l=2/H2 coefficient changes by O(t_i). The data show bounded exact Ricci/tdelta constants across small delta. This does not build a closed near-pinched metric and is not yet an EH/S3/Z2 neck, but it proves the exponential drift channel is not merely a scalar ODE artifact: an exact local Riemannian doubly-warped model realizes the needed Ricci scale after the shrinking-outer-radius repair.

The third result reframes positive collapse. Step 58's Chern--Weil pinning implies that in dimension four, tight positive Ricci pinching with Vol->0 and chi>0 forces curvature blow-up; bounded-curvature collapse would make int|W|^2 vanish, contradicting int|W|^2->8pi^2 chi. Therefore the collapse branch should not be pursued through regular principal S1 models; S2xS2 cannot be a principal S1 bundle over a closed 3-manifold because chi=4. The natural collapsed bubbling objects are plausibly ALF rather than pure ALE, but this is not proved. I computed only a scalar ALF mass proxy: on the R3 base the roots are ell and -ell-1, with mass pair (0,-1) and flux r^2W=-(2ell+1), so the ALE l=2 degeneracy condition is not the right ALF obstruction. A true result needs the tensor Lichnerowicz/Einstein operator on Taub--NUT and Euclidean Schwarzschild with gauge and positive-Einstein normalization.
