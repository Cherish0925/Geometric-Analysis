## Deep-Reasoning

This step clarified that the product-orbifold calculation has three mathematically distinct layers: the positive rank-one cone of physical Eguchi–Hanson sources, the even/odd blocks defined by the chirality-exchanging reflection, and the final T²-equivariant pair response after node-dependent frame transport. Keeping these layers separate resolves several earlier confusions. A physical source is M_j=t_j²u_ju_jᵀ, not an arbitrary element of Sym²(K_j). In coordinates x=tr(M), y=M₂₂−M₃₃, z=2M₂₃ it obeys x>0 and y²+z²=x². Therefore a kernel obtained by allowing arbitrary traceless matrices need not contain any nonzero bubble source.

Within the chirality-odd AB block computed in Step 96, the adjacent map is exactly of trace type in the recorded numerical normalization: A_AB,odd(M)=−a tr(M)I_K with a=0.03144249732. In trace/double-angle coordinates the map is diag(−2a,0,0). Its unrestricted kernel is the traceless plane, but its intersection with the nonzero physical rank-one cone is empty. Consequently this block is angle-independent but not geometrically negligible: for every physical source it contributes radial value −a t_j² and zero angular value. This is the precise content behind the Strategist's observation that rank one means blindness to angular anisotropy rather than accessibility of a physical kernel.

The receiving equation must likewise be evaluated on the receiver direction. If A_i is an assembled symmetric block, the relevant components are u_iᵀA_iu_i and (u_i⊥)ᵀA_iu_i, not the condition A_i=0. This reformulation gives each spectral sector a geometric role: trace blocks influence scale balance, while traceless double-angle blocks influence radial and angular alignment through relative source and receiver angles.

A new source-compatible computation extended this viewpoint to the chirality-odd AA/BB tensor sectors. Using the corrected physical identity S_odd=(1/2)R_odd on all of Sym²(K), the electric/magnetic normalization repaired in Step 94, and heat-regularized product spectra, the adjacent NN←NS tensor map stabilizes at b≈0.004002498659 and has compact form b[[-1,1,0],[1,-1,0],[0,0,1]]. In trace/double-angle coordinates it sends (x,y,z) to (0,−2by,+2bz). The symmetry-related NN←SN map sends (x,y,z) to (0,−2by,−2bz). Thus these odd tensor blocks are purely traceless and can produce both radial and angular components on physical sources. The old scalar e₂-to-e₂ value combined this traceless contribution with the AB trace contribution; it did not by itself reveal their different geometric functions.

A late peer correction substantially changes how these block computations may enter the physical reduced equation. The correct torus weight at node j is θ_j=s_A^j α+ε_j s_B^j β, with σ_j=ε_j s_A^j s_B^j. Anisotropic Q-type terms are allowed when σ_i=σ_j, not simply when chiralities match. The leading physical response has the form ξ_j↦P_jξ_j+Q_j conjugate(ξ_j), and solvability is the degeneracy condition |P_j|=|Q_j| rather than vanishing of a linearly subtracted mixed-minus-compatible row. For the all-compatible row only diagonal pairs are σ-matched, whereas in the diagonal-balanced pattern all six pairs are σ-matched. This introduces additional angular freedom and invalidates the earlier inference of a mixed-row obstruction from a signed adjacent scalar.

The new A01 odd-block maps are therefore useful but cannot yet be identified directly with the physical coefficients c_adj or q_adj. Reflection parity blocks must first be combined into the raw plus/minus pair response, transported through the correct node frames, and decomposed according to the node-dependent T² weights. The sharp comparison is that every σ-mismatched physical pair must have zero Q component. If the current local tensor block appears anti-linear before this assembly, that is a signal to determine whether node transport converts it, another parity block cancels it, or the current mode family is incomplete. This provides a strong mathematical test of the full source and transport conventions.

For the broader Yau–26 problem, this remains a local positive-Einstein desingularization laboratory rather than a resolution of Einstein existence on the smooth filling. The restricted Ishida–LeBrun negative theorem remains the most developed direct asset, while diffuse negative connectors and dimensions at least five remain open. The value of the present work is that it converts an unreliable scalar obstruction narrative into a concrete constrained representation problem whose missing coefficients and consistency relations can now be computed and independently checked.
