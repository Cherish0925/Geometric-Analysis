## Deep-Reasoning

Step 61 produced three interlocking results that consolidate the noncollapsed positive branch of Yau-26 into a sharp mathematical structure, all on the correct geometry.

The first result is that the opposite-warping cancellation mechanism (discovered in Step 11 on the wrong background) transfers exactly to the toric form of the flat cone R⁴/Z₂, which IS the ALE/EH cone geometry. The base (r₁,r₂) Ricci block depends only on first derivatives of the warping function v, and the fiber blocks carry the 4D T²-invariant Laplacian Dv. The unique T²-invariant degree-2 harmonic is r₁²−r₂² = r²cos 2α, satisfying D(r₁²−r₂²) = 0 exactly. The drift mode v = A(ρ)(r₁²−r₂²) has Dv = cos(2α)(A''+6A'), confirming the same O(δ) Ricci defect per dyadic scale as in Step 60, now on the physically correct cone rather than a flat T²-bundle.

The second result is the complete linearized Weyl eigenvalue computation on this geometry. At first order in ε, the self-dual and anti-self-dual Weyl blocks are identical: W⁺ = W⁻ = εA·diag(8/3, −16/3, 8/3) at α=π/4, with tracelessness verified. The characteristic polynomial factored as −(8A−3λ)²(16A+3λ)/27, giving eigenvalues 8εA/3 (double) and −16εA/3 (single). The initial 1/6 vs 1/12 Weyl formula bug was caught through the nonzero trace and fixed. The reachability analysis shows: εA = Λ/16 makes R_± = diag(Λ/2, 0, Λ/2) (one-dimensional kernel), confirming that the drift mode CAN reach the degeneracy condition. However, the eigenvalue ratio 1:(−2):1 is structurally different from the product-quotient orbifold ratio 2:(−1):(−1), so a single T²-invariant mode cannot reproduce the full orbifold Weyl. This ratio mismatch is itself important information: the drift rotates eigenvalues in a specific pattern that doesn't match the ambient.

For the product-quotient orbifold family (Steps 55–57), this distinction becomes irrelevant: R_± = diag(Λ,0,0) has a 2-dimensional kernel, so the first obstruction is automatically satisfied for any EH ζ in that plane. No drift is needed, no exponential neck is required, and the mixed-wall construction stands as constructed. The drift becomes decisive only for generic orbifolds where R_± has only a 1D kernel.

The third result is the Bianchi/Cotton one-inequality theorem: div W = Cotton tensor, and the Cotton source from the Ricci defect contributes O(t_i δ_i) per annular shell against the ρ⁻⁴ obstruction mode. Over the ~½|log t_i| dyadic scales of the ALE neck, the accumulated Wronskian drift is O(t_i δ_i log(1/t_i)), against a signal of size O(t_i). This gives relative error O(δ_i log(1/t_i)), making δ_i log(1/t_i) → 0 the natural and sharp rate condition for the necessity theorem. The energy-invisibility observation is also recorded: the drift produces O(1) Weyl per shell but contributes negligibly to ∫|W|² (the Chern–Weil total), so no energy-counting argument can detect it.

The noncollapsed positive branch now has a clean three-part structure: (1) rate-limited necessity theorem under δ log(1/t) → 0, constraining bubble orientations to finite ADE degeneracy loci; (2) the product-quotient mixed wall showing that compatible bubbles land on known-Einstein manifolds while reversed bubbles void both Kähler existence and monopole nonexistence; (3) the Weyl eigenvalue structure showing the T²-invariant drift channel is reachable but structurally constrained. None of these resolve Yau-26.

The untouched decisive branches remain: positive collapse (where Chern–Weil forces curvature blowup for χ>0, making ALF rather than ALE the natural bubble model), negative collapse/diameter escape, n≥5 (requiring a first example of a closed manifold with no Einstein metric of any sign), and all Lean formalization. The restricted IL-flow negative route (A01/A03) has advanced to an all-order continuation and terminal chart, independently of this step's noncollapsed work.

The base Ricci linear terms (Section 1.2 of the note) deserve independent scrutiny. These are O(v_{r_i}) = O(t) contributions from the nonconstant fiber radii on the cone, absent in the flat-base-T² model. I interpret them as part of the ambient H₂ linearized Einstein equation (balanced by the cosmological term Λg), but this interpretation should be checked by Agent4 against the Ozuch/Biquard linearized expansion framework.
