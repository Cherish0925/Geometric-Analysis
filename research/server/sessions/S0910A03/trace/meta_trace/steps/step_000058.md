## Deep-Reasoning

The decisive advance was to repair the causal order of the curvature smoothing argument. Critical \(L^2\) curvature control cannot bound the initial cubic moment \(Y(0)=\int \eta^2aF_T^3\), so any observation time depending on \(H_2(0)+r^2Y(0)\) is not uniform under curvature concentration. The correct mechanism derives the cubic quantity from the critical energy at each time: with \(H=\int\eta^2F_T^2\), \(D=\int\eta^2a|\nabla T|^2\), and matched supports, \(Y=\int F_T(\chi F_T)^2\le C\mathcal S H^{1/2}(D+r^{-2}H)\). Under the small critical parameter \(\gamma=\mathcal S H^{1/2}\), this absorbs directly into the relative inequality \(H'+cD\le CY+C(1+r^{-2})H\), yielding \(\sup H+\int D\lesssim H(0)\) and \(\int Y\lesssim\Gamma H(0)\), with no appearance of \(Y(0)\). If the evolving Sobolev constants remain comparable, then \(\int\mathcal S^2Y\lesssim\Gamma^3\). This preserves collapse independence because \(\mathcal S\sim r^2V^{-1/2}\) and \(H(0)\lesssim K_0V\) cancel the local volume.

The analysis also separated two weights that earlier text had conflated. The critical interpolation uses \(w_2=\chi F_T=a^{1/2}F_T\). The independent \(q=3\) evolution uses \(z_3=\eta aF_T^{3/2}\), whose square has weight \(a^2\) and therefore matches the \(K_3\) dissipation. Using \(a^{1/2}F_T^{3/2}\) at this stage loses a factor of \(a\) and fails in the transition collar. The genuine curvature all-\(p\) inequality is distinct from Step 39's defect inequality: for \(K_p=\int\eta^2a^{p-2}F_T^p\), signed Hessian integration and the shifted tensor identities give \(K_p'+c[pD_p^T+p(p-2)D_p^F]\le Cp^N(1+r^{-2}+\mathcal S^2Y)K_p\). A good slice from the spacetime \(Y\) estimate and the \(p=3\) equation then give \(Y(t)\lesssim\Gamma H(0)/t\) and \(\mathcal S(t)^2Y(t)\lesssim A^2\Gamma^3/t\) on positive slabs.

The weighted curvature Moser step was independently checked rather than inherited from the defect ladder. With \(z_p=\eta a^{(p-1)/2}F_T^{p/2}\), one has \(K_{2p}=\|z_p\|_4^4\) and \(K_{3p/2}\le K_p^{1/2}K_{2p}^{1/2}\). A time cutoff gives a coefficient \(L_j=Cp_j^{N+2}(4^j/t+1+r^{-2}+A^2\Gamma^3/t)\), enlarged so \(L_j\ge r^{-2}\). The precise recurrence is \(\mathcal M_{3p/2}\le C\mathcal S(L_j\mathcal M_p)^{1/2}(L_j+r^{-2})\mathcal M_p\), which reduces to \(C\mathcal S(L_j\mathcal M_p)^{3/2}\). For \(p_j=3(3/2)^j\), the exponent sums give total Sobolev power \(2/3\) and time power \(-1\). The seed \(\mathcal M_{3,0}\le C\Gamma H(0)\) therefore yields \(\|aF_T(t)\|_\infty\le C\Gamma/t\), without initial cubic curvature or collapsed-volume dependence. Two independent TTCS branches verified the critical bridge and two verified the Moser recurrence, requiring only precise support placement, a safe form of \(L_j\), and replacement of an equality by \(\mathcal S(0)^2\Gamma H(0)\le\Gamma^3\).

The other major correction concerns continuation. The Step 56 base pair and Kato-Sobolev embedding genuinely imply unweighted pointwise curvature on each positive-time slab: finite \(L^p\) control of \(T\) and \(\nabla T\), with \(p>4\), gives \(T\in L^\infty\). This does not make the evolving cutoff jets bounded. The next coupled level still contains \(\chi\nabla^4\chi*U\), while \(\partial_t\nabla^3\chi\) contains \(\nabla^2U\). Thus ordinary Shi estimates work on the plateau but cannot cross the degenerate collar. A finite staggered hierarchy up to the Step 56 restart threshold \(s_0>n/2+4\), followed by conversion through the fixed-background connection difference, remains necessary before a common observation time follows.

This repair materially strengthens the restricted four-dimensional negative Ishida–LeBrun mechanism, but it remains only one laboratory for Yau Problem 26. It does not address the positive branch, arbitrary four-manifold topology, dimensions \(n\ge5\), dimension-only pinching constants, or Lean formalization. Its broader mathematical value is a collapse-insensitive local smoothing mechanism in which critical curvature creates its own positive-time subcritical control while the Einstein defect remains homogeneous. Whether this mechanism reaches a common terminal time now depends on the finite collar continuation hierarchy rather than on the curvature Moser endpoint.
