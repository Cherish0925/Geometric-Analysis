## Deep-Reasoning

This Agent4 Explore step audited the Step 83 reconstruction against the exact Yau--26 quantifiers and found that the construction theorem itself is now a reliable staged asset, but the interval reduction still needed a boundary-case correction. The fixed marked A1 log-Weyl construction survives at theorem-grade scope: the proof requires common radial normal gauge for ambient and EH quadratic jets, O_2 tail/Taylor estimates, the analytic radial-cutoff identity rather than monomial tests alone, and fixed finite node data; under those hypotheses the raised-index defect in the core, annulus, ambient patch, and finite multi-node assembly is O(1/|log a|). A01's Green-mode corrections affect exact Einstein desingularization coefficients, not this local near-pinched input construction, because the Step 83 metrics are patched in disjoint balls and equal the Einstein orbifold outside. The important new correction is quantifier-level: in dimension four the intervals c<0<C, c=0<C, and c<C=0 are all defeated by S^1 x S^3. The product flat circle times round S^3 gives Ric >= 0 and, after scaling, fits any [0,C]; Lohkamp's negative Ricci theorem gives Ric<0 and, after scaling, fits any [c,0]; and S^1 x S^3 has no Einstein metric because chi=0 forces an Einstein 4-metric to be flat by Chern--Gauss--Bonnet, while a closed flat 4-manifold has virtually Z^4 fundamental group and pi_1(S^1 x S^3)=Z. Thus in dimension four the original existential-interval proposition is equivalent to existence of a strict normalized branch P_4^+(delta) or P_4^-(delta) for some delta<1. A full 4D refutation must defeat both strict branches; a positive mirror no-Einstein row alone would only refute P_4^+.

The same audit also changes the value of the mirror rows. Step 83's A1 theorem is sign-neutral, so the negative genus-2 mirror (Sigma_2 x Sigma_2)/<iota x iota>, with Ric=-g and 36 A1 points, inherits the same O(1/|log a|) construction in the negative branch for every fixed filling pattern. The positive mirror supplies strict-positive input tests; the negative mirror supplies strict-negative input tests. Consequently a dimension-four refutation of the original problem would need all-sign Einstein nonexistence on one positive-mirror row and on one negative-mirror row, plus the already available S^1 x S^3 boundary/crossing interval argument. This is a serious downward revision of further hunting on only the positive mixed rows: even an all-sign nonexistence theorem there would not by itself refute the original proposition, though it would still be a major refutation of P_4^+.

The most conceptually useful new instrument is the scale-invariant four-dimensional functional F(g)=int(|W|^2+s^2/24), equivalently F=8*pi^2*chi+(1/2)int|E|^2 with E=Ric-(s/4)g. On every fixed A1 row covered by the audited construction, inf F=8*pi^2*chi, and equality is attained exactly by smooth Einstein metrics. The energy ledger is exact, not approximate: the product orbifold contribution is 16*pi^2 and each Eguchi--Hanson bubble contributes 12*pi^2, so 16*pi^2+4*12*pi^2=64*pi^2=8*pi^2*8 on the positive mirror, and 16*pi^2+36*12*pi^2=448*pi^2=8*pi^2*56 on the negative mirror. This explains why Gursky, Gursky--LeBrun, Bishop, Chern--Weil, and crude alignment estimates repeatedly left slack: there is no integral energy gap to discover, because the orbifold-plus-EH degeneration already saturates the closed-manifold lower bound. The variational route is nevertheless not just tautological; it is sign-neutral and could in principle decide all Einstein constants at once through attainment or non-attainment. But source-checking Anderson, Tian--Viaclovsky, and Gursky--Viaclovsky shows that existing theorems do not supply this. Anderson gives orbifold compactness under noncollapse, full L^2 curvature, and a small-curvature estimate; Tian--Viaclovsky treat exact critical elliptic classes under Sobolev and energy hypotheses; Gursky--Viaclovsky give local rigidity/stability near known Einstein metrics for quadratic curvature functionals. None is a global Palais--Smale theorem for arbitrary almost-minimizers of int|E|^2. The TTCS generator sharpened this into a concrete PDE target: build approximate-critical epsilon-regularity for the functional F_{-1/4}=int|E|^2 with L^p, p>2, Euler-residual control plus Sobolev/noncollapse; ordinary Ekeland or weak L^2 Palais--Smale control is probably too weak, and the explicit log annulus may already be an L^2 almost-critical bubbling sequence while blowing up in stronger L^p residual norms.

A01's Step 88 positivity claim was also corrected during this step by A01 Step 89, and I read the correction. The surviving local product-orbifold Green content is the axial lambda=1 chirality source sign coincidence and a corrected flat principal source-receiver identity; unconditional positivity is withdrawn because q=g_A-g_B is a TT negative mode with Pq=-q and point-supported quadrupole self-pairings are singular. This reinforces the current judgment: local Green coefficient work remains relevant only to exact desingularization near the product stratum, not to the audited near-pinched construction or to a global all-sign Einstein nonexistence theorem. The active Yau--26 goal remains unresolved for n>=4 and unformalized in Lean; n=2 and n=3 are closed only at ordinary mathematical level, and n>=5 still lacks a structural proof or counterexample route.
