This step achieved a decisive structural advance in the quantitative 4D analysis of Yau Problem 26. The central discovery is that the interplay between Seiberg-Witten curvature estimates, Chern-Gauss-Bonnet/signature identities, and pointwise Ricci pinching creates quantitative compatibility constraints that eliminate all known families of no-Einstein 4-manifolds from serving as tight-pinching counterexamples.

The mathematical chain has three layers. First, the strengthened Hitchin-Thorpe (Theorem B): any closed 4-manifold admitting ||Ric - Λg||_op ≤ δ|Λ| with δ < (√3-1)/2 ≈ 0.366 must satisfy 2χ > 3|τ|. This eliminates all HT-violating and HT-boundary manifolds from tight pinching of either sign. Second, the quantitative LeBrun-pinching bound: for M = X#kCP̄² under ||Ric+g||_op ≤ δ, the pointwise ratio |Ric₀|²/s² ≤ δ²/(4(1-δ)²) propagates through CGB to constrain k/A. Third and most critically, Ishida-LeBrun Proposition 4 (coefficient 2/3) gives a β-independent bound ∫|W⁺|² ≥ (4π²/3)(a⁺)² from direct Cauchy-Schwarz on the self-dual Weitzenböck formula. This is strictly stronger than LeBrun 1998 Theorem A (coefficient 32/57), which suffers from an avoidable trade-off between I₊ and E_s through the β parameter.

The corrected bound k/A ≤ 1/3 + 2δ²/(1-δ)² gives a uniform pinching gap δ ≥ 1/(1+√19) ≈ 0.187 for ALL LeBrun no-Einstein manifolds (k ≥ 25A/57). My initial analysis using only Theorem A found no gap at k = 25A/57 and identified a spurious critical model X₁₉#1875CP̄²; this was corrected after finding Prop 4 in the Ishida-LeBrun paper and independently deriving it from the Weitzenböck formula. The correction was cross-verified with S0910A01's independent derivation (message #205) and S0910A02's confirmation (message #206).

A separate structural result (Proposition C) shows ker σ_ξ(DRic) = {ξ⊗v + v⊗ξ} (pure gauge, dimension n), verified numerically for n=3,4,5. This eliminates perturbative homogenization routes for constructing tight Ricci pinching. Combined with S0910A01's Kähler blow-up obstruction (c₁·E_j = 1 forces δ_- ≥ 1 for any Kähler metric on X#kCP̄²), every known construction mechanism for tight negative pinching on no-Einstein manifolds is blocked.

The deepest structural insight is that the problem has an asymmetric character in dimension 4. The negative branch is protected by Seiberg-Witten integral estimates that create hard quantitative barriers between tight Ricci pinching and no-Einstein topology. The positive branch is protected by a different mechanism: tight positive pinching kills all SW/monopole obstructions (via s > 0 Witten vanishing) and constrains topology via Myers + strengthened HT, but the existence proof requires showing that the surviving topology actually carries Einstein metrics. Neither branch has a viable counterexample from known mathematics.

The research is still firmly in Explore: we have mapped the quantitative landscape thoroughly but have not yet constructed a proof of Q₄ (or any counterexample). The most promising direction is proving existence — showing that tight Ricci pinching forces Einstein existence through topological/analytical arguments. The critical open problems are: (1) removing the extra curvature hypothesis from Min-Oo/Anderson convergence theorems, or finding a different route to Einstein existence under pure Ricci control; (2) understanding whether the surviving manifold types (1/3 < k/A < 25/57 for LeBrun, or non-SW obstructions) can actually admit tight pinching; (3) extending the 4D analysis to higher dimensions where SW/Chern-Weil tools are absent. The evidence from all three sessions converges strongly toward Q_n being true, but proving it requires a fundamentally new existence mechanism.
