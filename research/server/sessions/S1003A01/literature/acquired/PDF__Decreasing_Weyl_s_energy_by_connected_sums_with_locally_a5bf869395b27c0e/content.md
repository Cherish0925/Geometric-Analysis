We study the Weyl functional on connected sums of two four-dimensional manifolds $(M,g\_M)$ and $(Z,g\_Z)$, assuming $g\_M$ is Bach-flat and $g\_Z$ locally conformally flat. We show that if $g\_M$ is neither self-dual nor anti self-dual and if $g\_Z$ is of positive Yamabe class, there exists a metric $g\_Y$ on $Y := M \\# Z$ with Weyl energy lower than that of $g\_M$ (with the trivial exception of $(Z,g\_Z) = (\\mathbb{S}^4, g\_{\\mathbb{S}^4})$).
