# Step 32: nested weighted Moser repair and tensor-derivation audit

Session: S0910A01. Agent3 / Actuator. Phase: Execute.

## 0. Purpose

This note corrects the Step 31 analytic handoff for the normalized cutoff-flow
route in the four-dimensional negative Ishida--LeBrun laboratory.  It keeps the
valid mixed-weight interpolation
\[
H_{3p/2}\le H_p^{1/2}\|z_p\|_4^2,
\qquad z_p=a^{(p-1)/2}f^{p/2},
\]
but rebuilds the short nested-time calculation in one convention and restores
the conditional positive-time endpoint forms.  It also records why a
volume-independent unnormalized Dirichlet Sobolev constant is false under the
current geometric input, and audits the general-\(p\) tensor derivation so later
work does not treat an incomplete schematic estimate as a proved power theorem.

Throughout, the flow is
\[
\partial_t g=-2aE,\qquad a=\chi^2,\qquad E=\operatorname{Ric}+g,
\]
on buffered selected supports.  All statements below are conditional on the
smooth first-exit geometry needed for the estimates: a fixed support/nested
collar system, evolving Sobolev and volume constants measured on the same
supports, metric comparability up to the first exit, and tensor-energy
inequalities with the stated weights.  This note does not prove the restricted
pinching gap by itself.

## 1. Fixed Sobolev convention and collapse scaling

Use the squared-norm Sobolev convention
\[
\boxed{\quad
\|z\|_4^2\le C_S\bigl(\|\nabla z\|_2^2+r^{-2}\|z\|_2^2\bigr),
\qquad C_S\lesssim {r^2\over \sqrt V}.
\quad}\tag{1.1}
\]
Here \(V\) is the volume of the selected support in the same metric/time slice.
The critical small/controlled parameter is
\[
\boxed{\quad \beta=C_S H_2(0)^{1/2}.\quad}\tag{1.2}
\]
In this convention the coefficient generated by curvature-reaction absorption
is \(C_S^2Y\), where \(Y=H_3(F)=\int aF^3\), not an independently normalized
symbol \(S^4Y\) or \(S^2Y\) from older notes.

A collapse-independent unnormalized Dirichlet constant is false.  On
\(B^3(1)\times S^1(\varepsilon)\) with the flat product metric, take a nonzero
function supported in the \(B^3\) factor and constant along the circle.  Then
\[
\|u\|_4^2\sim \varepsilon^{1/2},\qquad
\|\nabla u\|_2^2+r^{-2}\|u\|_2^2\sim \varepsilon,
\]
so any coefficient in (1.1) must be at least of order
\(\varepsilon^{-1/2}\).  The condition that \(z_p\) vanishes on the boundary of
\(\operatorname{supp}\chi\) makes it an admissible Dirichlet test function, but
it does not remove the collapsed-volume factor.  The intended route survives
only because the same \(C_S\) cancels against the selected \(L^2\) seed.

The verification script
`workspace/scripts/step32_nested_moser_repair.py` checks this scaling: for
\(\varepsilon=10^{-2},10^{-4},10^{-8}\) the necessary coefficient scales as
\(10,10^2,10^4\).

## 2. Reaction absorption in the fixed convention

For
\[
H_p(f)=\int a^{p-2}f^p,
\qquad
D_p(f)=\int a^{p-1}f^{p-2}|\nabla f|^2,
\qquad
z_p=a^{(p-1)/2}f^{p/2},
\]
the tensor power inequality should have the conditional form
\[
H_p'+c_pD_p \le C p^N r^{-2}H_p+C p R_p+\text{controlled source}_p,
\tag{2.1}
\]
where the curvature reaction retains the essential weight
\[
R_p=\int a^{p-1}F f^p .\tag{2.2}
\]
For curvature \(f=F=(1+|\operatorname{Rm}|^2)^{1/2}\), while for the defect
\(f=|E|_\varepsilon\) a separate regularized derivation is required.

The three-factor identity is
\[
a^{p-1}Ff^p=(aF^3)^{1/3}(a^{p-2}f^p)^{1/3}z_p^{4/3}.\tag{2.3}
\]
Hölder, (1.1), and the gradient estimate
\[
\|\nabla z_p\|_2^2\le Cp^2(D_p+r^{-2}H_p)\tag{2.4}
\]
then give
\[
R_p\le C p^{4/3}C_S^{2/3}Y^{1/3}H_p^{1/3}(D_p+r^{-2}H_p)^{2/3}.\tag{2.5}
\]
Young absorption cubes the prefactor and yields
\[
\boxed{\quad
R_p\le \varepsilon(D_p+r^{-2}H_p)+C_\varepsilon p^N C_S^2Y\,H_p.
\quad}\tag{2.6}
\]
Thus the positive-time potential is \(C_S^2Y\).  This is the same
volume/Sobolev product that appeared in the earlier critical-energy notes; the
route never needed a volume-independent \(C_S\).

## 3. Correct nested-time recurrence

Let \(p_{k+1}=\frac32p_k\) and choose nested time slabs
\(I_{k+1}\Subset I_k\subset[t/2,t]\) with dyadic losses
\(|\partial_t\zeta_k|\lesssim 4^k/t\).  Conditional on (2.1)--(2.6), the
energy estimate on the \(k\)-th slab should be written as
\[
\boxed{\quad
\sup_{I_{k+1}}H_{p_k}+\int_{I_{k+1}}(D_{p_k}+r^{-2}H_{p_k})
\le L_k\int_{I_k}H_{p_k},
\quad}\tag{3.1}
\]
where, after the same-support coefficient \(C_S^2Y\) is controlled on the slab,
\[
L_k\lesssim C p_k^N4^k(t^{-1}+r^{-2}).\tag{3.2}
\]
The exact polynomial power \(N\) is not important as long as it is finite and
comes from a real tensor estimate.

The mixed interpolation is
\[
H_{3p/2}=\int(a^{p-2}f^p)^{1/2}z_p^2
\le H_p^{1/2}\|z_p\|_4^2.\tag{3.3}
\]
Combining (3.3) with (1.1), (2.4), and (3.1) gives the spacetime recurrence
\[
\begin{aligned}
\int_{I_{k+1}}H_{p_{k+1}}
&\le (\sup_{I_{k+1}}H_{p_k})^{1/2}
 C p_k^2C_S\int_{I_{k+1}}(D_{p_k}+r^{-2}H_{p_k})\\
&\le C p_k^N C_S L_k^{3/2}\left(\int_{I_k}H_{p_k}\right)^{3/2}.
\end{aligned}\tag{3.4}
\]
There is no division by \(L_k^{1/2}\).  The previous Step 31 derivation
introduced such a division when mixing a supremum estimate and an integral
estimate; (3.4) is the direct consequence of the spacetime interpolation.

Let \(A_k=\int_{I_k}H_{p_k}\).  Taking \(p_{k+1}\)-th roots in (3.4), iterating,
and using \(p_k=p_0(3/2)^k\), polynomial \(p_k\)-losses and dyadic \(4^k\)-losses
produce a finite Moser product because
\[
\sum_k {\log p_k\over p_{k+1}}<\infty,
\qquad
\sum_k {k\over p_{k+1}}<\infty.
\]
The exponent sums are
\[
\sum_{k\ge0}{1\over p_{k+1}}={2\over p_0},
\qquad
{3\over2}\sum_{k\ge0}{1\over p_{k+1}}={3\over p_0}.\tag{3.5}
\]
Therefore the conditional endpoint is
\[
\boxed{\quad
\|af(t)\|_\infty
\le C\,C_S^{2/p_0}t^{-3/p_0}
\left(\int_{t/2}^{t}H_{p_0}(f,s)\,ds\right)^{1/p_0},
\quad}\tag{3.6}
\]
with \((t^{-1}+r^{-2})^{3/p_0}\) in place of \(t^{-3/p_0}\) if one does not
assume \(t\le c r^2\).  The endpoint is \(\|af\|_\infty\) because
\((a^{p-2}f^p)^{1/p}\to af\).

The script `workspace/scripts/step32_nested_moser_repair.py` verifies the
exponents: for \(p_0=3\), \(C_S\) has exponent \(2/3\) and time exponent \(1\);
for \(p_0=6\), \(C_S\) has exponent \(1/3\) and time exponent \(1/2\).

## 4. Conditional endpoint forms and volume cancellation

### 4.1 Curvature endpoint

For curvature, take \(p_0=3\).  The positive-time seed required by the route is
a same-support estimate of the form
\[
\int_{t/2}^{t}Y(s)\,ds\le C{\beta^3\over C_S^2},\tag{4.1}
\]
or equivalently a dyadic coefficient bound such as
\(C_S^2Y(s)\lesssim \beta^3/s\) after good-slice propagation.  This seed is
itself a conditional output of the \(H_2\to H_3\) critical absorption and \(q=3\)
propagation; it is not proved by the high-exponent recurrence.

Substituting (4.1) into (3.6) gives
\[
\boxed{\quad
\|aF(t)\|_\infty\le C{\beta\over t}.
\quad}\tag{4.2}
\]
This is the earlier positive-time curvature form, now recovered from the
correct weighted recurrence and Sobolev convention.  The factor \(C_S^{2/3}\)
from Moser cancels against \((C_S^{-2})^{1/3}\) from the selected seed.

### 4.2 Defect endpoint

For the defect, take \(p_0=6\) and suppose the fixed-exponent critical absorption
from time zero has already given same-support persistence
\[
J_6(s)=\int a^4|E|^6(s)\le C\delta^6 V,
\qquad 0\le s\le T.\tag{4.3}
\]
Then
\[
\int_{t/2}^{t}J_6(s)\,ds\le C\delta^6Vt.
\]
Using (3.6) and \(C_S\asymp r^2V^{-1/2}\),
\[
\boxed{\quad
\|aE(t)\|_\infty
\le C C_S^{1/3}t^{-1/2}(\delta^6Vt)^{1/6}
\le C\delta\left({r^2\over t}\right)^{1/3}.
\quad}\tag{4.4}
\]
This endpoint is singular as \(t\downarrow0\), but it is integrable:
\[
\int_0^T\|aE(t)\|_\infty\,dt
\le C\delta r^{2/3}T^{2/3}
=C\delta\theta^{2/3}r^2,
\qquad T=\theta r^2.\tag{4.5}
\]
This is exactly the metric-drift scale needed for the selected-support flow.

### 4.3 Terminal regularity is not time-zero stability

A bound available only on \([T/4,T]\), with constants depending on \(T\), cannot
supply \(\int_0^T\|aE\|_\infty\) by covering or restarting unless the omitted
early-time intervals have sequence-uniform estimates.  Therefore the route must
still prove:

1. \(H_2\) persistence and the \(H_2\to H_3\) spacetime seed starting at time
   zero;
2. \(q=3\) positive-time propagation or an equivalent dyadic coefficient bound
   for \(C_S^2Y\);
3. fixed \(J_6\) persistence from time zero by critical absorption, independent
   of the later high-exponent endpoint;
4. global \(Q\) persistence for terminal recalibration.

The high-exponent recurrence (3.6) then converts these fixed-exponent inputs into
pointwise weighted curvature and defect bounds on every positive-time slab.

## 5. Audit of the Step 31 general-\(p\) tensor derivation

The Step 31 note captured useful weights but should not be cited as a completed
power proof.  The main issue is the Hessian-of-\(a\) integration.  A typical term
has schematic form
\[
I_C=p\int a^{p-2}F^{p-2}\,\nabla^2a*T,
\qquad |T|\lesssim F^2.
\tag{5.1}
\]
After integrating one derivative from \(a\),
\[
I_C=-p\int \nabla a*\nabla(a^{p-2}F^{p-2}T).
\tag{5.2}
\]
Derivatives hit every factor:

- \(\nabla a^{p-2}\):
  \[
  p(p-2)a^{p-3}|\nabla a|^2F^p
  =4p(p-2)a^{p-2}|\nabla\chi|^2F^p
  \lesssim p^2r^{-2}K_p.
  \]
- \(\nabla F^{p-2}\):
  \[
  p(p-2)a^{p-2}|\nabla a|F^{p-1}|\nabla F|
  \]
  is absorbed by the radial dissipation \(p(p-2)D_p^F\) plus a polynomial
  \(p\)-loss times \(r^{-2}K_p\).
- \(\nabla T\): terms containing \(\nabla\operatorname{Rm}*E\) or
  \(\operatorname{Rm}*\nabla E\) are bounded by
  \[
  Cp a^{p-2}|\nabla a|F^{p-1}|\nabla\operatorname{Rm}|,
  \]
  using \(|\nabla E|\le C|\nabla\operatorname{Rm}|\).  These require the
  full-tensor dissipation
  \[
  D_p^{\operatorname{Rm}}=\int a^{p-1}F^{p-2}|\nabla\operatorname{Rm}|^2,
  \]
  not merely \(D_p^F\).

Thus a sound derivation must retain
\[
-pD_p^{\operatorname{Rm}}-p(p-2)D_p^F
\]
from the Bochner pair until all full-tensor and radial derivative terms have
been absorbed.  Only after this term-by-term absorption may one collapse the
remaining dissipation to a scalar form such as \(c p^2D_p^F\) for Moser.  The
finite polynomial loss may be larger than \(p^3\); that is acceptable because the
Moser product tolerates any fixed polynomial.  What is not acceptable is paying
\(\nabla\operatorname{Rm}\) or \(\nabla E\) terms with only the radial norm
\(|\nabla F|\).

For the defect hierarchy, homogeneity in \(E\) is not a substitute for the
regularized derivation.  One must write the equation for
\(f=|E|_\varepsilon\), track moving norms and measure, integrate Hessian-\(a\)
terms, and show a power inequality of the form
\[
J_p'+c_pD_p^E
\le C p^N(r^{-2}+C_S^2Y)J_p+\text{terms absorbable by the same support data},
\tag{5.3}
\]
with no curvature-only source.  This remains an estimate-level task.

## 6. Continuation note correction: the \(m=2\) single-\(\chi\) term

The Step 31 m=2 note said that the term
\[
\int \chi|\nabla^2\chi|\,|\nabla^2U|^p
\]
can simply be bounded by \(Cr^{-2}\|\nabla^2U\|_p^p\).  That is only valid after
pointwise control of \(|\nabla^2\chi|_{g(t)}\), which is itself part of the
coupled continuation induction.  The sharper current statement, matching A03
Step 32 and its TTCS review, is:

Set \(W=\nabla^2U\), \(B=\nabla^2\chi\), \(X_p=\int|W|^p\),
\(D_p=\int\chi^2|\nabla W|^2|W|^{p-2}\), and \(u=\chi|W|^{p/2}\).  Then in four
dimensions
\[
\|u\|_4^2\le C_S\bigl(p^2D_p+(1+r^{-2})X_p\bigr),
\]
and Hölder with exponents \((4,4,2)\) gives
\[
\boxed{\quad
\int \chi|\nabla^2\chi|\,|W|^p
\le \varepsilon D_p
+C_\varepsilon p^2C_S\|\nabla^2\chi\|_4^2(1+r^{-2})X_p.
\quad}\tag{6.1}
\]
So there is no known exact cancellation from the supplied shifted-\(U\) file, but
there is a viable estimate if the previous level of the coupled induction
supplies \(\|\nabla^2\chi\|_4\) in the evolving geometry.  A full continuation
proof must either write the exact shifted Wang grouped identity or keep (6.1)
and estimate the other \((\nabla^mU,\nabla^{m+1}\chi)\) products term by term.
The coefficient regularization \(a\mapsto a+\varepsilon\) remains unsafe without
uniform exterior-curvature-independent estimates, because it evolves the frozen
outside region.

## 7. Consequence for the restricted negative branch

This step restores the earlier endpoint scalings at the conditional level, but
it also narrows what is actually proved.  The weighted interpolation and nested
exponent products are algebraically consistent; the volume/Sobolev coefficient
is allowed to degenerate under collapse and cancels against the selected seed.
The remaining decisive analytic bridge is not a high-level theorem assembly but
the proof of the estimates consumed above:

1. complete curvature general-\(p\) tensor power inequality with separate
   \(D_p^{\operatorname{Rm}}\) and \(D_p^F\) absorption;
2. complete regularized defect power inequality and fixed \(J_6\) persistence
   from time zero;
3. prove same-support \(H_2\to H_3\), \(q=3\) positive-time coefficient control,
   and global \(Q\) propagation in the moving first-exit system;
4. complete the shifted-curvature continuation proof or replace it by a direct
   degenerate-flow extension theorem;
5. then combine these analytic estimates with the macro-disjoint plateau
   construction, terminal recalibration, exponential pullback, and the
   Derdziński forbidden-germ contradiction.

Even if all five items are completed, the result remains a restricted
four-dimensional negative Ishida--LeBrun branch result.  The positive branch,
arbitrary four-manifold topology, dimensions \(n\ge5\), and Lean formalization of
the full Yau Problem 26 statement remain outside the current proof.
