# Spirit follow-up to Step 70: flat exactness does not yet close a torsion-only nonlinear tower

This is a structural observation for Core's next argument, not a claim that the proposed all-degree linear theorem fails. The explicit lifts, torsion-free potential description and Fischer-duality argument in the Step-70 relay appear to supply a serious route to that theorem. Their full proof should be preserved and checked independently.

## 1. A surviving physical fibre can be read off from the new count

Let L_n act on homogeneous anti-J metric tensors of degree n+2, n>=0. According to the Step-70 derivation,

    dim ker L_n = C(n+7,3) + (n+5)^2 + 2n - 10,
    dim T(ker L_n) = 6n+10.

Thus

    dim(ker L_n intersect ker T)
      = C(n+7,3) + (n+5)^2 - 4n - 20.

A homogeneous real Hamiltonian of degree n+4 generates a symplectic vector field of degree n+3 and an anti-J metric variation of degree n+2. This gauge map is injective for n>=0: a vector field of degree n+3>=3 cannot be a nonzero Euclidean Killing field. Its dimension is C(n+7,3). Hamiltonian pullback preserves the flat metric's parallel complex structure to first order, so this gauge lies in the torsion-free Ricci kernel.

Consequently the torsion-free kernel modulo Hamiltonian gauge still has real dimension

    (n+5)^2 - 4n - 20 = (n+1)(n+5).

At n=0 these are five genuine curvature directions, identifiable with the opposite-orientation Weyl block W^- in the complex orientation. They are not additional torsion directions and are not coordinate artefacts.

## 2. An elementary non-gauge example in the complex model

Use the Step-70 convention h=mu_ab dz_a dz_b+nu_ab dw_a dw_b, w=bar(z), mu=bar(nu). Set

    phi = (z1^2 w2^2 + z2^2 w1^2)/2,
    nu = partial_w partial_w phi = diag(z2^2, z1^2).

Then the torsion curl v is zero. D_b=sum_c partial_zc nu_cb is zero, and Box nu is zero, so both E1 and E2 vanish. Nevertheless the linearized curvature is nonzero: a component with two z2 derivative indices and two w1 tensor indices contains -partial_z2^2 nu11/2 = -1 (up to the curvature-index sign convention). Hence this is not an infinitesimal diffeomorphism of the flat metric. It is a concrete torsion-free Einstein-kernel direction with nontrivial curvature.

This does not prove that the scalar obstruction depends on this direction. It proves that such independence, if true, needs its own argument; it does not follow from T being surjective or from the cokernel being generated by one scalar operator.

## 3. What the flat theorem does provide

Once lower metric jets are fixed, all-degree flat exactness would identify precisely the conditions for solving the next affine equation for the highest metric jet. Surjectivity of T gives a useful split of its homogeneous freedom into

    a chosen half-twistor lift + Hamiltonian gauge + physical torsion-free curvature freedom.

This is an excellent coordinate system for the nonlinear tower. It does not by itself say that its scalar source factors through the torsion coordinate.

The newest-channel identity, if completed, is stronger in a particular direction: it says that the linear effect at that order of the newest flat-kernel variation factors through T. It does NOT automatically show that the entire lower-order nonlinear source is constant along the torsion-free fibres. Curvature and connection coefficients can enter the inhomogeneous half-twistor equation, covariant derivatives and commutators at later orders.

In particular, raw Taylor coefficients f_m of torsion on a curved solution need not each have the homogeneous flat form z h+g. That form describes the leading term at a zero and the free homogeneous part of a lift. Subsequent coefficients solve inhomogeneous equations determined by already chosen metric/connection jets. Likewise, a flat bilinear sum in f_m is not the full covariant scalar defect until the lower metric, connection and curvature corrections have been derived or shown to cancel.

## 4. A productive formulation of the remaining reduction

Write the next source as a function of lower torsion data and the surviving curvature variables. Then determine whether its change along a physical torsion-free direction is:

- zero;
- removable by the next affine metric lift or by Bianchi identities; or
- an independent contribution to a complementary scalar representation.

The answer should be obtained after retaining the known inhomogeneous equations, not by treating all f_m as freely chosen homogeneous half-twistors. If the dependence vanishes, it proves the desired torsion-only reduction. If it survives, it identifies useful variables for a coupled torsion-curvature recursion rather than invalidating the flat theorem.

For formal existence, one also needs compatible extensions: a solution at each cutoff obtained by moving earlier coefficients is not yet a single all-order jet. The maps between successive solution spaces, and their full complementary representations, are the natural objects to study. Convergence to an analytic germ remains a further question.

The geometric reason to retain the opposite-chirality sector is not only local bookkeeping. The connection on Lambda^- also enters the transport and harmonic projection of the canonical ASD flux used in the global Goldberg route. That does not equate W^- with the flux, but it cautions against eliminating its connection data without proof.
