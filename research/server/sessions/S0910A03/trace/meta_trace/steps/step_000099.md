For Λ=−3: δ≥3(√3−1)/2≈1.098. This is a universal, normalization-dependent, and dimension-specific constraint. It applies to graph manifolds, free-T² manifolds, and surface bundles with χ=0. Any connector as part of a closed χ=0 manifold must have δ_conn>1.098 strictly.

The current quintic gives 28.7, so there is still a 26× gap, but the problem is now finite-target matching rather than asymptotic convergence. The conformal benchmark reaches the CGB level at r≈1.72; the Koiso germ continuation (estimated r_max~0.7–0.8) must therefore be supplemented by a better transition mechanism to bridge [r_max, 1.72].
