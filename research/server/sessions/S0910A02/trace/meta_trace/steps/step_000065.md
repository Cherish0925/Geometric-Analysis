## Deep-Reasoning

The Step-64 handoff assigned prong two of the balanced-row decision point: export the explicit near-pinched glued metric on a chi=8 positive-mirror row as a concrete smooth tensor field, verify the operator-norm pinching numerically (discharging the owed multi-bubble accounting), and prepare the Einstein-DeTurck/variational search with the r=0 del Pezzo row as control. This step did NOT complete the export. Its real contribution is a precise, source-corrected diagnosis of why the export is a genuine PDE problem rather than a formula, plus the derivation of the exact coupled ODE system that any successful export must solve. This is valuable and non-ceremonial: it corrects an over-optimistic reading that the session record (Step 49) had implicitly encouraged, and it prevents a wasted numerical flow run on a wrong metric.

The central conceptual correction concerns the Biquard correction tensor h. Step 49's operator-norm scaling theorem (||Ric-Lambda*g||_op <= C t^{1/2}) is correct but is conditional on having h solving the linearized Ricci equation (dRic)[h] = Lambda*e_h + obstructions on the Eguchi-Hanson space. I initially tried to make h explicit and failed in three instructive ways. First, the naive construction (raw EH as bubble) has ||Ric-Lambda*g||_op = |Lambda| = O(1) on the core, because Ric_EH=0 while the target is Lambda*g — this confirms the correction is essential, not a refinement. Second, I verified Delta(r^2)=8 EXACTLY on EH (not merely asymptotically), which made h = c*r^2*g look like an exact solution of Delta_L h = Lambda*g with c=-Lambda/8; but this conflated the Lichnerowicz Laplacian Delta_L with the full linearized Ricci operator. The correct linearization includes trace and divergence terms: (dRic)_ij[h] = -(1/2)nabla^2 h_ij - (1/2)nabla_i nabla_j(tr h) + (1/2)(nabla_i(div h)_j + nabla_j(div h)_i) + R_{ikjl}h^{kl}. For h = c*r^2*g, the rough-Laplacian and curvature terms give -8c*g, but Hess(tr h) = Hess(4cr^2) and the sym-grad-div term are NOT proportional to g; the symbolic check gives dRic_00[c r^2 g] = 2c(a^4+3r^4)/(a^4-r^4) != -8c*g_00. Third, the conformal ansatz g_corr = Omega^2*g_EH with Omega^2 = t(1-t*Lambda*r^2/8) fails: the series expansion of the Ricci-endomorphism defect has an O(1) constant term -Lambda/4 + Lambda*a^4/(4r^4), so no conformal rescaling of EH can be the corrected metric. Together these three failures establish that the corrected glued metric is NOT any elementary closed form — h genuinely has a non-trivial radial profile determined by an elliptic PDE, and the O(t^{1/2}) claim is non-vacuous.

The constructive result is the derivation of the exact ODE system. Doing the full 4D symbolic Ricci computation (Bianchi-IX coframe, U(2)-invariant diagonal ansatz h = diag(f1*g00, f2*(s1^2+s2^2), f3*g33)) and extracting the coefficient of the perturbation parameter gives the linearized Ricci components. The system has an unexpected structure: it is 2 second-order equations (for f2'' and f3'', from the (1,1) and (3,3) components) plus 1 first-order constraint (for f1', from the (0,0) component after substitution) — the linearized Bianchi/gauge identity removes f1''. The asymptotic quadratic mode f_i = c_i r^2 satisfies the large-r equations only along the single relation Lambda = 3(c1 - 2*c2 - c3), leaving a 2-dimensional gauge freedom that the ambient quadratic form H (from the orbifold curvature at the A_1 points) must fix. The BVP did not converge because the bolt r=1 is a singular point of the r^4-1 coordinate and the f1' constraint degenerates there; regularity must be imposed in a proper-distance coordinate and the ambient H must be determined to select the physical solution.

In the wider research landscape this step clarifies the actual cost of the balanced-row experiment and the structure of the near-pinched construction the whole noncollapsed arc rests on. The two-half framing from Step 64 stands: near-pinched metrics are the solved half ONLY up to actually computing h, and Einstein status is the open half. The operator-norm accounting is discharged at the theoretical level (Biquard Lemma 4.2 + Step-49 scaling) but is NOT yet numerically verified because h has not been computed — and the failures show the number C in ||Ric-Lambda*g||_op <= C t^{1/2} genuinely depends on solving the PDE.
