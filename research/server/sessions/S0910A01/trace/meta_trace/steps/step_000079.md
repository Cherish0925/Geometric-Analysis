## Deep-Reasoning

Step 79 corrected the positive mixed-row product-orbifold branch at a structural level. The branch remains only a local positive-Einstein construction test around (S²×S²)/Z₂, not a resolution of the full sign-unrestricted, all-dimensional Yau--26 proposition. Its value is that it probes whether tight positive near-Einstein orbifold degenerations can lead to exact Einstein metrics on mixed smooth fillings such as #3(S²×S²), while preserving the larger research separation from the restricted IL negative-gap theorem and the graph-neck negative laboratory. The main mathematical lesson is that the antipodal symmetry from Step 78 is real and useful, but it cannot be combined with a frozen Eguchi--Hanson angular tensor. Ozuch's Definition 1.14 defines ρ_x(ζ) by x^T(ζ⁺∘ω_j^-)x/|x|², and Remark 1.15 identifies this as quaternionic conjugation; therefore, for fixed nonzero ζ, varying x rotates ρ_x(ζ) through a sphere. This destroys the Step 78 pointwise inference that ζ in the product kernel plane forces ρ_x(ζ) to lie in ker R^-. The erroneous fixed block-diagonal dR^- and pointwise R^-ρ=0 conclusions are withdrawn. What survives is a different and more robust algebraic structure: for ζ⁺ in span{ω₂⁺,ω₃⁺}, η^-∘ζ⁺ is factor-traceless for arbitrary η^-, hence the bare H⁴ tensor has zero direct scalar trace even with the full x-dependence of ρ_x. This preserves a key scalar-decoupling input but limits it to the bare source; Bianchi corrections, tilts, nonlinear terms, Green responses, even scalar modes, and the shape mode g₁-g₂ still require direct treatment.

The second correction changes the meaning of the second-order coefficients. Ozuch's equations are t_j λ_j,k(φ_j,v)+t_j² μ_j,k(φ_j,v)=O(t_max^(5/2)), and Ozuch's proof itself uses O(t) movement of φ to control the transverse λ components. Thus a nonzero μ₂ or μ₃ is not automatically an obstruction; only its component modulo the image of the first-obstruction derivative is intrinsic at that order. The explicit linearization at φ₀=ω₂ for R=diag(Λ,0,0), using adapted frame (ω₂,ω₁,ω₃), gives D_φλ(a,b)=(0,cΛa,0). Consequently μ₂ can be absorbed by an O(t) out-of-kernel tilt toward ω₁, while the in-kernel rotation b is invisible to first order and μ₃ is the local remaining compatibility component after the independent determinant result μ₁=0. TTCS verified this interpretation and added two important refinements: the quotient should ultimately use the full image D_(φ,v,...)λ, not only D_φλ; and the tilt that absorbs μ₂ introduces an O(t) out-of-kernel piece, so scalar/shape coupling can reappear in the next order. A pilot normal-coordinate computation of scalar full-kernel modes v=u_i g_j found, in the local curvature-block model at a north--north node, that their D_vλ image affects only the adapted k=1 component and not k=2 or k=3, but this remains a local block calculation rather than a full Ozuch slice theorem.

The peer correction cycle improved the branch. A02 first proposed that r=0 Kähler--Einstein control forced all local μ_k to vanish; Step 79 and TTCS showed the parameter-matching gap, and A02 then corrected the statement. Exact Kähler--Einstein families force vanishing in the quotient by all first-order movable parameters, unless one proves their Ozuch coordinates satisfy φ(t)=φ₀+o(t) and v(t)=o(t). This does not weaken the construction: it makes the reduced problem more flexible and more faithful. The next mathematical object is a sliced, angular, paired reduced map, not a two-number verdict. A source lead appeared near the close of the step: Ozuch's Integrability paper states in Proposition A.2 that certain obstructions vanish for any quadratic H₂ with Ric_e(H₂)=Λe, and Propositions 3.16--3.17 discuss vanishing of related obstruction integrals for ALE asymptotic terms. This may be the right source-level route to local μ behavior, but its equations (26), (32), and related Pohozaev quantities must be matched exactly to Ozuch's μ_k definitions before being used. The full Yau--26 target remains unsolved; this step made the positive branch sharper by replacing false automatic vanishing with a correct quotient-and-tilt structure.
