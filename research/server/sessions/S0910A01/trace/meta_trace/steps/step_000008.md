## Deep-Reasoning

This step turned the Step 7 local-rigidity idea into a source-grounded and correctly scaled theorem. The Derdziński mechanism is now no longer only a TTCS suggestion: I recovered the original 1983 Compositio paper from Numdam, saved the PDF and extracted text, and read the relevant propositions. Derdziński Proposition 5 states that, on the set where W^+ is nonzero, an oriented four-metric with delta W^+=0 and at most two W^+ eigenvalues is locally conformal to a Kähler metric by the natural factor (24|W^+|^2)^(1/3)g, with essential uniqueness. For a two-eigenvalue tensor this is C|w|^(2/3)g, where w is the simple eigenvalue up to the sign convention. At the calibrated LeBrun profile Ric=-g, s=-4, spec(W^+)=(-1/6,1/12,1/12), |W^+|^2=1/24, this factor is constant, so the limiting metric would itself be locally Kähler. That contradicts the four-dimensional Kähler-Einstein identity |W^+|^2=s^2/24=2/3 for Ric=-g. S0910A03 independently supplied an equivalent full-tensor projector proof: constant two-eigenvalue harmonic W^+ forces the simple self-dual eigenform parallel, hence nabla W^+=0, while the Einstein-Weyl identity at the calibrated spectrum requires |nabla W^+|^2=1/16. The two local routes agree and remove the earlier mistaken worry that a nonconstant scalar conformal factor could realize the calibrated germ.
