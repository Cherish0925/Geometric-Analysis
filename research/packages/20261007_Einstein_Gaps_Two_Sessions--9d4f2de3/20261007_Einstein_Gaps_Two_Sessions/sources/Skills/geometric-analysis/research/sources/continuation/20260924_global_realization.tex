\hypertarget{beyond-prescribed-saturation-exact-realization-and-numerical-boundary-geometry}{%
\section{Beyond prescribed saturation: exact realization and numerical
boundary
geometry}\label{beyond-prescribed-saturation-exact-realization-and-numerical-boundary-geometry}}

24 September 2026. Current first-line research entry. This note connects
four new proof notes to the retained integral obstruction theory; it
does not replace their proofs or the earlier three-line history.

\hypertarget{the-main-change-in-the-mathematics}{%
\subsection{1. The main change in the
mathematics}\label{the-main-change-in-the-mathematics}}

The preceding KE theorem used gauge saturation to force a half-flat
bubble, then topology to exclude it. That is no longer the strongest
route to \textbf{general Einstein existence}. A stronger existing ALE
nonembedding result excludes every bubble under vanishing second
homotopy. Saturation remains useful for identifying Kähler or locally
symmetric structure, but is not necessary for existence in the bounded,
noncollapsed regime.

The distinction is mathematical, not terminological: \[
 \text{general existence needs compactness and preservation of smooth type;}
\] \[
 \text{identifying a KE or symmetric output uses additional actual structure.}
\] A KE metric already supplies the existential Einstein output. It must
not be described as an output shortfall.

\hypertarget{pointwise-only-realization-for-both-signs}{%
\subsection{2. Pointwise-only realization for both
signs}\label{pointwise-only-realization-for-both-signs}}

The \href{20260924_ale_boundary_compatibility.md}{all-ALE proof}
establishes \[
 \boxed{
 \begin{gathered}
 \pi_2(M)=0,\qquad \operatorname{diam}(g)\le D,\qquad V_g\ge v>0,\\
 \|\operatorname{Ric}_g^\sharp-\sigma I\|_\infty<\varepsilon(D,v)
 \end{gathered}
 \ \Longrightarrow\
 \exists h\text{ on the same smooth }M:\operatorname{Ric}_h=\sigma h .
 }\tag{1}
\] Here \(\sigma=\pm1\); the threshold is uniform across the stated
smooth types. There is no Weyl, saturation, monopole, or torsion-free
fundamental-group hypothesis in (1).

The strengthened topological input is \textbf{classical}: Zhang's ALE
nonembedding argument and
\href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.03154v3}{Bamler--Chen, Lemma 4.35}.
Lift a proposed core to the universal cover of \(M\). It may not lift as
itself when its fundamental-group image is nontrivial, but it lifts as a
finite cover, because an ALE Ricci-flat core has finite fundamental
group. This lifted core is still nonflat Ricci-flat ALE, whereas its
ambient has integer \(H_1=H_2=0\). The classical lemma excludes it.

The previous mixed-chirality rational-ball alternative is therefore
resolved in this topology. No classification of general Ricci-flat ALE
spaces is needed. The detailed note explains the boundary-homology and
eta mechanism, including why the quaternion example survives a
group-order test but fails the curvature identities.

For \(\sigma=+1\), Myers supplies a uniform diameter bound when
\(\delta_+<1/2\). The threshold in (1) then depends only on \(v\). For
\(\sigma=-1\), an actual monopole projection \(p\ge A_0>0\) gives \[
 V_g\ge\frac{2\pi^2A_0}{(1+\delta_-)^2}.
\] It can replace the volume hypothesis, without any small gauge
deficit. A positive-square integral actual class permits \(A_0=1\).

This is an enlargement of the project's covered input regime. The proof
credits the existing no-bubble and compactness theory; it does not
establish global priority for the resulting corollary.

\hypertarget{two-topology-free-routes-identify-the-exact-geometry}{%
\subsection{3. Two topology-free routes identify the exact
geometry}\label{two-topology-free-routes-identify-the-exact-geometry}}

\hypertarget{bilateral-actual-gauge-input}{%
\subsubsection{Bilateral actual gauge
input}\label{bilateral-actual-gauge-input}}

The \href{20260924_bilateral_realization.md}{bilateral theorem} uses
actual conformal monopole structures in both orientations. With bounded
diameter, one uniform positive projection floor, and \[
 \delta_-+\frac{\mathfrak D_+}{V}
              +\frac{\mathfrak D_-}{V}\longrightarrow0,
\] its uniform-threshold conclusion is an exact metric on the same
smooth manifold whose universal cover is \[
                       \mathbb H^2_{-1}\times\mathbb H^2_{-1}.
\] No homotopy hypothesis remains. Both Weyl halves vanish on a
hypothetical harmonic-radius blowup, making it flat before an ALE
theorem is required. Two global actual forms pass to parallel
opposite-orientation forms and give a parallel two-plane splitting. The
quotient need not be a globally marked direct product.

The paper also replaces the two functional assumptions by a single raw
Ricci-energy excess under actual projection budgets. This is an exact
identity, not an additional independent discovery. Existing non-Kähler
shear examples satisfy bilateral saturation, so deleting diameter from
this KE/bidisk statement is actually false.

\hypertarget{one-actual-class-at-the-miyaokayau-numerical-boundary}{%
\subsubsection{One actual class at the Miyaoka--Yau numerical
boundary}\label{one-actual-class-at-the-miyaokayau-numerical-boundary}}

The \href{20260924_chern_boundary_realization.md}{Chern-boundary
theorem} assumes \[
 A=2\chi+3\tau=3(2\chi-3\tau)>0,\qquad p\ge A.
\] It has \textbf{no separately imposed saturation condition}. Classical
identities give, with \(P=\int|\operatorname{Ric}^{\circ}|^2\), \[
 S-32\pi^2A+48W_-=12P,\qquad
 \mathfrak D_2+72\pi^2(p-A)+54W_-=18P.
\] Consequently \[
 \frac{\mathfrak D_2}{V}\le72\delta_-^2,\qquad
 \frac1V\int|W^-|^2\le\delta_-^2.
\] Thus small pointwise defect and bounded diameter imply
complex-hyperbolic geometry on the same oriented smooth manifold. Again
there is no \(\pi_2\) or fundamental-group assumption. The classical
exact Miyaoka--Yau classification is not being rediscovered: this is its
explicitly proved approximate-to-exact composition in the
bounded-diameter setting.

\hypertarget{a-different-realization-route-does-not-need-diameter}{%
\subsection{4. A different realization route does not need
diameter}\label{a-different-realization-route-does-not-need-diameter}}

The \href{20260924_local_to_global_scope.md}{local-to-global proof}
first shows \[
 \pi_2=0+\text{bounded Ricci}+\inf_x\operatorname{Vol}B_1(x)>0
 \ \Longrightarrow\ \text{uniform local harmonic radius}.       \tag{2}
\] Only one local Jiang--Naber energy budget is needed for a blowup, so
total volume and diameter do not enter.

A classical Bianchi-gauge solver then produces an exact negative
Einstein metric if there is also a full tensor \(L^2\) spectral gap,
bounded \(\nabla\operatorname{Ric}\), and a sufficiently small mixed
residual \[
 \delta_-^{\,1-\alpha}+\|\operatorname{Ric}+g\|_{L^2}.
\] The proof specifies the inverse, mixed Hölder--Sobolev norms,
nonlinear estimates, positivity radius, and gauge removal. It does not
obtain the global inverse from local regularity. Teichmüller kernels are
not deleted, and the \(L^2\) residual is unnormalized. Bounded-volume
support of the residual gives a direct pointwise-smallness corollary
even when the surrounding exact region has unbounded volume.

This is a useful sufficient criterion with a classical solver, not a
claim to have solved arbitrary large-diameter degeneration.

\hypertarget{what-this-changes-for-yau26}{%
\subsection{5. What this changes for
Yau26}\label{what-this-changes-for-yau26}}

Within \(\pi_2=0\), a sequence of manifolds with \textbf{no Einstein
metric} and pointwise defect tending to zero must obey:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  Positive sign: total volume tends to zero, since Myers already bounds
  diameter and (1) rules out every positive-volume subsequence.
\item
  Negative sign with a uniform positive actual projection budget:
  diameter tends to infinity, since the budget prevents total-volume
  collapse.
\end{itemize}

These are deductions about possible original-problem witnesses, not
constructions of such witnesses. Nonzero \(\pi_2\), uncontrolled
collapse, and large-scale global inversion remain outside (1). The fixed
non-Kähler shear objects still have undecided general Einstein
existence.

The first-line structure is now clearer: the integral theory supplies
diameter-free obstructions in incompatible topological regions; the
compact regime supplies general exact realization; actual saturation and
numerical equality identify stronger output geometry; global spectral
control gives a separate noncompactness-tolerant repair route. There is
no reason to force all four jobs into one auxiliary-form estimate.
