## Deep-Reasoning

This step built the mode-diagonal indicial theorem and discovered the logarithmic resonance that determines Route A's precise analytic strength. The full Yau-26 proposition remains completely open.

The central mathematical insight is that on the flat A₁ cone R⁴/{±1}, the Taub/Wronskian boundary integral is mode-diagonal in the angular harmonic decomposition. Symmetric 2-tensors decompose by even angular degree l ∈ {0,2,4,...} (Z₂ parity), with homogeneous solutions at rates l and -(l+2). Mode-diagonality means the boundary integral ∫ Ric_e^{(2)}(h_l, k_{l'}) dv = 0 for l ≠ l'. This is confirmed by Ozuch 2021 equation (59): Ric_e^{(2)}(H⁴, H₂) = Σ_j R⁺_{kj} θ_l⁻ ∘ ω_j⁺/r⁴ lives in the same l=2 angular space as H⁴ itself.

The consequence is structurally powerful: the l=0 mass/volume mode has absolute size O(t^{2/3}) at the balance radius r=t^{2/3} — larger than the l=2 modes at O(t^{4/3}) — but it is completely invisible to the l=2 Wronskian by angular orthogonality. The l=2 pair (2,-4) carries signal t⁴ (radius-independent, since 2+(-4)+2=0), and this IS Ozuch's first obstruction R⁺·v=0 (Corollary 4.10). Higher l and cross-mode pairs are either zero or subleading. So the metric need not be 'cone + two modes + small' — it only needs the flux-visible l=2 component to be controlled.

However, I discovered a logarithmic resonance that the previous analysis missed. In the l=2 radial ODE f'' + 3f'/r - 8f/r² = S(r), a constant-rate source S=δ (from the Ricci defect) resonates with the r² homogeneous mode, producing a particular solution δr²log(r)/6. At the balance radius, this gives Wronskian contamination of order Cδt⁴log(1/t), hence relative error O(δ log(1/t)). The Bianchi identity div(Ric-Λg)=0 does NOT remove this because it constrains the total error across all angular modes, not the l=2 projection individually.

This resonance is the most precise obstruction Route A faces. If δ log(1/t) → 0 (which holds when t ∼ δ^α for any α > 0, a natural geometric-sequence condition), then Route A's conclusion follows: nonzero R⁺·v forces δ bounded away from zero. But without a rate coupling or the harmonic-radius epsilon lemma (which could give improved l=2 source regularity), the theorem carries this as an explicit hypothesis. This is a significant narrowing: from 'prove a global weighted normal form' to 'check one logarithmic rate condition.'

On the collapsed front, the strategist's identification proved exact and illuminating. The toric α-family bolts have exactly 8π² curvature energy each ((2A)²·(2π/A)·(π/A) = 8π²), independent of A. Rescaling by √(2A) sends the cosmological constant to zero, and the limit is Euclidean Schwarzschild at mass M=1/2 (verified numerically: M̂ → 0.5 monotonically). This identifies the collapsed adversary as an ALF bubble tree, one Schwarzschild bubble per rod. Critically, Euclidean Schwarzschild is NOT anti-self-dual (both W⁺ and W⁻ are nonzero) and NOT ALE (it's ALF with a circle at infinity), so the LeBrun-Ozuch admissibility hypothesis does not apply. This shows the collapsed and noncollapsed halves of P₄⁺ require genuinely different obstruction theories: Ozuch/Biquard for ALE bubbles in the noncollapsed case, edge-cone/Atiyah-LeBrun for ALF/Schwarzschild bubbles in the collapsed case. The Step-17 rigidity μ=1 ⟺ A=0 is exactly the exact-Einstein obstruction for the collapsed configuration.
