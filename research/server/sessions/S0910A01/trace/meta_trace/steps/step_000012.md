## Deep-Reasoning

This Execute step corrected the Step 11 collapse narrative by separating three objects that had been conflated: the cover of a prescribed large ball, the cover of a smaller buffered annular domain chosen before taking the cover, and the Chern-Weil density carried by the actual near-critical LeBrun bulk. The Taub-NUT-AdS computation now has a stable role. It is not evidence that all fixed-scale accessible covers fail. A large ball containing the NUT kills the short Hopf fiber, but that same core-containing region has divergent mean full-Weyl energy as ν→0 and is not a good centered region under the multiscale mean assumptions. At fixed annular distance from the NUT, a smaller buffered domain avoids the core, the Hopf circle survives in the relevant local fundamental group, and the cover unwraps it; the lifted annular metric tends to real hyperbolic 4-space of sectional curvature -1/3. Thus standard Taub-NUT is accessible on the smaller annuli but has macroscopic calibrated defect, and is inaccessible only for a larger domain whose curvature mean is too large. This preserves the fixed-scale chart-accessibility route rather than closing it. The Pontryagin story also changed. The near-critical estimates imply mean L1 convergence of P=2|W+|^2+s^2/24-|E|^2/2 to 3/4, so the normalized p1(Λ²_+) density follows volume up to a global o(A) error. The order-A characteristic mass is spread through the calibrated bulk, not hidden in tiny NUT cores. Basic collapse remains incompatible with this bulk density, but the surviving mechanism is diffuse non-basic mixed Λ²_+ connection curvature: scaled holonomy, O'Neill terms, nil commutators, vertical frame twist, or fiber-length/twist gradients may represent the Chern-Simons flux across collapsed regions. The controlled-chart Derdziński mechanism remains one of the strongest rigorous pieces, but only in its repaired form: a harmonic chart/control package is supplied as a hypothesis, finite-p W^{2,p} convergence is obtained from the harmonic-coordinate Ricci equation, and small-defect basepoint selection in the negative branch uses local maximal estimates rather than a Myers diameter. The new rank-one calculations show both promise and constraint. Constant scaled twist over a flat base is too basic to carry the needed four-form; variable fiber length plus scaled twist one-jets can produce nonzero local Λ²_+ Pontryagin coefficients, but the simplest concrete exponential-twist metric has Ricci diag(-u^2,-C^2/2,-C^2/2,C^2/2-u^2) and cannot even locally satisfy Ric=-κg with κ>0. Therefore the next mathematical object is not another NUT sample; it is a full rank-one and rank-two reduced collapse system coupling Ricci, Weyl spectrum, p1(Λ²_+) density, fiber-length/twist equations, and accessibility. This is still intermediate four-dimensional negative-branch work, not a solution of Yau 26, but it sharply refines the remaining obstruction problem.
