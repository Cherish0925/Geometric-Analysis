# Twelve-Round Proof Library

The original proofs, reviews and finite calculations are retained in full.
Read them with the current three papers; intermediate claims retain their
original dates and later arguments may strengthen or correct them.

The [current synthesis](../research/20260921.md) explains the mathematical
progression; [reproduction](reproduction.md#twelve-round-calculations)
gives standalone commands. The original numerical inputs are unchanged.

Two editorial corrections to the original notes: in `round11_closed_gluing.md`
the final reference to (15) means (13); in `round12_symmetry.md`, exclusion
of a local circle action means a nontrivial local **isometric** action.
Finite isometries and small nonsymmetric repairs are not excluded.
