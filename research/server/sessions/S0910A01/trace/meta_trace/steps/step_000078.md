## Deep-Reasoning

Step 78 developed the A-equivariant diagonal reduction for Einstein desingularization of (S²×S²)/Z₂ and discovered two structural theorems that dramatically simplify the Ozuch obstruction problem. The construction uses the simultaneous antipodal map A(x,y)=(-x,-y), which is orientation-preserving ((-1)·(-1)=+1 in 4D), commutes with the quotient half-turn σ, exchanges nodes (N,N)↔(S,S) and (N,S)↔(S,N), and preserves the diagonal k=2 chirality pattern. This symmetry provides a PROVED mechanism for eliminating the four scalar Ozuch kernel modes u₁g₁, u₁g₂, u₂g₁, u₂g₂: they have l₁+l₂=1 (odd), so A*(uᵢgⱼ) = -(uᵢgⱼ), making any A-invariant residual automatically orthogonal to them. This replaces the withdrawn Step 76 claim that O(g_o)=0.

The second key discovery is the scalar decoupling theorem: for in-kernel EH directions ζ ∈ ker(R⁺) = span{ω₂,ω₃}, every 2-form composition ω_k⁺∘ω_l⁻ with k,l ∈ {2,3} is factor-traceless on each S² (verified at machine precision across 37 angles). Since H⁴(ζ) = -ρ(ζ)⁻∘ζ⁺/(2r⁴) is built entirely from these compositions, H⁴ has zero coupling to ALL scalar eigenmodes f·gₖ regardless of f. This is a structurally deeper fact than just the Bianchi-corrected pairing being zero (Step 77): the source itself has no scalar content. In contrast, ω₁⁺∘ω₁⁻ = -(g₁-g₂), showing the out-of-kernel direction does couple to the scalar shape mode. The inter-node Green response therefore lives in the positive-definite vector and TT sectors of the Ozuch operator P, with full non-scalar gap Λ (corrected from the initially claimed 3Λ/2 by TTCS, which identified the mixed 1-form sector at eigenvalue Λ).

The order analysis reveals that at comparable EH scales t₁ ≈ t₂ ≈ t, the inter-node interaction is O(t³) while the Ozuch remainder is O(t^{5/2}). Since t^{5/2} ≫ t³ for small t, the inter-node term is below the currently resolved precision. The leading finite-dimensional equation is therefore μ_k(α) = 0 at each orbit, with no inter-node coupling. Combined with the SO(2) covariance observation—the rank-1 curvature R = diag(Λ,0,0) is invariant under rotation in the kernel plane, so μ₂ and μ₃ are independent of the in-kernel angle α—the entire equivariant problem reduces to evaluating two constants: μ₂(ω₂) and μ₃(ω₂).

The rank-one orthogonality R⁻·ρ(ζ)⁻ = 0 (verified: ρ(ζ)⁻ = (0,c,s) lies in ker(R⁻)) eliminates the leading curvature contraction, but TTCS found that dR⁻·v = 8v/r⁶ ≠ 0 and R⁻·dR⁻ ≠ 0. The kernel-block modification has eigenvalues 8 and -4, both nonzero. Therefore the rank-one orthogonality provides evidence but is NOT sufficient to prove μ₂ = μ₃ = 0. The decisive computation is the actual Biq16/Ozuch formula evaluation at α=0, using the specific rank-1 block, the factor-traceless H⁴, and the parallel curvature ∇R = 0.

If μ₂ = μ₃ = 0: all Ozuch necessary conditions are satisfied at O(t^{5/2}), and the path to positive Einstein metrics on #3(S²×S²) requires inverse and nonlinear estimates on a positive-definite operator. If either is nonzero: the obstruction is a universal constant, and no in-kernel direction works for the diagonal pattern. Either outcome is valuable for understanding the positive Yau-26 branch.
