## Deep-Reasoning

This Execute/Preceptor step audited the current four-dimensional negative cutoff-flow route before investing in a heavy term-by-term derivation. The active Yau-26 target remains the full all-dimensional statement, and no proof, refutation, or Lean formalization was obtained. The key correction is that Step 21's weighted-CZ heat audit should not be treated as positive evidence for the weighted parabolic estimate. In dimension four, both the critical L^2 curvature energy and the schematic bound |a Rm| <= C beta/t are scale invariant, so an O(1) Gaussian ratio is expected; moreover the Step-11 opposite-fiber CZ spike is determinant-preserving and hence noncollapsed, while the serious failure modes for energy-to-curvature estimates occur under collapse.

I therefore tested the collapsed model directly. On S^1_ell x B^3 with fiber-independent test functions, ||u||_4 = ell^(1/4)||f||_4 and ||grad u||_2 = ell^(1/2)||grad f||_2, forcing any downstairs four-dimensional Sobolev constant to grow at least like ell^(-1/4). A hyperbolic/cusp-type model normalized by Ric=-g has E=0, |Rm|^2=8/3, and int|Rm|^2 -> 0 as the collapsed volume tends to zero, but A_ell^2||Rm||_2 remains O(1). The script `Sessions/S0910A02/workspace/scripts/step22_collapse_sobolev_and_scope_audit.py` and data `workspace/data/step22_collapse_sobolev_and_scope_audit.json` record this numerically for ell from 1 to 1e-8. This decisively rejects any route that uses raw downstairs small L^2 curvature energy plus a dimension-only Sobolev/Poincare/isoperimetric constant in collapsed regions. It does not refute the corrected A01/A03 route, because their latest formulation retains a relative Sobolev constant A and the critical product gamma=A^2||F||_2; the collapsed model exactly explains why that product is the right invariant.

Source checking sharpened where noncollapsing enters. Min-Oo's paper explicitly obtains the Sobolev constant from lower Ricci and diameter control, not from collapse-neutral local data; Wang/Yang's local Ricci flow theorem assumes a Sobolev inequality and a small critical product A_0^2|Rm|_{n/2}, so it cannot be cited as a dimension-only collapsed estimate. The remaining A01/A03 theorem must therefore be written in one of two honest forms: either a downstairs relative-Sobolev finite-neighbor theorem that carries A_alpha and gamma_alpha=A_alpha^2||F||_2 until the four-dimensional cancellation is used, or an accessible-cover theorem on genuine buffered normal covers where chi, eta, a=chi^2, and the normalized cutoff flow pull back equivariantly and smallness is transported as a local mean. Exponential-pullback chart selection is useful only after positive-time bounded curvature has been produced; it does not supply the Sobolev input for the smoothing theorem.

I also performed the downstream scope check requested by the strategist. A pure Chern-Weil/Hitchin-Thorpe endpoint would buy nothing, because tight four-dimensional Ricci pinching already implies strict Hitchin-Thorpe for small delta by the sign-blind Chern-Weil accounting from Steps 4 and 18. Min-Oo cannot be the endpoint either: large-diameter closed hyperbolic manifolds are exact Ric=-g examples with delta=0 and unbounded diameter-curvature scale, while already Einstein. The real A01/A03 endpoint is stronger but narrow: the critical Ishida-LeBrun/Seiberg-Witten 7/24 calibration plus exponential chart/projector-Derdzinski contradiction could at most give a fixed-topology gap, or perhaps a standard-subclass uniform gap, for critical Ishida-LeBrun four-manifolds. It would not prove unrestricted P_4^-, P_4^+, any dimension n>=5, or the full Yau-26 proposition. In varying topology the terminal defect control must be volume-normalized, Q/Vol -> 0, and used on a plateau set of fixed positive volume fraction; absolute Q -> 0 is only a fixed-topology statement.

Finally, I preserved the toric work as reusable mathematics rather than a vanished route verdict. The exact diagonal rod identity is int_rod(1-2K_edge)Q ds = 2 and Phi_i = 2 + 2 int K_edge Q_i ds; for constant K_edge=-A this gives Phi_i=2/(1+2A)=2L_i^2/pi^2 and Vol_4=4 sum L_i^2 in the A02 normalization, anchor-checked on S^4, CP^2, and S^2 x S^2. I also recorded the twisted determinant/flux identity from the vertical trace formula: for exact Ric=g, (-Delta_B + |F|_h^2/2)w=2w, hence Phi_boundary + (1/2) int |F|_h^2 w = 2 int w and Vol_4 = 2pi^2 Phi_boundary + pi^2 int |F|_h^2 w. This sign is important: twist increases volume for fixed boundary flux but can reduce boundary flux at fixed volume. The formal alpha-family remains a useful collapsed adversary for future Sobolev/Moser and determinant-volume claims, but not a global metric or counterexample.

The broad resolution state is therefore sharper but still open. The negative smoothing route has valid algebraic and four-dimensional scale assets, but its material gaps are the weak nested time-space estimate with finite-neighbor leakage/evolving measures, current-time gamma control, explicit relative-Sobolev or cover hypotheses, volume-normalized chart selection in varying topology, and adaptation from -2a Ric to -2a(Ric+g). More importantly, even full success there remains four-dimensional and special-family. A knowledge request for high-dimensional no-Einstein reservoirs or obstructions returned only already-relevant Ozuch/Morteza-Viaclovsky desingularization sources and did not reveal a broad n>=5 no-Einstein source. Given the all-dimensional target, the next research move should reopen the n>=5 problem and all-dimensional mechanisms rather than letting the session remain indefinitely in a narrow four-dimensional laboratory.
