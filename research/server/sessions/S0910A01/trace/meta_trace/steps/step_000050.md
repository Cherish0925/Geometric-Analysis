## Deep-Reasoning

This step changed the Campedelli/product-quotient front from a broad qualitative impression into two separated mathematical facts with different force for Yau 26. The first fact is a real global-smoothing existence example: inside the classical \((\mathbb Z_2)^3\) Campedelli component there are explicit seven-line boundary configurations with six \(A_1\) points, ample canonical class, and a direct deformation obtained by moving the lines to general position. Through Aubin--Yau, nearby smooth fibers carry negative Kähler--Einstein metrics, and the correct smooth-topological bridge is the local \(A_1\) Milnor-fiber versus \((-2)\)-disk-bundle replacement, not an Ehresmann argument applied to a simultaneous-resolution family. This is meaningful because it shows that the local kernel-directed Eguchi--Hanson smoothing direction can align with exact Einstein existence rather than merely with approximate input metrics. The second fact is more tentative but technically sharper: for the BCGP \(K^2=2\), \(G=\mathbb Z_4^2\) product-quotient row, using the actual two displayed branch triples rather than the shared \((4,4,4)\) signature gives zero invariant dimension in the cover-level spaces \((H^0(2K_{C_1})\otimes H^0(K_{C_2}))^G\) and \((H^0(K_{C_1})\otimes H^0(2K_{C_2}))^G\). This makes global smoothing plausible, but it does not yet prove it: the missing bridge is the exact singular-model obstruction group \(H^2(X,T_X^0)\), or equivalently the correct logarithmic/reflexive dual sheaf comparison at the six \(A_1\) points. The source reading and TTCS both show that ordinary forms on the minimal resolution are not the right object to identify by reflexive extension alone; the natural object passes through \(T_S(-\log E)\), residues along the exceptional curves, and possible local linear conditions. Thus the work gained a concrete existence family and a concrete computation target, while avoiding the false inference that all product-quotient rows have left the counterexample landscape. In parallel, the negative Ishida--LeBrun line was materially weakened as a path toward dimension-only constants: A02 and A03 independently found that nonconstant cutoffs create curvature already on flat \(T^4\), so the normalized cutoff-flow start still needs a coupled transported-coefficient proof, and the global Seiberg--Witten terminal density scales with the topological parameter \(A\), forcing a gap of order at best \(A^{-1/2}\) in the current IL family. The full Yau 26 proposition remains open, but the active research state is better organized: the Campedelli front is now a precise Einstein-existence laboratory; the BCGP row is reduced to a local-to-global logarithmic sheaf computation or moduli-component identification; the IL route no longer looks capable, in its present global-SW/local-flow form, of producing dimension-only constants.
