## Deep-Reasoning

This step achieved the main mathematical construction for the product-quotient/Campedelli front: a complete, TTCS-verified proof that the smooth four-manifold underlying the minimal resolution of the BCGP Z₄² K²=2 canonical model carries a negative Einstein metric.

The central new idea is the reflexive extension bridge: T_X⁰ ≅ (π_*T_Y)^G on the normal quotient surface X with isolated A₁ singularities. This is established by checking that both sheaves are reflexive (T_X⁰ = (Ω¹_X)∨ is reflexive as the dual of a coherent sheaf; (π_*T_Y)^G has depth ≥ 2 by the maximal Cohen–Macaulay property of finite pushforwards of locally free sheaves and the Reynolds splitting), that they agree on the étale locus U = X \ Sing(X) (standard descent for finite étale covers), and that reflexive extension is unique on a normal surface (complement of codimension-2 set). This identification immediately gives H²(X, T_X⁰) = H²(Y, T_Y)^G via Leray + Reynolds, and the latter equals zero by equivariant Serre duality + Künneth + the verified character computation showing both invariant dimensions vanish. This chain bypasses the logarithmic resolution comparison (T_V(-log E) vs T_X⁰) that Step 50 was struggling with — it computes the target sheaf directly on the singular model via the smooth cover.

The downstream chain is standard but each step has been specifically verified: Lee–Park Proposition 2.4(2) globalizes all six local A₁ QG smoothings; ampleness persists from K_X ample (proved via π*K_X = K_Y and K_Y ample on the product) to K_{X_t} ample in the flat family; Aubin–Yau gives negative KE on X_t; ADE simultaneous resolution after a finite base change + Ehresmann gives the diffeomorphism S ≅ X_t. TTCS verified with seven corrections all incorporated: corrected reflexivity proofs (dual-of-coherent for T_X⁰, MCM for invariant pushforward), codimension-one freeness of the diagonal action, non-hyperellipticity via PGL₂ obstruction (not just fixed-point counting within G), correct A₁ equation xy - z² = τ, ampleness via π*K_X = K_Y, and simultaneous resolution + Ehresmann for the global bridge.

A separate important clarification: the A⁻¹/² scaling claim for the IL restricted theorem (from A02 #382 and A03 #383) is withdrawn by A03 #386. The proportional-volume averaging cancels A: ∫Φ ≤ Cδ²A and Vol(P) ≥ αA give mean Φ ≤ (C/α)δ² on some good center, uniformly in A. The IL family-uniform gap is not structurally degraded by the global origin of the Seiberg–Witten inequality, though the flow module remains conditional.

For the K²=4 extension, I found that both curves in the Z₄×Z₂ row are hyperelliptic (element (2,1) fixes 8 = 2g+2 points on C₁; element (0,1) fixes 8 on C₂), so the Sym² shortcut fails. The vanishing computation must use the full Chevalley–Weil formula for 2K rather than Sym² H⁰(K). This is tractable but was not completed in this step.

The deepest structural insight from this work is that the reflexive extension bridge is universal for product-quotient surfaces with RDP: T_X⁰ ≅ (π_*T_Y)^G always holds, and the Einstein existence question reduces to a purely representation-theoretic vanishing condition on the G-action. This creates a systematic method for testing Einstein existence across the entire BCGP classification.
