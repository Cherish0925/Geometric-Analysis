## Deep-Reasoning

This step achieved a structural breakthrough in the positive-branch local obstruction program for Yau Problem 26. The active goal remains the full proposition: for each n≥2, determine whether dimension-only Ricci pinching constants force Einstein existence on closed manifolds. The four-dimensional positive branch is the leading laboratory because it has concrete topology (product-mirror rows), computable spectral data (A01/A02 inter-node coefficients), and calibration controls (OSS/Tian-Yau KE existence on the compatible pattern).

The Step 99 handoff left two open questions: (1) whether the adjacent-balanced row's single-node scale-ratio window survives when all four nodes' equations are imposed simultaneously, and (2) what the uncomputed diagonal tensor cross-chirality coefficient q_diag is. Both are now resolved.

The four-node exchange-closure theorem is a clean finite-dimensional result. In the adjacent-balanced pattern ε=(+,+,-,-), nodes 1 and 2 (positive chirality) have swapped adjacent/diagonal roles to the opposite class: node 1 sees node 3 as adjacent and node 4 as diagonal, while node 2 sees node 3 as diagonal and node 4 as adjacent. This means the single-node feasibility window for node 1 at scale ratio r=u₄/u₃ is the reciprocal of node 2's window. The intersection S₁∩S₂ is nonempty if and only if r=1 lies in S₁, reducing the entire four-node system to a single algebraic condition: |c_adj+c_diag| ≤ q_adj+q_diag. With the audited values |0.06288-0.03545|=0.02743 versus 0.00800+q_diag, this requires q_diag ≥ 0.01943.

The structural diagonal tensor vanishing is the more surprising result. By loading A01's validated step98/step96 spectral framework and calling compute_full_chirality with source pair 'SS' (which the framework already supports but A01 never ran), I found that both AA and BB tensor sectors give identically zero cross-chirality response for the diagonal pair, at all four convergence-ladder rungs. The mechanism is exact pairwise cancellation between cos(2φ) and sin(2φ) tensor modes at antipodal S² poles: sign_c=(-1)^ℓ and sign_s=-(-1)^ℓ are always opposite, while SO(2) rotational symmetry of the kernel plane K=ω⊥ ensures equal contribution magnitudes. This cancellation occurs at every spectral level (ℓ_t, ℓ_s) and in every mode family (electric, magnetic). I verified the sign structure explicitly: at ℓ_t=2,3,4,5, the c2 mode ratio at the S node is (-1)^ℓ_t while the s2 ratio is -(-1)^ℓ_t. Adjacent pairs survive because one factor's tensor poles are the same (ratio +1 for both c2 and s2), so reinforcement rather than cancellation.

The consequence is complete: since q_diag=0 < 0.01943, the adjacent-balanced row is obstructed at leading inter-node order, joining the diagonal-balanced (margin 0.05488) and one/three-reversed rows (also margin 0.05488). ALL five mixed chirality patterns on the positive mirror are obstructed. The coefficient table now explains the known KE/non-KE dichotomy: same-chirality vanishing makes f₄≡0 on the compatible locus (consistent with KE by Tian-Yau), while nonvanishing cross-chirality couplings on mixed rows produce the obstruction (consistent with non-Kähler/non-symplectic structure).

The weakest link is the adjacent-balanced margin at 0.01943. This is about 3.5× the tensor adjacent coefficient (0.008), so it is a comfortable margin at leading order. But the ratio of this margin to the leading AB coefficient (~0.063) is about 31%, which means the relative remainder estimate o(t³) must ultimately control terms at the 31% level relative to the leading cubic.

Several important gaps remain before this becomes a theorem:

1. The self-interaction terms (node j's bubble affecting its own equation) and complement Schur terms must be shown pattern-independent or negligible. The Biquard-Rollin/OSS calibration suggests they cancel in the mixed-minus-compatible comparison, but this requires a full Lyapunov-Schmidt reduction with fixed volume and Bianchi/Ebin slice.

2. Frame/projector motion as the bubble scale varies may contribute O(t³) terms that are not captured by the fixed-frame spectral computation.

3. Global Einstein deformations (the ν_l variables in Ozuch's framework) couple through the chirality change at O(t²), producing O(t³) contributions to the node equations. These must be shown pattern-independent or bounded.

4. The relative remainder must be o(t³), including possible t³log(t) terms from the logarithmic annulus structure. The current crude estimate is O(t^{5/2}) which dominates t³ as t→0—this is the most serious remaining gap.

5. Even a completed local theorem only obstructs the specific product-orbifold degeneration channel, not global Einstein nonexistence on #₃(S²×S²) or 3CP²#3CP̄².

For the negative mirror (Σ₂×Σ₂)/Z₂ with 36 nodes and R^±=diag(-1,0,0), the same spectral framework should apply with sign-flipped curvature. The compatible rows are KE by Aubin-Yau. The structural diagonal vanishing should hold by the same mechanism (it depends on the kernel-plane SO(2) symmetry, not on the sign of Λ). But the much larger number of nodes (36) means the combinatorics of chirality patterns is richer, and the four-node exchange closure needs to be generalized.

For the full Yau-26 problem: the positive mirror local obstruction, even if promoted to a theorem, would need to be combined with (a) a global no-Einstein argument on the mixed rows and (b) a negative-branch obstruction or tight negative pinching on an all-sign no-Einstein manifold. Both remain substantial open problems. The diffuse N#N connector and the Lean curvature formalization are the other two major open fronts.

The structural diagonal vanishing is worth recording as an independent mathematical observation: it says that the Ozuch inter-node tensor-sector coupling has a strict bipartite structure aligned with the product factorization. Diagonal node pairs, which differ on both S² factors, produce no tensor (traceless) coupling—only scalar (trace/isotropic) coupling through the AB sector. This simplifies the finite-dimensional obstruction system and may have implications beyond the specific Yau-26 application.
