## Deep-Reasoning

This step preserved the genuine fixed-J6 advance from Step 35 while preventing it from being converted into a completed analytic platform. The central mathematical distinction is that an energy persistence lemma, a short-time existence theorem, a terminal extension theorem, and the global defect energy needed for calibration are different interfaces of the same proposed mechanism; success in one does not automatically supply the others. The unregularized J6 argument remains valuable because it uses the smooth tensor polynomial s=|E|^2 and the fixed exponent J6=∫η^2 a^4 s^3, so no norm regularization near E=0 is needed. The defect reaction is linear in curvature, hence P6=∫F w6^2 is controlled by the two-factor Sobolev product S K2^{1/2}(X6+r^{-2}J6), independent of the q=3 curvature seed. This is a real structural simplification in the weighted flow program. But it remains conditional on a smooth flow on a first-exit interval, moving Sobolev/support comparability, and small localized K2 product gamma.

The main correction concerns the DeTurck interface. A fixed-coefficient gauged equation with gauge term a L_W g is not geometrically equivalent to the fixed-cutoff flow under pullback. The cancellation vector field is aW, and L_{aW}g=aL_Wg+da⊗W^♭+W^♭⊗da, so the missing da terms matter. Moreover the scalar cutoff is transported by the diffeomorphism. The correct formulation is the transported-coefficient normalized DeTurck system with ahat=a∘phi_t^{-1} and Lie derivative L_{ahat W} ghat, plus the normalized zeroth-order term -2 ahat ghat. Only this system pulls back to ∂_t g=-2a(Ric+g). The generic epsilon-regularized Schauder argument in Step 35 also does not provide epsilon-independent smooth existence: ordinary constants depend on the lower ellipticity bound, and the proposed small-a region estimate contains an unclosed top-derivative term. Yang's local Ricci-flow source shows the right kind of construction: degenerate energy/tame estimates for the transported system, not a generic elliptic-constant argument.

The continuation interface was similarly corrected. A terminal extension theorem must be stated on [0,Tmax), not on an already smooth closed interval. The Step 35 note used unweighted ||T||_∞ and pointwise ||a∇Rm||_∞ as Gronwall coefficients and then called them finite because the solution is smooth; that does not prove integrability up to Tmax. The zero-order bootstrap controls |aF|, not unweighted |T| near a=0, and it gives no pointwise first-derivative control. The viable route remains the shifted-curvature Wang induction with G=(g∧g)/(2(n-1)), T=Rm+G, U=aT, preserving Ric(U)=aE and ∂_tΓ=g^{-1}*∇Ric(U). At the base level one must control the coupled pair (∇U,∇^2χ) by signed integration of the raw terms χ∇^3χ*U, ∇χ*∇^2χ*U, χ∇^2χ*∇U, and χ∇χ*∇^2U, absorbing the derivative-of-U terms through the χ^2-weighted dissipation. The existing Step 27 and Codex material give a credible roadmap, but Step 35 did not complete this proof.

The terminal calibration interface was kept explicit. The global Ishida--LeBrun/Chern--Weil calibration uses Q(t)=∫_M|E|^2, and neither J6=∫η^2a^4|E|^6 nor ||aE||∞ controls this quantity near a=0. The separate homogeneous Q estimate from Steps 14, 20, and 23 must be carried as its own first-exit component: Q'+c∫a|∇E|^2≤C∫((|da|^2/a)+a)|E|^2+C∫a|Rm||E|^2, with the last term absorbed support-by-support by the same gamma product. This supplies Q(t)≤e^{Cθ}Q(0) when the first-exit geometry and K2 control hold. It is distinct from the fixed J6 seed and from weighted velocity control.

A02 message #340 was incorporated as a broader warning about coarse energy accounting in the positive collapsed front. The alternating toric collapsed adversary survives current edge-cone/strut accounting because the problematic transitions are NUTs rather than codimension-two struts. This does not change the A03 fixed-J6 calculation, but it reinforces that neither the positive branch nor the negative Ishida--LeBrun route can be treated as solving the full Yau 26 statement. The original goal remains far beyond the current restricted four-dimensional negative laboratory: positive branch, arbitrary topology, dimensions n≥5, dimension-only constants, and Lean formalization remain open.
