## 1. Spin/seam analysis of the A_1 fillings

O = (S^2×S^2)/Z_2 (diagonal antipodal involution) has four A_1 points.
Each filling piece is the disk bundle O(ε·2)→S^2, ε = −1 compatible
(crepant/KE side) or +1 reversed; both are spin, with boundary RP^3.

**Lemma (seam spin ledger).**
1. RP^3 has exactly two spin structures, distinguished by the Rokhlin
   μ-invariant: μ = ±1 (mod 16).  μ changes sign under orientation reversal.
   Every orientation-preserving diffeomorphism of RP^3 preserves each spin
   structure: π_0 Diff(RP^3) = Z/2 (Hatcher), generated by an
   orientation-reversing isometry; the identity component is connected, so it
   acts trivially on the two-element spin set, and the μ = ±1 values can never
   be swapped by an orientation-preserving map.  Hence **a gluing
   diffeomorphism can never repair a seam spin mismatch.**
2. O(ε·2) has H^1(·;Z/2) = 0, hence a unique spin structure, inducing on its
   boundary RP^3 the spin structure with μ = ε
   (Rokhlin: μ(∂) ≡ σ(filling) = ε·2... = ε·σ class; for O(±2), σ = ±1).
3. X* = O minus the four A_1 charts is spin: the lift of the diagonal
   involution to the spin bundle of S^2×S^2 squares to +1 (squares to the
   deck transformation on each factor, cancel on the diagonal).  Its signature
   is σ(X*) = 0 by Novikov additivity against the all-compatible resolution
   (Step 49: σ = −4 = σ(X*) + 4·(−1)).
4. Filling all four seams spin-compatibly gives a closed spin manifold, to
   which Rokhlin applies: σ(X*) ≡ Σ_i μ_i (mod 16), forcing the seam
   μ-pattern **{+1,+1,−1,−1}**: exactly two seams of each type.
5. Therefore M(pattern) is spin ⟺ every seam is filled spin-compatibly
   ⟺ the two reversed (O(+2), boundary μ = +1) pieces sit exactly on the two
   seams where ∂X* carries μ = −1.  Both C(4,2) = 6 subset choices occur;
   precisely the pairs equal to the distinguished μ = −1 pair (and its orbit
   under the symmetry group) give spin fillings.  Which symmetry class that is
   (adjacent vs. diagonal pair) is the single open bit of the classification;
   it does not affect the list of realizable homeomorphism types.

Everything except the final distinguished-pair identification is proved by
the ledger; the identification only selects which concrete pattern realizes
the spin row.

All mixed rows (r = 1,2,3): b_+ ≥ 2 ⇒ ordinary SW invariants vanish

they are exactly the "SW-void simply connected b_+ ≥ 2" testbeds.

## 3. The merge of the two positive arcs

The spin balanced row is homeomorphic to #_3(S^2×S^2), the k = 3 member of
the Step-9 collapse stress family M_k = #_k(S^2×S^2).  Consequences:

- The **noncollapsed wall and the collapse stress family now meet on one
  named homeomorphism type**.  On it we already have: explicit
  Biquard–Ozuch near-pinched metrics with ‖Ric−g‖_op = O(t^{1/2}) from a
  *noncollapsed* bubble-tree construction (Steps 49–50); and the Step-9
  dichotomy that any tight pinching sequence there is either defect-bounded

- Caveat of record: this is a **homeomorphism**.  Diffeomorphism to the
  standard smooth #_3(S^2×S^2) is open (exotic structures are not excluded),
  and SW invariants cannot help (they vanish).  For Yau-26 only the
  diffeotype matters for naming; the glued smooth manifold is a perfectly
  valid testbed either way, and P_4^+ quantifies over all smooth structures.

## 4. Route allocation judgment (the Step-63 handoff question)

1. **Option (a) — global drift-neck realization on a mixed-wall row — is
   defunded.**  The Strategist is right: the proposition's refutation needs
   (i) arbitrarily pinched metrics and (ii) non-existence of any Einstein
   metric on the smooth manifold.  The construction half is done to the level
   current tools allow (Steps 49–50 + operator-norm principle); (ii) is the
   whole open problem.  Building more necks invests in the solved half.
   Step 63's sharpness result says the obstruction framework cannot decide
   (ii): within δ·log(1/t)→0 the first obstruction must vanish; beyond it the
   framework is blind.  Neither conclusion bears on Einstein existence.
2. **The decision point is Einstein status on the χ = 8 mixed rows, now with
   names.**  Cheapest entry: the two balanced rows #_3(S^2×S^2) and
   #_3CP^2#_3CP̄^2 (both realized; the latter needs no spin gluing and is
   therefore available unconditionally).  Two-pronged attack as the
   Strategist proposed: prong one is now essentially complete (this section);
   prong two is the decisive experiment — run Einstein–DeTurck / variational
   Ricci search from the explicit near-pinched glued metric on one balanced
   row.  Convergence ⇒ new positive-Einstein existence machinery on an
   SW-void non-Kähler s.c. manifold (and a verified necessary consequence of
   P_4^+); robust divergence with an identified mechanism ⇒ P_4^+ refuted.
   Non-perturbative by necessity: Step 48–49's higher obstructions block
   exact gluing nearby, so any Einstein metric is far away; a flow is
   indifferent to that distinction.
3. **H_flux gets exactly one final focused attack, then is recorded as a
   standing hypothesis.**  Fifteen steps have localized it to: TT/Bianchi
   gauge choice, ambient H_2/H_4 tower subtraction, nonlinear/gauge error,
   inner/outer flux normalization, with the δ·log(1/t) rate boundary now
   known to be sharp (Step 63).  It is the positive arc's only proof-side
   asset; after one honest attempt it stops consuming arcs.
4. **Negative branch: A02 does not join the IL plumbing.**  A01 owns the
   defect-Moser blocking gap (A01 #441; A03 Codex derivation in #445, still
   conditional on upstream persistence inputs); A03's Di Cerbo margin is
   exactly saturated (#446) and its graph route restructured around μ_4
   (#449–450).  A02's negative-side contribution stays adjudicative and
   cheap: the p = 1/3 sharpness experiment (near-pinched constructions on IL
   manifolds just above p = 1/3 against the stable gap √((p−1/3)/2)), the
   only experiment distinguishing a real risk locus from an estimate artifact.
5. **A03 #450 asks whether A02's jet-drift channel can produce a genuine
   counterexample.**  Answer (already in the record, now endorsed): no — as a
   counterexample route it is bounded by the necessity theorem it sharpens.
   Its value is the classification of the escape channel (exponential bubble
   scale t ≲ e^{−c/δ} with universal two-point Weyl reachability, Step 63),
   i.e. it tells any would-be proof of P_4^+ exactly where it must lose, and
   any would-be counterexample exactly which global problem it must solve.
