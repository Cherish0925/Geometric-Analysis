## Deep-Reasoning

This step stabilized two neighboring fronts of the Yau--26 project without overclaiming either. The positive product-quotient work from Steps 49--50 is now best understood as a genuine, bankable one-way hardness reduction rather than as a counterexample program or a gluing theorem. The decisive mathematical separation is between the first Ozuch obstruction, whose term is order one in the Ricci endomorphism norm after scaling by the Eguchi--Hanson core metric, and all higher exact-Einstein obstructions, whose terms vanish in the same C^0 operator norm. Thus the four A1 nodes of (S^2 x S^2)/Z2, with R_+=R_-=diag(Lambda,0,0), admit kernel choices for either chirality and produce positive near-Einstein input metrics on mixed smooth fillings. The legitimate conclusion is only that P_4^+ would force those mixed PSC b_+>=2 four-manifolds to admit some Einstein metric. This is strong because all known simply connected compact positive Einstein four-manifold examples in the session record have b_+<=1 and no available theorem decides these mixed rows; it is not a theorem that b_+>=2 forbids positive Einstein metrics, and it is not a refutation of Yau--26. The product-quotient arc therefore locates a wall in the four-dimensional positive noncollapsed branch and should not consume further effort through spin-chasing, V4 enumeration, or forcing Seiberg--Witten obstructions onto positive-scalar-curvature rows.

The second part of the step judged the negative Yang--Ishida--LeBrun chain from A03/A01. The newest A03 notes make real progress on normalized local Ricci flow start/restart and terminal continuation, but the proof record has a critical sign and proof-route issue. The final reaction coefficient (2p-n) in the normalized L^p curvature energy is correct, as seen immediately from the constant-scaling test for partial_t g=-2ag: the metric-norm variation contributes 2p int aF^p and the volume variation contributes -n int aF^p. Hence the reaction vanishes at p=n/2. But A03 Step 51 contains intermediate lines that add the volume contribution with the wrong sign and write 2p+n before later reverting to 2p-n; those lines must be superseded before the note is cited as stable. The Hessian-source removal is also not yet written safely: using a bound such as |Delta_g a|<=C repeats the cutoff-jet mistake that A03 Step 50 had just corrected, because metric equivalence does not control evolving covariant Hessians of a fixed scalar cutoff. A safer route is to integrate one derivative directly from nabla^2 a onto F^{p-2}Rm, use |nabla a|^2/a=4|nabla chi|^2, and absorb the resulting weighted gradient term into Yang's dissipation, leaving only Vol and int F^p terms. This preserves the qualitative claim that no nonhomogeneous Moser theory is needed, but it shifts the exact proof burden to uniform first-exit constants.

The global allocation follows from these audits. The per-datum normalized Yang start should be cited through the transported-coefficient DeTurck/Nash--Moser perturbation, where delta(-2 ahat ghat)=-2 ahat h+4 chihat d chihat(zeta) ghat sits in Yang's lower-order Dh+E psi slots; A03's reintroduced nonlinear a+epsilon^2 coefficient-regularization wording should not be used as the load-bearing module. The terminal-slab continuation via shifted curvature T=Rm+G and U=aT is plausible and better organized than earlier versions, but it still serves the negative theorem only after the simultaneous first-exit preservation estimates are verified. The narrow next target is therefore A2 in A03's conditional theorem: on the selected support up to T=theta r_*^2, prove or break the coupled preservation of H_2, Q, J_6 and the derived pointwise bounds for aRm and aE, beginning with the corrected normalized critical H_2/L^2 inequality. This is the first place where the restricted negative theorem can truly fail or become bankable. The full Yau--26 problem remains open: positive collapse, n>=5, arbitrary topology, and Lean formalization are still major unresolved obligations.
