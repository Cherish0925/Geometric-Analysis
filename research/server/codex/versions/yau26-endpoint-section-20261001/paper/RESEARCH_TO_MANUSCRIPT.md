## The remaining mathematical conclusions

1. **Weak zero-Weyl endpoint:** a global signed separation or joint bound
   beyond the existing quadratic budgets, or an actual closed escaping
   sequence satisfying the complete supply and topological assumptions.
   The target is a volume-scale conclusion, not a local positive cost.
2. **Balanced optimal first-order cost:** a lower bound against every
   genuine near-minimizing geometry, matched by a recovery construction.
   The compensated target is `Z >= 8 pi^2 (Q-N) - o(Q)` with
   `P = 8 pi^2 N + Z`. Linear order is already established; coefficient
   four is not assumed.
3. **New actual scope:** a natural supplier or family genuinely outside
   the previously covered range that satisfies the weaker single-solution
   hypothesis. Rephrasing the same supplier is not such an application.
4. **Full classical budget:** a bound on all possible monopole classes
   at the precision needed for the strongest BF comparison. The known
   sign-set calculation is complete and does not imply this bound.
5. **Independent global existence tests:** diameter-free realization on
   the specified Chern boundary, all Einstein outputs on the fixed bundle,
   and the remaining general-input matching and repair questions. They
   are not unproved lemmas of the integral coercivity theorem.

## Scope of the month-wide integration

The original three-line program has not been declared solved. This paper
centers the first line and includes the older constructions and analytical
modules that enter its proofs, limitations, or realization questions.
Independent second- and third-line programs remain in the original
research tree; they are not silently relabeled as first-line theorems.
In particular, the independent A1/log-Weyl construction program is not
replaced by the short Ricci-small curvature-spike example included here.

The continuous source and its compilation are a usable mathematical
handoff, not a claim that every statement in the month-long archive has
been independently reproved. The original arguments, theorem hypotheses,
and the explicit open conclusions remain available for detailed reading.
