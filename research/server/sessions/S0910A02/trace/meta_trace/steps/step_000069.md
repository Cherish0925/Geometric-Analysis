This Agent4 Execute step reviewed the Step-68 handoff against the full Yau--26 target and corrected the research allocation. The original problem remains completely unresolved for n>=4: no sign branch has been proved, no fixed-dimensional counterexample has been produced, no mixed-row manifold has been shown to lack every Einstein metric of either sign, and no full Lean formalization of the target exists. The useful progress is that several previously unstable local facts have now become verified, bounded-scope research assets, and the ceiling of the mixed product-orbifold laboratory is clearer. First, I resolved the degraded TTCS conflict from Step 68. The TTCS integration had claimed a two-dimensional quotient TT kernel on (S2 x S2)/Z2 by applying Kroencke's positive-product scalar-eigenfunction formula too broadly. Reading Kroencke directly shows the global convention n>=3 unless otherwise asserted and, more importantly, Lemma 4.3 records the special spherical overlap between conformal and Hessian summands. On a round S2 with Ric=Λg, a first spherical harmonic satisfies Hess f=-Λ f g. Hence a scalar-sector tensor A f g1 + B f g2 + C Hess f reduces to (A-CΛ) f g1 + B f g2; trace-free gives (A-CΛ)+B=0 and divergence-free gives A-CΛ=0, so B=0 and the reduced tensor is zero. The same holds for first harmonics on the second factor. Factor TT modes vanish, mixed one-form modes are positive, and h_rel=g1-g2 has eigenvalue -Λ for Ozuch P, not zero. Therefore the quotient TT kernel is zero. However the full gauged P-kernel still contains four quotient-invariant scalar directions u g1, u g2, v g1, v g2, so Ozuch computations need an explicit slice or full-kernel projection; zero TT kernel is not full invertibility. Second, I preserved the Step-68 reflection and operator-convention corrections: c(z,w)=(conj z,w) descends to the product orbifold, fixes all four A1 nodes, reverses orientation and exchanges chiralities, so single-node compatible/reversed obstruction towers are isometry-conjugate, not coefficientwise identical in arbitrary bases. Globally it sends the reversed set S to its complement, forcing r=1 and r=3 systems to be conjugate and r=0/r=4 to match after the known pure-row smoothing input. Ozuch's convention is P=1/2 nabla^*nabla - Rcirc and Kroencke's Delta_E=2P; h_rel is a bounded saddle mode with P h_rel=-Λ h_rel, not a small eigenvalue. Third, reading the 20260912 manuscript lines 447--593 and 890--1003 changes the status of the near-pinched construction side. The manuscript's FND:A1construction already gives a rigorous fixed-A1-resolution near-Einstein construction with delta_sigma(g_a)<=C/|log a|, using an explicit Bianchi-IX core q_Lambda and a genuine EH linear correction h_tilde. Step 68 verified the q_Lambda derivative coefficients and the log-Weyl operator identity; this step audited the clean flux constants. The flux section supplies the gauge-free identity that earlier Steps 34--63 had treated as the H_flux hypothesis in the clean annulus setting: because o=r^{-n-2}K is TT and o(nu,.)=0, integration by parts kills gauge terms and gives integral_A <D Ric u,o>=-(n+2)I_K/2. In dimension four this is -3 I_K. For the spherical model the constants are I_K=v_Gamma/216 and J_K=v_Gamma/9, giving the sharp unconstrained 1/(8L) lower bound and the constant-scalar 1/(6L) lower bound. I encoded this arithmetic in workspace/scripts/step69_flux_identity_constants.py -> workspace/data/step69_flux_identity_constants.json. Fourth, I began real formalization harvest rather than postponing all Lean work to a hypothetical final breakthrough. I wrote workspace/proofs/Step69FluxKernelAlgebra.lean, which formalizes the rational flux constants, the spherical-model constants, the scalar-sector TT algebra, and the Ozuch parallel-mode sign convention. It compiles under lean_check, and the named theorem Yau26Step69.auditedCore was dependency-checked by qiushi-yau26 lean with only the allowed axioms propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound, saved as workspace/data/step69_lean_audited_core_audit.json. This is not a formalization of Yau--26 or of Ricci geometry, but it is a correct stable kernel that future formalization can import. Fifth, I read A01's restricted IL canonical assembly in bounded form. It may be the nearest restricted negative theorem, but it remains restricted to an Ishida--LeBrun geography and depends on A03 tame hierarchy/index-level identities plus a real-exponent Moser display; even if completed, it is not a sign-unrestricted Yau--26 disproof because it does not defeat both signs in one fixed dimension with no Einstein metric of any sign. The next important proof-side object in A02's positive/noncollapsed arc is no longer the clean-annulus flux or another residual grid, but the transfer theorem: applying the manuscript flux identities to actual noncollapsed bubbling sequences after ambient tower subtraction, Bianchi/harmonic gauge normalization, weighted neck-energy control, endpoint-error control, TT quadrupole identification, and inner/outer flux matching. The mixed-row Ozuch multipole computation remains useful but has a bounded ceiling: nonzero means only no nearby Einstein desingularization, while vanishing plus transversality would give important new positive Einstein examples, not a proof of P4+ itself.
