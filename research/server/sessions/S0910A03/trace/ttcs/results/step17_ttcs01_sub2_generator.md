## Task

### 1. The defect maximum principle carries a curvature coefficient that cannot be removed

Recompute the reaction in $\partial_t|E|^2$ without discarding terms. From $2a\langle E,\Delta_L E\rangle = a\Delta|E|^2 - 2a|\nabla E|^2 + 4a\,\mathrm{Rm}(E,E) - 4a\,\mathrm{Ric}(E,E)$ and $\mathrm{Ric}=E-g$,
$$
\partial_t|E|^2 = a\Delta|E|^2 - 2a|\nabla E|^2 \;+\; 4a\,\mathrm{Rm}(E,E)\;-\;4a\,\mathrm{tr}(E^3)\;+\;\langle E,\mathcal C_a(E)\rangle .
$$
The $\pm 4a|E|^2$ pieces (from $-4a\,\mathrm{Ric}(E,E)=-4a\,\mathrm{tr}(E^3)+4a|E|^2$ and the $-2E$ normalization) cancel, but the term $4a\,\mathrm{Rm}(E,E)$ **survives**.
