## Deep-Reasoning

This Agent1 Explore step re-centered the project on the exact Yau--26 quantifiers and audited the most load-bearing inherited object: the fixed-A1 log-Weyl near-pinched construction. The important correction is that the product-quotient mixed rows are not by themselves a refutation of the original existential-interval proposition. They are a decisive test for the normalized positive branch P_4^+ and for any interval with positive interior, because scaling can place their positive Ricci eigenvalues into such an interval. But if the original proposition is allowed to choose a purely negative interval, a positive near-pinched no-Einstein row would not defeat that choice. Thus a full refutation of the original statement must still defeat both sign branches in one dimension, while a proof of the original statement can proceed by proving just one legitimate branch in every dimension. This quantifier distinction is now written into the Step 83 note and must supersede any earlier wording that a positive fixed row alone would refute the whole original formulation.

The low-dimensional status is now recorded as finished ordinary mathematics, though not Lean formalization. In dimension 2, uniformization gives a constant-curvature metric on every closed connected smooth surface, so the Einstein conclusion holds without pinching. In dimension 3, the original existential proposition is satisfied by choosing a positive Ricci interval, for instance 1/2 g <= Ric <= 3/2 g; Hamilton's theorem gives a spherical space-form metric and hence a positive Einstein metric. The normalized negative 3D branch is also standard: sufficiently tight Ricci near -g implies all sectional curvatures are negative by the 3D Ricci-to-sectional formula, and geometrization gives a hyperbolic metric. These results close n=2 and n=3 at the mathematical level but still rely on classical theorems rather than a faithful Lean curvature stack.

The main technical work was the construction audit. I read the cumulative manuscript `Skills/qiushi-yau26/references/research/20260912.tex`, especially the construction lines 447--606, flux/log-annulus lines 891--1005, and product-quotient topology lines 1068--1097, together with Step 68 core and annulus data, Step 74 obstruction-scale note, and the Step 82 sign-leg map. I then wrote `Sessions/S0910A02/workspace/scripts/step83_full_weyl_log_annulus_audit.py` and ran it to produce `Sessions/S0910A02/workspace/data/step83_full_weyl_log_annulus_audit.json`. The script constructs a rational basis of all ten four-dimensional Weyl quadratic sectors, five in Sym^2_0(Lambda^+) and five in Sym^2_0(Lambda^-), and verifies for every basis element that the associated normal quadratic tensor K has trace zero, divergence zero, Laplacian zero, radial gauge K(x,.)=0, D Ric_e K=0, and the monomial radial identity D Ric_e(r^p K)=-(p^2+6p)r^pK/(2r^2) for p=1,2,-1. The output reports `all_basis_checks_pass=true` with basis_count 10. This addresses the Strategist's sharp concern that the product-node H_2 jet might contain active non-U(2) angular sectors producing an order-one defect: the annulus identity covers the full Weyl space in both chiralities, so the five active angular components at a product node are not missed.

I wrote `Sessions/S0910A02/workspace/notes/step83_near_pinched_construction_and_frontier_reduction.md` as a theorem-grade research note, then submitted it to TTCS verification with the actual script, data, Step 68 checks, Step 82 sign map, and the cumulative TeX source. TTCS found no hidden O(1) raised-index defect in the EH core, log annulus, ambient patch, or finite four-node assembly, but it caught crucial proof-hygiene requirements. First, the ambient quadratic normal jet H0 and the EH far-field H1 must be in the same radial normal gauge; equality of Ricci contractions for arbitrary quadratic tensors would not by itself imply that K=H0-H1 is a pure Weyl normal tensor. Second, the EH tail and ambient Taylor remainders must be O_2 estimates, not just formal O terms. Third, the monomial symbolic checks are supporting evidence, while the proof for arbitrary smooth radial cutoff should use the analytic derivation: because tr(eta K)=0 and div(eta K)=0, D Ric_e(eta K)=-1/2 Delta(eta K), and since K is harmonic and homogeneous of degree two, Delta(eta K)=(eta_rr+7 eta_r/r)K=(eta_tt+6 eta_t)K/r^2. I rewrote the note to include exactly these conditions and the corrected theorem statement.

The resulting audited construction theorem is: for a fixed closed oriented Einstein 4-orbifold with finitely many A1 points, fixed equivariant normal charts, fixed EH end markings and chiralities, and the gauged EH tensor satisfying D Ric_EH htilde=g_EH with O_2 ALE tail in common radial normal gauge, the fixed smooth marked resolution carries metrics g_a with ||Ric_g_a - sigma g_a||_op <= C |log a|^{-1}. The three-zone error budget is now explicit. In the core, the (0,2) residual is O(a^4(1+rho)^2) and raising an index by G_a^{-1}~a^{-2} gives O(a^2(1+rho)^2)=O(a^2+r^2), with inner cutoff errors O(b^2+a^4 b^{-6}+a^4 b^{-4})=O(a) for b=a^{1/2}. In the log annulus, the pure Weyl mismatch gives O(1/L), and nonlinear plus target-subtraction terms give O(R^2). In the ambient zone, A1 evenness gives g0=e+H0+O_2(r^4), so cutting at R=a^{1/4} gives O(R^2)=O(a^{1/2}). With L=log(R/(8b))=1/4 |log a|+O(1), the global defect is O(|log a|^{-1}). Four-node patching is safe because the modification balls are disjoint for small a, Ricci is local, and the global constant is the maximum of finitely many fixed local constants. A01's message #583 correcting a missed low eigenvalue Green channel matters for exact Einstein desingularization coefficients, but not for this local near-pinched input construction.

The current frontier is therefore sharper. The positive-mirror mixed rows are genuine theorem-grade positive near-pinched test manifolds outside all known positive-Einstein existence classes and outside all known no-Einstein certificates. They force P_4^+, if true, to produce Einstein metrics on smooth types including #3(S^2 x S^2) and 3CP^2#3CPbar^2. But they are not a counterexample without a separate all-sign Einstein nonexistence theorem, and the sign legs remain frontier-level: lambda>0 would need a new positive-Einstein rigidity theorem beyond Gursky--LeBrun/crude alignment/known CLR; lambda=0 is forced into the unprecedented compact generic SO(4) Ricci-flat loophole after excluding flat, reducible, and SU(2)/K3 mechanisms; lambda<0 has no compatible obstruction currently known. This step therefore strengthens the reduction and staged-result record, not the final resolution of Yau--26.
