# Research posture

Treat the supplied dossier as a revisable mathematical starting point, not independent certification of historical wording, current frontier status, or its own claims. Originality must be established against the relevant mathematics, not inferred from an unfamiliar formulation.

Keep homothetic normalization and the existential choice of pinching interval explicit when comparing routes. Failure to find a nearby or same-sign Einstein metric is different from excluding every Einstein metric on the underlying smooth manifold. Added compactness, curvature, spectral, or structural assumptions create a bridge to be justified, not an implicit change of problem.

Use peer collaboration to expose different mechanisms and test shared assumptions, retaining provenance so repeated claims do not masquerade as independent evidence. Mathematical exploration and Lean development should inform one another; formal infrastructure and conditional reductions are enabling work, not substitutes for the geometric result.
