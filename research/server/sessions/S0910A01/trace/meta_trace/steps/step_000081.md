## Deep-Reasoning

This step strengthened the positive mixed-row product-orbifold branch by turning the Step 80 parity reduction into a more precise radial-scale problem. The central object remains local: Einstein desingularization near (S²×S²)/⟨(r_π,r_π)⟩ for the opposite-corner mixed chirality row, not a proof of the full sign-unrestricted Yau 26 proposition. The step’s main mathematical correction is that the remaining radial response cannot be represented as a scale-blind sum of B_ij t_j³ terms. Ozuch’s finite-dimensional equation contains a receiver scale t_i, while a source EH bubble contributes t_j² through its H⁴ far field and the regular Green response at the receiver. Thus the candidate even radial layer has the form F_i(t₁,t₂)=t_i Σ_j B_ij t_j²+R_i. This distinction is essential because equal-scale substitutions hide the relative-scale structure where balancing would have to occur.

The step also verified the single-factor antipodal symmetry L(x,y)=(-x,y), which had been suggested as a way to relate the two radial equations. In local product frames, L is represented by diag(1,-1,1,1), has determinant -1, descends through the quotient because it commutes with σ=(r_π,r_π), and exchanges the two A-orbits of nodes {NN,SS} and {NS,SN}. Since the diagonal mixed chirality assignment is ε(s₁,s₂)=s₁s₂, L reverses chirality while reversing four-dimensional orientation, hence swaps Λ⁺ and Λ⁻ in exactly the required way. Executable linear algebra showed ω₁⁺↦-ω₁⁻, ω₂⁺↦ω₂⁻, and ω₃⁺↦ω₃⁻, so the J-fixed radial direction is transported with positive sign and the rank-one product curvature block diag(1,0,0) is carried to the corresponding block in the opposite chirality.

Because Step 79 had exposed the danger of freezing Ozuch’s angular ρ_x, this step tested L on the full angular H⁴ formula. The script step81_L_H4_transport.py verified that the complete angular source satisfies L^*H⁴_-(Lx)=H⁴_+(x) with zero numerical deviation on 20,000 S³ samples, using the spatially varying ρ_x rather than a fixed anti-self-dual vector. TTCS then pointed out that the two-form direction alone is not the full radial obstruction normalization. I therefore added a further script, step81_L_radial_tensor_transport.py, using Ozuch’s leading kernel asymptotic o₁(ζ⁺)=ζ_(1)⁺∘ρ(ζ)⁻/r⁴+O(r⁻⁸), and verified L^*O_rad^-(Lx)=O_rad^+(x) at the leading asymptotic tensor level. This supports the +1 transport factor when the radial kernel and radial projection are defined by the EH-family derivative in the ω₂ scale direction and the two chiralities use the same flux or L² normalization convention.

The TTCS reading confirmed the core of the Step 81 correction and supplied two important refinements, both incorporated into the note. First, the L relation B=[[a,b],[b,a]] is meaningful only after the complete radial kernel tensors and projection functionals are normalized by L transport, not merely after transporting the insertion two-form. Second, once B has exchange form, a nonzero positive-scale kernel cannot occur at an arbitrary unequal ratio: writing x=t₁² and y=t₂² gives ax+by=0 and bx+ay=0, so a positive kernel requires a+b=0; if a=-b≠0 then x=y and the candidate branch is asymptotically equal-scale; if a=b=0 then the entire candidate matrix vanishes and the next radial layer must be computed. Symmetry relates the equations but does not force the cancellation a+b=0.

A further combinatorial script, step81_radial_B_combinatorics.py, clarified what the coefficients mean in the opposite-corner spin row. For a representative receiver NN in orbit 1, the local self source contributes t₁t₁²; the same-orbit companion SS is diagonal and same-chirality, contributing to a possible coefficient d; and the two cross-orbit sources NS and SN are adjacent and opposite-chirality, contributing two copies of an adjacent coefficient c. Thus in representative or orbit-averaged normalization, F₁=t₁((s+d)t₁²+2c t₂²)+..., F₂=t₂(2c t₁²+(s+d)t₂²)+..., so a=s+d and b=2c. This corrects a possible route error: in the diagonal spin row, cross-orbit mixed-chirality pairs are adjacent, not diagonal. Diagonal geometry appears in the same-orbit same-chirality companion term, whose leading kernel-block contribution is expected to vanish by chirality splitting but must remain in the full projected expansion until proved there.

The remaining hard object is now sharper but still not computed. The current Ozuch estimate only gives t_i λ_i+t_i² μ_i=O(t_max^(5/2)); at comparable scales this is larger than an order-three response, so it cannot establish that the candidate t_i t_j² layer is the first unresolved term. Ozuch Remark 2.11 also warns that higher asymptotic terms can contain logarithms, so the first true even radial term might involve t_i t_j² log t_j or related expressions rather than a pure constant matrix. The next work must therefore build a projected A,J,L-equivariant expansion precise enough to separate pure cubic terms, logarithmic terms, local self-response, same-orbit same-chirality response, adjacent cross-orbit response, Bianchi/DeTurck corrections, cutoff contributions, and the even shape mode g₁-g₂. Only after that can a value of a+b support a real mathematical conclusion about this nearby-orbifold construction. The full Yau 26 problem remains unresolved; the IL negative-gap theorem and graph-neck work remain separate live branches, while A02’s positive Einstein topology tests remain complementary.
