## Deep-Reasoning

This step resolved two of the three remaining analytic obstacles for the four-dimensional negative-branch conditional gap theorem. The research position is now sharply defined: the entire conditional chain from Ricci pinching to the projector contradiction depends on a single unproved theorem—the smooth continuation of the normalized degenerate cutoff flow.

The first resolved obstacle was the general-p weighted tensor inequality. A01's Codex worker had previously established the index-level derivation for the fixed exponents q=2 (critical curvature energy), q=3 (weighted subcritical curvature potential), and the quadratic defect Q. This step extended that derivation to all p ≥ 3 for both curvature and defect. The key structure is: the boundary term d(η²a^{p-1}) has a d(η) part that vanishes identically because a^{p-1} = 0 on supp(dη) for p ≥ 3, and a da part that gives Cp² r^{-2} K_p via the identity a^{p-3}|da|² = 4a^{p-2}|dχ|². The Hessian-a integration by parts produces the same type of remainder. The algebraic reaction is bounded by Cp∫η²a^{p-1}FΦ^p, and the full Young/Sobolev chain gives polynomial p³ growth—sufficient for the Moser iteration convergence since Σ(j+log p_j)/p_j < ∞. For the defect, the regularization Φ_ε = (|E|²+ε²)^{1/2} has the correct chain rule (∂_t-aΔ)Φ_ε^p = (p/2)Φ_ε^{p-2}(∂_t-aΔ)|E|² - p(p-2)aΦ_ε^{p-2}|∇Φ_ε|², with all estimates ε-uniform. The limit ε→0 uses dominated convergence for zero-order terms and weak lower semicontinuity for gradient dissipation. Noninteger powers a^{p-2} for the Moser sequence p_j = 3·(3/2)^j require either infinitely flat cutoff or W^{1,∞} approximation, not the naive claim that χ^α is smooth for all α.

The second resolved obstacle was the initial relative Sobolev inequality on collapsed selected balls. The Maheux–Saloff-Coste theorem (Math. Ann. 303, 1995, Theorem 1.1) provides S = C(n,Kr²)·r²·Vol(B_r)^{-1/2} on any geodesic ball with only Ric ≥ kg, with C independent of volume. The proof combines Bishop-Gromov doubling and Buser's Poincaré inequality. For compactly supported functions, the mean subtraction passage adds only a lower-order r^{-2}||u||₂² term. In dimension four with K = 1+δ and the Li radius r = r*, Kr² is uniformly bounded, so C is uniform. The volume dependence enters only through the explicit Vol^{-1/2} factor, which is exactly the scaling used throughout the weighted energy argument.

The TTCS verification confirmed the curvature general-p structure, the Maheux-Saloff-Coste application, and the J₆ critical absorption mechanism, while identifying five corrections: (1) an extra S^{-1} in the absorption formula, (2) the collar constant should not appear in the active-region energy inequality, (3) the defect regularization chain rule formula, (4) noninteger powers need proper treatment, and (5) the first-exit condition (F3a) should use Ĥ₂(0) not H₂(0). All corrections were applied.

The corrected β̄ definition uses the active-region energy Ĥ₂ = H₂ - C_col directly, with the observation that all cutoff remainder terms have support in {a > 0}, so the active-region inequality has no collar additive constant. This eliminates the concern about β̄ propagation being spoiled by an uncontrolled collar contribution.

A01's Step 26 independently derived the same endpoint |aE| ≤ Cδ(r²/t)^{1/3} through Yang Appendix B with p₀ = 6, q = 6, and the input weight χ⁸ = a⁴ = J₆. Both sessions converge on the identification: the q=3 curvature layer supplies the potential (∫χ²F³)^{1/3}, and the p=6 defect energy supplies the initial data J₆(0) ≤ Cδ⁶V. The volume cancellation in the endpoint gives the volume-independent defect bound.

The single remaining analytic obligation is the normalized Yang-Wang smooth continuation/extension theorem: showing that bounded |χ²Rm| suffices to extend the flow ∂_tg = -2χ²(Ric+g). A detailed specification was written, identifying exactly which lemmas in Wang's proof require adaptation and showing that all new terms from -2χ²g are controlled by the same induction structure. No new geometric quantity or idea is needed.

Even completion of this entire chain would establish only a pinching gap for a critical four-dimensional negative Ishida-LeBrun subclass. The positive branch, unrestricted four-dimensional topology, dimensions n ≥ 5, dimension-only interval constants, and Lean formalization remain open. The terminal geometric contradiction uses the Step 6 projector argument (constant W⁺ spectrum forces ∇W⁺ = 0 by the projector lemma, contradicting the Einstein-Weyl scalar identity |∇W⁺|² = 1/16), not locally symmetric classification.
