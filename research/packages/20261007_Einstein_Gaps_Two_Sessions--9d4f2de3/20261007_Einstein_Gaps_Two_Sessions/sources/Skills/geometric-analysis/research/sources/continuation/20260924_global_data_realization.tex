\section{Global data that turn allowed geometry into exact Einstein
existence}\label{global-data-that-turn-allowed-geometry-into-exact-einstein-existence}}

24 September 2026. This continuation advances three separate existence
problems left by the \href{20260924_frontier_bridges.md}{previous
development}. The first detects global bundle topology from an input
metric; the second solves the full deformation problem in a
growing-cover family with genuine kernels; the third determines the
whole topology of a singular filling from an actual harmonic form. Each
has a complete English proof below.

\hypertarget{ricci-control-can-force-finite-monodromy-not-merely-small-local-deformation}{%
\subsection{1. Ricci control can force finite monodromy, not merely
small local
deformation}\label{ricci-control-can-force-finite-monodromy-not-merely-small-local-deformation}}

The \href{20260924_cycle_holonomy_realization.md}{cycle theorem}
concerns an oriented genus-\(f\) surface bundle over a closed surface of
genus at least two. Horizontal transport of the actual vertical metric
defines the accumulated conformal deformation \(\mathcal J_g(\gamma)\)
around a based loop. If a single reference fiber has hyperbolic
conformal systole at least \(\iota\), then \[
 \max_{\gamma\ \mathrm{in\ a\ generating\ set}}
       \mathcal J_g(\gamma)<\varepsilon_{f,\iota}
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \begin{gathered}
 \text{finite mapping-class monodromy},\\
 \text{exact negative KE metric on the same smooth total space}.
 \end{gathered}
\] Properness on the thick part of Teichmüller space forces the
generating classes into \textbf{one finite group}. Nielsen realization
and surface-bundle classification then construct the metric. Finite
monodromy is a conclusion, not an assumption. The theorem needs no small
Ricci error once the cycle condition is known.

The \href{20260924_ricci_monodromy_realization.md}{Ricci composition}
supplies that condition directly for a Riemannian surface submersion
with minimal fibers and hyperbolic base. O'Neill's definite horizontal
Ricci identity gives \(\|S_X\|\le\sqrt{\delta_-}|X|\). Short generators
of the base give the sufficient condition \[
        2\operatorname{diam}(B)\sqrt{\delta_-(g)}
                       <\varepsilon_{f,\iota}.              \tag{1}
\] The fiber metrics need not themselves be hyperbolic. There is no
bound on total-space diameter, injectivity radius or volume. The proof
also allows an arbitrary base metric, adding \(\|(K_B+1)_+\|_\infty\) to
\(\delta_-\) in (1).

This completes an actual pointwise-input-to-Einstein-output implication
for the stated submersion class, including the zero-signature case where
the older signature obstruction says nothing. It does not assert that an
arbitrary near-Einstein metric carries this global fibration.

Two actual Dehn-twist families distinguish the missing information:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  A thick fiber with a long base collar has \(\delta_-\to0\) and two
  globally closed, normalized opposite forms with
  \(\|\nabla\omega_\pm\|_\infty\to0\), but retains a nonzero accumulated
  cycle deformation.
\item
  A pinched fiber over a fixed base has the same local smallness and
  \(\mathcal J_g\to0\), but the reference conformal thickness vanishes.
\end{itemize}

Both lie in the actual bilateral monopole near-saturation regime and
have odd first Betti number, hence no Kähler metric. Neither is a proof
of general Einstein nonexistence. They show that even exact closedness
and uniform near-parallelism do not replace global cycle and moduli
control.

\hypertarget{all-teichmuxfcller-modes-can-be-retained-and-solved-with-a-sharp-scale-loss}{%
\subsection{2. All Teichmüller modes can be retained and solved, with a
sharp scale
loss}\label{all-teichmuxfcller-modes-can-be-retained-and-solved-with-a-sharp-scale-loss}}

The \href{20260924_integrable_kernel_cyclic_repair.md}{full-kernel
theorem} uses \(M_m=B_m\times F\), where \(B_m\) is a degree-\(m\)
cyclic cover of a fixed hyperbolic surface and \(F\) is fixed. The exact
reference metric is \(h_m=b_m+f\). Its gauged Einstein operator has the
full kernel \(T_{B_m}\oplus T_F\), whose dimension grows with \(m\).

The note proves a uniformly controlled family \(H_m(k)\) integrating
\textbf{every} kernel direction. It proves the remaining equations
vanish on that actual family, rather than deleting their projection. For
every tensor perturbation in harmonic gauge with
\(\|g-h_m\|_{C^{3,\alpha}_{\rm uloc}}<c m^{-2}\), \[
 \|g-H_m(\Pi_m(g-h_m))\|_{C^{3,\alpha}_{\rm uloc}}
       \le C m^2\|\operatorname{Ric}_g+g\|_{C^{1,\alpha}_{\rm uloc}}.
                                                               \tag{2}
\] The constants are independent of cover degree. The proof controls the
growing-rank TT projection, establishes the scalar Green estimate in the
actual supremum norm, and retains the complete tensor decomposition.

The loss in (2) is not a weakness removable by better bookkeeping.
Actual smooth metrics satisfy \[
 \|\operatorname{Ric}_{g_m}+g_m\|_{C^{1,\alpha}_{\rm uloc}}
       \le C m^{-4},\qquad
 \operatorname{dist}_{C^{3,\alpha}_{\rm uloc}}
       (g_m,\{\operatorname{Ric}_h=-h\})\ge c m^{-2}.          \tag{3}
\] The distance is to \textbf{all} normalized Einstein metrics,
including diffeomorphic pullbacks, not only a selected product family. A
slow harmonic-circle function multiplying a fiber Teichmüller
deformation produces (3). Local geometry remains uniformly bounded and
noncollapsed.

The prior positive-coercivity repair theorem genuinely excluded these
kernels. The new result solves them in this family, but the inverse must
pay for slow spatial coefficients even after every exact zero mode is
integrated. These manifolds already admit Einstein metrics; the result
quantifies repair and its failure of uniformity, not new smooth
topology.

\hypertarget{an-actual-harmonic-form-can-determine-the-whole-singular-filling}{%
\subsection{3. An actual harmonic form can determine the whole singular
filling}\label{an-actual-harmonic-form-can-determine-the-whole-singular-filling}}

The \href{20260924_harmonic_contact_milnor_matching.md}{harmonic/contact
theorem} starts with the previous bounded-diameter gauge-saturated
orbifold limit. Let \(H_j\) be the self-dual harmonic representative of
the actual class projection. The new analytic step upgrades its known
mean alignment to \[
               -2\pi H_j\longrightarrow\omega_\infty
                       \quad\text{in }C^{1,\beta}
\] on every fixed regular collar. Thus the actual boundary of each whole
singular filling carries the canonical \textbf{strong convex} contact
structure.

If \(H_j\) is zero-free on that whole filling, and either the limiting
singularity is \(A_1\) with filling \(b_2=1\), or the filling is a
rational homology ball, classical symplectic filling theory determines
its smooth type. In the rational-ball case it also forces the
singularity to be Wahl. Explicit extension of every possible boundary
mapping class then proves the \textbf{relative, actually marked Milnor
matching} previously assumed. This applies to the whole filling, with
any internal bubble scales.

A global Q-Gorenstein smoothing now gives a negative KE metric on the
original smooth \(M_j\). The remaining assumptions are actual
zero-freeness in the cores, the stated whole-filling homology, and
global smoothability. Small averaged deficit has not been asserted to
imply them. Conversely a non-Wahl singular point filled by a rational
homology ball forces a zero of this specified harmonic form in its core.
