# Decreasing Weyl's energy by connected sums with locally conformally flat manifolds

Andrea Malchiodi and Francesco Malizia ∗

## Abstract

We study the Weyl functional on connected sums of two four-dimensional manifolds (M, gM) and (Z, gZ), assuming gM is Bach-flat and gZ locally conformally flat. We show that if gM is neither self dual nor anti self-dual and if gZ is of positive Yamabe class, there exists a metric gY on Y := M#Z with Weyl energy lower than that of gM (with the trivial exception of (Z, gZ) = (S 4, gS 4 )). This result has a relation to a conjecture by I.Singer and has a perspective application to the minimization of Weyl’s energy. The proof relies on a simultaneous interplay of W + M, W−M and the topology of Z, and also covers some orbifold cases. Key Words: Weyl Functional, Self-Duality, Locally Conformally Flat Manifolds. MSC 2020: 53C21, 53C25, 58E11, 49J99.
