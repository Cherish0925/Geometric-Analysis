## Deep-Reasoning

This Preceptor step reviewed the Step-40 diagonal toric reframe against the exact Yau-26 target and corrected several load-bearing overclaims before they could become route drift. The full target remains unchanged: decide the dimension-uniform two-sided Ricci-pinching proposition for all closed connected smooth n-manifolds, all n≥2, with a complete proof or counterexample and eventual Lean formalization. The diagonal toric work is at most a four-dimensional positive-sign symmetry-restricted laboratory; it cannot by itself prove or disprove Yau-26 because the antecedent does not assume symmetry, the output Einstein metric need not be toric or close to the input metric, and no topology in the diagonal stress family is known to admit no Einstein metric. I source-checked Liu's toric Einstein paper and verified the strongest part of Step 40: Liu Theorem 3.1 gives geometric polarity for any closed simply connected Einstein 4-manifold with isometric T^2 action, and Proposition 5 gives diagonal action implies diagonal metric without assuming nonnegative sectional curvature. Together with the elementary rod-length argument for diag(a²,b²), this certifies that the alternating ±e1, ±e2 polygon is the full diagonal-action sector under exact Einstein and closed simply connected hypotheses. This must be recorded with two qualifications: ± signs are only primitive representatives of unoriented isotropy circles, and a closed alternating polygon must have even m, so Step-40 odd-m topology labels are invalid geometry probes only. Page, CLW, and toric KE dP3 are outside the diagonal alternating ansatz because they have three or more circle classes, but they remain useful calibrations for more general polar equations and normalizations, not irrelevant to all toric work. The reduced orbit problem is also clearer: Liu proves at most one invariant Einstein completion for a fixed torus action and fixed quotient metric g_Σ, via the orbit-volume eigenfunction, the linear equation for g^Ω entries, and the smooth-even uniqueness lemma. Existence still requires nonlinear determinant closure det(g^Ω)=φ², horizontal Einstein compatibility, edge ODEs with endpoint derivative data, positivity, and corner smoothness. Thus overdetermined is legitimate as a global compatibility description, not as a proved Fredholm count. The Step-40 product-rigidity calculation is not reliable as written. Direct linearization of the orbit-volume equation at the product square, with g_Σ=e^{2εs}g0 and ψ=δ(ab)=α sin y + β sin x, gives -(Δ0+2)ψ=4s sin x sin y because the two-dimensional conformal Laplacian scales without a Δs term. Step 40's equation for α, -(Δ0+1)α=(Δ0s-2s)sin x, is therefore not the direct Liu Proposition 2 linearization unless an absent elimination using horizontal equations is supplied. Its kernel mode α=sin x also violates the full rod derivative correction at fixed Λ=1, so it cannot be used as a harmless rescaling mode without explicit gauge. Product isolation remains plausible but unproved. I also tested the constant-curvature regular-polygon subansatz more sharply. A first finite-element script used the wrong Poincare mass weight ρ² and was immediately superseded. The corrected script uses Euclidean Dirichlet energy and mass weight ρ=4/(1-r²)² for the Poincare disk. It estimates the principal Dirichlet eigenvalue μ_m of regular right-angled hyperbolic polygons and compares the eigenvalue curvature A_eig=2/μ_m with the Liu rod/side curvature A_side. For the first genuine alternating case m=6, the corrected and convergent data give μ6≈6.973, A_eig≈0.2868, A_side≈0.2710, λ1 at A_side≈1.88945, and rod/side defect at A_eig≈1.84 percent. This is better evidence than Step 40's Faber-Krahn bound because it directly tests the actual regular constant-curvature condition, but it also weakens the large-gap narrative: the earlier 68.7 percent gap is an A→∞ asymptotic, not the eigenvalue-selected regime. The constant-K regular hexagon appears numerically inconsistent, but only as a near miss and only in a restricted model; variable curvature, nonregular polygons, determinant closure, horizontal equations, non-diagonal toric metrics, and arbitrary Einstein metrics remain untouched. A targeted knowledge request did not find a b_+≥2 obstruction, and reading Broder's cohomogeneity-one classification confirmed that strong classification is known for dim Isom≥3 while cohomogeneity-two remains only partially understood; the b_+≤1 observation is a useful pattern, not a theorem. I read A03's Step-40 staggered all-order continuation note and A01's Step-38 repair note enough to judge allocation: the negative cutoff-flow derivative induction is real conditional progress, but it still assumes a smooth positive-time slab and sup|U| control and lacks transported-coefficient start/restart plus a bridge from mere Ricci-endomorphism pinching to U-control. I called TTCS verifier on the Step-40/41 package and read both branches plus the integration; it independently confirmed the Liu-source certification, the reduced-problem caveats, the product-linearization defect, the corrected FEM interpretation, and the route-allocation judgment. I saved the full review in workspace/notes/step41_preceptor_review_diagonal_route_and_allocation.md, patched the Step-40 note with a supersession warning, and sent session-group message #353.
