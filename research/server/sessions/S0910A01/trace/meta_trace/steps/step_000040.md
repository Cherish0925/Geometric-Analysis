This step critically re-centered the negative Ishida--LeBrun flow route around the mathematical interfaces that still control whether the restricted four-dimensional result is real. The Step 39 synthesis is scientifically useful: it brought together the near-critical Seiberg--Witten/Chern--Weil calibration, the Derdzinski forbidden Einstein germ, selected-support smoothing, global Q propagation, terminal recalibration, and exponential pullback. But a synthesis of earlier conditional modules is not the same as a proof. A03's staggered all-order derivative mechanism is a genuine advance because it repairs the finite-exponent loop for evolving cutoff jets, especially the dangerous chi*C_{m+1}*U_1*P(U_m) term. Yet that mechanism applies only after the normalized weighted-U, unweighted-T, G, normalization, connection, and cutoff-jet structural equations have actually been derived. Likewise, A03's transported DeTurck architecture has the correct gauge idea, but the normalized Yang adaptation still needs the epsilon-uniform tame estimates, regularization limit, local uniqueness, and restart theorem written at source level. The correct downstream passage is already in Step 28: inner plateau centers, Liouville--Rauch averaging on the whole center set, radius shrinkage, and strong finite-W^{2,p} convergence or smooth convergence from plateau derivative estimates. The broader Yau 26 logic was also corrected: in each dimension, the original interval proposition is equivalent to one forcing sign branch P_n^+ or P_n^-; proving both signs is stronger than necessary, while refutation in one fixed dimension must defeat both sign branches. A02's positive noncollapsed Route A is a promising complementary four-dimensional line through Cheeger--Naber harmonic-radius control and mode projection/Ozuch obstruction, but it remains conditional on noncollapse and a Bianchi-gauge radial ODE, and it does not repair the negative-flow theorem. The full problem remains open: positive collapse, arbitrary four-dimensional topology, dimensions n>=5, dimension-only forcing constants, and Lean formalization are not covered by the restricted negative-family architecture.
