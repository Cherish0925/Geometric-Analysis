# Critical metrics on connected sums of Einstein four-manifolds

 Authors: Matthew J. Gursky , Jeff A. Viaclovsky 

### Abstract

We develop a gluing procedure designed to obtain canonical metrics on connected sums of Einstein four-manifolds. The main application is an existence result, using two well-known Einstein manifolds as building blocks: the Fubini-Study metric on $CP2\mathbb{CP}^2$ CP 2 and the product metric on $S2×S2S^2 \times S^2$ S 2× S 2. Using these metrics in various gluing configurations, critical metrics are found on connected sums for a specific Riemannian functional, which depends on the global geometry of the factors. Furthermore, using certain quotients of $S2×S2S^2 \times S^2$ S 2× S 2 as one of the gluing factors, critical metrics on several non-simply-connected manifolds are also obtained.
