These identities are consistent with the K_X framework and provide additional constraints on the charge distribution.

I initially claimed that because the drift curvature Ric_f contains +Qγ, the Bochner gains +Qe which is favorable and can be dropped to recover the OT ceiling. This would have made the OT obstruction δ<0.246 apply automatically to all charged connectors. The net twist contribution is 3Q⟨∇σ, d(β∘U)⟩ = -3Q⟨∇α,∇σ⟩ (diagonal constant charge), which has no fixed sign and depends on the orbit-area gradient correlation. I verified this cancellation symbolically. The tension estimates become |τ_σ| ≤ Q + Cδ, so the resolvent source is Q-dependent and the OT ceiling cannot be directly used.

The structural consequence is profound: the net twist Bochner contribution vanishes at both the cusp (Q→0) and the separator (|∇σ|→0), concentrating in the transition region of bounded base extent. This means the cusp behavior of the orbit energy is effectively OT regardless of twist, while the transition region accumulates the Q-dependent cost. The transition-region localization is the key new insight for the next steps.
