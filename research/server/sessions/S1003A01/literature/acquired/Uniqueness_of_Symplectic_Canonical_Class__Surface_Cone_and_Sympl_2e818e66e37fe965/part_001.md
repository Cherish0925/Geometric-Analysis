Let M be a smooth, closed oriented 4-manifold. An orientation-compatible symplectic structure on M is a closed 2-form ω such that

First of all, on Ω M , there is a natural equivalence relation, the de-formation equivalence. ω1 and ω2 in Ω M are said to be deformation equivalent if there is an orientation-preserving diffeomorphism φ such that φ∗ω1 and ω2 are connected by a path of symplectic forms. Clearly, the group of orientation-preserving diffeomorphisms acts on the set of connected components of Ω M , and the number of deformation classes of symplectic structures is just the number of the orbits of this action. Secondly, there is a map of canonical class K : Ω M −→ H 2(M ; Z).
