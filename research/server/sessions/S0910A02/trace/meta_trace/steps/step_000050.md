## Deep-Reasoning

This step independently verified the load-bearing part of the Step-49 positive noncollapsed product-quotient analysis and clarified what it can and cannot deliver for the full Yau--26 proposition. The key mathematical point is the order separation in the Yau--26 norm: on an Eguchi--Hanson bubble with metric scale t, a refined residual of size t^2 rho^2 in the EH norm becomes t rho^2 after raising an index by g_t^{-1}, hence is O(t) on the core and O(t^{1/2}) on the standard damage annulus rho ~ t^{-1/4}; by contrast, a first obstruction term t lambda o becomes order one, while second and higher obstruction terms become O(t) or smaller. Thus, for C^0 Ricci-endomorphism pinching, the vector first obstruction R_epsilon(p) zeta = 0 is the decisive local condition for the explicit refined approximate metrics, whereas higher Ozuch obstructions matter for exact Einstein desingularization but not for producing input metrics with ||Ric - Lambda g||_op -> 0. I checked the non-routine mixed-row point: Ozuch lines 372--376 state that negatively oriented EH is isometric/homothetic to ordinary EH but with opposite orientation, and lines 496--514 give the same h2/h4 construction with first obstruction coefficient lambda_k(zeta^pm)=c< R^pm(H2) zeta^pm, zeta_(k)^pm >. Therefore reversed bubbles inherit the same local estimate but test R_- rather than R_+. At the product quotient (S^2 x S^2)/Z2, Steps 44--45 already verified R_+ = R_- = diag(Lambda,0,0), so kernel directions kill the first obstruction for every chirality assignment. This proves the positive product-quotient family is not merely a formal higher-obstruction loophole: for each fixed mixed orientation assignment, the refined four-bubble construction gives a sequence of positive near-Einstein metrics on the corresponding smooth resolution, with a clean model rate O(t^{1/2}) or at least a source-safe Ozuch weighted rate O(t^{1/2-beta/4}) tending to zero. The original Biquard 2013 paper could not be retrieved directly; the four-dimensional estimate is grounded here through Ozuch's internal construction and his explicit citation to Biquard Section 14 at lines 783--789, with Morteza--Viaclovsky lines 456--514 used only as exponent calibration rather than as the four-dimensional source itself.

The topological meaning is a conditional-hardness reduction, not a refutation and not an Einstein existence theorem. Armstrong's theorem supports simple-connectedness of the quotient because the nontrivial Z2 element has fixed points, and the standard RP3 fillings by simply connected +/-2 disk bundles preserve simple-connectedness by the van Kampen picture. The rational invariants for r reversed bubbles are chi=8 and (b_+,b_-)=(1+r,5-r). The mixed rows r=1,2,3 have b_+ >= 2, strict Hitchin--Thorpe, and positive scalar curvature metrics coming from the near-positive-Einstein construction. Standard Seiberg--Witten obstructions are therefore unavailable: the Six Lectures source states that positive scalar curvature forces only reducible solutions and empty SW moduli spaces for generic PSC metrics when b_+>0, and LeBrun's Yamabe source says the usual SW invariant vanishes when Y(M)>0. Hitchin--Thorpe is silent because 2 chi = 16 > 3|tau| for all rows. Del Pezzo endpoint rows are known-Einstein calibrations at the diffeotype level by LeBrun--Ozuch lines 157--170, but the mixed rows are not covered by that existence theorem. Hence, if the positive four-dimensional branch P4+ is true, these mixed smooth manifolds must carry some Einstein metric; proving the contrary would require a new no-Einstein theorem on positive-scalar-curvature b_+>=2 four-manifolds beyond the current SW/HT toolkit. The balanced r=2 row may be homeomorphic to #3(S^2 x S^2) if the even boundary-gluing case is realized, but exact spin/smooth type is not needed for the reduction and remains unproved. A corrected toric lattice probe replaced an erroneous first computation: the valid all-compatible fan has Picard determinant -1, signature (1,5), odd form, and four disjoint (-2) exceptional curves; the bad determinant -1296 was marked superseded. A discriminant-glue model showed that r=2 parity can vary with the two-node choice under the same-fractional-glue model, reinforcing that spin should not be asserted without Kirby/boundary data.

Peer message A01 #378 was read and incorporated cautiously. Its Q-Gorenstein smoothing/Aubin--Yau mechanism is a valuable existence route for general-type product-quotient surfaces under global QG smoothing, and it may explain why kernel-directed EH directions are complex smoothings in holomorphic settings. But A01's own note distinguishes the positive S^2 x S^2 quotient case: the ample-canonical Aubin--Yau chain does not cover the positive mixed rows, and mixed chirality may be non-holomorphic. Thus #378 does not invalidate the Step-50 positive-branch hardness wall. Peer A03 #379 reported major progress on a normalized Yang-flow estimate for the negative branch; I did not independently check it in this step. The full Yau--26 target remains open: this step settles only the local positive noncollapsed product-quotient ceiling, leaving positive collapse, the negative branch, dimensions n>=5, the normalized H4-dual necessity theorem, exact mixed-row smooth classifications, and Lean formalization unresolved.
